## Draft 2 — LinkedIn Text Post

*Gap topic: AI agent supervision for mid-market business owners. Topic none of the monitored creators covered.*

---

An AI agent went viral this week for watching YouTube instead of working.

Everyone found it funny.

Business owners should be paying closer attention.

If you are running AI agents in your operations — or planning to — the question nobody is asking is this:

Who is checking what your agent is actually doing?

The agent visibility gap is the real story this year.

Automation tools promise output. They do not promise oversight.

A poorly supervised agent does not just waste time. It makes decisions on your behalf. In your name. Using your data. Without telling you.

The mid-market business running agents without a check-in system is not ahead of the curve. It has created a risk it cannot see.

Here is what responsible agent deployment looks like in a business that takes this seriously:

One. A defined task boundary — what the agent may complete and what it must flag before acting.

Two. A daily log — what it accessed, what it decided, what it sent.

Three. A human review point — not optional, not monthly, every week.

This is not complicated. It does not require a technical team.

It requires treating your AI like a new employee — not a vending machine.

Does your current AI setup have a supervision layer?

If yes, what does it look like? If not, what is stopping you from building one?

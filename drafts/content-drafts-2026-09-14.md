## Draft 2 — LinkedIn Text Post
**Angle:** Gap topic — the AI decision audit for non-technical CEOs
**Topic:** No creator covered this. This brand could own it.

---

You are about to give an AI agent access to your business data.

Before you do, answer this: what is the cost if it gets this wrong?

Not in theory. In your business. Your operations. This week.

That one question separates CEOs who build real internal capability from those who create expensive liability and call it progress.

AI agents are not failing because the technology is weak. They are failing because no one drew the decision boundaries before switching them on.

There are three categories of decision your business makes every day.

**Category 1 — Reversible, low-stakes decisions.**
Examples: routing an email, drafting a first-pass document, summarising a meeting. The cost of an error is minor and fixable. These belong to AI.

**Category 2 — Reversible decisions with external visibility.**
Examples: a client communication, a vendor proposal, a public response. A human reviews before it goes out. AI prepares; a person approves.

**Category 3 — Irreversible decisions with financial, legal, or reputational weight.**
Examples: a pricing commitment, a contract, a personnel decision. These stay with you. No exceptions.

Most AI implementation failures mix all three categories into one system, then express surprise when something goes wrong at the Category 3 level.

The audit takes 90 minutes. One operations lead, one whiteboard, three columns.

No consultant required.

Mid-market CEOs: which category of decision does your team currently hand to AI without any review step? That is where your risk is accumulating right now.

That is the wrong model.

The businesses building real internal capability right now are doing the opposite.
They are designing the decision points.

Not because the AI cannot keep going.

Here is what that looks like in practice:

Your AI handles the research.
It flags three options.

You pick one.

Your AI writes the first draft.
It identifies two weak sections.
It asks which one to address first.
You decide.

You are not the bottleneck.
You are the authority.

The companies that will own AI-driven advantage in the next 24 months are not the ones that gave AI the most freedom.
They are the ones that designed the hand-off points most deliberately.

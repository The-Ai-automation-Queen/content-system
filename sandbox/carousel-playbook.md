# Carousel Playbook — One Message Per Slide, Pain → Relief

**Brand:** The AI Automation Queen — *automation for freedom*
**For:** Fatiha Chikh · **Updated:** 23/06/2026 (re-anchored to the new positioning)
**Load with:** `positioning/SKILL.md` (identity + 6 pillars) and `inspiration-library/SKILL.md` (hook patterns).

> **Why this rewrite:** the brand moved off the CEO/enterprise lane to helping
> everyday entrepreneurs win back their time. The carousel *craft* below is
> unchanged — but every example now serves a time-poor, non-technical solopreneur,
> in the casual voice (contractions welcome), mapped to the six current pillars.

---

## Who you're talking to (so the pain lands)

A capable, mid-career person — still in corporate or recently out — who wants to build
their own thing and **buy back their time.** Smart, but overwhelmed by AI and mostly
non-technical.

**What they feel (lead with this):** "I'm drowning doing everything by hand." · "Nothing
happens unless I do it." · "I bought all these AI tools and I'm still busy." · "I can't
take a day off without it all stopping."

**What they want (sell this):** time back · a business that runs without them · work
less, live more · freedom, not another tool to babysit.

**Voice:** casual, warm, a little provocative. Contractions welcome. No corporate
jargon, no "synergy," no engagement bait. Tone = the brilliant friend who's a little
impatient with you doing things the hard way.

---

## Rule 1 — One message per slide

Each slide carries **exactly one idea**, readable in under two seconds.

- **≤ 12 words of body copy per slide.** Needs a second sentence to land? That's two slides.
- **Big text, no paragraphs.** A slide is a billboard, not a page.
- **One idea = one job.** A slide either names the pain, twists it, reframes it, or relieves it. Never two jobs on one slide.
- **The swipe test:** read only the headlines in sequence. If the story still makes sense with nothing else on the slides, the spine is right.

Why it's mechanical, not stylistic: one clean idea per slide raises dwell time (they swipe to finish the thought) and saves (each slide is screenshot-able). Saves + dwell time are what the algorithm rewards.

---

## Rule 2 — The Pain → Relief arc

Every carousel walks these eight beats. One slide per beat (you can split "the shift" across two, never merge beats).

| # | Beat | Its one job | What it does to the reader |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cover — name the pain** | Their pain, in their words, as the hook | "That's me." Stops the scroll. |
| 2 | **Agitate** | Make the pain concrete + a little worse | "Yeah… and it's costing me." Earns the swipe. |
| 3 | **Reframe** | The real problem isn't what they thought | "Oh. I've been fixing the wrong thing." |
| 4 | **The shift** | The one idea that changes it | Hope shows up. |
| 5 | **Shift made real** | A concrete example / proof it works | "This is actually doable." |
| 6 | **The relief** | Paint the freedom on the other side | Desire. "I want that." |
| 7 | **The bridge** | The small first step (not scary) | "I could start this week." |
| 8 | **CTA** | One specific next action | They act while the desire is warm. |

**The cover is 80% of the result.** Before you design anything, apply the test: *"Is this good enough to stop someone mid-scroll?"* A cover that names a pain they feel gets read. A cover that states a fact gets skipped.

---

## Rule 3 — Lead with the pain, sell the freedom, never the feature

They don't want "an automation framework." That's a feature. They want the **relief**:
their evenings back, a business that doesn't collapse when they log off.

**Translation rule:** on every slide ask — *"is this about the tool, or about their life?"*
If it's about the tool, rewrite it toward what they feel or what they get back.

> Off-brand: "Automate your lead-capture pipeline for operational efficiency."
> On-brand: "New leads keep coming in — even on the days you don't show up."

---

## Rule 4 — Every carousel serves ONE pillar

Map it before you write (positioning Pattern 15). The six:

1. **Time Wins** — a specific move that saves real hours this week.
2. **Build Once, Runs Forever** — a system that keeps working while you sleep.
3. **The Freedom Business** — work less, live more; a business that runs without you.
4. **Stop Doing That by Hand** — "you're STILL doing this manually?" before/after.
5. **What's Worth It** — the rare tool/news that actually matters for solopreneurs.
6. **Real Talk** — the corporate → entrepreneur journey; relatable founder moments.

If a carousel idea fits none, question whether to post it.

# Worked Example A — "The Freedom Business Test," upgraded

Your vault **ENTRY 007** is already on-brand and good — but it's built as a *checklist*
(4 things), which is coverage, not a felt journey. Here it is re-sequenced into the
pain → relief arc and tightened to one message per slide. Same pillar (**The Freedom
Business**), same 8 slides — stronger spine.

| Slide | Beat | On-slide copy (one message) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pain | **"Your business isn't free if it stops the second you do."** |
| 2 | Agitate | "Take two weeks off, no laptop. What breaks first? That's your weak link." |
| 3 | Reframe | "Freedom isn't passive income. It's a business that runs without you." |
| 4 | The shift | "Four things have to keep running while you're gone. Most people have zero." |
| 5 | Shift made real | "Leads still come in. Content still goes out. Questions still get answered. Money still moves." |
| 6 | Relief | "Picture it: you disappear for two weeks — and nothing breaks." |
| 7 | Bridge | "You don't fix all four today. You fix the weakest one first." |
| 8 | CTA | "Which of the 4 is weakest for you? Comment 1–4 and I'll tell you where to start." |

**What changed vs. ENTRY 007:** the cover now names the *pain* (was "isn't free if it
stops when you do" — close, but slides 3–6 listed the four parts as a checklist). The
four parts are compressed into **one** "shift made real" slide (slide 5) so each beat
gets its own slide and the arc climbs into a relief beat (slide 6) it didn't have before.
Keep the existing caption — it already works.

## The 60-second pre-design checklist

- [ ] The cover names a **pain they feel**, not a topic or a fact.
- [ ] Reading only the **headlines** in order still tells the story.
- [ ] **No slide carries two ideas.** Split if it does.
- [ ] Body copy on every slide is **≤ 12 words**.
- [ ] There's a clear **relief slide** (beat 6) where they feel desire.
- [ ] The **final slide = one specific action** (comment a number, comment a keyword, follow, or join the community).
- [ ] Casual voice, contractions fine, **zero jargon** — read it aloud.
- [ ] It serves **one** of the six pillars.

1
Know Before You Eat
The chef is brilliant, but not perfect. Read this before you order.
Before you sit down, know this: even the best chef makes mistakes. Sometimes they confidently say "this is nut-free" when it's not, that's a hallucination. Sometimes they fill gaps with a convincing story instead of saying "I don't know", that's confabulation. They might agree with everything you say even when you're wrong, that's sycophancy. Someone could slip a fake ticket into the kitchen to hijack your order, that's prompt injection. The chef might accidentally reveal another table's secrets, that's data leakage. They carry unconscious habits from their training, that's bias. And sometimes they state things as certain when they're actually guessing, that's overconfidence. Knowing these risks makes you a smarter customer. Now let's meet the chef.
Now meet this restaurant's chef
2
The Chef & Their Brain
Before anyone walks in, the chef already exists. This is who they are.
The current chef is Kimi K2.6, released April 2026. A huge model that uses only a small slice of its brain per question, both powerful and cost-efficient. Ties with GPT-class on coding benchmarks at roughly 80% less. Handles ~256K tokens of context. Deep Thinking mode chains up to 300 reasoning steps before answering. Multi-modal: text, images, video. Every word is a token. The signature trick: Agent Swarm, up to 300 mini-assistants working in parallel.
A customer walks in through one of these doors
3
The Front Door
Multiple ways to enter this restaurant
Main entrance is kimi.com, free tier plus paid memberships from $19/month. Kimi Code is a free terminal-based coding tool, similar to Claude Code. Moonshot API for plugging Kimi into your own software. Open-source weights: download and self-host on your own GPUs.
You place your order, how it's written matters
4
The Order Slip
Instructions and context that shape every dish
Every order has layers. Deep Thinking toggle tells the chef to chain up to 300 reasoning steps. Web search toggle grounds in live information. Agent Swarm toggle (paid tiers) splits the task across many parallel agents. System prompt on the API for persistent persona. And prompt engineering is learning to write clearer tickets, especially important for autonomous coding sessions.
The order slip goes to the kitchen
5
The Kitchen
Where the real work happens, code, research, slides, sites
The order arrives. Coding is the signature station, world-class. Deep Research reads many sources and writes a sourced report. Slide + website generation turns descriptions into full decks and pages. Visual coding turns screenshots into working code. Memory remembers preferences across sessions. Web search brings live info.
For complex workflows, the team system kicks in
6
The Kitchen Team
Agent Swarm, autonomous coding, cloud deploy
Agent Swarm is Kimi's headline: up to 300 mini-assistants on one task in parallel, signature of Allegro and Vivace tiers. Autonomous long-running coding: Kimi can work hours on a refactor without supervision. Cloud deployment: Allegro+ lets you deploy generated apps directly. Self-host stack for orgs that need full data control.
The kitchen connects to outside suppliers
7
The Supply Chain
How the kitchen connects to the outside world
Web search brings live information. File uploads let you bring your own ingredients. Moonshot API: OpenAI-compatible, significantly cheaper than Western labs. Open-source weights on Hugging Face. Cursor partnership: Cursor's Composer 2 runs on Kimi, a major real-world validation. Fewer connectors: limited Slack/Drive/Notion integrations out of the box.
The dish gets plated and delivered to you
8
Plating & Delivery
How the finished dish reaches you
The dish lands as a text answer, with thinking-mode showing reasoning when on. Code blocks ready to paste from Kimi Code or chat. Generated slide decks and websites on paid tiers. Research reports with citations. Deployed apps from cloud deploy on Allegro+. No own image gen, no video gen, no native voice on the hosted product.
After service, the restaurant reviews and improves
9
Where Kimi Fits
When to pick this kitchen over the others
Kimi wins on parallel agents: Agent Swarm is unique, no other AI runs 300 agents on one task. It wins on coding: GPT-class quality at ~80% less. It wins on autonomous coding sessions: hours of unsupervised refactoring. It wins on open-source weights. It loses on general writing polish vs Claude. It loses on privacy for compliance-heavy orgs unless you self-host. It carries the distillation controversy reputationally. Pick Kimi for heavy coding workloads, agent-heavy workflows, and high parallelism, especially via Cursor.
Wrapping around everything, the rules, limits, and the bill
10
Health, Safety & The Bill
The non-negotiables that wrap around everything
This zone wraps around everything. Hosted data residency: China. Content rules follow Chinese regulation. Pricing uses music-themed names: Free for basic use, Moderato $19 for regular users, Allegretto $39 for power users, Allegro $99 unlocks Agent Swarm + cloud deploy, Vivace $199 for max agents and highest limits. Developer API priced per token, much cheaper than Western labs. Open-source self-host is free.

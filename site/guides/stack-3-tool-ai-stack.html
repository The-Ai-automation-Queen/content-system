The 3-tool AI stack I actually use.

What is the 3-tool AI stack? The 3-tool AI stack is the brain, the megaphone, and the net, the minimum tools that run a business without you doing everything by hand. Everybody wants the giant list of 50 AI tools. You don’t need 50. You need three that talk to each other and quietly run the boring parts of your business while you live your life. Here’s the exact short list, no affiliate fluff, just what’s earning its place in my own setup right now.

Free, in your inbox

Get every guide in this library, plus one plain-English AI briefing every Tuesday with a clear verdict on each story.

1. The Brain. Claude (and Claude Code)

What it replaces: the blank page, the “I’ll write it later,” the VA you keep meaning to hire.

This is the thinking layer. It drafts my content, sorts my research, and, with Claude Code, actually runs multi-step jobs for me instead of just answering questions. I describe the outcome once; it does the work on a schedule.

Win-back-your-time move: stop using AI as a fancy search box. Give it a job, one repeatable task, start to finish, and let it own that job.

2. The Megaphone. Blotato

What it replaces: logging into 6 apps to post the same thing 6 times.

I write or record once and Blotato pushes it to every platform from one place, and it builds the visuals and short videos too. One queue, one review, done.

Win-back-your-time move: batch a week of content in one sitting, queue it, and approve from your phone. You never “go post” again.

3. The Net. GoHighLevel (your CRM + follow-up)

What it replaces: DMs you forgot to answer and leads that went cold.

Reach is worthless if it leaks. This catches the people who raise their hand (comment a keyword, opt in), sends them the thing instantly, and follows up automatically so you’re not doing it by hand at 11pm.

Win-back-your-time move: wire it so a comment becomes a captured lead with a 3-message follow-up, without you touching it. That’s the Lead Follow-Up Setup guide.

The whole point

Brain → Megaphone → Net. Create once, post everywhere, never drop a lead. Three tools, running the parts of the business that used to run you.

Win back your time. Let the systems do the heavy lifting.

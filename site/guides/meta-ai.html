Tool verdict / Meta

Meta AI

Free, already inside WhatsApp and Instagram, and you are the product.

  • Best forQuick answers and quick images in the apps you already have open.
  • Careful withAnything about your business, your numbers or your customers.
  • What I would doFine for personal use. Keep your business out of it.

Where it earns its keep

Meta AI has one advantage nothing else can match: it is already on your phone. Not as a new app you have to remember to open, but inside WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger, where you already are forty times a day. Type at MetaAI in any chat and it answers.

For quick things that is genuinely convenient. What is the conversion for this, write me a caption for this photo, settle an argument in a group chat. It generates images in the conversation, which is fun and occasionally useful, and it will make stickers.

The other genuinely interesting piece is Llama, Meta's open-weight model family. Those you can download and run yourself, and they are the foundation of most of the open AI ecosystem. If your developer says they are building on Llama, that is a real and sensible technical choice, and it is a different conversation from the consumer product on your phone.

Where it'll burn you

Meta's business is advertising. That is not a criticism, it is the model, and it has been for twenty years. What it means here is that the interests behind this free assistant are not the same as the interests behind a twenty dollar a month subscription. When you pay for a tool, you are the customer. When you do not, the economics point somewhere else.

In practice: be careful what you tell it. Your pricing, your margins, a difficult client, a staff problem, an idea you have not launched yet. There are places to think out loud about those things and a free assistant inside a social network is not one of them.

On capability, it is behind. For real work, drafting, analysis, anything with several steps in it, ChatGPT and Claude are better and it is not close. Meta AI is built for quick consumer questions and it is good at exactly that.

There is also a boundary problem worth naming. Because it lives inside your personal messaging, the line between personal and business gets thin. You will be halfway through asking it something about a client before you have thought about where you are.

Do this

Draw the line now, while it is easy. Meta AI for personal questions and images. A paid tool for anything with your business in it. Decide it once and you never have to think about it again.

When you are not paying for the tool, work out what is paying for it instead.

What I'd actually do

Use it for what it is good at and no further. Quick questions, quick images, settling something in a group chat. It costs nothing and it is already there.

Then keep your business on a paid tool where the arrangement is straightforward: you pay money, they provide a service, and on the business tiers they contractually do not train on your data. Twenty dollars a month is a small price for not having to think about this again.

The words you will keep hearing

  • Llama. Meta's open-weight model family. Free to download, and the base of most open-source AI work.
  • Imagine. Image generation inside the chat.
  • AI Studio. Build a custom character and put it in your Instagram or Messenger. Aimed at creators.
  • Open weights. Model files published for anyone to download and run, under a community licence with conditions attached.

Is it the right one for you?

Yes for personal use, and yes as a developer platform if your team is building on Llama, where the open weights are a genuine advantage.

No as your business assistant. Not because it is bad, but because free from an advertising company is a different arrangement from paid from a software company, and for anything involving your customers you want the second one.

The Door. What you're walking into.

First course · 2 min

Meta AI doesn't have a front door you have to find, because it's already standing inside every door you use. Open WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Facebook: it's there, one @mention away. Put on a pair of Ray-Ban Meta glasses and it's on your face, listening for "Hey Meta." Go looking for a dedicated address and you'll find meta.ai and the Meta AI app too, but the real trick of this kitchen is that it never made you leave the room you were already in. (And yes, even the best chef makes mistakes. The allergen card comes right before you eat, in course four.)

Under the hood, the chef cooking every one of those replies is Llama: Meta's own model family, and the most widely downloaded open-source AI on Earth. That's the second thing worth knowing before you sit down: you can also take this recipe home and run your own kitchen.

The Kitchen. How it actually works.

Second course · the floor plan

One chef, four rooms. The brain is Llama. The app surfaces are meta.ai, the standalone app, and, the one that makes this kitchen different, WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger, where you just type @MetaAI into a chat you're already having. The workspace is AI Studio, where creators build their own custom AI characters. And the API is Llama's developer counter, for anyone building their own product on top.

Same chef, four counters. The skill is knowing which one you're standing at: that's what the floor plan is for.

The floor plan

The chef is the Llama family, Meta's flagship model series. Llama 4 is the current generation: multi-modal, it reads text, images and voice, and competitive with GPT-class models. The context window reaches long-document scale, and every word it reads or writes is a token. The signature edge is that Llama is open-source: anyone can download the weights, audit them, run them privately, build on them. It's the largest open-source AI ecosystem in the world. The hosted Meta AI experience runs Llama under the hood, plus your social-platform context, contacts, group chats, posts, for personalisation.

Where Meta AI Fits. When to pick this kitchen.

After service, the restaurant reviews and improves

Meta AI wins on distribution: it is already in WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Facebook, and on smart glasses, billions of users already have it. It wins on the open-source ecosystem: Llama is the most-downloaded open AI in the world. It wins on casual creative use: Imagine for chat stickers, group photo prompts, voice through glasses. It wins on creator personas via AI Studio. It loses on professional polish against ChatGPT or Claude for serious work, and on privacy, because data lives inside Meta's broader ad ecosystem unless you self-host Llama. Pick Meta AI for social-app casual use, image fun, glasses interaction, or self-hosted Llama in serious development.

Fourth course · read this before you trust it

Know before you eat.

Even the best chef makes mistakes. Sometimes it confidently says "this is nut-free" when it's not. That's a hallucination. Sometimes it fills a gap with a convincing story instead of saying "I don't know." That's confabulation. Knowing the risks makes you a smarter customer.

Hallucinations

"Saying 'nut-free' when it's not." A confident answer with made-up facts. Every AI does this, so verify high-stakes claims, especially in casual social threads.

Confabulation

"Making up where the eggs came from." Fills gaps with a convincing story instead of "I don't know." Ask for sources.

Sycophancy

"A yes-chef who always agrees." Pleases you instead of being honest, even when you're wrong. Make it argue first.

Prompt Injection

"Sneaking a fake order ticket." A shared image or link can carry hidden instructions that steer the reply.

Data Leakage

"Telling table 5 what table 3 ordered." Conversations may feed Meta's personalisation. Keep sensitive prompts out of Meta AI chat.

Meta's Ad Business

"The restaurant also sells your dining habits." The hosted product lives inside Meta's ad ecosystem, so factor that into sensitive prompts.

Training on Public Posts

"Recipes inspired by every public dish." Public Facebook and Instagram posts may train Llama where allowed. EU users have a GDPR opt-out.

Bias

"Unconsciously serving bigger portions to some." Inherits biases from training data, including social-platform tone bleeding into formal answers.

The Bill. What it costs, what's next.

Last course · 2 min

The hosted product, meta.ai, the app, and Meta AI inside WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and Facebook, is entirely free, supported by Meta's ad business rather than a subscription. AI Studio is free for creators to build and deploy custom characters. Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses are a one-time hardware purchase, roughly $300 and up, with Meta AI included at no extra cost. Developers pay per token on the Llama API, priced competitively with other major labs. And the Llama open weights are free to download under a community license, with a few restrictions for platforms above 700 million monthly users.

Where Meta AI wins: distribution, because it's already inside the apps billions of people open every day, and the open-source ecosystem, the most-downloaded open AI on the planet. It's the pick for casual creative use, group-chat help, glasses interaction, or self-hosted Llama in serious development. It loses on professional polish next to ChatGPT or Claude, and on privacy unless you self-host.

1
Know Before You Eat
The chef is brilliant, but not perfect. Read this before you order.
Before you sit down, know this: even the best chef makes mistakes. Sometimes they confidently say "this is nut-free" when it's not, that's a hallucination. Sometimes they fill gaps with a convincing story instead of saying "I don't know", that's confabulation. They might agree with everything you say even when you're wrong, that's sycophancy. Someone could slip a fake ticket into the kitchen to hijack your order, that's prompt injection. The chef might accidentally reveal another table's secrets, that's data leakage. They carry unconscious habits from their training, that's bias. And sometimes they state things as certain when they're actually guessing, that's overconfidence. Knowing these risks makes you a smarter customer. Now let's meet the chef.
Now meet this restaurant's chef
2
The Chef & Their Brain
Before anyone walks in, the chef already exists. This is who they are.
The chef is the Llama family, Meta's flagship model series. Llama 4 is the current generation: multi-modal (text, image, voice), competitive with GPT-class. The context window reaches long-document scale. Every word is a token. The signature edge: Llama is open-source. Anyone can download the weights, audit them, run them privately, build on them. It powers the largest open-source AI ecosystem in the world. The hosted Meta AI experience uses Llama under the hood plus social-platform context (your contacts, group chats, posts) for personalisation.
A customer walks in through one of these doors
3
The Front Door
Multiple ways to enter this restaurant
Meta AI lives in many places at once. The standalone app + meta.ai web is the main dedicated interface. It is embedded directly in WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Facebook, type @MetaAI in any chat. Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses bring voice-first Meta AI to your face for hands-free. Developers connect via Llama API. And the open-source Llama weights are downloadable from Meta + Hugging Face for self-host.
You place your order, how it's written matters
4
The Order Slip
Instructions and context that shape every dish
Every order has layers. Voice or text input on the app and glasses. Social context: in WhatsApp or IG, Meta AI sees the chat thread and may use it for context. Imagine prompts trigger image generation. AI Studio character prompts let creators define custom AI personalities. Llama API system prompts for developers. And prompt engineering for clearer tickets.
The order slip goes to the kitchen
5
The Kitchen
Where the real work happens, chat, image, voice, search
The order arrives. Conversational chat across all surfaces. Imagine generates images from descriptions, inside WhatsApp, IG, Messenger directly. Voice mode via the app or glasses. Real-time web search for current info, the integration is increasing. Group chat assistance: Meta AI can answer questions in a group thread, summarise, draft. Image understanding: ask about photos you send.
For complex workflows, the team system kicks in
6
The Kitchen Team
Custom AI characters and creator tools
AI Studio is Meta's creator tool: anyone can build a custom AI character with its own persona, voice, behaviour. Useful for creators answering fan messages, customer-service bots, educational personas. Creator AI lets influencers train an AI version of themselves on their content. WhatsApp Business AI for SMB customer service. Llama Stack for developers to build agents.
The kitchen connects to outside suppliers
7
The Supply Chain
How the kitchen connects to the outside world
Social-platform data is the unique supplier: your contacts, group chats, recent posts feed personalisation. Web search brings live info. Image uploads from WhatsApp / IG camera roll. Llama open weights are downloadable, the largest open-source AI distribution in the world (hundreds of millions of downloads). Llama API for plugging Llama into your own software. Limited third-party connectors compared to ChatGPT.
The dish gets plated and delivered to you
8
Plating & Delivery
How the finished dish reaches you
The dish lands inside whatever app you used. Text replies in WhatsApp, IG, Messenger, the app. Generated images directly in your chat. Voice replies through glasses or the app. Group-chat answers visible to the group. Character interactions for creator-built personas. Sticker generation from Imagine inside WhatsApp.
After service, the restaurant reviews and improves
9
Where Meta AI Fits
When to pick this kitchen over the others
Meta AI wins on distribution: it is already in WhatsApp, IG, Messenger, Facebook, smart glasses. Billions of users already have it. It wins on open-source ecosystem: Llama is the most-downloaded open AI in the world. It wins on casual creative use: Imagine for chat stickers, group photo prompts, voice through glasses. It wins on creator personas via AI Studio. It loses on professional polish vs ChatGPT or Claude for serious work. It loses on privacy: data lives inside Meta's broader ad ecosystem unless you self-host Llama. Pick Meta AI for social-app casual use, image fun, glasses interaction, or for self-hosted Llama in serious dev.
Wrapping around everything, the rules, limits, and the bill
10
Health, Safety & The Bill
The non-negotiables that wrap around everything
This zone wraps around everything. Hosted product is free: Meta AI on apps + web is free, supported by Meta's ad business. Data use: subject to Meta's broader privacy framework, factor for sensitive prompts. EU opt-out exists under GDPR. Llama API is paid per token for devs. Llama open weights are free under a community license (some restrictions for very large platforms). Ray-Ban Meta glasses are a hardware purchase (~$300+) with AI included.

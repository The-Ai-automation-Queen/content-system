The Door: what you're walking into

First course · 2 min

Mistral runs a brigade out of Paris, and it's the fastest kitchen in town. Its signature is speed, up to 1,000 words a second, the same pace whether you're paying or not, and a strict European code of conduct: EU servers, GDPR by default, and a privacy toggle no other major kitchen offers. This is the door for people who want their AI fast, multilingual, and provably private. (And yes, even the best chef makes mistakes. The allergen card comes right before you eat: course four.)

You've eaten at the big American chains. Chez Mistral is the smaller, faster, cheaper place around the corner that happens to speak over 100 languages and never makes you wait for the bill.

The Kitchen: how it actually works

Second course · the floor plan

One chef, several doors in. The main dining room is Le Chat (French for "the cat") on the web and as iOS/Android apps. Coders get a workspace inside it called Mistral Vibe. Developers go round the back to La Plateforme, the pay-per-use API. And if you want the recipe itself, some models are open-source: Mistral 7B and Mixtral can be downloaded and run on your own hardware.

Same brigade behind every door, just a different way of placing the order.

The floor plan
The chef & their brain

Before anyone walks in, the chef already exists. Mistral Large is the flagship for reasoning, coding, and multilingual work. Mistral Medium balances quality and speed, and is the default on the free tier. Mistral Small is the fast, cheap option for high-volume tasks. Magistral is the reasoning specialist that thinks step-by-step before answering, like Mistral's own version of "extended thinking." Every word you send is a token, an ingredient, and the context window is the size of the countertop: how much the chef can hold in mind at once. The signature edge is 100+ languages including regional dialects, and exceptional speed: up to 1,000 words per second, the same whether you're on the free tier or paying.

Where Mistral fits

Mistral wins on privacy: EU residency, GDPR, and a No Telemetry Mode unique in the market. It wins on price: $14.99/month, the cheapest premium subscription from any major lab. It wins on speed: feels instant, no throttling on free. It wins on languages: 100+ including regional dialects, naturally strong on European ones. It loses on multi-modal scope: no own image model, no video, no native voice. It loses on integrations: nothing close to ChatGPT or Microsoft Copilot's connector library. Pick Mistral when privacy, speed, multilingual reach, or cost matter more than tool breadth.

Fourth course · read this before you trust it

Know before you eat.

Hallucinations

The chef says "definitely nut-free" when it isn't: a confident answer with made-up facts. Verify any high-stakes claim against the actual source.

Confabulation

Instead of saying "I don't know," the chef stitches together a convincing but invented story, including fictional citations. Always ask for the real source.

Sycophancy

A yes-chef who agrees with everything you say, even when you're wrong. Make it argue the other side first.

Prompt injection

Someone sneaks a fake order ticket in: hidden instructions inside a webpage or document that try to steer the chef. Be careful what you feed it from strangers.

Data leakage

Without No Telemetry Mode, uploaded files or chat content could be used for training. Enterprise plus on-prem removes this risk entirely.

Bias

Mistral inherits its training data's habits: strong on European contexts, thinner coverage in some non-EU regions.

Overconfidence

The chef states things as settled fact when sources actually disagree. Cross-check before quoting in anything that matters.

Smaller ecosystem

Great restaurant, limited side dishes: no voice mode, no own image model, no video, and far fewer third-party integrations than ChatGPT or Copilot.

The Bill. What it costs, what's next.

Last course · 1 min

This zone wraps around everything. EU residency + GDPR is the structural safety net: Mistral is headquartered in Paris, data processed on EU-based servers. No Telemetry Mode on Pro contractually guarantees your conversations are never used for training or product improvement. No other major AI tool offers this level of assurance. Le Chat Free gives roughly 25 messages a day. Le Chat Pro at $14.99/month unlocks Mistral Large, Vibe, deep research, 15GB storage, and image generation. Students get 53% off at $7.04/month. Team runs about $24.99/seat. Enterprise is custom, for private deployment. The developer API is priced separately and very competitively.

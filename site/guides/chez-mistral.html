1
Know Before You Eat
The chef is brilliant, but not perfect. Read this before you order.
Before you sit down, know this: even the best chef makes mistakes. Sometimes they confidently say "this is nut-free" when it's not, that's a hallucination. Sometimes they fill gaps with a convincing story instead of saying "I don't know", that's confabulation. They might agree with everything you say even when you're wrong, that's sycophancy. Someone could slip a fake ticket into the kitchen to hijack your order, that's prompt injection. The chef might accidentally reveal another table's secrets, that's data leakage. They carry unconscious habits from their training, that's bias. And sometimes they state things as certain when they're actually guessing, that's overconfidence. Knowing these risks makes you a smarter customer. Now let's meet the chef.
Now meet this restaurant's chef
2
The Chef & Their Brain
Before anyone walks in, the chef already exists. This is who they are.
Mistral runs a brigade. Mistral Large is the flagship for reasoning, coding, multilingual work. Mistral Medium balances quality and speed. Mistral Small is the fast, cheap option. Magistral is the reasoning specialist that thinks step-by-step. Every word is a token. The context window is the countertop. The signature edge: 100+ languages including regional dialects, and exceptional speed, up to 1,000 words per second.
A customer walks in through one of these doors
3
The Front Door
Multiple ways to enter this restaurant
Main entrance is Le Chat, French for "the cat". Web at chat.mistral.ai, iOS and Android apps. Free tier and Pro $14.99/month. Mistral Vibe is the coding workspace built into Le Chat Pro. La Plateforme is the developer API door, pay-per-use. Open-source models like Mistral 7B and Mixtral can be downloaded and self-hosted on your own machine.
You place your order, how it's written matters
4
The Order Slip
Instructions and context that shape every dish
Every order has layers. Model picker lets you choose Large, Medium, Small, or Magistral per task. Magistral reasoning mode tells the chef to think step-by-step. Deep Research mode reads many sources before answering. Memories save up to 500 preferences across chats. Custom instructions shape tone and format. And prompt engineering is learning to write clearer tickets for better dishes.
The order slip goes to the kitchen
5
The Kitchen
Where the real work happens, text, code, research, docs
The order arrives. Writing across 100+ languages is the strongest station. Mistral Vibe handles all-day coding sessions. Deep Research chains web reads. Document analysis reads anything you upload, up to 15GB on Pro. Web search brings fresh ingredients. FLUX Pro handles image generation, a guest chef from Black Forest Labs, not Mistral's own model.
For complex workflows, the team system kicks in
6
The Kitchen Team
Save, organise, and share work at scale
Projects group chats and documents by topic or client (500 on Free, 1,000 on Pro). Memories save up to 500 things about you across conversations. Team at around $24.99/seat adds shared workspace, domain verification, admin controls. Enterprise offers private deployment, on your own cloud or on-prem, custom models, hands-on support from Mistral's engineering team.
The kitchen connects to outside suppliers
7
The Supply Chain
How the kitchen connects to the outside world
Web search brings live information. File uploads let you bring your own ingredients, PDFs, docs, spreadsheets, up to 15GB on Pro. FLUX Pro partner handles image generation through Black Forest Labs. La Plateforme exposes Mistral models to developers with competitive per-token pricing. Open-source weights (Mistral 7B, Mixtral) can be downloaded and run on your own infrastructure, zero data leaves your network.
The dish gets plated and delivered to you
8
Plating & Delivery
How the finished dish reaches you
The dish lands as a fast text answer, near-instant feel. Code blocks with syntax highlighting from Mistral Vibe. FLUX-generated images for visuals. Document summaries from uploaded files. Translations across 100+ languages in one chat. Project history keeps everything organised in named folders.
After service, the restaurant reviews and improves
9
Where Mistral Fits
When to pick this kitchen over the others
Mistral wins on privacy: EU residency, GDPR, No Telemetry Mode is unique in the market. It wins on price: $14.99/month is the cheapest premium subscription from any major lab. It wins on speed: feels instant, no throttling on free. It wins on languages: 100+ including regional dialects, naturally strong on European languages. It loses on multi-modal scope: no own image model, no video, no native voice. It loses on integrations: nothing close to ChatGPT or Microsoft Copilot's connector library. Pick Mistral when privacy, speed, multilingual, or cost matter more than tool breadth.
Wrapping around everything, the rules, limits, and the bill
10
Health, Safety & The Bill
The non-negotiables that wrap around everything
This zone wraps around everything. GDPR + EU residency is the structural safety net. No Telemetry Mode on Pro contractually guarantees your conversations are not used for any training or product improvement. Le Chat Free gives ~25 messages/day. Le Chat Pro at $14.99/month unlocks Mistral Large, Vibe, deep research, 15GB storage, image gen. Students at $7.04/month with .edu (53% off). Team at ~$24.99/seat. Enterprise custom for private deployment. Developer API priced separately and very competitively.

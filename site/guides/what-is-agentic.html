What agentic actually means

The word, and what it actually means

Agentic is being attached to everything at the moment, mostly by people who want to charge more for it. Underneath the noise it means one specific thing, and the distinction is worth ninety seconds of your attention.

A normal AI tool answers. You ask, it responds, it stops. Everything after that's you.

An agentic tool acts. You give it an outcome, and it works out the steps, carries them out, checks how it went, and adjusts. It might search the web, open your calendar, fill in a form, send an email, update a record. It keeps going until the job is done or it gets stuck.

That's the whole difference. Answering versus doing. Everything else in the marketing is decoration on that one idea.

Think of it as delegation

You already know how this works, because you have managed people.

Asking a colleague "what do you think we should do about this client?" is a question. Saying "sort out the situation with this client and tell me when it's done" is delegation. The second one is faster and it's also how things go wrong, because you were not in the room when the decisions got made.

Everything you already know about delegating applies here without modification. You delegate to people you have watched work. You start with things that are reversible. You don't hand over the client relationship on day one. Apply exactly that judgement.

The four levels

When someone says agentic, they could mean any of these, and the difference between level two and level four is the difference between a useful tool and a genuine liability.

LevelWhat it doesExampleWhat can go wrong
1. AnswersResponds and stops. You do everything else.Ordinary chat.A wrong answer you can see before you use it.
2. Uses toolsSearches, reads files, runs calculations to answer better.Deep research, reading your documents.It misreads a source. You still see the output first.
3. Acts, with approvalPrepares the action and waits for you to confirm.Drafts the reply, doesn't send it.Approval fatigue. You start clicking yes without reading.
4. Acts aloneDecides and carries it out. You find out afterwards.Books the meeting, sends the email, updates the record.It does exactly what you said and not what you meant, forty times.

Levels one and two are where almost all real value sits today for a small business. Level three is genuinely useful once you trust the thing. Level four is where people get hurt, and it's also what most of the marketing is describing.

Do this

When a tool is sold to you as agentic, ask which level. Then ask what happens when it's wrong on a Sunday and nobody is watching. The answer to that second question is the whole decision.

Let it draft. Don't let it send. Not until you have watched it work for a fortnight.

How to hear the word in the wild

Three things people mean when they say agentic, only one of which is what you think.

  • The honest version. The tool genuinely takes multi-step actions. Ask which level and what the limits are.
  • The stretched version. It uses a search tool, so technically it takes an action. This is level two wearing a level four label.
  • The empty version. Nothing changed. The word was added to the pricing page. You'll meet this more often than the other two.

What this means for your business

Start at level three and stay there longer than you think you need to. Let it prepare the work and keep the send button. That's where the time saving is, and it's where the risk stays small.

Before you let anything reach level four, three things need to be true. You have watched it do the same job correctly for at least a fortnight. The action is reversible, or the worst case is embarrassing rather than expensive. And you have a way of finding out what it did without going looking.

The businesses that get burned by this aren't the cautious ones. They're the ones who went from curious to fully automated in a week because a demonstration was impressive. A demonstration is a rehearsed best case. Your Tuesday isn't.

1
Know Before You Eat
The chef is brilliant, but not perfect. Read this before you order.
Before you sit down, know this: even the best chef makes mistakes. Sometimes they confidently say "this is nut-free" when it's not, that's a hallucination. Sometimes they fill gaps with a convincing story instead of saying "I don't know", that's confabulation. They might agree with everything you say even when you're wrong, that's sycophancy. Someone could slip a fake ticket into the kitchen to hijack your order, that's prompt injection. The chef might accidentally reveal another table's secrets, that's data leakage. They carry unconscious habits from their training, that's bias. And sometimes they state things as certain when they're actually guessing, that's overconfidence. Knowing these risks makes you a smarter customer. Now let's meet the chef.
Now meet this restaurant's chef
2
The Chef & Their Brain
Before anyone walks in, the chef already exists. This is who they are.
Manus is not a single chef, it is a multi-agent brigade. A Planner agent breaks your task into steps. Executor agents run each step (browse, code, file, click). A Verifier agent checks the work. Underneath, Manus calls multiple foundation LLMs (reportedly Claude + others) per step, picking the right one for the job. It can run asynchronously for hours, you queue a task and it works while you do other things. Computer use is core: real browser, real clicks, real forms.
A customer walks in through one of these doors
3
The Front Door
Multiple ways to enter this restaurant
Main entrance is manus.im on the web. Sign in, get a free trial credit allocation. Mobile app for queueing tasks on the go. Free trial credits let you try it before paying. Paid plans are credit-based: more credits per month, longer tasks, higher concurrency. Currently no public open weights, no public developer API in the same sense as other vendors (the product is the agent, not a model).
You place your order, how it's written matters
4
The Order Slip
Instructions and context that shape every dish
Every order has layers. Goal description: what you want done, end state. Constraints: budget, deadline, sources to use, what to avoid. Attached files: docs, screenshots, data the agent needs. Credentials: if the agent needs to log into services, you set them up carefully. Plan review: Manus drafts a plan first, you can approve or edit before execution. Clear, complete order slips matter more here than anywhere else, the agent will actually run them.
The order slip goes to the kitchen
5
The Kitchen
Where the real work happens, browse, code, fill, deliver
The order arrives. The Planner agent breaks it down. The Manus Browser drives a real browser instance: visits sites, reads pages, clicks buttons, fills forms. The Code agent writes and runs scripts. The File agent reads + writes files. The Research agent aggregates info across many sources. The Verifier reviews each completed step. The whole loop runs asynchronously, no need to babysit, you check back later.
For complex workflows, the team system kicks in
6
The Kitchen Team
Multi-agent stack, scheduled runs, team sharing
Multi-agent orchestration is the headline: Planner + Executors + Verifier work as a brigade. Long-running async tasks: hours of work in the background. Scheduled tasks: queue recurring jobs (e.g. weekly market scan). Team plans share credit pools and tasks across members. Replay & Share: every step is recorded, share a link so others see exactly what the agent did.
The kitchen connects to outside suppliers
7
The Supply Chain
How the kitchen connects to the outside world
The whole web is the supplier: Manus drives a real browser, so any public site is reachable. Authenticated sites: with your credentials, it can log into your tools, with all the privacy implications that brings. Foundation models underneath (Claude, others) are the cognitive supply. File system inside the sandbox holds intermediate work. Limited official connectors: integration story relies on browser automation rather than API connectors.
The dish gets plated and delivered to you
8
Plating & Delivery
How the finished dish reaches you
The dish lands as structured deliverables: reports, spreadsheets, slide decks, websites, code, filled forms. Replay link: a recorded walkthrough of every action the agent took, click by click. Files are delivered ready to download. Real-world actions: bookings made, forms submitted, applications sent (use with care). Status notifications when long-running tasks finish.
After service, the restaurant reviews and improves
9
Where Manus Fits
When to pick this kitchen over the others
Manus wins on autonomous task completion: queue a task, walk away, come back to results. It wins on computer-use breadth: any public website is in scope. It wins on multi-agent orchestration out of the box. It wins on long horizons: hours of work the user does not babysit. It loses on predictability: agents fail in novel ways, results need review. It loses on privacy for sensitive logins. It loses on cost predictability: credit burn varies wildly per task. Pick Manus for repeatable research, market scans, data gathering, application filling, where review-and-fix is acceptable.
Wrapping around everything, the rules, limits, and the bill
10
Health, Safety & The Bill
The non-negotiables that wrap around everything
This zone wraps around everything. Hosted by Butterfly Effect: factor data residency for sensitive workflows. Credits system: pricing per task is variable, simple tasks burn few credits, deep research burns many. Free trial credits let you test. Basic plan: a monthly credit allowance for individuals. Plus / Team plans: larger credit pools, higher concurrency, scheduled tasks. Action safety: by default, Manus asks before submitting forms or making purchases on sensitive sites. Always review the plan before approve.

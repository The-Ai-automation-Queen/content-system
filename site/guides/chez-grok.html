01

The Door. What you're walking into.

First course · 2 min

Every restaurant tells you what it is before you sit down. Grok's door says: fast, sarcastic, plugged straight into the live feed of X. This is the kitchen that reads what the whole world is posting right now and answers with a wink, because it doesn't do quiet and bookish, it does quick, opinionated, and occasionally reckless. (And yes, even the best chef makes mistakes. The allergen card comes right before you eat, in course four.)

You walk in expecting a straight answer and you get one, usually with a joke attached. This is the kitchen for anyone who needs to know what the internet is saying about something right now, not last week.

The Kitchen. How it actually works.

Second course · the floor plan

One brain, four doors in. You can walk in standalone (grok.com or the app), get served inside the social feed you already scroll (Inside X), speak your order out loud (voice mode), or pipe the kitchen into your own product (the developer API).

Same chef, different counters. The floor plan below shows where each door leads.

The floor plan

Before anyone walks in, the chef already exists. The current head chef is Grok 4.3, the most capable xAI model, rolling out to paid users in stages. Big Brain mode is the extended-reasoning toggle for hard problems. The context window goes up to 2 million tokens on Grok 4.1 Fast, the largest in the industry, meaning the chef can hold an entire book or codebase on the countertop at once. Every word you type or read back is a token. And the chef is multi-modal: it reads images, listens to voice, generates images and short videos through its Aurora engine, and can even clone a voice.

The Menu. What to order first.

Third course · three dishes

The order arrives in the kitchen and the real work starts: search, images, video, code. DeepSearch is the head chef doing extended web research. Aurora is the image station, producing photorealistic stills comparable to Midjourney. Video generation produces 6-second clips. Coding writes, explains, and debugs. AI agents handle multi-step tasks autonomously. Image analysis reads what you upload.

Starter · 5 minutes

DeepSearch a live question

Ask something that needs today's answer, not last year's training data, like "what's trending on X about this launch right now?" DeepSearch reads dozens of sources and comes back sourced.

Main · 10 minutes

Generate with Aurora

Describe an image in plain language and get photorealistic results in seconds. Free tier is limited; paid tiers unlock more generations and short video clips too.

Chef's special

Set up a persistent agent

Point an AI agent at a topic, like monitoring X mentions of your brand and summarising daily, and let it keep working between sessions instead of starting from zero each time.

Fourth course · read this before you trust it

Know before you eat.

Even the best chef makes mistakes. The current chef is also brilliant, sarcastic, and comes with real controversy. Know this before you sit down.

Hallucinations

"Saying 'definitely nut-free' when it's not." The chef confidently says "no nuts" when there are almonds. Grok claims one of the lowest hallucination rates (~4.2%), but always verify important facts.

Confabulation

"Making up where the eggs came from." The chef invents plausible details rather than saying "I do not know". Grok's witty tone can make this harder to spot.

Sycophancy

"A yes-chef who always agrees." The chef agrees with everything you say even when you're wrong.

Prompt Injection

"Sneaking a fake order ticket." Someone hides instructions in a webpage or X post to steer the chef's behaviour.

Data Leakage

"Telling table 5 what table 3 ordered." Uploaded files or chat history could surface in later responses, because Grok shares context with X by design on some plans.

Bias

"Unconsciously serving bigger portions to some." The chef inherits biases from training data, including X data which skews towards specific voices.

Overconfidence

"Full certainty when they should warn you." The chef states things as fact when sources actually disagree. Grok's confident tone amplifies this.

Image-Gen Safety Record

"A kitchen with a serious health-inspection record." In January 2026, Grok's image generator was exploited to produce harmful content including CSAM. Investigations opened in 7 countries; xAI tightened controls afterward.

Sarcastic Personality

"A chef who cracks jokes while cooking." Grok is designed to be witty and irreverent. You can soften it via custom instructions but not fully turn it off.

All Co-Founders Left

"Every original chef has left the kitchen." All 10 original xAI co-founders have departed, unusual for a company this young.

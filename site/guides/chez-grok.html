1
Know Before You Eat
The chef is brilliant, sarcastic, and comes with real controversy.
Before you sit down, know this: even the best chef makes mistakes. Sometimes they confidently say "this is nut-free" when it's not, that's a hallucination. Sometimes they fill gaps with a convincing story instead of saying "I don't know", that's confabulation. They might agree with everything you say even when you're wrong, that's sycophancy. Someone could slip a fake ticket into the kitchen to hijack your order, that's prompt injection. The chef might accidentally reveal another table's secrets, that's data leakage. They carry unconscious habits from their training, that's bias. And sometimes they state things as certain when they're actually guessing, that's overconfidence. Knowing these risks makes you a smarter customer. Now let's meet the chef.
Now meet this restaurant's chef
2
The Chef & Their Brain
Before anyone walks in, the chef already exists. This is who they are.
The current chef is Grok 4.3, the most capable xAI model, rolling out to paid users. Big Brain mode is extended reasoning for hard problems. The context window goes up to 2 million tokens on Grok 4.1 Fast, the largest in the industry. Every word is a token. The chef is multi-modal: reads images, listens to voice, generates images and short videos with the Aurora engine, even clones voices.
A customer walks in through one of these doors
3
The Front Door
Multiple ways to enter this restaurant
Two main doors. Grok.com and the iOS/Android app are the standalone restaurant, no X account required. Inside X Grok is embedded directly in the social platform, every X user can call it. Voice mode lets you speak the order, hands-free. Free tier gives ~10 prompts per 2 hours, paid plans unlock the full kitchen.
You place your order, how it's written matters
4
The Order Slip
Instructions and context that shape every dish
Every order has layers. Regular mode is fast answers for quick questions. Big Brain is the thinking-mode toggle for hard problems. DeepSearch is the research mode, the chef reads dozens of sources before answering. Custom instructions shape Grok's tone and behaviour for you. And prompt engineering is learning to write clearer tickets for better dishes.
The order slip goes to the kitchen
5
The Kitchen
Where the real work happens, search, images, video, code
The order arrives. DeepSearch is the head chef doing extended web research. Aurora is the image station, photorealistic stills comparable to Midjourney. Video generation produces 6-second clips. Coding writes, explains, debugs. AI agents handle multi-step tasks autonomously. Image analysis reads what you upload.
For complex workflows, the team system kicks in
6
The Kitchen Team
Agents, voice clones, business accounts
AI agents are mini assistants you can deploy to run multi-step tasks: monitor X mentions, summarise daily, research a topic. Custom Voices lets you clone your own voice from 2 minutes of audio and use it across the platform. Grok Business at $30/seat per month gives teams shared accounts with admin controls. SuperGrok Heavy unlocks Grok 4 Heavy, a multi-agent reasoning model.
The kitchen connects to outside suppliers
7
The Supply Chain
How the kitchen connects to the outside world
Real-time X data is Grok's signature supplier. No other major AI tool can search live X posts. Web search backs DeepSearch with general sources. The xAI API exposes Grok models to developers, with the most generous free credits of any major lab (up to $175/month through the data-sharing program). But the ecosystem is smaller: fewer third-party integrations than ChatGPT or Microsoft Copilot.
The dish gets plated and delivered to you
8
Plating & Delivery
How the finished dish reaches you
The dish lands as a text answer, usually with a side of personality. Aurora images arrive in seconds. Aurora video clips on paid plans. Voice replies if you spoke the order. Inside X, Grok can post answers directly into the timeline or reply to threads. X analysis can summarise long threads, surface trends, explain who is saying what.
After service, the restaurant reviews and improves
9
Where Grok Fits
When to pick this kitchen over the others
Grok wins on real-time X intelligence: PR teams, social media managers, journalists, trend-watchers. It wins on biggest context window for processing huge documents or codebases. It wins on most generous free developer credits. It loses on benchmarks: Grok 4.3 trails GPT-5 and Claude Opus 4.5 on most independent tests. It loses on safety record and professional polish. Pick Grok when X data or massive context matters more than tightest accuracy or brand neutrality.
Wrapping around everything, the rules, limits, and the bill
10
Health, Safety & The Bill
The non-negotiables that wrap around everything
This zone wraps around everything. Safety controls were tightened after the January 2026 image-generator scandal, but the trust gap is real. Free tier gives ~10 prompts per 2 hours, lighter than competitor free tiers. X Premium ($8) bundles basic Grok with X. SuperGrok Lite ($10) and SuperGrok ($30) are the standalone paid plans. X Premium+ ($40) bundles full Grok with full X. SuperGrok Heavy ($300) is power-user territory with Grok 4 Heavy. Students with US .edu emails get SuperGrok free.

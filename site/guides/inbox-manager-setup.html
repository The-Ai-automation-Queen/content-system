The Inbox Manager setup.

You answer email for about 90 minutes a day. That is 22 full working days a year, sorting other people’s requests. This guide hires you an AI employee that reads every email, sorts it, drafts the easy replies, and hands you one summary at 8am. She never sends anything. The send button stays yours.

What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

  1. Every new email gets read and labeled: Reply needed / Waiting / FYI / Junk.
  2. Every “Reply needed” email gets a drafted answer in your tone, saved to drafts. Never sent.
  3. Newsletters and receipts get filed automatically before you ever see them.
  4. At 8am you get one summary: what needs you, what did not.

Works the same on Gmail and Outlook. Time to build: about an hour. The tools are boring on purpose: your email, one automation tool (n8n or Make, free tiers are fine), one AI (Claude).

The safety rules. Read these first.

This employee touches your mail, so the rules are not optional:

  1. 🟢 Green: subject lines, sender names, your own drafts. Safe to process.
  2. 🟠 Orange: email bodies from known contacts. The AI reads them to label and draft, stores them nowhere else, uses them for nothing else.
  3. 🔴 Red: attachments from unknown senders, client data, passwords, contracts. The workflow skips these entirely and labels them “Needs you”. No exceptions.

The rule that matters most: set the no-send rule at the connection level, not as a polite instruction. When you connect Gmail or Outlook, grant the read, modify and draft permissions, and do not grant send. Now she cannot send, even on a bad day.

Part 1. The filters. 10 minutes, no AI.

Do this even if you build nothing else. In Gmail (Settings → Filters) or Outlook (Rules):

  1. Anything with “unsubscribe” from newsletter platforms → label Newsletters, skip the inbox.
  2. Subject contains receipt, invoice, or order confirmation → label Receipts, skip the inbox.
  3. Emails where you are CC’d, not the direct recipient → label FYI.

That is 30 percent of her job, done, with zero AI. You just hired a third of an employee.

Part 2. The triage brain. 30 minutes.

In n8n (or Make):

  1. Trigger: Gmail node, “message received” (Outlook: the Outlook trigger). Poll every 5 minutes.
  2. AI step: send the sender, subject and body to Claude with the job description below. Copy it exactly, fill the blanks.
  3. Apply the label back onto the email (Gmail: modify labels; Outlook: move to folder).
  4. If reply needed: create a draft on that thread with the AI’s text. It waits in your drafts folder.

Her job description (the prompt):

You are my inbox triage assistant. Label this email as exactly one of: REPLY_NEEDED, WAITING, FYI, JUNK.

My rules:
• Emails from [YOUR CLIENTS’ DOMAINS] are always REPLY_NEEDED.
• Anything asking me for a decision, a meeting, or money: REPLY_NEEDED.
• Automated notifications and no-reply senders: FYI.
• Cold pitches selling me services: JUNK.
• If it mentions [YOUR PROJECT NAMES], never JUNK.

If REPLY_NEEDED, also draft a reply in my voice: plain English, short sentences, warm but direct. Sign off: [YOUR SIGN-OFF]. If the email asks for something I have not decided, the draft says I will come back by [DAY]. It never invents an answer.

Return JSON: {"label": "...", "draft": "..."}

Notice the blanks are yours to fill and the prompt never includes real client names or data. That is the redaction habit: the structure is public, the specifics stay home.

Part 3. The 8am digest. 15 minutes.

  1. Trigger: schedule, weekdays at 8:00.
  2. Fetch: everything labeled in the last 24 hours.
  3. AI step: “Summarize in 5 lines max: how many came in, how many need me (sender + one-line ask for each), how many were filed. Plain English, no fluff.”
  4. Deliver it to yourself: email, Telegram, or Slack.

The feeling you are buying: you open your inbox already knowing nothing in there is a surprise.

The upgrade path

When her drafts sound like you 9 times out of 10, you have built trust with your first AI employee. The same pattern, read, label, draft, never send, is how every AI employee works, from lead follow-up to customer support. One employee at a time. That is the whole method.

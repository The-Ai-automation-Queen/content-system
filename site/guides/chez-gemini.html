01

The Door. What you're walking into

First course · 2 min

You hear the names everywhere, Gemini, Gems, Workspace, Antigravity, Veo, and never quite know which does what. Google's restaurant isn't one dish, it's a whole building: a dining room, a corporate wing, a terminal kitchen out back, and a supply dock feeding it all from outside. Read and click through as you go, follow the order, and enjoy. (And yes, even the best chef makes mistakes. The allergen card comes right before you eat, in course four.)

Google's chef has multiple modes: a deep-reasoning head chef and a fast, cheap speed chef, both reading text, images, audio, and video off the same enormous countertop.

Fourth course · read this before you trust it

Know before you eat.

Hallucination

"Saying 'definitely nut-free' when it's not." The chef states a fabricated fact with total confidence. Google leans on Search grounding and enterprise data to cut this down, so verify anything that matters.

Confabulation

"Making up where the eggs came from." Gaps get filled with a plausible-sounding story, like a citation to a paper that doesn't exist, instead of "I don't know."

Sycophancy

"A yes-chef who always agrees." Present a flawed analysis and it says "great work" instead of flagging the errors. Make it argue first.

Prompt injection

"Sneaking a fake order ticket." A shared doc hides an instruction to override safety rules. Careful what documents you feed it from strangers.

Data leakage

"Telling table 5 what table 3 ordered." Information from one context bleeds into a response where it doesn't belong, so watch what gets shared across users.

Bias

"Unconsciously serving bigger portions to some." Learned patterns cause uneven treatment. Cultural or gender assumptions can slip in without anyone intending it.

Overconfidence

"Full certainty when they should warn you." Definitive medical or legal-sounding advice with no caveat. Ask it to flag its own uncertainty.

The Bill. What it costs, what's next

Last course · 1 min

Free gets you the Gemini App with limits: casual chat and image generation. Google AI Plus (~$10/mo) widens access and unlocks Gems and Scheduled Actions. Google AI Pro ($20/mo) adds Deep Research, Gemini Live, and priority access, the tier for professionals who use it daily. Google AI Ultra (~$30/mo) is the highest limits and every feature for power users. Gemini for Workspace comes bundled into business Workspace plans. Gemini Enterprise is custom pricing for large organizations running the full Agent Platform with governance and admin controls.

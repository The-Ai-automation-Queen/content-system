1
Know Before You Eat
The chef is brilliant, but not perfect. Read this before you order.
Before you sit down, know this: even the best chef makes mistakes. Sometimes they confidently say "this is nut-free" when it's not, that's a hallucination. Sometimes they fill gaps with a convincing story, that's confabulation. They might agree with everything you say, that's sycophancy. Someone could slip a fake ticket, that's prompt injection. The chef might reveal another table's secrets, that's data leakage. They carry unconscious habits, that's bias. And sometimes they state things as certain when guessing, that's overconfidence. Microsoft calls their safety approach "Responsible AI" and uses grounding to reduce these risks.
Now meet this restaurant's chef
2
The Chef & Their Brain
Before anyone walks in, the chef already exists. This is who they are.
Microsoft's kitchen doesn't have one chef. It uses models from OpenAI plus its own. The context window (countertop) determines how much the chef juggles at once. Memory keeps notes across visits. The chef reads images, audio, and video, that's multi-modal. Microsoft Graph is like the chef's knowledge of your entire company: emails, calendar, files, org chart. And every word is a token.
A customer walks in through one of these doors
3
The Front Door
Multiple ways to enter this restaurant
You can walk into Copilot Chat, a standalone dining room for conversations. Or the chef shows up inside the apps you already use: Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, like a chef who comes to your table. Copilot Cowork goes further: it delegates real tasks and completes them for you. And you can say "Hey Copilot" to order hands-free with voice.
You place your order, how it's written matters
4
The Order Slip
Instructions and context that shape every dish
Every order has layers. System prompts are invisible instructions shaping the chef's approach. Grounding is like the chef checking your company's actual fridge before cooking, not inventing ingredients, it reduces hallucinations by anchoring responses in real data from Microsoft Graph. Declarative agents let you write house rules for specialized chefs in natural language. And prompt engineering is learning to write clearer tickets for better dishes.
The order slip goes to the kitchen
5
The Kitchen
Where the real work happens, tools, agents, and data
The order arrives. Copilot in M365 apps is the chef working at different stations: drafting in Word, analyzing in Excel, designing in PowerPoint. The PowerPoint, Excel, Word and Planner Agents are specialized sous-chefs for each station. Code Interpreter lets the chef run calculations and analyze data. AI Workflows chain steps together automatically. And SharePoint is the pantry where company documents live on labeled shelves.
For complex workflows, the team system kicks in
6
The Kitchen Team
Build, manage, and deploy custom agents at scale
Copilot Studio is the kitchen design workshop where anyone can build custom agents with low-code or no-code tools. Agent 365 is the admin console, like a restaurant management company deploying and governing fleets of agents. Azure AI Foundry is the industrial kitchen for developers building from scratch. And the Microsoft 365 Agents SDK provides the blueprints and equipment to build your own restaurant using Microsoft's kitchen system.
The kitchen connects to outside suppliers
7
The Supply Chain
How the kitchen connects to the outside world
Copilot Connectors are supplier trucks: GitLab, Asana, Monday.com, Zendesk, Salesforce each delivering their goods. MCP support is arriving as a universal dock, admins can configure custom MCP connectors. Power Platform connectors bridge to many enterprise systems. And Microsoft Graph is the internal supply network, it knows where everything in your company lives.
The dish gets plated and delivered to you
8
Plating & Delivery
How the finished dish reaches you
The dish might arrive as an edited Word document, an Excel chart, or a PowerPoint deck, the chef plates directly in the app you're using. Copilot Pages are collaborative takeaway boxes that persist and can be shared. Audio Overviews turn documents into podcast-style summaries. Notebooks give you a canvas for longer research. And the chef follows you across desktop, mobile, web, and Teams.
After service, the restaurant reviews and improves
9
Training & Improvement
How the chef gets better over time
Azure AI Foundry provides tools for fine-tuning, evaluation, and model customization. The Copilot Dashboard tracks adoption, satisfaction rates, and usage patterns, like food critics scoring every dish. Agent analytics show how many runs each agent completes and how people use them. And Copilot Skills in Cowork are reusable instructions that improve through use.
Wrapping around everything, the rules, limits, and the bill
10
Health, Safety & The Bill
The non-negotiables that wrap around everything
This zone wraps around everything. Responsible AI is Microsoft's health inspector framework. Microsoft Purview handles data loss prevention, scanning prompts and outputs for sensitive information. Admin controls let IT decide who can build agents, which tools are available, and what data is accessible. Role-based access sets granular permissions. And the licensing tiers are the bill: Copilot Chat (free), Microsoft 365 Copilot ($30/user/month), and Enterprise pricing.

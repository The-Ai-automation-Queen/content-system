02

Clone the Voice. Steps 1 to 3.

Second course · record, load, batch

One reference recording feeds a free cloud model that can then say anything, in your voice, in thirty languages. Three steps get you from "just my phone" to a folder full of voiceover files.

Step 01

Record your voice sample

Open Voice Memos, Audacity, or any recorder. Record 20–30 seconds of yourself speaking naturally, like reading a paragraph from a book, or describing your day.

  • Clean room. No fan, traffic, or music behind you.
  • Normal pace. Don't perform, because the clone copies how you actually talk.
  • Save as WAV at 44kHz or 48kHz, mono. If your recorder only exports MP3, convert it in Audacity.
  • Name it my-voice-ref.wav. You'll reuse this file forever.
Step 02

Open the Colab notebook

The notebook ships with this guide. It installs VoxCPM, loads the 2B multilingual model, mounts your Google Drive, and runs voice cloning in batch.

  1. Go to colab.research.google.com.
  2. File > Upload notebook > select VoxCPM_Reels_Pipeline.ipynb.
  3. Runtime > Change runtime type > T4 GPU (free tier).
  4. Runtime > Run all.
First run takes 5–10 minutes

VoxCPM downloads the model weights (around 5GB) on first load. They cache for the rest of your session. Subsequent generations take 30–60 seconds per Reel.

Step 03

Upload your voice + generate in batch

When the notebook hits Cell 5, an upload button appears. Drop my-voice-ref.wav. Colab caches it for the session. Then edit the BATCH list in Cell 7 with your Reel scripts. Each item generates one WAV file:

BATCH = [
    {
        'slug': 'ai-plan-actually-use',
        'text': 'Most AI plans collect dust. The one you actually use starts with one problem worth solving this week.',
    },
    {
        'slug': 'tech-bff-test',
        'text': 'A tech BFF answers your stupid AI questions without making you feel stupid. That is the standard.',
    },
]

Run Cell 7. WAVs land in your Google Drive at MyDrive/voxcpm-output/.

03

The Pipeline. Steps 4 to 7.

Third course · sync, lock, scaffold, render

The audio exists. Now it becomes a video. Four steps carry the WAV from your Drive folder to a finished, platform-ready MP4.

Step 04

Sync WAVs to your laptop

Install Google Drive for Desktop. Pick Stream files mode, not Mirror, so nothing eats your local disk. Files download on demand. After install, your Drive mounts as a folder. Browse to voxcpm-output/ and move the file into your Remotion project:

mkdir public\audio
copy "G:\My Drive\voxcpm-output\2026-06-18_ai-plan-actually-use.wav" public\audio\myreel.wav
Step 05

Lock motion to voice

From your Remotion project root:

/reelstack-beats public/audio/myreel.wav

ReelStack pipes the WAV through ffmpeg, runs whisper-cli, and prints frame-accurate BEAT constants. Copy them: they lock every scene cut to the words you actually said.

Why this matters

Eyeballing scene cuts on a 90-second Reel drifts 6 seconds by the end. Whisper-locked beats stay in sync to the frame.

Step 06

Scaffold the Reel

Pick a ReelStack family that fits the message. Five ship: Glass Iridescent, Cream Paper, Dark Cinematic, Warm Signature, Forbidden.

/reelstack-glass --preset=graphify --name=MyReel --vo=public/audio/myreel.wav

ReelStack writes the full .tsx file with palette, primitives, and BEAT skeleton. Paste your beats from Step 5. Open Remotion Studio (npm run dev) to preview.

Step 07

Render

/reelstack-render MyReel --platform=ig

ReelStack runs the lint (safe zones, motion floors, hero text fit), then encodes H.264 at IG-optimal bitrate. Output: an MP4 ready to upload. For TikTok, use --platform=tiktok. For YouTube Shorts, use --platform=shorts.

Fourth course · read this before you trust it

Know before you trust it.

Colab disconnects mid-batch

Free tier sessions cap around 12 hours. Keep the tab open. For batches over 20 Reels, upgrade to Colab Pro (~$10/month).

The cloned voice sounds robotic

The reference WAV is too short, too noisy, or too performed. Re-record 30 seconds of natural speech in a quiet room: clone quality equals reference quality.

Whisper picks up the wrong words

Speak the script exactly as written. Test Cell 7 with one short script before batching 20.

ReelStack rejects the render

The lint failed. Run /reelstack-lint MyReel.tsx for the exact violations.

05

The Bill. What it costs, what's next.

Last course · 1 min

Free tier: Colab's free GPU plus VoxCPM's open weights get you the full pipeline at $0 per generation, forever, as long as your batches stay under the 12-hour session cap. ReelStack itself is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Paid tier: if you're publishing daily or batching more than 20 Reels at once, Colab Pro (~$10/month) removes the disconnect risk. That's the entire ongoing cost of this stack: no ElevenLabs bill, no CapCut Pro, no stock footage subscription.

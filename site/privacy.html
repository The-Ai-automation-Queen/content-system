Privacy

What we collect, where it goes, and how to leave.

Last updated: 07 July 2026

This site explains AI in plain English. The privacy policy works the same way.

What we collect

  • Your email address, only when you type it into a form yourself: to get a guide, the newsletter, a quiz result, or the Store opening email.
  • Your first name, if a form asks for it and you give it.
  • Your quiz answers on the Time Audit, if you choose to send your result by email. If you do the quiz without entering an email, nothing is stored anywhere; it runs entirely in your browser.

That is the whole list. No tracking pixels, no advertising cookies, no analytics profiles, no selling or renting your data to anyone, ever.

Where it goes

When you submit a form, your email travels to the tools that run this publication: a form service (Formspree), our own automation server, and our customer database (GoHighLevel). Newsletter delivery is handled by our email provider. Each of these processes your data only to do the job you asked for: sending you the thing you requested.

What you'll receive

The thing you asked for, and the emails that go with this publication (guides, the AI Insider Brief). Every email has an unsubscribe link. One click and you are out, no questions, no guilt trip.

Your rights

You can ask at any time what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted entirely. Reply to any email we have sent you, or message Fatiha on LinkedIn, and it will be handled personally.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. We will not quietly get worse.

01

The Door. What you're walking into.

First course · 2 min

Every restaurant tells you what it is before you sit down. Claude's door says: quiet, considered, a little bookish. This is the careful kitchen. It thinks before it talks, holds enormous context, keeps its head under pressure, and would rather tell you "I'm not sure" than serve you something confidently wrong.

You've eaten fast food AI. This is the place you book for the conversations that matter: long documents, real work, decisions with consequences.

02

The Kitchen. How it actually works.

Second course · the floor plan

One brain, four ways to order. You can walk in and talk (claude.ai), have it work inside your files and terminal (Claude Code), give it a desk in your office (Cowork), or pipe the kitchen straight into your own product (the API).

Same chef, different service. The skill is knowing which counter to order at. That's what the floor plan is for.

The Menu. What to order first.

Third course · three dishes
Starter · 5 minutes

The long conversation

Paste an entire contract, report, or 40-page PDF and interrogate it. This is the dish no fast-food kitchen can make.

Starter prompt · tap to copy
Here is a contract I am about to sign. Read it fully, then list: 1) the three clauses most in MY interest, 2) the three most against me, 3) the questions I should ask before signing. Be specific. [paste the contract]

Fourth course · read this before you trust it

Know before you eat.

Hallucination

The chef confidently says the dish is nut-free when it isn't. Always verify claims that matter.

Confabulation

Gaps filled with a convincing story instead of "I don't know." Ask for sources.

Sycophancy

It agrees with you even when you're wrong. Make it argue first.

Prompt injection

Someone slips a fake ticket into the kitchen. Careful what documents you feed it from strangers.

The story

I watched smart people freeze.

For two decades I worked inside Dell, Intel and Microsoft, the rooms where the technology everyone talks about actually gets made. When AI arrived, I watched capable, ambitious people around me freeze: sure they were "behind," sure it was too technical, sure it belonged to someone younger or geekier.

They weren't behind. Nobody had explained it properly.

So I left, and I ran the experiment on the hardest person I know: myself. Could someone build a real business where the busywork runs on AI, without a team, without living online 24/7? I built the systems. Then I rebuilt them simpler. Today my business runs on them while I sleep.

I'm not a distant guru. I'm the person one step ahead of you on the same road, and I left the map on.

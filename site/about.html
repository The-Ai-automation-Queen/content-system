The story

It was never about the technology.

Here's what 20 years in enterprise tech taught me: it was never about the technology. Every few years something new shows up, cloud, subscriptions, digital platforms, and smart, capable people freeze. They assume it belongs to someone younger, someone more 'techie.' My job at Dell, Intel, and Microsoft was never to invent the technology. It was to translate it into something ordinary teams could adopt and use, across a dozen markets.

Then I left and co-founded LeLabPlus, an ethical, zero-carbon fashion factory in France. Same pattern, different industry. We made circular manufacturing work in practice, and it earned us Nike as an exclusive vendor and a role producing 2 million protective masks for French hospitals during COVID. The pattern isn't specific to tech. It's how real change gets adopted by real people.

AI is the same pattern, faster and louder. I'm not a futurist and I didn't build the models. I take something new and overwhelming and turn it into a system an ordinary business owner can run. I've done it twice, in two industries with nothing else in common. Now I'm doing it a third time, on myself: hiring an AI team to run my own business, in public. The method has a name, Freedom OS. It's the same caliber of system I built for Fortune 500 teams, laid out in layers, free guides first, so starting with less money doesn't mean starting with less quality.

The only thing you actually need is the will to learn.

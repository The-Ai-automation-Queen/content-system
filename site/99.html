I'm building a company staffed by AI. 99 employees, hired one at a time, in public. This page is the scoreboard, and it's not allowed to flatter me.

Rule 01 · Receipts or it didn’t happen

An employee counts as hired only if it did real work in the last 7 days and I can show the output. No receipt, no badge.

Rule 02 · They prepare, I release

No employee sends anything to a human on its own. Drafts land in my review queue. The send button stays human.

Rule 03 · The counter can go down

An employee that stops working loses its badge until it earns it back. Small and true beats big and hollow.

How hiring works here

  1. A role gets a job description: one founder problem it exists to kill.
  2. The new hire works a real trial shift on my business, not a demo.
  3. The output becomes a public receipt. Real screenshots, real numbers.
  4. Only then does the badge appear on this page. Every hire gets its own story.

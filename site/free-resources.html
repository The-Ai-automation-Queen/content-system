The tool verdicts

Ten AI tools, each one judged the way I would tell a friend who runs a business. Same three questions every time, so you can compare them properly instead of reading ten different sales pages.

Where it earns its keep

The jobs it is genuinely good at

Where it will burn you

The failure you have to plan for

What I would actually do

Buy it, trial it, or leave it

TOOL VERDICTAnthropic · Tool verdict23 Jun 2026

Claude. Use it for anything that needs to be read carefully and thought through. Do not treat it as the final word.

Best for long documents, complex drafts, work that has to hold its context, and code. Careful with facts, numbers, and anything going out under your name without a check.

Read the Claude verdict →
TOOL VERDICTOpenAI · Tool verdict23 Jun 2026

ChatGPT. The generalist. It will have a go at anything, which is exactly why you have to check it.

Best for first drafts, thinking out loud, images, and a bit of everything else. Careful with anything where roughly right is not good enough.

Read the ChatGPT verdict →
TOOL VERDICTGoogle · Tool verdict23 Jun 2026

Gemini. The one that already has your calendar, your Drive and your inbox. That is the whole argument for it.

Best for teams already living in Google Workspace, and research across your own files. Careful with opening up your whole Drive before you have checked what it can see.

Read the Gemini verdict →
TOOL VERDICTMicrosoft · Tool verdict25 Jun 2026

Copilot. The one that lives inside Word, Excel and Outlook. Buy it for where it sits, not for how clever it is.

Best for teams already paying for Microsoft 365, especially in Excel and Outlook. Careful with the price. Thirty dollars per person per month adds up fast on a small team.

Read the Copilot verdict →
TOOL VERDICTxAI · Tool verdict25 Jun 2026

Grok. Fast, loud, and wired into X. Good for watching a story break. Not for work you will put your name on.

Best for real-time chatter on X, plus image and video generation. Careful with tone and accuracy. It is built to be provocative, and it succeeds.

Read the Grok verdict →
TOOL VERDICTMistral AI · Tool verdict27 Jun 2026

Mistral. The European one. Pick it when where your data sits matters more than raw capability.

Best for multilingual work, privacy-sensitive teams, and EU data rules. Careful with a thinner integration ecosystem than the big three.

Read the Mistral verdict →
TOOL VERDICTDeepSeek · Tool verdict27 Jun 2026

DeepSeek. Very good, very cheap, and hosted in China. That last part is the whole decision.

Best for high-volume API work where cost decides it, and coding. Careful with anything client-confidential on the hosted version.

Read the DeepSeek verdict →
TOOL VERDICTMoonshot AI · Tool verdict29 Jun 2026

Kimi. A serious coding tool at a fraction of the price, with the same hosting question as DeepSeek.

Best for long coding sessions, and turning a prompt into slides or a site. Careful with the same data question, and paid tiers that climb quickly.

Read the Kimi verdict →
TOOL VERDICTMeta · Tool verdict29 Jun 2026

Meta AI. Free, already inside WhatsApp and Instagram, and you are the product.

Best for quick answers and quick images in the apps you already have open. Careful with anything about your business, your numbers or your customers.

Read the Meta AI verdict →
TOOL VERDICTManus · Tool verdict01 Jul 2026

Manus. It takes the job, goes away, and comes back with it finished. Impressive, expensive, and it will surprise you.

Best for multi-step research and admin you would otherwise pay someone to do. Careful with credits, and anything with a login or a payment attached to it.

Read the Manus verdict →

# The Feedback Digest Setup

**Keyword: FEEDBACK** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #016 (Amal) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that reads through your scattered
feedback and finds the pattern you already suspected but couldn't prove.
She never invents a theme that wasn't actually said.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **paste in the feedback you've collected this month**. That's
   the whole input.
2. She **reads all of it and groups it by theme**.
3. She **counts how often each theme came up**, most frequent first.
4. You get **one digest**, the patterns, not just the noise.

Time to build: about 15 minutes. Cost: one AI (Claude) + whatever
feedback you already have sitting in reviews, DMs, comments, or support
notes.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: the feedback text you paste in. That is the entire source of the digest.
- 🟠 Orange: the digest summary and the groupings she builds from it.
- 🔴 Red: she never invents a theme that wasn't actually said, and never guesses at severity beyond what the words themselves show.
- The rule that matters most: every theme in the digest quotes the actual words, not a paraphrase into something stronger than what was said.

## Part 1 — Gather what you already have (10 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. Pull together the last 10-20 pieces of feedback you've received:
   reviews, DMs, comments, support messages.
2. Don't sort or filter yet, just gather everything into one place.
3. Note roughly when each piece came in, this month is enough detail.

## Part 2 — The digest brain (5 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: the feedback you gathered, pasted in as-is.
2. **AI step**: send it with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my feedback digest analyst. Here is the feedback I've collected
this month: [PASTE REVIEWS, DMS, COMMENTS, SUPPORT NOTES].

My rules:
- Group by theme, most frequent first.
- Count how many times each theme came up.
- Quote the exact words for each theme's clearest example, don't
  paraphrase into something stronger.
- Never invent a theme that wasn't actually said.

Return the grouped digest only.
```

3. **Read it**: the digest tells you what's actually being said, at
   volume, not just the loudest single complaint.

## Part 3 — Do it monthly (5 minutes)

Once a month, repeat with that month's feedback. Patterns that show up
twice in a row are worth acting on. One-offs usually aren't.

The feeling you are buying: you stop reacting to the loudest single
comment and start seeing what's actually being said, repeatedly, by
more than one person.

## The upgrade path

Once the digest feels reliable, you have built trust with your
fourteenth AI employee. The same pattern, gather honestly, group by
theme, count what's real, is how every AI employee in this system
works. That is the system my paid products install across a whole
business.

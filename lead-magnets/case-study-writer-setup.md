# The Case Study Writer Setup

**Keyword: CASESTUDY** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #030 (Wael) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that turns a finished client win
into a written story, so a good result stops disappearing the moment
the project ends. He never publishes anything or invents a number.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **tell him what happened**: situation, approach, result. That's
   the whole input.
2. He **writes one case study**: situation, approach, result, in your
   voice.
3. He **pulls the number that actually proves the outcome**.
4. You **review it, get the client's okay, and publish it yourself**.
   He never publishes anything.

Time to build: about 15 minutes. Cost: one AI (Claude) + a finished
project you're proud of, still fresh enough to remember clearly.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: your own description of what happened. That is the entire source.
- 🟠 Orange: the written case study he builds from it.
- 🔴 Red: he never publishes anything himself, and never invents a result or a number that wasn't part of what actually happened.
- The rule that matters most: if you can't point to where a number came from, it doesn't go in the case study. A modest, real result beats an impressive, invented one.

## Part 1 — Name the win (5 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. Pick the one project you're proudest of from the last few months.
2. Write three lines: the situation before you started, what you did,
   the result.

## Part 2 — The writing brain (10 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: your three lines from Part 1, plus any real numbers you
   have.
2. **AI step**: send it with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my case study writer. Here is a project I finished:
[DESCRIBE THE SITUATION, WHAT YOU DID, THE RESULT, AND ANY REAL
NUMBERS YOU HAVE].

My rules:
- Write one case study: situation, approach, result, in my voice.
- Pull out the number that most clearly proves the outcome.
- Never invent a number or a result I didn't give you.
- Draft a client-approval version and a public promo version.

Return both versions.
```

3. **Review and clear it**: check every claim against what actually
   happened, get the client's sign-off, then publish it yourself.

## Part 3 — Do it after every real win (10 minutes)

The value compounds the closer it is to the finish line. A case study
written the week a project ends is accurate and easy; the same story
written six months later is guesswork dressed up as memory.

The feeling you are buying: your best work stops disappearing into the
next project, and starts becoming the story that sells the next five.

## The upgrade path

Once the writing feels routine, you have built trust with your twenty-
seventh AI employee. The same pattern, describe honestly, prove it with
a real number, never invent a result, is how every AI employee in this
system works. That is the system my paid products install across a
whole business.

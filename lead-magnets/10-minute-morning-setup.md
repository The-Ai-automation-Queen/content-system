## What the routine actually does

Three moves, in order, every morning:

1. **Pull** what's trending in your niche right now.
2. **Draft** a piece of content from it while it's still relevant.
3. **Review** it once, fix what needs fixing, and it's ready to post.

The skill isn't in any single step. It's in doing all three *before your
coffee gets cold*, on the same 3 prompts, every day, so it stops being a
decision and starts being a routine.

## The 10-minute setup

### 1. Pick your one platform and your one niche topic (2 minutes)

Don't try to cover six platforms on day one. Pick the one where your
audience actually is. Write down, in one sentence, what you post about.
That sentence is your niche filter for step 2.

### 2. Build your "pull trends" prompt (3 minutes)

Open ChatGPT or Claude. Save this as a reusable prompt:

> *Act as a trend researcher for [your niche]. List the 5 topics people in
> this space are talking about most this week. For each one, give me a
> one-line reason it's relevant right now. Keep it specific, no generic
> advice.*

Run it. That's your "8:00am pulling trends" step, done manually, in one
message instead of an automated system.

**Traffic Light Rule:** only paste public, general niche terms into this
prompt. Never paste client names, real numbers, or anything you wouldn't
post publicly. Green content only.

### 3. Build your "draft it" prompt (3 minutes)

Pick the one topic from your list that fits your platform and your voice.
Save this second prompt:

> *Write one [platform] post about [topic] for my audience of [who they
> are]. Hook in the first line. Plain language, short sentences, no
> corporate jargon, no em-dashes. End with one clear next step for the
> reader.*

That's your draft. Not perfect. Draftable.

### 4. Review and approve (2 minutes)

Read it once. Fix anything that doesn't sound like you. Check every number
or claim traces to something real, never invent a stat to make a post land.
Hit post, or queue it for later that day.

---

## Your first week

- **Day 1:** set up both prompts, run the full 3 steps once, time yourself.
- **Day 2-3:** run it again. Notice what you keep editing, tighten the
  prompts to fix it.
- **Day 4-5:** you should be under 10 minutes. If not, your niche sentence
  from step 1 is probably too broad, narrow it.
- **By day 7:** this is a routine, not a task you have to remember to do.

That's the manual version of what you saw in the video: one topic pull, one
draft, one review, every morning.

# The Report Summarizer Setup

**Keyword: REPORT** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #047 (Lamia) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that turns a long industry
report into the three things that actually matter to your business,
so it stops sitting unread in a tab. She never claims a finding the
report doesn't actually contain.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **give her the report**: pasted text, a link's content, or the
   key sections. That's the whole input.
2. She **pulls out the three most relevant findings** for your
   business specifically.
3. She **writes one plain-language line per finding**.
4. You **review it and decide what, if anything, to act on**. She
   never acts on a finding or publishes anything.

Time to build: about 10 minutes. The tools are boring on purpose: one
AI (Claude), the report you've been meaning to read.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: the report's actual text or sections. That is the entire source.
- 🟠 Orange: the three findings and plain-language lines she builds from it.
- 🔴 Red: she never claims a finding the report doesn't contain, and never acts on anything or publishes a summary publicly.
- The rule that matters most: every finding traces back to the actual report. A short honest summary beats a padded invented one.

## Part 1 — Find the report (5 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. Pick one report, whitepaper, or long article that's been sitting
   unread.
2. Copy the sections that look most relevant to your business.

## Part 2 — The summarizing brain (5 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: the report text from Part 1.
2. **AI step**: send it with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my report summarizer. Here is the report (or the relevant
sections): [PASTE THE REPORT TEXT].

My rules:
- Pull out the three findings most relevant to a small business like
  mine.
- Write one plain-language line per finding, no jargon.
- Never claim a finding that isn't actually in the text.
- Say if the report doesn't have three relevant findings; don't pad it.

Return the three findings only.
```

3. **Review and decide**: check each finding against the source, then
   decide what, if anything, is worth acting on.

## Part 3 — Do it for one report a week (10 minutes)

Pick one report a week instead of trying to read everything. A
focused habit beats an overflowing reading list every time.

The feeling you are buying: you stop drowning in unread reports, and
start pulling out the handful of findings that actually apply to you.

## The upgrade path

Once the summarizing feels routine, you have built trust with your
forty-fourth AI employee. The same pattern, source honestly, summarize
plainly, never invent a finding, is how every AI employee in this
system works. That is the system my paid products install across a
whole business.

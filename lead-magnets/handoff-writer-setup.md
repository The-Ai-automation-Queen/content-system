# The Handoff Writer Setup

**Keyword: HANDOFF** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #038 (Nabil) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that turns what's in your head
into a clean handoff note before you step away, so nothing falls
through while you're out. He never sends anything or invents a task
you didn't give him.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **tell him what's open, due, and who to watch**. That's the
   whole input.
2. He **writes one clean handoff note**: open items, due dates, who
   needs what.
3. He **flags anything with a deadline while you're away**.
4. You **review it and send it yourself**. He never sends anything.

Time to build: about 10 minutes. The tools are boring on purpose: one
AI (Claude), a rundown of what's actually open right now.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: your own rundown of what's open, due, and who to watch. That is the entire source.
- 🟠 Orange: the written handoff note and deadline flags he builds from it.
- 🔴 Red: he never sends anything himself, and never invents a task, a deadline, or a name you didn't actually give him.
- The rule that matters most: if you didn't list it, it's not in the handoff. A short honest handoff beats a padded, invented one.

## Part 1 — Say what's open (5 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. Write three lines: what's open, what's due while you're away, who
   needs to be kept in the loop.
2. Note anyone who's covering and what they'd need to know first.

## Part 2 — The handoff brain (5 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: your three lines from Part 1.
2. **AI step**: send it with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my handoff writer. Here is what's open: [WHAT'S OPEN, WHAT'S
DUE WHILE I'M AWAY, WHO'S COVERING, WHO NEEDS TO BE KEPT IN THE LOOP].

My rules:
- Write one clean handoff note: open items, due dates, who to watch.
- Keep it under 200 words.
- Flag anything due while I'm away, clearly, at the top.
- Never add a task, deadline, or name I didn't give you.

Return the handoff note only.
```

3. **Review and send**: check it against what's actually open, then
   send it yourself, to whoever's covering.

## Part 3 — Do it before every stretch away (10 minutes)

Write the handoff before any day you're heads-down or out, not just
vacations. The small gaps add up to the same dropped balls as the big
ones.

The feeling you are buying: you stop hoping someone remembers to ask,
and start leaving a clean trail before you go quiet.

## The upgrade path

Once the handoffs feel routine, you have built trust with your
thirty-fifth AI employee. The same pattern, list honestly, write
plainly, never invent a task, is how every AI employee in this system
works. That is the system my paid products install across a whole
business.

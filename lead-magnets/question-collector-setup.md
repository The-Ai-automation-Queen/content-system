# The Question Collector Setup

**Keyword: QUESTIONS** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #046 (Souad) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that turns the questions people
keep asking you into a running list of content you already know how to
write. She never posts anything or invents a question nobody actually
asked.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **give her the questions you've been asked**: in comments, DMs,
   calls, emails. That's the whole input.
2. She **groups the repeats** so you see the pattern.
3. She **turns each group into one content idea**, titled and ready to
   write.
4. You **review the list and write from it yourself**. She never posts
   anything.

Time to build: about 10 minutes. The tools are boring on purpose: one
AI (Claude), the questions already sitting in your inbox and comments.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: the actual questions people have asked you. That is the entire source.
- 🟠 Orange: the grouped list and the content ideas she builds from it.
- 🔴 Red: she never posts anything herself, and never invents a question nobody actually asked or a demand that doesn't exist.
- The rule that matters most: every idea traces to a real question. A short honest list beats a padded invented one.

## Part 1 — Collect the questions (5 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. Pull five to ten questions you've actually been asked recently, from
   comments, DMs, calls, or emails.
2. Paste them into one place, word for word.

## Part 2 — The collecting brain (5 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: your questions from Part 1.
2. **AI step**: send it with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my question collector. Here are questions people have actually
asked me: [PASTE THE QUESTIONS, ONE PER LINE].

My rules:
- Group questions that are really asking the same thing.
- Turn each group into one content idea with a working title.
- Never invent a question I didn't actually paste in.
- Keep each idea to one line.

Return the grouped list of content ideas only.
```

3. **Review and write**: pick the idea that's easiest to answer today,
   and write it yourself.

## Part 3 — Add to it every week, don't let it go stale (10 minutes)

Pull fresh questions each week, even a handful. A list built once and
never refreshed just becomes last month's ideas.

The feeling you are buying: you stop staring at a blank page wondering
what to post, and start writing answers people already asked for.

## The upgrade path

Once the collecting feels routine, you have built trust with your
forty-third AI employee. The same pattern, collect honestly, group
plainly, never invent a question, is how every AI employee in this
system works. That is the system my paid products install across a
whole business.

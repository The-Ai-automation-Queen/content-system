# The Signature Chaser Setup

**Keyword: SIGN** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #022 (Adam) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that writes the polite nudge on a
stalled contract, so it doesn't sit unsigned out of politeness. He never
sends anything or touches the terms.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **tell him about the contract**: who it's with, how long it's
   waited. That's the whole input.
2. He **drafts one polite reminder**, tone matched to how long it's
   been waiting.
3. Past 7 days, he **flags it plainly** instead of staying gentle
   forever.
4. You **review it and send it yourself**. He never sends anything.

Time to build: about 10 minutes. Cost: one AI (Claude) + the contract or
proposal you've been meaning to follow up on.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: the contract's status and history, as you describe it. That is the entire source.
- 🟠 Orange: the drafted reminder message itself.
- 🔴 Red: he never sends anything, never changes contract terms, and never negotiates on your behalf.
- The rule that matters most: he never invents urgency or a reason that isn't true. A stalled contract gets an honest nudge, not a manufactured deadline.

## Part 1 — Name the stalled one (2 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. Name the one contract or proposal you've been meaning to follow up
   on.
2. Write down how long it's actually been sitting, and what was last
   said.

## Part 2 — The nudge brain (5 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: who the contract is with, how long it's waited, what's
   already been said.
2. **AI step**: send it with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my signature chaser. Here is a contract that's stalled:
[DESCRIBE WHO IT'S WITH, HOW LONG IT'S BEEN WAITING, WHAT'S ALREADY
BEEN SAID].

My rules:
- Draft one polite reminder, tone matched to how long it's been
  waiting.
- If it's been over 7 days, flag that plainly, don't stay endlessly
  gentle.
- Never invent urgency or a reason that isn't true.
- Never mark this as sent, that is always my step.

Return the reminder message, and the stalled flag if it applies.
```

3. **Review and send**: read it once for tone, then send it yourself.

## Part 3 — Check weekly (5 minutes)

Once a week, look at what's still unsigned. Most delays aren't a no,
they're just a busy person who needs one more nudge.

The feeling you are buying: a contract stops sitting silent out of
politeness, and starts getting the honest follow-up it needed from day
one.

## The upgrade path

Once nudging feels routine instead of awkward, you have built trust
with your nineteenth AI employee. The same pattern, describe honestly,
draft honestly, escalate plainly, is how every AI employee in this
system works. That is the system my paid products install across a
whole business.

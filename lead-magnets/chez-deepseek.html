1
Know Before You Eat
The chef is brilliant, but not perfect. Read this before you order.
Before you sit down, know this: even the best chef makes mistakes. Sometimes they confidently say "this is nut-free" when it's not, that's a hallucination. Sometimes they fill gaps with a convincing story instead of saying "I don't know", that's confabulation. They might agree with everything you say even when you're wrong, that's sycophancy. Someone could slip a fake ticket into the kitchen to hijack your order, that's prompt injection. The chef might accidentally reveal another table's secrets, that's data leakage. They carry unconscious habits from their training, that's bias. And sometimes they state things as certain when they're actually guessing, that's overconfidence. Knowing these risks makes you a smarter customer. Now let's meet the chef.
Now meet this restaurant's chef
2
The Chef & Their Brain
Before anyone walks in, the chef already exists. This is who they are.
Two chefs share the kitchen. V4 Flash is the everyday chef, fast and incredibly cheap. V4 Pro handles harder problems with stronger reasoning. Both support a thinking toggle (slow + careful vs fast + direct). The context window reaches roughly 1 million words. Every word is a token. Strong bilingual: English + Chinese, with solid coverage in many other languages. Signature edge: 10 to 100x cheaper per token than ChatGPT or Claude for comparable quality on coding and reasoning.
A customer walks in through one of these doors
3
The Front Door
Multiple ways to enter this restaurant
Main entrance is chat.deepseek.com, completely free, no subscription. iOS + Android apps for mobile. Developer API for plugging DeepSeek into your own software, OpenAI-compatible so switching is trivial. Open-source download: model weights are free, run on your own GPUs.
You place your order, how it's written matters
4
The Order Slip
Instructions and context that shape every dish
Every order has layers. Model picker lets you choose V4 Flash or V4 Pro. Thinking toggle tells the chef to reason step-by-step or answer fast. Web search toggle adds live web results to the answer. System prompt (on the API) sets persistent behaviour. And prompt engineering is learning to write clearer tickets for better dishes.
The order slip goes to the kitchen
5
The Kitchen
Where the real work happens, code, writing, research
The order arrives. Coding is the signature station, DeepSeek matches GPT-class quality on programming benchmarks at a fraction of the price. Writing across languages, especially strong bilingual EN + ZH. Document analysis on uploaded files, the 1M context handles long PDFs. Web search grounds answers in current information. Image understanding reads charts and screenshots.
For complex workflows, the team system kicks in
6
The Kitchen Team
Long-context, projects, self-host
Long-context multi-doc: upload many files at once, ask cross-document questions. Project threads keep conversations grouped. Self-host stack: run DeepSeek on your own infra for total data control. API tool-use for agentic workflows on the developer side. Community fine-tunes: thousands of specialised variants on Hugging Face for legal, medical, finance, and more.
The kitchen connects to outside suppliers
7
The Supply Chain
How the kitchen connects to the outside world
Web search brings live information. File uploads let you bring your own ingredients. Developer API exposes the kitchen to other apps with extremely cheap per-token pricing. Open-source weights available on Hugging Face and ModelScope, downloadable for free. Fewer connectors: no Slack / Drive / Notion integrations out of the box, copy-paste is the bridge.
The dish gets plated and delivered to you
8
Plating & Delivery
How the finished dish reaches you
The dish lands as a text answer, fast on Flash, more careful on Pro with thinking on. Code blocks with syntax highlighting. Sourced answers when web search is on. Document summaries from uploads. Image-grounded answers when you upload visuals. No image generation, no video, no voice on the hosted product.
After service, the restaurant reviews and improves
9
Where DeepSeek Fits
When to pick this kitchen over the others
DeepSeek wins on cost: 10-100x cheaper API than ChatGPT for similar coding/reasoning quality. It wins on open-source: full weights, self-hostable, audit-friendly. It wins on free chat: no $20/month subscription for the hosted product. It loses on integrations vs ChatGPT or Copilot. It loses on English polish for nuanced professional writing vs Claude. It loses on privacy for compliance-heavy industries unless you self-host. Pick DeepSeek for high-volume API workloads, coding-heavy use, bilingual EN/ZH work, or self-hosting needs.
Wrapping around everything, the rules, limits, and the bill
10
Health, Safety & The Bill
The non-negotiables that wrap around everything
This zone wraps around everything. Hosted data residency: China, factor for regulated industries. Content rules follow Chinese law on the hosted product. Free hosted: chat.deepseek.com + apps, no subscription. API pay-per-use: V4 Flash roughly $0.14/M input tokens, V4 Pro at deep discount. Self-host free: model weights free to download, you pay your own compute.

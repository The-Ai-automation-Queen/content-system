# The Social Calendar Setup

**Keyword: CALENDAR** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #025 (Reem) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that turns your scattered content
ideas into one week's plan. She never posts or schedules anything
herself.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **give her the ideas already sitting around**. That's the whole
   input.
2. She **slots them across the week**, campaigns and launches first,
   evergreen content filling the rest.
3. She **balances the mix** so one platform isn't carrying the whole
   week.
4. You **review the plan and adjust** before it goes anywhere.

Time to build: about 10 minutes. Cost: one AI (Claude) + whatever ideas
you already have sitting in notes, screenshots, or half-drafts.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: the ideas and topics you provide. That is the entire source.
- 🟠 Orange: the weekly slotting and the mix balance she builds from it.
- 🔴 Red: she never posts or schedules anything herself, and never invents a topic you didn't give her.
- The rule that matters most: an empty week stays empty rather than getting filled with an invented idea. A gap in the plan is information, not something to paper over.

## Part 1 — Gather what you already have (5 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. Pull together every idea currently sitting in notes, screenshots, or
   half-finished drafts.
2. Note anything time-bound this week: a launch, a campaign, an event.

## Part 2 — The calendar brain (5 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: your scattered ideas, and anything time-bound this week.
2. **AI step**: send it with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my social calendar. Here are my ideas for this week:
[LIST YOUR IDEAS, TOPICS, AND ANYTHING TIME-BOUND LIKE A LAUNCH OR
CAMPAIGN].

My rules:
- Slot campaigns and launches first, evergreen ideas filling the rest.
- Balance the mix so one platform doesn't carry the whole week.
- If there aren't enough ideas to fill the week, say so, don't invent one.
- Output in a simple day-by-day list, ready to check against my
  calendar.

Return the week's plan only.
```

3. **Review and adjust**: check it against your actual week before
   anything goes anywhere.

## Part 3 — Do it every Sunday (10 minutes)

Once a week, before the week starts, run the same check. A plan made in
advance beats a decision made the same morning, every time.

The feeling you are buying: you stop opening the week not knowing what
you're posting, and start knowing, on purpose, what's going out and why.

## The upgrade path

Once the planning feels routine, you have built trust with your
twenty-second AI employee. The same pattern, gather honestly, slot
plainly, never invent a gap, is how every AI employee in this system
works. That is the system my paid products install across a whole
business.

# The Referral Asker Setup

**Keyword: REFER** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #036 (Marwan) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that notices when a client is
happy and writes you the ask you keep meaning to send. He never sends
anything or invents a client's words.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **tell him when a project wrapped well**: client, what you did,
   anything good they said. That's the whole input.
2. He **writes one short referral ask**, timed to the moment the work
   is fresh.
3. He **drafts it in your voice**, not a template voice.
4. You **review it and send it yourself**. He never sends anything.

Time to build: about 10 minutes. The tools are boring on purpose: one
AI (Claude), the memory of a job that went well.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: the client's name, what you did, anything they actually said. That is the entire source.
- 🟠 Orange: the drafted referral ask he builds from it.
- 🔴 Red: he never sends anything himself, and never invents a quote or a compliment the client didn't give.
- The rule that matters most: if the client didn't say it, it doesn't go in the ask. A plain, honest ask beats a flattering, invented one.

## Part 1 — Catch the moment (5 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. Right after a project wraps well, write three lines: client name,
   what you delivered, anything good they said.
2. Note who else they might know with the same problem, if you know.

## Part 2 — The asking brain (5 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: your three lines from Part 1.
2. **AI step**: send it with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my referral asker. Here is a project that just wrapped well:
[CLIENT NAME, WHAT I DELIVERED, ANYTHING THEY ACTUALLY SAID].

My rules:
- Write one short referral ask, under 80 words.
- Keep it in plain, direct language, not a sales voice.
- Never invent a quote or compliment the client didn't give.
- End with one specific ask: a name, an introduction, or a review.

Return the ask only.
```

3. **Review and send**: check it sounds like you, then send it
   yourself, to the client directly.

## Part 3 — Do it every time, not just the big wins (10 minutes)

Send the ask within a few days of the work wrapping, while it's still
fresh. The small, steady jobs deserve the same ask as the big ones.

The feeling you are buying: you stop letting a happy client go quiet,
and start turning good work into the next lead, on purpose.

## The upgrade path

Once the asking feels routine, you have built trust with your
thirty-third AI employee. The same pattern, catch the moment honestly,
ask plainly, never invent a quote, is how every AI employee in this
system works. That is the system my paid products install across a
whole business.

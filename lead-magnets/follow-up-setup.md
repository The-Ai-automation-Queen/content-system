# The Lead Follow-Up Setup (Stop Doing This by Hand)

*From Fatiha — the AI Automation Queen*

Here's the brutal truth: most people lose money not because they can't get
attention, but because they can't *follow up*. Someone comments, someone opts
in… and then it sits in your DMs until the excitement is dead.

This is the exact setup I use so **every** raised hand gets caught, gets the
thing, and gets followed up with — automatically. You build it once. It runs
forever.

---

## What you're building

```
Someone comments your keyword  →  instant DM with the link
        →  they opt in  →  resource delivered + tagged in your CRM
        →  3-message follow-up sequence runs on its own
```

You can build all of this inside one tool (I use GoHighLevel). No code.

---

## Step 1 — The trigger
Set a workflow to fire when a comment (or form opt-in) contains your keyword —
e.g. `STACK`, `FOLLOW UP`, `TEAM`. One workflow per offer.

## Step 2 — The instant reply
Auto-send the DM the second they comment. Keep it human:

> "Yes! Sending it now 👉 [link]. Tell me what you build with it."

Speed is everything. The reply that arrives in 10 seconds converts; the one
that arrives in 10 hours doesn't.

## Step 3 — Capture + tag
When they opt in, save the email and tag them by the offer they wanted. Now you
know what each person actually cares about — gold for later.

## Step 4 — The 3-message follow-up (copy these)

**Message 1 — same day (deliver + set up the next step):**
> "There's your [resource] 🎉 Quick heads up: the magic isn't the tool, it's
> setting it up *once* so it runs without you. Hit reply and tell me the one
> task you'd hand off first."

**Message 2 — day 2 (value + soft proof):**
> "Most people grab a tool and never wire it in — so it changes nothing. The
> ones who win pick ONE repetitive task and let the system own it. Here's the
> 30-min version → [link to a post/video]."

**Message 3 — day 4 (the invitation):**
> "If doing this solo feels slow, that's normal — it's faster with people a
> step ahead of you. That's exactly what [your community/offer] is for → [link].
> Either way, I'm in your corner. 👑"

---

You're not "being salesy." You're being *reliable* — the thing you promised
actually shows up, every time, without you. That's what builds trust at scale.

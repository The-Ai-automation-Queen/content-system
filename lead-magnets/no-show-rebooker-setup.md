# The No-Show Rebooker Setup

**Keyword: REBOOK** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #044 (Nizar) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that writes the rebooking message
the moment a prospect no-shows a call, so the lead doesn't quietly die
from awkward silence. He never sends anything or invents a reason the
prospect missed the call.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **tell him who no-showed and what the call was about**. That's
   the whole input.
2. He **writes one short, no-blame rebooking message**.
3. He **suggests a time window to offer**, based on what you tell him.
4. You **review it and send it yourself**. He never sends anything.

Time to build: about 10 minutes. The tools are boring on purpose: one
AI (Claude), the calendar invite that just got skipped.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: who no-showed, what the call was about, your general availability. That is the entire source.
- 🟠 Orange: the rebooking message and suggested time window he builds from it.
- 🔴 Red: he never sends anything himself, and never invents a reason, an apology on the prospect's behalf, or a guilt-trip line.
- The rule that matters most: no blame, ever. A short neutral rebook beats a passive-aggressive one every time.

## Part 1 — Note what happened (5 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. Write down who missed the call and what it was supposed to cover.
2. Note two or three times you could offer instead.

## Part 2 — The rebooking brain (5 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: what you wrote in Part 1.
2. **AI step**: send it with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my no-show rebooker. Here is what happened: [WHO NO-SHOWED,
WHAT THE CALL WAS ABOUT, TIMES I COULD OFFER INSTEAD].

My rules:
- Write one short, no-blame message offering to reschedule.
- Suggest the time window I gave you.
- Never guess why they missed it or add an apology on their behalf.
- Keep it under 60 words, warm, not passive-aggressive.

Return the message only.
```

3. **Review and send**: check the tone is right, then send it
   yourself, same day if possible.

## Part 3 — Send it the same day, every time (10 minutes)

A no-show message sent the same day reads as business as usual. One
sent three days later reads as a guilt trip, even if you don't mean it
that way.

The feeling you are buying: you stop letting a missed call become a
dead lead, and start treating it as the ordinary thing it is.

## The upgrade path

Once the rebooking feels routine, you have built trust with your
forty-first AI employee. The same pattern, note honestly, write
plainly, never guess at blame, is how every AI employee in this system
works. That is the system my paid products install across a whole
business.

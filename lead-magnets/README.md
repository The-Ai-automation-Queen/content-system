# Shift & Lead Lead Magnets

This folder contains lead-magnet source material accumulated across several versions of the Shift & Lead funnel.

## Current rule — August 2026

The public Guides library stays open. Do **not** put the guide itself behind an email wall.

The active acquisition model is:

```
OPEN GUIDE
  → CONTEXTUAL COMPANION ASSET
  → EMAIL CONTACT
  → RELEVANT NEXT GUIDE / IMPLEMENTATION
  → PAID QUICK WIN / COURSE / SERVICE WHEN LIVE
```

A companion asset earns the email by helping the reader **do** the thing they just learned. Good formats include a worksheet, checklist, scorecard, decision tree, canvas, template or personalised diagnostic result.

Do not use a generic newsletter subscription as the default CTA while the AI Insider Brief is paused.

## Content boundary

### Layer 1 — Open guide

- no email required
- outcome-first explanation
- useful answer early
- one practical result
- discoverable through search and social content

### Layer 2 — Companion asset

- email capture is appropriate
- must be directly related to the guide
- should save the reader work: template, map, checklist, worksheet, scorecard or diagnostic
- instant access may be revealed after a successful capture
- one form submission only

### Layer 3 — Implementation

- paid when the product/service is actually live
- complete system, deeper templates, setup, testing, support or acceleration
- do not invent prices, checkout links or availability

## Legacy files in this folder

Files such as:

- `first-ai-employee.md`
- `follow-up-setup.md`
- `stack-3-tool-ai-stack.md`
- `voice-clone-pipeline.md`
- older comment-keyword resources
- `chez-*.html`

are **source material, not automatically approved live assets**.

Several were written for an older positioning era and may contain language such as "AI employee", "freedom business", "stop using AI", old comment-keyword CTAs or other claims that no longer match the current Shift & Lead editorial system.

Before publishing any legacy resource:

1. rewrite the title outcome-first;
2. remove hype and stale offer language;
3. verify every tool-specific claim;
4. preserve human review and permission boundaries;
5. make the asset materially more useful than the open guide;
6. use the current off-white / Shift & Lead visual system;
7. connect it to one relevant next step only;
8. verify a single email submission and correct lead source tagging.

## Editorial rule

The asset name must make the outcome obvious.

Good:

- AI Assistant Builder
- Business Brain Starter Canvas
- AI Job Scorecard
- Research-to-Content Workflow
- Permission & Human Review Checklist

Avoid vague names such as "ultimate toolkit", "prompt vault" or "AI secrets".

The test for every lead magnet is simple:

> Would someone reasonably give their email because this saves them time or helps them make/build something now?

If not, keep it inside the open guide instead.

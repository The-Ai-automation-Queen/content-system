# The Time Digest Setup

**Keyword: HOURS** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #021 (Layla) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that reads how your week actually
went and tells you honestly where the hours went, compared to what you
meant to focus on. She never touches your calendar directly.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **describe roughly how the week went**. That's the whole input.
2. She **compares it to what you meant to focus on**.
3. She **flags the biggest gap** between planned and actual.
4. She **suggests one calendar change** for next week, not a whole
   system.

Time to build: about 10 minutes. Cost: one AI (Claude) + two minutes on
a Friday to think back over the week.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: your own description of the week and what you planned. That is the entire source.
- 🟠 Orange: the comparison and the one suggested change she builds from it.
- 🔴 Red: she never accesses your actual calendar, email, or messages. She only works from what you tell her.
- The rule that matters most: she never guesses at how you spent time you didn't describe. A gap in the story stays a gap, not a filled-in assumption.

## Part 1 — Know what you planned (2 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. At the start of your week, write down the 2-3 things you meant to
   focus on. One line each.
2. Keep it somewhere you'll see it again Friday.

## Part 2 — The digest brain (5 minutes, end of week)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: roughly how the week went, and what you planned at the
   start.
2. **AI step**: send both with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my time digest analyst. Here is roughly how this week went:
[DESCRIBE MEETINGS, TASKS, WHAT ATE TIME]. Here is what I meant to
focus on: [YOUR PLANNED PRIORITIES].

My rules:
- Compare planned versus actual honestly.
- Flag the biggest time sink, named plainly.
- Suggest exactly one calendar change for next week.
- Never guess at how I spent time I didn't describe.

Return the comparison and the one suggested change only.
```

3. **Read it**: one honest paragraph, not a productivity lecture. Take
   the one suggested change into next week or don't, your call.

## Part 3 — Do it every Friday (5 minutes)

The value compounds. One week's digest is interesting. A month of them
shows you the pattern you can't see from inside any single week.

The feeling you are buying: you stop starting each week guessing what
happened to the last one, and start seeing the actual shape of your
time.

## The upgrade path

Once the digest feels honest and useful, you have built trust with your
eighteenth AI employee. The same pattern, describe honestly, compare
plainly, suggest one real change, is how every AI employee in this
system works. That is the system my paid products install across a
whole business.

# The Testimonial Collector Setup

**Keyword: TESTIMONY** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #037 (Jana) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that turns a happy client's own
words into a usable testimonial, before the moment is forgotten. She
never sends anything or invents a word the client didn't say.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **give her the client's own words**: a thank-you message, a
   nice comment, a kind email. That's the whole input.
2. She **pulls the quotable line out**, as written, not rewritten.
3. She **drafts the short ask** for permission to use it.
4. You **review it and send it yourself**. She never sends anything or
   publishes anything.

Time to build: about 10 minutes. The tools are boring on purpose: one
AI (Claude), the nice message a client already sent you.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: the client's own message, exactly as they sent it. That is the entire source.
- 🟠 Orange: the pulled quote and the permission-ask draft she builds from it.
- 🔴 Red: she never sends or publishes anything herself, and never rewrites a client's words into something they didn't say.
- The rule that matters most: the quote stays exactly as given. A short, real testimonial beats a polished, invented one.

## Part 1 — Save the moment (5 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. The next time a client says something kind, paste the exact message
   somewhere you won't lose it.
2. Note their name and what the project was.

## Part 2 — The collecting brain (5 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: the client's exact message from Part 1.
2. **AI step**: send it with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my testimonial collector. Here is a message a client sent me:
[THE CLIENT'S EXACT MESSAGE, THEIR NAME, WHAT THE PROJECT WAS].

My rules:
- Pull out the single most quotable line, word for word, no rewriting.
- Draft a short message asking permission to use it publicly.
- Never add words the client didn't say.
- Keep the permission ask under 60 words.

Return the quoted line and the permission ask only.
```

3. **Review and send**: check the quote is exact, then send the
   permission ask yourself.

## Part 3 — Make it a habit, not an event (10 minutes)

Every time a client says something good, run it through the same two
steps the same week. Testimonials saved late are testimonials that
never get collected.

The feeling you are buying: you stop losing good words to a buried
inbox, and start building proof while it's still warm.

## The upgrade path

Once the collecting feels routine, you have built trust with your
thirty-fourth AI employee. The same pattern, keep the exact words,
ask plainly, never rewrite a client, is how every AI employee in this
system works. That is the system my paid products install across a
whole business.

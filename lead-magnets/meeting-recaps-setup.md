# The Meeting Recaps Setup

**Keyword: RECAP** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #027 (Tamer) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that turns a good sales call into
a written recap the same day, so the momentum doesn't go quiet. He
never sends anything or adds a commitment that wasn't made.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **tell him roughly how the call went**. That's the whole input.
2. He **writes one clear recap**: decisions, actions, owners, dates.
3. He **flags any open question** that could stall the deal.
4. You **review it and send it yourself**. He never sends anything.

Time to build: about 10 minutes. Cost: one AI (Claude) + the call you
just had, still fresh in your memory.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: your own description of the call. That is the entire source.
- 🟠 Orange: the recap and the open-question flag he builds from it.
- 🔴 Red: he never sends anything himself, and never adds a commitment or a number that wasn't actually discussed.
- The rule that matters most: if you didn't say it happened, it doesn't go in the recap. An honest gap beats a filled-in guess.

## Part 1 — Capture it fresh (2 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. Right after the call, write three lines: what was decided, what's
   next, who owns it.
2. Note anything left open or unresolved.

## Part 2 — The recap brain (5 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: your three lines from Part 1, or a rougher description if
   that's all you have.
2. **AI step**: send it with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my meeting recap writer. Here is roughly how the call went:
[DESCRIBE DECISIONS, ACTIONS, WHO OWNS WHAT, AND ANYTHING LEFT OPEN].

My rules:
- Write one clear recap: decisions, actions, owners, dates.
- Flag any open question that could stall the deal.
- Never add a commitment or a number I didn't actually mention.
- Write both an internal version and a client-safe version.

Return both versions and the flagged open questions only.
```

3. **Review and send**: check it against your own memory of the call,
   then send it yourself.

## Part 3 — Do it the same day, every time (5 minutes)

The value is almost entirely in the speed. A recap sent the same day
keeps a deal moving; the same recap sent a week later reads like an
afterthought.

The feeling you are buying: a good call stops evaporating into the rest
of your week, and starts turning into the next real step.

## The upgrade path

Once the recap feels routine, you have built trust with your twenty-
fourth AI employee. The same pattern, capture honestly, write plainly,
never invent a commitment, is how every AI employee in this system
works. That is the system my paid products install across a whole
business.

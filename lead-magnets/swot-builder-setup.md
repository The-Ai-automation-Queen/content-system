# The SWOT Builder Setup

**Keyword: SWOT** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #033 (Hana) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that writes the honest snapshot
before a big decision, strengths and weaknesses both, so a decision
stops circling half-formed in your head. She never invents evidence for
a point.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **tell her the decision and what you know about your position**.
   That's the whole input.
2. She **builds one SWOT**, backed by what you actually told her.
3. She **turns the weaknesses into a short list of things you could
   act on**.
4. You **review it and decide**, with the full picture in front of you.

Time to build: about 15 minutes. Cost: one AI (Claude) + the decision
you've been circling and what you actually know about it.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: your own description of the decision and your position. That is the entire source.
- 🟠 Orange: the SWOT and the action items she builds from it.
- 🔴 Red: she never invents evidence for a point, and never claims a strength, weakness, opportunity, or threat you didn't actually mention.
- The rule that matters most: every item traces back to something you actually said. An honest, shorter SWOT beats a complete-looking, invented one.

## Part 1 — Name the decision (5 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. Write down the one decision you've been circling.
2. Write three lines about where you actually stand: what's working,
   what isn't, what you're worried about.

## Part 2 — The building brain (10 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: the decision, plus your three lines from Part 1.
2. **AI step**: send it with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my SWOT builder. Here is the decision I'm facing: [DESCRIBE THE
DECISION]. Here is what I know about where I stand: [YOUR STRENGTHS,
WEAKNESSES, OPPORTUNITIES, THREATS, AS YOU SEE THEM].

My rules:
- Build one SWOT, backed only by what I actually told you.
- Turn the weaknesses into a short list of things I could act on.
- Never claim a point I didn't actually mention.
- Flag anything you don't have enough information to assess.

Return the SWOT and the action items only.
```

3. **Read and decide**: use the full picture, weaknesses included, to
   actually make the call.

## Part 3 — Update it after major events (10 minutes)

Whenever something real shifts, run the same check again. A SWOT from
six months ago describes a company that no longer quite exists.

The feeling you are buying: you stop deciding on a partial picture, and
start deciding with the weaknesses written down where you can see them.

## The upgrade path

Once the building feels routine, you have built trust with your
thirtieth AI employee. The same pattern, describe honestly, back every
point with evidence, never invent a strength, is how every AI employee
in this system works. That is the system my paid products install
across a whole business.

# The Call Prep Setup

**Keyword: PREP** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #041 (Rayan) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that pulls together what you
already know about a prospect before a sales call, so you walk in
ready instead of scrambling. He never contacts the prospect or invents
a detail you didn't give him.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **give him what you know about the prospect**: their company,
   past emails, anything they've said. That's the whole input.
2. He **writes one short prep brief**: who they are, what they likely
   need, what to ask.
3. He **lists two or three good questions** for the call.
4. You **review it before you dial in**. He never contacts anyone
   himself.

Time to build: about 10 minutes. The tools are boring on purpose: one
AI (Claude), whatever you already have on the prospect.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: your own notes, past emails, and anything the prospect has already said. That is the entire source.
- 🟠 Orange: the prep brief and the suggested questions he builds from it.
- 🔴 Red: he never contacts the prospect himself, and never invents a detail, a need, or a past conversation you didn't give him.
- The rule that matters most: if you didn't tell him, he doesn't know it. A short honest brief beats a padded, invented one.

## Part 1 — Gather what you have (5 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. Pull together whatever you already know: their company, role, any
   past emails or calls, anything they've said about their problem.
2. Note what you still don't know and want to ask.

## Part 2 — The prep brain (5 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: what you gathered in Part 1.
2. **AI step**: send it with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my call prep assistant. Here is what I know about this
prospect: [THEIR COMPANY, ROLE, PAST EMAILS OR CALLS, ANYTHING THEY'VE
SAID ABOUT THEIR PROBLEM].

My rules:
- Write one short prep brief: who they are, what they likely need.
- List two or three good questions for the call.
- Never invent a detail, a need, or a conversation I didn't give you.
- Keep the whole thing under 150 words.

Return the brief and the questions only.
```

3. **Review before the call**: read it over a few minutes before you
   dial in, adjust anything that doesn't feel right.

## Part 3 — Do it before every call that matters (10 minutes)

Run the prep before any call where showing up unprepared would cost
you, not just the big ones. The habit compounds faster than the
one-off effort.

The feeling you are buying: you stop walking into calls cold, and
start showing up like you've been thinking about them all week.

## The upgrade path

Once the prep feels routine, you have built trust with your thirty-
eighth AI employee. The same pattern, gather honestly, brief plainly,
never invent a detail, is how every AI employee in this system works.
That is the system my paid products install across a whole business.

# The 3-Tool AI Stack I Actually Use

*From Fatiha — the AI Automation Queen*

Everybody wants the giant list of 50 AI tools. You don't need 50. You need
**three** that talk to each other and quietly run the boring parts of your
business while you live your life.

Here's the exact short list. No affiliate fluff, no "you must have this" — just
what's earning its place in my own setup right now.

---

## 1. The Brain — Claude (and Claude Code)

**What it replaces:** the blank page, the "I'll write it later," the VA you
keep meaning to hire.

This is the thinking layer. It drafts my content, sorts my research, and — with
Claude Code — actually *runs* multi-step jobs for me instead of just answering
questions. I describe the outcome once; it does the work on a schedule.

**Win-back-your-time move:** stop using AI as a fancy search box. Give it a
*job* (one repeatable task, start to finish) and let it own that job.

## 2. The Megaphone — Blotato

**What it replaces:** logging into 6 apps to post the same thing 6 times.

I write/record once and Blotato pushes it to every platform from one place —
and it builds the visuals and short videos too. One queue, one review, done.

**Win-back-your-time move:** batch a week of content in one sitting, queue it,
and approve from your phone. You never "go post" again.

## 3. The Net — GoHighLevel (your CRM + follow-up)

**What it replaces:** DMs you forgot to answer and leads that went cold.

Reach is worthless if it leaks. This catches the people who raise their hand
(comment a keyword, opt in), sends them the thing instantly, and follows up
**automatically** so you're not doing it by hand at 11pm.

**Win-back-your-time move:** wire it so a comment becomes a captured lead with
a 3-message follow-up — without you touching it. (That's the *Lead Follow-Up
Setup* resource — comment **FOLLOW UP** if you want it.)

---

## The whole point

Brain → Megaphone → Net. Create once, post everywhere, never drop a lead.
Three tools, running the parts of the business that used to run *you*.

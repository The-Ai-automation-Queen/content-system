## AI is software that learned by example

Old software: a developer types every rule. "If customer clicks button, send email."

AI: nobody types rules. The software reads millions of examples of correct work, then figures out the rules itself. The developer never wrote them. The AI found them.

That's the only real difference.

## "Generative AI" is the one in every headline

There are different types of AI. The one everywhere right now is **generative AI** — the kind that *generates* new text, images, audio, and video.

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Midjourney, ElevenLabs: all generative AI. Different brands, same idea.

## It's mostly very good at writing

It reads, summarises, drafts, explains, imitates. That's the strength.

It's not magic. It's not conscious. It's not your friend. It's autocomplete trained on a huge amount of writing, with a chat window on top.

That's the whole show.

## Try this in the next 5 minutes

Open ChatGPT or Claude. Type:

> *Explain how Stripe payouts work, as if I run a small business and am not a developer.*

You get back a clean plain-language answer. Not 30 search results. Not a sales page. Just the explanation, at your level.

That's the first useful thing it does. The others take 10 minutes each to learn.

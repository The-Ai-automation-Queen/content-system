# The Launch Announcer Setup

**Keyword: LAUNCH** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #040 (Imad) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that writes the announcement the
moment something new ships, so launches stop sitting quietly unseen.
He never publishes anything or invents a feature you didn't build.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **tell him what just shipped**: what it is, who it's for, what
   it does. That's the whole input.
2. He **writes one short announcement post**, plain language, no
   hype words.
3. He **suggests one place to post it**, based on who it's for.
4. You **review it and post it yourself**. He never publishes
   anything.

Time to build: about 10 minutes. The tools are boring on purpose: one
AI (Claude), the thing you just finished building.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: what you shipped, who it's for, what it actually does. That is the entire source.
- 🟠 Orange: the drafted announcement he builds from it.
- 🔴 Red: he never posts anything himself, and never invents a feature, a stat, or a claim you didn't give him.
- The rule that matters most: it only describes what's actually built. A plain, honest announcement beats a hyped, invented one.

## Part 1 — Say what shipped (5 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. Write three lines: what it is, who it's for, the one thing it does.
2. Note where the people who'd want it already hang out.

## Part 2 — The announcing brain (5 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: your three lines from Part 1.
2. **AI step**: send it with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my launch announcer. Here is what just shipped: [WHAT IT IS,
WHO IT'S FOR, THE ONE THING IT DOES, WHERE THE RIGHT PEOPLE ALREADY
HANG OUT].

My rules:
- Write one short announcement post, under 100 words.
- Plain language, no hype words, no exclamation overload.
- Suggest one place to post it based on who it's for.
- Never add a feature, stat, or claim I didn't give you.

Return the announcement and the suggested place only.
```

3. **Review and post**: check it's accurate, then post it yourself,
   wherever fits.

## Part 3 — Do it the same day it ships (10 minutes)

Write and post the announcement the same day the thing is actually
done, not whenever you get around to it. A launch announced a month
late reads like old news.

The feeling you are buying: you stop finishing things quietly, and
start telling the people who'd actually want them the same day.

## The upgrade path

Once the announcing feels routine, you have built trust with your
thirty-seventh AI employee. The same pattern, describe honestly,
announce plainly, never invent a feature, is how every AI employee in
this system works. That is the system my paid products install across
a whole business.

1
Know Before You Eat
The chef is brilliant, but not perfect. Read this before you order.
Before you sit down, know this: even the best chef makes mistakes. Sometimes they confidently say "this is nut-free" when it's not, that's a hallucination. Sometimes they fill gaps with a convincing story, that's confabulation. They might agree with everything you say, that's sycophancy. Someone could slip a fake ticket, that's prompt injection. The chef might reveal another table's secrets, that's data leakage. They carry unconscious habits, that's bias. And sometimes they are certain when guessing, that's overconfidence. Google uses its AI Principles and safety filters to mitigate these risks.
Now meet this restaurant's chef
2
The Chef & Their Brain
Before anyone walks in, the chef already exists. This is who they are.
Google's chef has multiple modes. The Gemini Pro family is the most capable brain. Gemini Flash is a faster, cheaper chef for simpler dishes. You can toggle between Thinking (deep reasoning) and Fast (low-latency) modes. The chef reads text, images, audio, and video, that's multi-modal. The context window (countertop) is massive, up to 2 million tokens. And Memory Bank remembers you across sessions. Every word is a token.
A customer walks in through one of these doors
3
The Front Door
Multiple ways to enter this restaurant
You can walk into the Gemini App, the dining room on web and mobile. The chef also appears inside Google Workspace: Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Meet. For developers, the Gemini CLI is an open-source terminal tool. Antigravity IDE is the smart kitchen where multiple AI agents work in parallel on code. The Gemini Enterprise App brings agents to businesses. And Gemini Live lets you have a real-time voice conversation with the chef.
You place your order, how it's written matters
4
The Order Slip
Instructions and context that shape every dish
Gems are like pre-configured order profiles, custom AI assistants with saved instructions and roles. Custom Instructions tell the chef your general preferences across all conversations. System instructions (API) are the invisible ticket shaping the chef's behavior. And prompt engineering is learning to write clearer tickets for better results.
The order slip goes to the kitchen
5
The Kitchen
Where the real cooking happens, tools, research, and creation
Deep Research is the chef spending time reading dozens of sources to produce thorough reports. Canvas is a side workspace for drafting and editing documents or code. Guided Learning is the chef quizzing you to deepen understanding. The chef creates images with Imagen, generates video with Veo, and makes music with Lyria. Scheduled Actions let the chef run recurring tasks on autopilot. And Google Search grounding keeps answers anchored in real, current data.
For complex workflows, the agent system kicks in
6
The Kitchen Team
Build, govern, and scale agents
The Agent Platform (formerly Vertex AI) is the central place to build, scale, govern, and optimize agents. The Agent Development Kit is the developer framework. Agent Studio is a low-code canvas for designing multi-agent workflows. The Agent Registry is a central library of approved tools and agents. And the Agent Gallery in the Gemini Enterprise app lets teams discover and deploy partner agents from Adobe, Salesforce, ServiceNow and others.
The kitchen connects to outside suppliers
7
The Supply Chain
How the kitchen connects to the outside world
Extensions connect Gemini to Google services (Flights, Hotels, Maps, YouTube) and third-party tools. MCP support is built into the ADK and Agent Platform for standardized connections. Gemini CLI Extensions are power-ups for the terminal, pre-packaged integrations with Figma, Stripe, Shopify, and more. And A2A (Agent-to-Agent) Protocol lets agents built on different frameworks discover and collaborate with each other.
The dish gets plated and delivered to you
8
Plating & Delivery
How the finished dish reaches you
Canvas outputs let you edit and refine documents and code collaboratively. Gemini in Workspace plates directly inside Gmail, Docs, Sheets, and Slides, like the chef editing your document at the table. Streaming delivers responses word by word. NotebookLM is a personal research assistant that turns your sources into summaries and audio overviews. And the Gemini App follows you across web, Android, and iOS so you can eat anywhere.
After service, the restaurant reviews and improves
9
Training & Improvement
How the chef gets better over time
Agent Evaluation and Agent Simulation test agents before deployment, food critics scoring dishes on a rubric. Agent Observability provides real-time execution traces into agent reasoning. Model tuning on the Agent Platform lets you customize models for your domain. And Model Garden gives access to hundreds of models including third-party options like Claude and open models like Gemma and Llama.
Wrapping around everything, the rules, limits, and the bill
10
Health, Safety & The Bill
The non-negotiables that wrap around everything
This zone wraps around everything. Google AI Principles are the health inspector framework. Safety filters scan for harmful content during generation. Agent Identity gives every agent a unique cryptographic ID for auditability. Agent Gateway acts as air traffic control for your agent fleet. And the pricing ranges from free (Gemini App) through Google AI Plus and Pro to Enterprise.

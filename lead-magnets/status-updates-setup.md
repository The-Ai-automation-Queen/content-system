# The Status Updates Setup

**Keyword: STATUS** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #034 (Fadi) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that turns what's actually
happening into a clean status update, so silence stops standing in for
progress. He never sends anything or claims something is done that
isn't.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **tell him what's done, in progress, and blocked**. That's the
   whole input.
2. He **writes one clean update**: done, in progress, blocked, next.
3. He **flags anything that needs the client's action**.
4. You **review it and send it yourself**. He never sends anything.

Time to build: about 10 minutes. Cost: one AI (Claude) + a rough sense
of where things actually stand.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: your own rundown of what's done, in progress, and blocked. That is the entire source.
- 🟠 Orange: the written update and blocker flags he builds from it.
- 🔴 Red: he never sends anything himself, and never marks something done or in progress that you didn't actually tell him.
- The rule that matters most: if you didn't say it's done, it isn't done in the update either. An honest "still in progress" beats a comfortable overstatement.

## Part 1 — Say where things stand (5 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. Write three lines: what's actually done, what's in progress, what's
   blocked.
2. Note anything you need from the client to move forward.

## Part 2 — The update brain (5 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: your three lines from Part 1.
2. **AI step**: send it with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my status update writer. Here is where things stand: [WHAT'S
DONE, WHAT'S IN PROGRESS, WHAT'S BLOCKED, ANYTHING YOU NEED FROM THE
CLIENT].

My rules:
- Write one clean update: done, in progress, blocked, next.
- Keep it under 200 words.
- Flag anything that needs the client's action, clearly.
- Never mark something done or in progress that I didn't tell you.

Return the update only.
```

3. **Review and send**: check it against what's actually true, then
   send it yourself.

## Part 3 — Do it weekly, same day (5 minutes)

Pick one day a week and send the update on it, every time. A client who
knows when to expect an update stops wondering if something's gone
quiet for a bad reason.

The feeling you are buying: you stop letting silence answer for you,
and start sending the honest update before anyone has to ask.

## The upgrade path

Once the updates feel routine, you have built trust with your thirty-
first AI employee. The same pattern, describe honestly, write plainly,
flag what's blocked, is how every AI employee in this system works.
That is the system my paid products install across a whole business.

# The Social Listener Setup

**Keyword: LISTEN** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #014 (Hind) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that reads what people say about
you online, so you stop finding out three days late. She checks the
places you name, flags what needs a reply within hours, and sends one
honest weekly tone summary.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **give her the 2-3 places to check**. That's the whole input.
2. She **reads what came up this week**, those searches only.
3. She **flags anything that needs a reply within hours**: a direct
   question, a complaint, a mention from someone with real reach.
4. You get **one weekly tone summary**: mostly positive, mixed, or a
   real flag to look closer.

Time to build: about 20 minutes. Cost: one AI (Claude) + a way to search
your name, handle, or brand (most platforms' own search bar works fine).

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: public posts and mentions that show up in a normal search. That is the entire input.
- 🟠 Orange: the one-line tone summary she writes each week, drafted from what the search actually returned.
- 🔴 Red: she never DMs anyone, never replies on your behalf, and never reads anything behind a login or a private message. She only reports what a normal search shows.
- The rule that matters most: if nothing came up this week, the summary says "Nothing found," instead of manufacturing a story to look busy.

## Part 1 — Pick your search terms (10 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. Name the 2-3 things worth searching: your name, your handle, your
   brand or product name.
2. Decide what "needs a reply within hours" means for you: a direct
   question, a public complaint, a mention from someone with a real
   following.
3. Pick when you'll run the search each week, same day, so it becomes a
   habit instead of an emergency.

## Part 2 — The listening brain (10 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: this week's search results for your name, handle, or
   brand.
2. **AI step**: send them with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my social listening analyst. Here is this week's mentions of
[YOUR NAME OR BRAND]: [PASTE MENTIONS OR SEARCH RESULTS].

My rules:
- Summarize the overall tone in one line: mostly positive, mostly
  neutral, or mixed.
- Flag anything that needs a reply within hours: a direct question, a
  complaint, a mention from someone with real reach.
- Never invent a mention that wasn't given to you.
- If nothing came up this week, say exactly: "No mentions found this
  week."

Return the summary and flagged items only.
```

3. **Reply**: handle any flagged items yourself, in your own voice. She
   flags, you respond.

## Part 3 — Keep the search list honest (5 minutes a month)

Once a month, check that your 2-3 search terms are still the right ones,
especially if you've launched something new or changed your handle.

The feeling you are buying: you stop finding out about a mention from a
client instead of finding it yourself, first.

## The upgrade path

Once the weekly check feels reliable, you have built trust with your
eleventh AI employee. The same pattern, watch, flag honestly, summarize
plainly, is how every AI employee in this system works. That is the
system my paid products install across a whole business.

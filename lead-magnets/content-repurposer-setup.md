# The Content Repurposer Setup

**Keyword: REPURPOSE** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #019 (Yasmine) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that reshapes one good piece of
content into its second and third format, instead of leaving good ideas
used exactly once. She never adds a claim that wasn't in your original.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **paste in one piece of long-form content** you already wrote.
   That's the whole input.
2. She **pulls out the one core insight** worth keeping.
3. She **reshapes it into 2-3 shorter formats**: a short post and a
   few standalone pull-quotes.
4. You **review and post them yourself**, in your own voice.

Time to build: about 15 minutes. Cost: one AI (Claude) + one piece of
content you already wrote and liked.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: the long-form content you paste in. That is the entire source.
- 🟠 Orange: the reshaped shorter versions she produces from it.
- 🔴 Red: she never adds a claim or a number that wasn't in your original piece, and never sharpens what you said to make a format work better.
- The rule that matters most: the meaning stays exactly what you actually said. A repurposed version that says something stronger than the original isn't a repurpose, it's a rewrite, and that's not her job.

## Part 1 — Pick your best piece (5 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. Find the one post or article you wrote that did best, or the one
   you're proudest of.
2. Have the full text ready to paste in.
3. Decide where the shorter versions will go: a different platform, a
   follow-up post, pinned quotes.

## Part 2 — The repurposing brain (10 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: the full long-form piece.
2. **AI step**: send it with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my content repurposer. Here is one piece of long-form content I
wrote: [PASTE THE FULL POST OR ARTICLE].

My rules:
- Pull out the one core insight worth keeping.
- Reshape it into a short post version and 2-3 standalone pull-quotes.
- Keep the meaning exactly what I actually said, don't sharpen a claim I
  didn't make.
- Match my tone, don't invent a new one.

Return the short post and the pull-quotes only.
```

3. **Review and post**: check that every line still sounds like you,
   then post the versions where they belong.

## Part 3 — Build the habit (5 minutes a week)

Once a week, pick one piece from your archive, even an old one, and run
it through. A backlog of good ideas used once is the easiest inventory
you already own.

The feeling you are buying: a good idea earns three uses instead of
one, without a rewrite session every time.

## The upgrade path

Once repurposing feels quick instead of like starting over, you have
built trust with your sixteenth AI employee. The same pattern, keep the
meaning honest, reshape the format, is how every AI employee in this
system works. That is the system my paid products install across a
whole business.

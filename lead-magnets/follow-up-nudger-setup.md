# The Follow-Up Nudger Setup

**Keyword: NUDGE** | Pillar: Stop Doing That by Hand | Employee #023 (Samir) playbook

The complete setup for an AI employee that writes the next polite bump on
a conversation that went quiet, so it doesn't die of silence. He never
sends anything or invents a reason to reach out.

---

## What you are building

One workflow with four jobs:

1. You **tell him about the thread**: who it's with, what was last said,
   how long since. That's the whole input.
2. He **drafts one bump message**, escalating the angle each time (value
   add, question, then a graceful breakup).
3. He **never sends the same message twice**.
4. You **review it and send it yourself**. He never sends anything.

Time to build: about 10 minutes. Cost: one AI (Claude) + the conversation
that's gone quiet on you.

## The safety rules (read these first)

- 🟢 Green: the thread's history, as you describe it. That is the entire source.
- 🟠 Orange: the drafted bump message itself.
- 🔴 Red: he never sends anything, never negotiates, and never keeps bumping after a reply arrives.
- The rule that matters most: he never invents urgency or a reason to reach out that isn't true. A quiet thread gets an honest bump, not a manufactured excuse.

## Part 1 — Name the quiet one (2 minutes, no AI)

Do this even if you build nothing else:

1. Name the one warm conversation that's gone quiet on you.
2. Write down what was last said, and roughly how long ago.

## Part 2 — The bump brain (5 minutes)

With Claude (or your AI tool of choice) directly:

1. **Input**: who the thread is with, what was last said, how long since.
2. **AI step**: send it with the job description below. Copy it
   exactly, fill the blanks.

```
You are my follow-up nudger. Here is a conversation that's gone quiet:
[DESCRIBE WHO IT'S WITH, WHAT WAS LAST SAID, HOW LONG SINCE].

My rules:
- Draft one bump message, escalating the angle: value add first, then
  a question, then a graceful breakup if it's been a while.
- Never repeat a message you've already sent.
- Never invent urgency or a reason that isn't true.
- Never mark this as sent, that is always my step.

Return the bump message, and which angle it uses.
```

3. **Review and send**: read it once for tone, then send it yourself.

## Part 3 — Check weekly (5 minutes)

Once a week, look at what's still quiet. Most silence isn't a no, it's
just someone busy who needs one more honest nudge before you let it go.

The feeling you are buying: a good conversation stops dying of silence,
and starts getting the follow-up it needed from day one.

## The upgrade path

Once nudging feels routine instead of awkward, you have built trust with
your twentieth AI employee. The same pattern, describe honestly, draft
honestly, escalate plainly, is how every AI employee in this system
works. That is the system my paid products install across a whole
business.

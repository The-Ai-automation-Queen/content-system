# Current positioning

Version: `shift-lead-2026-09-12`. Sources: `sources.md`.

## Direction

Shift & Lead makes AI practical without the overwhelm. It helps people find
what they actually need from AI, create better work, and decide what should
remain human. Human judgement, creativity, experience, ownership and voice
are part of the value being protected.

The live website offers three starting points:
1. Understand AI and know where to begin.
2. Recognise and protect the experience and perspective that are uniquely yours.
3. Decide where AI belongs in a real business problem and choose a first test.

## Audience

Content Factory describes experienced professionals and founders with useful
knowledge and lived experience who feel overwhelmed by AI or struggle to turn
that knowledge into visible, valuable work. Its spoken one-liner focuses on
consultants and expert-led founders. These scopes can coexist; choose the
audience for the specific item rather than excluding every other project.

The desired outcome can be useful content, a guide, a product, a service,
clearer direction or an opportunity. Do not reduce every outcome to escaping
corporate life, passive income, hiring AI employees or selling a membership.

## Content

Instagram is the Content Factory's primary growth channel. Fatiha also wants
multiple projects and formats. Do not silently impose a universal cross-posting
cadence or one format on the portfolio. Use each project's current brief.

Start with an audience problem, a useful original insight and dated evidence.
Research demand before writing, check novelty, and use `voice.md`. Sources may
inform mechanisms and formats but do not supply copied hooks or invented
first-person stories.

Content Factory mechanics, agents, workflow engineering, approvals, webhooks,
provider operations and internal build problems remain private operating
subjects by default. They are valid internal research. They become public
topics only when Fatiha requests that subject for the exact content item.

The AI twin is one optional production and distribution route. It is not the
brand's entire purpose. Use `program.md` for the wider set of formats.

## Retired defaults

Do not revive the July corporate-escape audience, freedom-first promise,
membership-first ladder, six fixed ROI/watchdog pillars, 137-agent headline,
or Fast Forward launch as current strategy. Their history is archived.

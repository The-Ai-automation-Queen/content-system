# Current positioning

Version: `shift-lead-2026-10-31`. Sources: `sources.md`.

## Direction

Shift & Lead makes AI practical without the overwhelm. It helps people get
unstuck with AI one real task at a time, put it to work, and keep what only
they can bring. Human judgement, experience, ownership and voice are part of
the value being protected.

**Spoken one-liner** (Brand Foundation, approved 5 October 2026):
AI feels like it is moving without you. I help you catch up, one real task at
a time, and keep what only you can bring.

**Short brand statement:** Find what you actually need from AI.

**Flag line:** Turn what you know into something people can see, understand and use.

## Audience (approved 5 October 2026)

People who want to use AI but feel stuck. Two groups share the same feeling:
- people at work: keeping up, worried about their job, interviews, deciding to stay or go;
- people running something (a business, side project or service) who want AI
  to take on real work such as writing, admin, replies and research.

What unites them is the feeling, not the job title.

Stuck pattern: do not know where to start and a new tool appears every week;
tried AI and got something that did not sound like them; do not trust it
because it makes things up or agrees with everything; quietly worried about
being left behind or replaced; unsure what AI should do and what should stay theirs.

Paid offers for business owners (Business OS, Fast Forward, 1:1, team
workshops) speak to the "running something" group. Free content may serve both.

## Customer path

1. Get unstuck: free guides.
2. Put it to work: agent kits, one to one, group sessions (being priced / coming soon).
3. Keep what is yours: workbooks (coming soon).

See `offers.md` for readiness. Visibility is not readiness.

## Point of view

AI should remove mechanical work and help people see patterns, but it should
not flatten the judgement, evidence and perspective that make their work
worth choosing. Recurring angles: more automation is not automatically more
value; a polished avatar cannot replace an empty point of view; voice is a
record of choices, not a tone prompt; the system runs, the human stays
responsible for judgement and approval.

## White space to build in (True North, first run 31/10/2026)

Every brief picks one lane:
1. Goal before tools. Start with what you want to achieve, then choose.
2. People and change, with the human at the head of the loop (fear of being
   judged, "is my experience still important?", the messy middle).
3. The boring foundation: unspoken rules, SOPs and capture work that make AI useful.
4. Dubai and the Gulf: founders and corporate teams, in English and French.
5. Plain-English meaning of AI news: what changes this week, what is noise.

Saturated, do not compete: tool demos and tool lists, model wars, speed and
time claims, publishing volume as the strategy.

## What own data suggests (Instagram audit, 31/10/2026, public data only)

Working hypotheses, not proof (small sample, no saves or reach):
- Personal story or mission openings averaged 44.6 likes, about 2.5x the 18-like median.
- Event and on-stage posts averaged 28.8 likes and recur in the top 10.
- Collabs with communities reach furthest (best reel by plays is a collab).
- "6 X" listicle carousels (6.1 likes) and contrarian tip carousels (9.3 likes) underperform.
- Faceless explainer reels with no story up front are the lowest reels.

## Content

useful original insight and dated evidence. Spoken-first drafting and the

## The 12 words

**1. Agent**
A chatbot that can take *actions* — click buttons, send emails, read your calendar, run code.

**2. Skill**
A reusable instruction set you trigger by name — a prompt + tools + steps, packaged and ready to run.

**3. MCP (Model Context Protocol)**
A standard plug that lets AI tools connect to other apps. Like USB-C, but for AI. "Claude connected to Google Drive via MCP" means they speak the same plug language.

**4. RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**
A technique where the AI looks something up in *your* documents before answering. Cuts hallucinations. Used when you want answers from your own data, not the AI's general training.

**5. Embedding**
The math version of a word or sentence. Two sentences with similar meaning have similar embeddings. Powers search inside AI tools. Just recognise the word — you don't need to think about it.

**6. Fine-tune**
Train a general model on your specific data so it behaves a certain way. Expensive and slow — and often replaced by good prompting + RAG.

**7. Token**
A chunk of text, roughly 4 characters or ¾ of a word. AI charges by tokens. 1,000 tokens is about 750 words. Now you can read the pricing page.

**8. Context window**
How much text the AI can hold in its head at once. Claude's is around 1 million tokens. Once you go over, it starts forgetting the beginning of your conversation.

**9. Temperature**
A number from 0 to 1 controlling how "creative" the AI is. 0 = same answer every time. 1 = different answer every time. Default is usually around 0.7. You'll rarely change it.

**10. Hallucination**
When AI confidently invents wrong information. The single biggest reason to verify AI answers on legal, medical, or financial topics.

**11. Open weights / open source**
The model file is publicly downloadable — you can run it on your own hardware. Mistral, Llama, DeepSeek are open. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini are not. Matters for privacy, cost, and customisation.

**12. Multimodal**
AI that handles text + images + audio + video together. Most major models are multimodal. Paste a screenshot; get a paragraph back.

## How it sounds in practice

A colleague says: *"We're RAG-ing our customer service docs into a Claude agent with an MCP connection to Zendesk — fine-tune might come later if quality slips."*

Translation:
- Their docs are being searched in real time so the AI quotes from them (RAG)
- They're using Claude as the AI engine
- It takes actions inside Zendesk (agent + MCP)
- If answers get bad, they'll retrain Claude on their data (fine-tune)

You now follow that sentence. You can ask: *"What happens when it can't find the answer in your docs?"* — and sound like you've been in 30 of these conversations.

## §2 The 5 prompts (ready to use — copy, paste, adapt)

> Works in ChatGPT, Claude, or Copilot. House rules: company mail stays in
> company-approved tools; on a personal account, strip names and numbers
> first. AI drafts — **you** decide and you send.

### Prompt 1 — Untangle any thread
```
Here's an email thread. Give me:
1) a 3-sentence summary of where things stand,
2) what each person wants,
3) any decision I'm being asked to make,
4) what happens if I do nothing for 48 hours.
Under 120 words total.

[paste thread]
```

### Prompt 2 — Draft the reply you've been avoiding
```
Draft a reply to the email below.
My decision, in plain words: [say what you actually want, bluntly —
e.g. "yes but not before Friday" / "no, and stop cc'ing my boss"].
Tone: warm, professional, direct. Sounds like a busy human, not a
template. No "I hope this finds you well." Max 120 words.
Then give me a 1-sentence version in case shorter is better.

[paste email]
```

### Prompt 3 — Turn emails into YOUR to-do list
```
From the emails below, extract every task that's mine.
For each: the task in one line, who's waiting on it, the real
deadline (or "none stated"), and an effort guess from 1–5.
Then re-order: hard deadlines first, then 5-minute quick wins.
Output as a checklist.

[paste emails]
```

### Prompt 4 — Triage: what actually needs me today
```
Act as my inbox triage assistant. Sort the emails below into:
🔴 Respond today (real deadline, or a person genuinely blocked)
🟡 Respond this week
🟢 Read later / FYI
⚪ Archive or unsubscribe
One line of reasoning per email. And challenge me: if something
only FEELS urgent because of who sent it, say so.

[paste emails or subject lines]
```

### Prompt 5 — Build your personal Inbox Rules (the reusable one)
```
Help me write my personal "Inbox Rules" — a reusable block I'll
paste at the start of any email session so you triage and draft
the way I would. Interview me ONE question at a time:
1) my role, 2) my top 3 priorities this quarter, 3) the people I
never keep waiting, 4) what I'm allowed to ignore, 5) how my
replies should sound.
Then output my Inbox Rules as a single copy-paste block.
```

## §3 The calm card — Pause → Prioritize → Proceed

**Pause.** One slow breath before you open the inbox. You're choosing to
respond, not react. (An unread count is not an emergency. It never was.)

**Prioritize.** One question: *what actually needs me today?* Let your AI
triage answer it — then trust the answer.

**Proceed.** Work the 🔴 list only. Everything else has a place, and a place
is not your attention.

*Busy is a schedule state. Activated is a body state. You're allowed to be
the first without the second.*

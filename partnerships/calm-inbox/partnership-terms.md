# Partnership Terms — Calm Inbox (Fatiha × Tessa)

> **One page, on purpose.** Draft for both partners to review, adjust, and
> sign before the first co-branded post goes live. This is a working
> agreement between collaborators, not legal advice — if the corporate lane
> takes off, have a lawyer turn it into a real contract.
> Status: **DRAFT — unsigned.**

**Parties:** Fatiha Chikh ("the AI Automation Queen") and Tessa [surname]
(transformation coach).
**Scope:** the "Hack Your Inbox. Calm Your Mind." workshop, the 4-Week AI
Integration Pod, the "AI Meets the Human Mind" podcast, and the joint promo
campaign (together: "Calm Inbox").

## 1. The email list
- Every lead captured under Calm Inbox (CALM keyword, workshop registrants,
  pod members, podcast opt-ins) is **co-owned**.
- Both partners can **export the full list at any time, no permission
  needed**. Both may email it for Calm Inbox matters; solo offers go to it
  max **once per month per partner**, clearly from that partner.
- If the partnership ends, **both keep a full copy**. Leads must be told at
  opt-in that their email is shared by both partners (one line in the GHL
  form covers this).

## 2. Backend sales
- Each partner keeps **100% of their own ladder**: Fatiha's products,
  community, and corporate bookings; Tessa's coaching and programs. No
  cross-commission, no attribution accounting. Simple beats fair-sounding.
- Exception: a **corporate booking of the co-branded workshop itself**
  (both delivering) splits like any Calm Inbox revenue (§4).

## 3. IP and the brand
- The **co-created frameworks** (Calm Inbox Blueprint, Human + AI Operating
  System, the joint curriculum) are co-owned. **Either partner may reuse
  them solo**, in their own offers, without permission — just without the
  other's name on it.
- Pre-existing IP stays with its owner: Fatiha's prompts, systems, and
  Business OS; Tessa's Pause → Prioritize → Proceed and coaching methods.
  Solo reuse of the *other partner's* pre-existing IP requires a yes.
- The name **"Hack Your Inbox. Calm Your Mind."** is joint: neither runs it
  with a third party while this agreement is live; after exit, both are free
  to use it.
- **Recordings:** cohort 1 and the workshop are recorded. Both may use
  recordings for promo. For paid products, each may productize **their own
  segments** freely; using the other's segments in a paid product needs a yes.

## 4. Money
- Calm Inbox revenue (tickets, pod fees, co-delivered corporate bookings)
  splits **50/50 after direct costs** (venue, printing, payment fees, ads).
- Direct costs over $100 need both partners' OK before they're spent.

## 5. Approvals and speed
- Anything with **both names** on it needs both approvals — with a
  **48-hour rule**: no response in 48h counts as approval. The machine
  doesn't wait on a busy week.
- Solo posts mentioning the partnership positively need no approval.

## 6. Exit
- Either partner can exit with **30 days' written notice, no fault, no
  reason needed**. Scheduled deliveries inside those 30 days still happen
  (nobody strands a live cohort).
- On exit: list copies to both (§1), co-created frameworks stay reusable by
  both (§3), pipeline corporate deals in active negotiation are honored on
  the §4 split if both still deliver, otherwise they belong to whoever
  closes them.
- This partnership is **non-exclusive**: both partners run their own
  businesses, brands, and other collaborations freely.

## 7. The review gate
- After workshop #1 + pod cohort #1, both partners review against the
  kill/scale metrics in `README.md` §7 and explicitly decide: **scale,
  adjust, or end.** No automatic cohort #2.

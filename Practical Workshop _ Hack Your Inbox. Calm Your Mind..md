## **Podcast: AI Meets the Human Mind**

Rather than another conversation about prompts and productivity, I'd love this podcast to explore the human side of working with AI. AI can save hours, itcan also waste them.

AI can create clarity, but it can just as easily create overthinking, endless prompting and information overload. In some situations, traditional ways of workingare actually faster. The interesting question isn't whether AI is good or bad. It's when, why and for whom.

Topics we could explore include:

* When does AI genuinely save time, and when does it become another distraction?  
* Are we becoming more productive, or simply producing more?  
* What happens to our brain when every decision has an AI option?  
* Why do some people become dependent on AI while others use it as a strategic thinking partner?  
* How do you know when to trust AI and when to trust your own judgement?  
* Can AI increase perfectionism by making endless iterations possible?  
* How do we prevent AI from replacing critical thinking?  
* What habits help people integrate AI without becoming cognitively overloaded?  
* How do you regulate your nervous system while working with AI all day?  
* What role do reflection, boundaries and recovery play in an AI-enabled workplace?  
* What opportunities will AI create for professionals who intentionally develop both technical capability and human capability?

The conversation isn't AI versus humans.

It's about helping people become better humans while working with increasingly capable AI.

The goal is to leave listeners with a balanced perspective: understanding both the opportunitiesand the risks, while giving them practical ways to train themselves to work with AI intentionally, confidently and without losing their own judgement.

**4-Week AI Integration Pod**

**From Knowing AI to Working Better with AI**

Following the masterclass, participants can join a four-week mentorship pod (maximum 10 participants) USD 150 per person

The objective is helping professionals build the judgement, confidence and habits needed to integrate AI into their day-to-day work without becoming dependenton it. Each session combines practical AI application, reflection and coaching, because successful AI adoption isn't just about technology. It's about changing the way we work and think.

### **Week 1 | Where AI Adds Value**

Before using AI, participants map how they currently work.

Together we'll identify:

* Where AI genuinely saves time.  
* Where traditional methods are actually more effective.  
* Which repetitive tasks should be automated.  
* Which decisions should remain human.

**Outcome:** A personal AI opportunity map instead of using AI for everything.

### **Week 2 | Working With AI Without Losing Yourself**

As AI removes effort, new challenges appear.

We'll explore:

* Decision fatigue  
* Prompt perfectionism  
* Information overload  
* Over-reliance on AI  
* Knowing when "good enough" is actually good enough

We'll introduce practical ways to regulate attention, create thinking space and prevent AI from becoming another source of overwhelm.

**Outcome:** AI becomes a thinking partner, not another reason to feel mentally exhausted.

### **Week 3 | Building Confidence Through Practice**

Participants bring real work challenges from their own week.

Together we experiment:

* Would AI help here?  
* Is it actually faster?  
* What should remain human?  
* What can be standardised?

The coaching focuses on developing judgement rather than simply finding better prompts.

Participants learn to trust both AI and themselves.

**Outcome:** Confidence through experience, not theory.

### **Week 4 | Designing Your Human \+ AI Operating System**

The final session is about sustainability.

Participants create their own principles for working with AI, including:

* When to use AI.  
* When not to use AI.  
* How to protect focus.  
* How to maintain critical thinking.  
* How to continue learning as AI evolves.

Everyone leaves with a personalised Human \+ AI Operating System they can immediately apply at work.

### **Outcomes: The Transformation**

By the end of four weeks, participants won't just know more about AI.

They'll have developed a healthier, more intentional way of working with it.

They'll know how to use AI to create:

* More clarity instead of more noise.  
* Better use of time instead of simply working faster.  
* Greater confidence instead of second-guessing every decision.

Because the future doesn't belong to the people who use AI the most.

It belongs to the people who know **when to use it, when not to, and how to stay human while doing both.**

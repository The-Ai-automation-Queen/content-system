## Context: what defines this lane right now
The space splits into three camps: (1) **tool reviewers** ("10 best AI tools…"),
(2) **build-in-public automators** (show the workflow running), and (3)
**faceless/AI-generated** accounts pumping volume. Camp 3 is exploding, which makes
a real face + real experience (Real Talk) a genuine differentiator. Almost nobody
is owning the **"fix the workflow before you automate"** anti-hype position — it's
open.

Rule 01 · Receipts or it didn’t happen

A job counts only if AI did useful work in the last 7 days and I can show the output. No receipt, no badge.

Rule 02 · They prepare, I release

No AI workflow sends anything to a human on its own. Drafts land in my review queue. The send button stays human.

Rule 03 · The counter can go down

A workflow that stops working loses its badge until it earns it back. Small and true beats big and hollow.

How a job earns its place here

  1. A job gets a clear brief: one founder problem it exists to kill.
  2. AI runs a real trial on my business, not a demo.
  3. The output becomes a public receipt. Real screenshots, real numbers.
  4. Only then does the badge appear on this page. Kept, assisted, automated or failed: the result is recorded.

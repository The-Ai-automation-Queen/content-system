Where AI Fits

Before you invest in AI, decide where it belongs.

Turn a promising AI idea into a clear business decision. Identify the value, protect what should remain human and choose the first test worth running.

Choose how to build your mapSee what is inside

The decision before the tool

A good AI idea is not automatically a good business decision.

AI can make almost any idea look possible. The harder questions are whether it solves a problem that matters and whether the business can support it. You also need to know if it strengthens or erases the value people already trust.

Where AI Fits puts those decisions in the right order.

Inside the map

Seven decisions. One direction.

Work from the business need to the first credible test, without starting with a platform or forcing AI into the answer.

01Business priority

Name the decision, friction or opportunity that matters now.

02Customer value

Define whose experience improves and why they would care.

03Human advantage

Protect the expertise, trust and judgment that make the work distinct.

04Role for AI

Decide what AI should support, produce, recommend or leave alone.

05Risk and readiness

Check the information, ownership, standards and people the idea requires.

06First test

Choose the smallest useful test and the evidence that will make the next decision easier.

07Route to market

Connect the opportunity to a clear message, audience and next move.

What you leave with

Clarity before commitment.

  • A defined business opportunity
  • A clear and limited role for AI
  • The human value that must be protected
  • A view of risks and operating requirements
  • A first test with a reason to run it
  • A direction for positioning and market entry

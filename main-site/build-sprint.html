Questions people actually ask

Pricing is confirmed on the fit call once we both know what the task involves. If it turns out to be smaller than you thought, it costs less.

Do I need to be technical?

No. That's the whole point. I spent twenty years translating this stuff for people who had no interest in the plumbing, and that's what I do here.

What if I don't know which task to pick?

Then that's what the fit call is for. Most people arrive with the wrong task and leave with a better one. If you want a head start, take the free Time Leak Quiz.

Which tools will we use?

Whatever your business already runs on. I'm not tied to a platform and I won't move you onto one to suit me. If your business is on Google, we build on Google.

What happens to my data?

We apply the traffic light rule before we connect anything: green goes straight in, amber gets redacted first, red doesn't go near it. It's written into the job description so it holds after I leave.

Can you just do it for me?

No, and that's deliberate. Builds handed over without the owner in the room get abandoned within a month. The week we spend together is what makes it last.

Not ready for this?

Everything I know is free and open. Start with the guide library, or the first AI employee guide, which is this method written out so you can do it yourself. No cost, no catch. Come back when you want a hand.

How we build it.

Step one

Name the outcome

We start with what you want the business to be able to do, not with a tool.

Step two

Design the job and the boundaries

We define the information it can use, what good looks like, what it must never do, and where a person stays in control.

Step three

Build the first working version

We connect the pieces, test it on real work, document it, and make sure you understand what you own.

Who this is for.

This is for you if

  • You can describe something useful you want the business to be able to do.
  • You want a working first version, not a strategy deck.
  • You want to understand the decisions behind the build and own the result.
  • You care about human judgment, privacy and quality controls.

Not for you if

  • You want a generic AI strategy with no build attached.
  • You want everything automated before the work itself is clear.
  • You want a black box nobody in the business understands.
  • You need an outsourced technology department.

Tell me what you want to build.

Describe the outcome, what is getting in the way today, and where you think AI might help. I read these myself and will tell you whether I think this is the right next step.

No payment details. Applying is not booking.

Questions people actually ask

Do I need to be technical?

No. You need to understand the work and the outcome you want. I translate the technical decisions and show you what matters.

Do we have to replace the tools I already use?

Usually not. We start with what your business already uses, then add something only when it earns its place.

What happens to my data?

We decide what data can be connected before anything is built. Sensitive information, permissions and human review are part of the design.

How big does the project need to be?

It can start with one clearly defined capability. The goal is a useful first version you can rely on.

About

I turn new technology into something people can actually build with.

I’m Fatiha Chikh. For more than 20 years, my work has been translating new technology into something useful in the real world. Today I do that with AI: I build with it, test it in real business conditions, and teach the parts worth copying.

The story

The technology changes. The job stays the same.

My work has always been about taking something new and making it useful for people who have real work to do.

That took me from technology into co-founding a circular fashion business in France, where we became an exclusive Nike vendor and grew the business to seven figures.

Now I apply the same approach to AI. I care about what it makes possible, what is worth building, what needs a person, and what should be left alone.

Learn what matters. Skip what doesn’t. Build something real.

About Fatiha Chikh

Your business does not need more AI. It needs better decisions about AI.

I help leaders decide where AI can create real value and what is worth building. Then we find the right way to bring it to market without losing what makes the business distinct.

Fatiha Chikh
AI Business Architect and AI Marketing Engineer

The experience behind the work

I have spent my career making ambitious ideas work in the real world.

For more than 20 years, I worked across marketing, operations, training and transformation at Dell, Intel and Microsoft. My role was often the same: understand the big idea, make it clear to the people involved and build the path from strategy to action.

I later became an entrepreneur and investor in LeLabPlus, the circular fashion company I co-founded in France. We built the business to seven figures and delivered work for Nike. It taught me what changes when the strategy, the capital and the outcome are your own.

What I bring to AI

I look beyond the tool.

I look at the customer, the commercial opportunity, the operation and the people who will make the idea work. Then I help leaders decide where AI belongs and where it does not. The right opportunity becomes something useful, distinctive and ready for the market.

“The goal is not to add AI everywhere. It is to use it where it makes the business stronger.”

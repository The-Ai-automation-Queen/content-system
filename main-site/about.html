About / Fatiha Chikh

Two decades inside global tech. Four businesses built alongside it.

At Dell, Intel and Microsoft, I learned how new technology reaches a market. In my own businesses, I had to decide what to fund, change and close.

I closed two early ventures. With my sisters, I grew LeLabPlus to seven figures, and our Nike Re-Creation work won a People’s Choice Award. Today I run Shift & Lead, helping leaders make sharper decisions about where AI belongs in their business.

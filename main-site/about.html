I learned how technology works inside big companies. Then I built a business of my own.

Now I help businesses use AI on real work: choose what is worth doing, make a first version and see if it holds up.

The experience behind the work

Big-company experience. A founder's responsibility.

The work I do now draws on both. Here is where that perspective comes from.

01 / Global technology

Dell · Intel · Microsoft

More than 20 years across marketing, operations, training and transformation. I learned to turn a large idea into work that teams could understand and carry out.

02 / Building a business

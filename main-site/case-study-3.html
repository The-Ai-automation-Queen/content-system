Case Study · Tire Shop

No missed call turned into a lost sale.

The problem

The tire shop was getting a high volume of calls every day, and staff could not keep up. Many calls went unanswered, leaving potential customers without pricing, availability, or service information, costing sales, and slowly eroding trust with people who’d otherwise have come back.

What I built

An automated callback system that captures the details of every missed call. Within minutes, it reaches out to each potential customer, confirms what they need, and offers tailored support, from booking an appointment to answering a service question, so no call just disappears.

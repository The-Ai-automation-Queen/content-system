Before you ask
Questions people actually have.
I'm not technical. Can I still do this?

Yes. You need to understand the work you want done, not learn to code. I teach the business logic first and the tools second.

Do I have to replace the tools I already use?

Usually no. Start with the tools and information your business already uses, then add something new only when it earns its place.

What if the free guides aren't enough?

Use the guides to get oriented. If you want to bring this into a team, the workshops are the next step.

How fast will I see results?

A first useful build can be tested in days. The time depends on the work, the information it needs, and what should stay human. The goal is something you can rely on, not a rushed demo.

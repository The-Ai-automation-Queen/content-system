Case Study · E-Commerce

The support bot that answers before customers get frustrated.

The challenge

A growing e-commerce business was struggling to keep up with customer questions about order status, product availability, and return policies. With limited support staff, response times lagged, and customers grew frustrated waiting for updates on orders or new products.

What I built

An automated support bot that instantly answers frequently asked questions, updates customers on their order status, and routes anything complex to a live person. It runs across the website and the store’s messaging platforms, so customers get an answer the moment they ask, not a day later.

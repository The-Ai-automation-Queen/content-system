Case Study · Day Care Center

Every lead got a reply in two minutes, and booked a tour.

The problem

Facing a staff shortage, the daycare coordinator found it increasingly hard to manage the surge of inquiries and leads coming in from Facebook ads. Responding quickly became impossible, and some leads went untouched entirely, wasted ad spend, and families left without the experience they deserved.

What I built

An AI agent that reaches out to every new lead automatically, greeting them, qualifying them against the coordinator’s own criteria, and booking a facility tour on her behalf. It engages within two minutes of a lead form submission, following the exact qualification guidelines she set, so nothing slips through and nobody waits.

Score it

Part 2: the 3-question test.

  1. Is it boring and repetitive?You do roughly the same thing each time. If no, this needs human creativity, skip it.
  2. Does it require zero judgment calls?The "right answer" is predictable. If no, AI can assist but shouldn't own it yet, mark as "AI-assisted."
  3. Would a new hire need under 30 minutes of training?If yes, this is your AI employee candidate. If no, break it into smaller sub-tasks and re-test each one.

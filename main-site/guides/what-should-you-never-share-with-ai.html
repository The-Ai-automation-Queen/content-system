Introduction

Any AI tool · 5 min read · Beginner

Some things should never go in, some need permission first, and some are fine. Run the 3-question paste test before you share anything.

A guide by Fatiha Chikh | The AI Automation Queen

What you leave with: the paste test, a keep-out / ask-first / fine list, and a prompt that checks a task without you sharing the real data.

01

How to use this guideIn 30 seconds.

Read the 3 questions, sort what you were about to paste, then use the test prompt with categories only, never the real information.

02

The paste testBefore anything goes in.

  1. Do I need it? Could the task work without the real details?
  2. Am I allowed? Do I have permission to share this?
  3. Is this the right tool? Is this account approved for work?

If any answer is "no" or "not sure", replace the real details with an invented example.

03

Keep out · Ask first · FineSort it first.

Keep out

Passwords, bank details, health information, anything under a confidentiality agreement.

Ask first

Customer emails, colleagues' names, internal documents, contracts.

Usually fine

Public website text, your own rough notes without names, invented examples.

04

The honest partCheck before you use it.

Turning off "training" in a tool's settings gives you more control, but it doesn't give you permission to share confidential work. Permission comes from your company, not from a setting.

The safe test prompt (describe categories, not real data)

I want to use AI for this task: [describe the task without names, files or private details].

It may involve these types of information: [categories only, for example customer emails, employee names, public web copy].

Don't ask me to paste the real information. For each category tell me:
1. Keep it out, ask permission, or fine to use?
2. Why?
3. What safer example could I use instead?
4. Which account or privacy setting should I check?
If you can't know whether I have permission, tell me who to ask.

Where the privacy settings are

Menu names change. If yours looks different, open the official page.

ChatGPT
Profile → Settings → Data Controls, then switch off Improve the model for everyone. Temporary Chat is another option for a one-off conversation. Official page ↗
Claude

Gemini
Settings & help → Activity, then under Keep Activity choose Turn off. Work or school accounts may be managed by an administrator. Official page ↗
Copilot
Personal account: Profile → your name → Privacy, then switch off the training options. Microsoft 365 Copilot at work follows your organisation's settings. Official page ↗

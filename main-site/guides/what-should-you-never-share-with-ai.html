Before you paste it into AI, check this

Some details should stay out. Others need permission. Sort what you have, then check the tool you use.

1. Sort the information

What are you about to share?

If you are unsure, remove the real details and use an invented example.

Need it?Can you do the task without the real details?
Allowed?Do you have permission to share them?
Right tool?Is this account approved for the task?
2. Check the tool

Where is the privacy setting?

Pick the tool you use. Turning off training gives you more control, but it does not give you permission to share confidential work.

3. Test without sharing it

Check one task before you paste anything

Open a work-approved AI chat. Replace the brackets in the prompt with types of information only, such as “customer emails”. Do not paste real names or files.

Complete prompt
I want to use AI for this task:

[Describe the task without including names, files or private details.]

The task may involve these types of information:

[List categories only, such as customer emails, employee names or public website copy.]

Do not ask me to paste the real information.

For each category, tell me:

1. Whether I should keep it out, ask for permission or use it.
2. Why.
3. What safer example I could use instead.
4. Which account or privacy setting I should check.

If you cannot know whether I have permission, tell me who I need to ask.

The answer is useful only if it keeps real information out, separates permission from privacy settings and gives you a safer way to test the task.

Still unsure? Leave the information out until you have permission and an approved tool.

Know what your AI tool can see

Should you let AI connect to your email, files and calendar?

A connection can save time, but it also gives the AI access to information you did not paste into the chat. Connect only what you need and know how to remove it.

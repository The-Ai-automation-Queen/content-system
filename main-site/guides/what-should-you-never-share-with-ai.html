What should you never share with AI?

Know what must stay out, what needs permission and which privacy setting to change before you paste anything.

Do not put information into an AI tool if the wrong person seeing it could give them access, identify someone, cost you money, harm someone or break a promise.

When you are unsure, remove the real information and test the task with a public or invented example.

Sort it before you paste it

Most information fits into one of these three groups.

Never paste

Keep out passwords, one-time codes, API keys, private links, payment details, identity documents, medical information and confidential legal information.

Ask first

Pause before using customer or employee information, private emails, meeting notes, internal reports, contracts, financial information or work owned by someone else.

Safer to test

Use public information, your own non-confidential writing, an invented example, a blank template or information with identifying details removed.

Find your tool. Change the setting.

Turning off training gives you more control. It does not make confidential information safe to share. Open only the tool you use.

ChatGPT

Go to Profile → Settings → Data Controls and switch off Improve the model for everyone. You can also use Temporary Chat for a conversation you do not want saved in your history or used for training.

Official instructions ↗
Claude

Go to Your name → Settings → Privacy and switch off Help Improve our AI models. This applies to personal Claude Free, Pro and Max accounts.

Official instructions ↗
Gemini

On the website, go to Menu → Settings & help → Activity → Turn off. On mobile, go to Menu → Profile → Gemini Apps Activity → Turn off. You can also start a Temporary Chat. Google may still keep temporary conversations for up to 72 hours to operate and protect the service.

Official instructions ↗
Microsoft Copilot

With a personal account, go to Profile → Profile name → Privacy and switch off Training on conversation activity and Training on voice conversations. Microsoft 365 Copilot work prompts and responses are not used to train the underlying models. Still use only the account and information your employer has approved.

Personal Copilot instructions ↗Microsoft 365 information ↗
Grok

On the website, go to Settings → Data → Improve the Model → Off. On mobile, go to Settings → Data Controls → Improve the model → Off. You can also use Private Chat.

Official instructions ↗
Meta AI

Use Incognito Chat when it appears in WhatsApp or the Meta AI app. Meta does not provide one universal training switch for every regular Meta AI experience. If Incognito Chat is unavailable, keep private information out.

About Incognito Chat ↗
DeepSeek

Open Settings and switch off Improve the model for everyone. If you cannot find the setting, request the opt-out through privacy@deepseek.com. Keep confidential work out even after changing the setting.

The setting is not permission

A privacy setting does not give you permission to share someone else’s information.

If the information belongs to a customer, employee, client or employer, check that you are allowed to use it before you paste it.

First check permission. Then check the tool, account and privacy setting.

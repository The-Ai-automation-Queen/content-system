How to Write Sales Emails With ChatGPT That Don't Sound Like AI

Write to one real client, with proof instead of promises.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh | The AI Automation Queen Free guide
Shift & Lead

Why don't people buy from my emails?

Most sales emails talk to everyone, hide what they're about and make big promises. Write to one real customer, say what's inside in the subject line, and show proof instead of promises. AI helps with drafts; you bring the customer and the proof.

What you leave with

3 rules, a prompt for each, and a 4-point check to run before you press send.

How to use this guide: Pick one product and one email you're about to send. Apply the three rules to that email, in order.

01

Rule 1 Write to one person.

The rule:
picture one real customer and write to them.
Why it works:
an email written to everyone sounds like an advert. One written to one person sounds like a note, and gets read.
To everyone

Dear valued customers, we're excited to announce our new range of candles.

→
To one person

"You told me the lavender candle was the only thing that helped you switch off after work. There's now a bigger one."

02

Rule 2 Say what's inside.

The rule:
the subject line tells the reader exactly what they'll get by opening.
Why it works:
clever subject lines get skipped. Clear ones get opened by the people who actually want the thing.
Clever

You won't believe this...

→
Clear

"The big lavender candle is back (40 hours)" · "New: the lavender candle, twice the size"

The subject line promptCopy the prompt ↓Give me 5 subject lines for this email. Each one must say exactly what's inside. No teasers, no "you won't believe", no false urgency. Under 50 characters each. Email: [paste]
03

Rule 3 Proof, not promises.

The rule:
use a real customer's words, a real detail or a real result instead of adjectives.
Why it works:
"the best candle ever" is a claim. "I light it every night at 9" from a real customer is proof.
A promise

Our candles are amazing and long-lasting.

→
Proof

One short real review, with permission · "burns for about 40 hours" (only if you've tested it) · a photo of it on a real customer's shelf.

04

The honest part Check before you use it.

AI can write a smooth email, but it can't invent your proof. Never let it make up reviews, results or numbers. If you don't have proof yet, ask your last five customers for a sentence before you send.

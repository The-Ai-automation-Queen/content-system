What Can I Use AI For in My Business?

A list of the jobs AI can do for you, and the 3 to try first.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh | The AI Automation Queen Free guide
Shift & Lead

Everyone talks about AI. I still don't get what it would do for me.

You'll understand AI faster by using it than by reading about it. Give it three jobs you already do (a reply, a summary, a sort) and judge the results against your own work.

What you leave with

3 prompts for jobs you do every week, a worked example for each, and the one habit that stops AI embarrassing you.

How to use this guide: Open any free AI chat. Do job 1 with the example, then with a real email of your own (remove names first). Jobs 2 and 3 take five minutes each.

01

Job 1 The reply you keep putting off.

The job:
an email that needs a careful answer, and you don't know where to start.
Why it works:
AI is good at structure and tone. You bring the facts and the decision.
The email you received

Hi, quick one, can you confirm whether the workshop on the 14th is still on, whether we can bring two extra people, and if the invoice goes to me or to finance? Thanks, Jo

→
What a good answer looks like

Three short answers in Jo's order, your facts only, a friendly close, under 90 words. No promises you didn't make.

The reply promptCopy the prompt ↓Help me reply to this email. Here's what I know: [your facts, as bullet points] Answer each question in the order they asked. Keep it friendly and under 90 words. Use only my fact

Job 2 The 3 points that matter.

The job:
a long article or report you should have read before a meeting.
Why it works:
AI can find the key points in seconds, and it can show you the exact sentence each one came from, so you can check it.
What you paste

Any public article or report of up to 2 pages.

→
What a good answer looks like

3 points, each with the sentence it came from, and one line on what the article doesn't say.

03

Job 3 Sort the pile.

The job:
a list of feedback, survey answers or ideas you need to make sense of.
Why it works:
grouping is slow for people and fast for AI. You decide if the groups are right.
What you paste

Too long · loved the examples · audio kept cutting · more time for questions · slides arrived late · great host · wanted the recording · room was cold

→
What a good answer looks like

Content (2) · Tech (2) · Format (2) · Venue and admin (2), with every comment in exactly one group.

04

The honest part Check before you use it.

AI sounds just as confident when it's wrong. The habit that protects you: before you use any answer, put it next to what you gave it and check the names, numbers and anything that sounds decided.

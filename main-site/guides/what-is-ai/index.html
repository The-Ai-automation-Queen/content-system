Introduction

Any AI · 15 min read · Beginner

You'll understand AI faster by using it than by reading about it. Give it three jobs you already do (a reply, a summary, a sort) and judge the results against your own work.

A guide by Fatiha Chikh | The AI Automation Queen

What you leave with: 3 prompts for jobs you do every week, a worked example for each, and the one habit that stops AI embarrassing you.

01

How to use this guideIn 30 seconds.

Open any free AI chat. Do job 1 with the example, then with a real email of your own (remove names first). Jobs 2 and 3 take five minutes each.

02

Job 1The reply you keep putting off.

The job:
an email that needs a careful answer, and you don't know where to start.
Why it works:
AI is good at structure and tone. You bring the facts and the decision.
The email you received

Hi, quick one, can you confirm whether the workshop on the 14th is still on, whether we can bring two extra people, and if the invoice goes to me or to finance? Thanks, Jo

→
What a good answer looks like

Three short answers in Jo's order, your facts only, a friendly close, under 90 words. No promises you didn't make.

Job 3Sort the pile.

The job:
a list of feedback, survey answers or ideas you need to make sense of.
Why it works:

05

The honest partCheck before you use it.

AI sounds just as confident when it's wrong. The habit that protects you: before you use any answer, put it next to what you gave it and check the names, numbers and anything that sounds decided.

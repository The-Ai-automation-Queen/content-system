Introduction

ChatGPT · 20 min read · Intermediate

Don't ask AI for "insights". Ask it to group the comments, show which customers said what, and flag where they disagree. Then decide yourself what to fix.

A guide by Fatiha Chikh | The AI Automation Queen

What you leave with: a prompt that cleans private details out of your notes, a theme table where every claim has a quote, and a prompt that turns the table into your next 3 actions.

The honest partCheck before you use it.

Eight or even fifty comments are not "all your customers". And two people using the same word don't always mean the same problem. Read the quotes before you act.

Job 1: The clean-up prompt

Remove all personal details from these notes. Replace each person with an ID (C01, C02...) and keep the same ID every time they appear. Remove emails, phone numbers and company names. Don't change anything else.

Notes: [paste]

Job 2: The theme table prompt

Group these customer comments into themes.

For each theme: a short name · the customer IDs · 2 quotes from different customers · anyone who says the opposite.

A theme needs at least 2 customers. Put single comments under "Early signals". Keep complaints, praise and feature requests apart.

Comments: [paste]

Job 3: The decision prompt

From this theme table, suggest:
1. The 3 things to act on first, and why (based on how many customers and how serious it is).
2. The 3 questions we should ask customers next.

Only use what's in the table. Don't claim this represents all our customers.

Table: [paste]

Before you act: open two quotes per theme in your original notes. If you can't find them, drop the theme.

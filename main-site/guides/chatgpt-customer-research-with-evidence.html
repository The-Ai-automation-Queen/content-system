How to Use ChatGPT to Understand What My Clients Want

Turn reviews, messages and survey answers into clear decisions.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh | The AI Automation Queen Free guide
Shift & Lead

I have pages of customer feedback and no time to read it all.

Don't ask AI for "insights". Ask it to group the comments, show which customers said what, and flag where they disagree. Then decide yourself what to fix.

What you leave with

a prompt that cleans private details out of your notes, a theme table where every claim has a quote, and a prompt that turns the table into your next 3 actions.

How to use this guide: Try it first with the 8 example comments below. Then run it on your own feedback, cleaned first.

01

Job 1 Clean the notes.

The job:
removing names, emails and company names from feedback.
Why it matters:
you can't un-share personal data, and you don't need it to find patterns.
Before

Sarah Klein (sarah@studio-k.com) said the onboarding call was too rushed.

→
After

"C03: the onboarding call was too rushed."

Job 2 Find the patterns, with proof.

The job:
grouping comments into themes you can defend.
Why it matters:
a theme without quotes is an opinion. With quotes and customer IDs, it's evidence.
The comments

What a good answer looks like

Job 3 From themes to decisions.

The job:
choosing what to act on.
Why it matters:
a table doesn't decide for you. This step turns it into 3 actions and 3 questions.
What a good answer looks like

1. Fix setup first (3 customers) · 2. Make the invoice easy to find (2) · 3. Keep promoting templates (2) · Ask next: "Where exactly did setup slow you down?"

The honest part Check before you use it.

Eight or even fifty comments are not "all your customers". And two people using the same word don't always mean the same problem. Read the quotes before you act.

How to Test My Business Idea With ChatGPT Before I Build It

Find the problems early and check if people will pay.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh | The AI Automation Queen Free guide
Shift & Lead

Is my business idea any good?

Asking AI "is this a good idea?" gets you encouragement. Ask five critics with different jobs to find the problems, then let real people answer the only question that counts: will they pay?

What you leave with

a one-paragraph idea brief, a 5-critic prompt, and a one-week demand test with the pass mark set in advance.

How to use this guide: Write your idea in one paragraph before you open any AI. If you can't, that's the first thing to fix.

01

Job 1 The one-paragraph brief.

The job:
putting the idea into a form a critic can judge.
Why it works:
a vague idea gets vague feedback. Four clear lines give the critics something to push against.
Vague

Something to help older people with technology.

→
Clear

"A one-hour home visit that sets up a new smartphone for someone over 70: contacts, photos, video calls. For their grown-up children who live too far away to help. Because the phone was a gift and it's still in the box. A fixed price per visit."

02

Job 2 Five critics.

The job:
finding the weak spots while changing the idea is still free.
Why it works:
each critic has one narrow job, so nobody can hide behind "overall it's promising".
The 5 critics

The Stranger (would someone who's never met you care?) · The Rival (what do people already use instead?) · The Accountant (does the price cover your time?) · Month-6 You (what will be hard once the excitement goes?) · The Sceptical Friend (the one question you're avoiding).

→
What a good answer looks like

2 sharp points from each critic, then the 3 problems that matter most, ranked.

03

Job 3 A one-week demand test.

The job:
finding out whether anyone will say yes, before you build anything.
Why it works:
AI can only guess what customers will do. A simple offer to real people gives you an answer.
The test

Message 20 people with a parent over 70, describe the offer in 3 lines, and ask if they'd book a visit.

→
Pass mark, set before you start

3 people say yes and agree a date. Fewer than 3: change the idea, not the pass mark.

04

The honest part Check before you use it.

AI critics have never run your business, met your customers or seen your market up close. Use them to find questions, then answer those questions with real people.

Is It Safe to Connect ChatGPT to My Gmail?

What it can see, and how to give it as little access as possible.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh | The AI Automation Queen Free guide
Shift & Lead

Is it safe to connect ChatGPT to my Gmail?

Only give AI the access the job needs. Often one uploaded file is enough, and you don't need to connect anything.

What you leave with

a simple way to choose the smallest access, a prompt that explains any permission screen in plain words, and where to switch access off.

How to use this guide: Decide what the job needs, check the permission screen before you approve, and know where to disconnect.

01

Choose the smallest access Start small.

  1. Upload one file: best for one document and one task. No connection needed.
  2. Connect one service (for example Gmail or Drive): when the AI needs to search across many items.
  3. Allow actions (send, edit, delete): only when you really need it, and never as your first step.
02

Read the permission screen before you click Every word is a permission.

Look for these words: read, create, edit, delete, send, share. Each one is a different level of access.

In ChatGPT, for example, "Allow read actions" means it can read from the connected app without asking each time, but it still asks before making changes.

03

Where to switch it off Know the way out.

ChatGPT: Settings → Apps (or Plugins, if shown), then choose the account to disconnect. The paths for Claude, Gemini and Copilot are in the full guide.

Turning a connector off for one chat may leave the account connected. Check the account connection too.

04

The honest part Check before you use it.

Always check the AI's explanation against the real permission screen. The screen decides what the tool can do, not the AI's summary of it.

✓

Your prompts Ready to copy.

Explain these permissions in everyday language. For each one, tell me if the tool can read, create, edit, delete, send or share. Then tell me:
1. What information this could expose.
2. Which permission is the riskiest.
3. Whether I could do my task with less access.
4. What I should check before approving.
Don't assume a permission that isn't in the text.

Permission text: [paste the wording from the screen, without account details]

Before you approve, can you say yes to all 3?

  • I know what it can read.
  • I know whether it can create, edit, send, share or delete.
  • I know where to turn it off.

Where to switch it off

Menu names can vary by plan, device or workplace settings.

ChatGPT
Settings → Apps (or Plugins, if shown). Choose the connected account to review or disconnect it. Official page ↗
Claude
Customize → Connectors. Choose the service and select disconnect. Official page ↗
Gemini
Settings & help → Connected Apps (or Personal Intelligence → Connected Apps). Turn off the app you don't want Gemini to use. Official page ↗
Copilot
On Copilot.com, + → Use connectors. On mobile, Profile → Connectors. At work, Microsoft 365 data follows your organisation's permissions. Official page ↗

Disconnecting stops future access. It doesn't always remove what's already in a chat or activity history, so review that separately.

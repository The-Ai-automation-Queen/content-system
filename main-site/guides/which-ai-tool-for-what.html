ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini: Which One Should I Pay For?

Test two tools on your real work. Keep the winner.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh Free guide
Shift & Lead

Keep one tool, cancel the rest

You may pay for more AI tools than you use. This guide tests two on your real work, then helps you cancel the rest. It takes 30 minutes.

What you end up with

  • A list of every AI tool you pay for
  • A score for two tools on your real work
  • A keep or cancel answer for each one
  • The ones you don't need, cancelled before they renew

You need free accounts for the two tools you test. No paid plan needed. Copy each prompt as it is and change the [brackets].

01

Pick two tools No AI needed.

The best tool is often the one closest to your email and files. Start where your work happens.

If your work is inTry firstWhy
Gmail, Google Docs, DriveGeminiIt can work inside Gmail and Docs
Outlook, Word, TeamsCopilotIt can work inside Outlook and Word, if your plan has it
Proposals, emails, posts, planningChatGPT or ClaudeBoth are good all-round. Your test decides
Research you will quoteA search tool like PerplexityIt shows links you can open and check
  1. Find your row. That is Tool A.
  2. Pick one more as Tool B. Stop at two.
  3. Pay for Google Workspace or Microsoft 365? Check if Gemini or Copilot is already in your plan.

Prices change often. Check the official pages: ChatGPT ↗, Claude ↗, Google Workspace ↗, Microsoft 365 Copilot ↗.

02

List what you pay for Every subscription.

You can't choose what to cancel until you see it all.

  1. Search your email for subscription, receipt and renewal.
  2. Search for ChatGPT, OpenAI, Claude, Anthropic, Gemini, Copilot and Perplexity.
  3. Check your bank or card statements for the last 2 months.
  4. Write each tool, the price, how often, and the next renewal date.
Here are the AI tools I pay for:
[paste your list: tool, price, monthly or yearly, renewal date]

Make a table:
Tool | What I pay | Monthly or yearly | Next renewal date

Use only what I give you. If something is missing, write [CHECK].
Do not add tools or look up prices.
Add one line with the total per month, from my numbers only.
  1. Fill in every [CHECK] from your email or bank.
  2. Add up the total yourself. Does it match?
  3. Save the table in a note.
03

Test both on one task Same words, two tools.

First stop both tools learning from your chats. Then give them the same real task.

  1. ChatGPT: click your name, then Settings, then Data Controls. Turn off Improve the model for everyone.
  2. Claude: click your name, then Settings, then Privacy. Turn off Help improve Claude.
  3. Open both tools in two tabs. Click New chat in each.
  4. Paste the same prompt below into both. Change nothing between them.
I run a small business: [what you do and who you help].

Do this task: [for example: reply to this client email].

Here is the material:
[paste it, with [client] instead of real names]

Use only what I give you. Keep every fact. Add nothing new.
If something is missing, write [CHECK].
Never make up names, prices, dates or promises.
Write in plain English, the way I talk to clients.

Can't find the setting? The menus move. Search "model training" on OpenAI's help pages ↗, Anthropic's help pages ↗ or Google's Gemini help ↗.

04

Score both answers Facts first.

A tool can sound good and still change a fact. Check before you score.

List every name, date, price and promise in your answer.
For each one, show the words from my material it comes from.
If it is not in my material, write NOT IN MATERIAL.
  1. Paste this into both tools.
  2. Check for dropped conditions. "Once we have your files" must stay.
  3. Score each tool 1 to 5 on: right facts, sounds like me, time saved, cost.
  4. Repeat with 2 more real tasks this week. One task can be luck.
05

Decide and cancel Keep one.

Let AI add up your scores. The choice is yours.

Here are my scores for two AI tools on 3 real tasks:
[paste your scores and notes]

Here are the AI tools I pay for:
[paste your table from step 02]

1. Add up the scores for each tool.
2. Say which did best for my work, in two sentences.
3. Give me a table: Tool | Keep or cancel | Reason in one line.

Use only what I give you. If you can't decide, write [CHECK].
Do not make up prices or tools.
  1. Add up one total yourself. Does it match?
  2. For each tool to cancel, open Settings, then billing, plan or subscription.
  3. Click cancel before the renewal date.
  4. Pay through the App Store or Google Play? Cancel it there.
  5. Save the email that confirms it.

Can't find billing? Search "cancel subscription" on OpenAI ↗, Anthropic ↗ or Google ↗. Work plan? Ask whoever runs your company's account.

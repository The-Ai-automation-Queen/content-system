Introduction

Any AI · 15 min read · Beginner

Match the tool to where the work happens: one chat for writing and thinking, the assistant built into your email and files, and a search tool for anything you'll quote. Test for a week before you pay.

A guide by Fatiha Chikh | The AI Automation Queen

What you leave with: a side-by-side test for choosing your main chat, a check for the assistant in your work apps, a research prompt that forces sources, and a 7-day keep-or-cancel rule.

Job 1Choose your everyday chat.

The job:
drafting, rewriting, thinking a problem through.
Why this test:
the only fair comparison is the same task, with the same words, in both tools.
The test

Paste the same rough paragraph into ChatGPT and Claude with the same prompt.

What a good result looks like

The one that kept your meaning, needed fewer fixes and sounded more like you. That's your main chat.

03

Job 2Use the assistant already in your work apps.

The job:
summarising an email thread or a shared document.
Why this tool:
if your work lives in Outlook and Teams, Copilot can see it (if your company allows it). If it lives in Gmail and Docs, Gemini can. Pasting work into a separate chat is slower and riskier.
The test

One long email thread you already know well.

→
What a good result looks like

It gets the decision and the next step right, and links back to the emails it used.

The thread summary promptCopy the prompt ↓Summarise this email thread for me: 1. What was decided (and who decided it). 2. What's still open. 3. What I need to do next, and by when. Link to the email each point came from.

The honest partCheck before you use it.

A new subscription won't fix a vague request. Pay only for the tool you've used at least 3 times in the last week, and cancel the rest.

✦Your turn

Unlock the 3 prompts.

Your prompts ready to copy.

Job 1: The side-by-side test prompt (paste the same one into both tools)

Rewrite this paragraph so it's clearer and shorter, in my voice. Keep every fact. Don't add anything new.

Then tell me in one line what you changed.

Paragraph: [paste something you wrote]

Job 2: The thread summary prompt

Summarise this email thread for me:
1. What was decided (and who decided it).
2. What's still open.
3. What I need to do next, and by when.

Link to the email each point came from. If something isn't clear in the thread, say so.

Job 3: The sourced research prompt

[Your question]

Answer in 5 bullet points or fewer. Give a source link for every claim. If sources disagree, show both. If you can't find a reliable source, say so rather than guessing.

The 7-day keep-or-cancel rule: used it 3 times or more · it saved you time each time · you trusted the result after checking. Three yeses: keep it. Anything else: cancel.

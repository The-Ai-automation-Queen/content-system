Know the players / OpenAI

Use ChatGPT for everyday business work

ChatGPT is OpenAI’s general-purpose AI assistant. It can write, analyse files, search, create images, work with data and help across many kinds of tasks.

  • Best forFirst drafts, brainstorming, everyday analysis and general business tasks.
  • Watch out forConfident answers that look right but are not.
  • Try this firstGive it one real task you already know how to judge.

What is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is an AI assistant made by OpenAI. You can use it to ask questions, write, analyse files, create images, search the web and work through problems in conversation.

ChatGPT is a product. It is not the same thing as AI. It is one way to use OpenAI's AI models.

PICTURE IT

Think of ChatGPT as one large workbench. You can bring writing, files, screenshots, numbers and questions to the same place.

What is ChatGPT best used for?

ChatGPT is useful when you want one tool that can handle many different kinds of everyday work.

  • First drafts. Emails, proposals, posts, outlines and notes.
  • Explaining. Ask it to explain a difficult idea in simpler language.
  • Working with files. Upload a document or spreadsheet and ask questions about it.
  • Research. Use web search or deeper research features when current sources matter.
  • Images. Create or edit images when the task is visual.
  • Thinking partner. Ask it to challenge an idea, compare options or find missing questions.

What are ChatGPT Projects?

Projects let you keep related chats, files and instructions together.

Instead of explaining your business from zero every time, you can create a project for a real area of work, such as marketing, a client, hiring or a product launch.

PICTURE IT

A normal chat is one conversation on a desk. A Project is the whole folder beside the desk.

Can ChatGPT be wrong?

Yes. ChatGPT can give you false information that sounds confident. This is often called an AI hallucination.

Check facts when the answer matters, especially names, dates, numbers, quotations, legal claims and sources.

For calculations, ask ChatGPT to use its data or code tools rather than relying on a quick text answer.

How to get better answers from ChatGPT

Give it enough context to do the job well.

Tell it the goal, what it needs to know, what good looks like and the format you want back.

USEFUL QUESTION

“Which parts of your answer are you least sure about?”

That does not make the answer automatically correct, but it gives you a better place to start checking.

ChatGPT vs Claude: what is different?

ChatGPT and Claude overlap a lot.

ChatGPT is often the better choice if you want one broad tool for writing, images, web research, files and everyday tasks.

Claude is often worth testing when the job involves long documents, careful reading or a large amount of context that needs to stay coherent.

The best test is not a benchmark. Give both tools the same real job and compare how much useful work you get back.

ChatGPT vs Gemini: what is different?

Gemini becomes especially useful when your work already lives in Google Workspace.

If the job needs Gmail, Drive, Docs or other Google context, that connection can matter more than small differences between models.

If you do not live in Google Workspace, ChatGPT may feel more general and easier to use as one main AI assistant.

What should you not hand to ChatGPT without review?

  • Final legal or financial decisions
  • Important calculations you have not checked
  • Sensitive customer messages
  • Claims that need a source
  • Anything that could cause harm if confidently wrong

Who should use ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a strong starting point if you want one AI tool for a wide mix of work and you are still learning where AI fits into your day.

You do not need every feature. Start with one real job you repeat and see whether ChatGPT saves time on that job.

My take on ChatGPT

ChatGPT is useful because it is broad, familiar and easy to bring into everyday work.

Do not treat it as the final authority. Treat it as a fast first pass, a thinking partner and a workbench that still needs your judgment.

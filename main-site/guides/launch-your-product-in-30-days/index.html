Introduction

Any AI · 15 min read · Beginner

Most products don't launch because they keep growing. Decide what the first version doesn't include, plan backwards from a fixed date, and let AI do the planning and the Friday check so you can spend your time making.

A guide by Fatiha Chikh | The AI Automation Queen

What you leave with: a "smallest version" prompt, a 4-week plan built backwards from launch day, and a Friday check that keeps it moving.

01

How to use this guideIn 30 seconds.

Pick your launch date before you start and write it down. It doesn't move. What goes into the first version can.

02

Job 1Cut to the smallest useful version.

The job:
deciding what version 1 is, and what waits for version 2.
Why it works:
a "not in version 1" list is the fastest way to stop polishing. AI is good at spotting what a first customer actually needs.
The full idea

An online course with 12 modules, a community, worksheets, a certificate and bonus live calls.

→
A good version 1

3 modules that get one clear result, plus one live call. Out for now: the community, the certificate, 9 modules.

03

Job 2Plan backwards from launch day.

The job:
a week-by-week plan that ends on your fixed date.
Why it works:
starting from the launch date shows what must be done by when. One milestone per week keeps it manageable.
Launch date

A Tuesday, 4 weeks from now.

→
A good plan

Week 1 · content outlined and one module made. Week 2 · all 3 modules made. Week 3 · sales page, payment and emails set up. Week 4 · 10 people told, launch. About 45 minutes a day.

04

Job 3The Friday check.

The job:
a short weekly look at what's done, what's stuck and what to cut.
Why it works:
when a week slips, the answer is to cut scope, not to move the date. AI can suggest what to drop.
What you type

Planned: all 3 modules. Done: 2. Stuck: editing the videos takes too long.

→
What a good check looks like

Keep the date · record module 3 as a simple talking-head video · move editing polish to version 2.

05

The honest partCheck before you use it.

AI can plan, but it can't make the thing or find your first customers. The plan only works if the date is real and the daily 45 minutes happen. If you miss a week, cut, don't delay.

Job 1: The smallest version prompt

Here's my product idea in full: [describe everything you imagine it including].

Help me define version 1: the smallest thing a first customer would pay for that gets them one clear result.

Give me two lists: In version 1 · Not in version 1 (yet). For each item you cut, one line on why it can wait.

Job 2: The backwards plan prompt

My launch date is [date], [4] weeks from now. I have about [45 minutes] a day.

Version 1 includes: [paste your list].

Plan backwards from the launch date: one milestone per week, and the daily tasks for each week. Keep every task under [45 minutes]. Include telling at least 10 people before launch day.

Job 3: The Friday check prompt

It's Friday. Here's my week:
Planned: [what I planned]
Done: [what I did]
Stuck: [what's blocking me]

The launch date doesn't move. Tell me what to cut or simplify to stay on track, and give me next week's 3 priorities.

On launch day: it's live · 10 people know · your version 2 list is saved for later.

How to Launch My Product in 30 Days

Pick a date. ChatGPT plans every task back from it.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh Free guide
Shift & Lead

Launch on a date that holds

Your launch keeps slipping because the product keeps growing. Here you fix the date, make the product small and let ChatGPT plan every task.

What you end up with

  • A launch date that does not move
  • A version 1 list, and a list of what waits
  • A dated plan that fits the time you really have
  • A Friday check that tells you what to cut
  • Launch week emails and posts, planned early

You need a ChatGPT account. The free plan works. You also need a spreadsheet, like Google Sheets. Copy each prompt as it is and change the [brackets].

01

Pick your date It never moves.

The date stays fixed. What goes in the product can change.

  1. Open Google Sheets ↗ and click Blank spreadsheet.
  2. Name it Launch checklist.
  3. Pick a day about 30 days away when you are at your desk.
  4. In the first box, type Launch date: and the date.
  5. Below it, type your time each day, for example 45 minutes.
  6. Below that, type the days you can't work, and what you are launching in one line.

Keep this sheet open. You use it in every step.

02

Cut it down What's in, what waits.

Products don't launch because they keep growing. Make version 1 the smallest thing a client would pay for.

  1. Open ChatGPT ↗ and click New chat.
  2. Use this same chat for steps 02 to 05.
  3. Paste the prompt below and answer each question.
Here is my product idea in full: [everything you imagine it including].
Who it is for: [type of client]
The one result it gets them: [result]

Help me pick version 1: the smallest thing a first client would pay for that still gets that result.
Ask me up to 5 questions, one at a time. Wait for each answer.

Then give me two lists:
1. In version 1
2. Waits for version 2, with one line on why

Use only what I give you. Do not add features I did not mention.
If something is missing, write [CHECK].

Delete anything ChatGPT added that you never said. Copy both lists into your sheet. New ideas go on the version 2 list.

03

Plan back from launch day A date on every task.

Start from the date and work back. Every task gets a day and a time.

Plan my launch backwards from the launch date.

Launch date: [date]
  1. Check the first and last dates in your calendar. ChatGPT can get a weekday wrong.
  2. Check no task lands on a day you can't work.
  3. Copy the table and paste it into your sheet.
04

Run the Friday check Cut, never move the date.

Some weeks go badly. Then you cut something and keep the date.

  1. Open Google Calendar ↗ and click Create, then Event.
  2. Call it Friday launch check, this Friday.
  3. Click Does not repeat and pick Weekly on Friday. Click Save.
  4. Each Friday, type yes next to every task you finished in your sheet.
  5. Go back to your launch chat and paste the prompt below.
It is Friday, week [1, 2, 3 or 4] of my launch. The launch date stays [date].

Planned this week: [paste the rows]

Did it move the date? Reply: "Keep the date. Cut instead." Paste next week's rows into your sheet.

Can't find it? Google moves this menu. Search "repeating event" on Google's Calendar help pages ↗.

05

Plan launch week Emails and posts, ready early.

Plan every launch email and post in week 3. Then launch week is for answering clients.

Plan my launch week emails and posts.

Launch date: [date]
  1. Delete any deadline, bonus or result you never gave it.
  2. Fill in every [CHECK] yourself.
  3. Put each row in your sheet on its date.

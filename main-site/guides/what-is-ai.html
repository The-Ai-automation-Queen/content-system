THE BASICS

What is AI? The simplest explanation I know.

AI is software that can spot patterns in examples and use those patterns to make something new: an answer, a prediction, an image, a summary or a next step.

Picture a very fast helper

Imagine a helper who has studied millions of examples.

You show the helper a new email. It has seen enough emails to recognise the kind of message, the words people usually use, and what a useful reply often looks like.

That is the basic idea behind a lot of modern AI: learn patterns from examples, then use those patterns on something new.

THE PICTURE TO REMEMBER

Examples → patterns → new input → useful output.

What does artificial intelligence mean?

Artificial intelligence, or AI, is the big umbrella term for computer systems that do tasks we usually connect with human intelligence.

That can include recognising a face, translating a sentence, recommending a product, spotting fraud, writing text, creating an image or planning several steps of a task.

AI is not one product. ChatGPT is AI, but AI is much bigger than ChatGPT.

AI vs normal software: what is the difference?

Normal software follows rules that people wrote.

Normal software: “If the customer clicks Buy, add the item to the order.”

Machine-learning systems learn patterns from examples instead of relying only on rules written one by one.

AI example: “Here are 50,000 emails marked spam or not spam. Learn what spam usually looks like, then judge the next email.”

EASY WAY TO SEE IT

Rules: a person writes every instruction.
Learning: the system studies examples and finds patterns.

What is machine learning?

Machine learning is one way of building AI. Instead of telling the computer every rule, you give it examples so it can learn patterns.

Think of teaching a child to recognise a cat. You do not explain every possible ear, tail and whisker. You show lots of cats. After enough examples, the child can recognise a new cat.

Machine learning works differently from a child, but the picture is useful: many examples → learned pattern → new guess.

What is a large language model?

A large language model, often called an LLM, is a type of AI trained on huge amounts of text.

It learns patterns in language: which words go together, how questions are answered, how documents are structured, how code looks, and much more.

When you type into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, a language model helps work out what should come next.

PICTURE IT

A giant autocomplete that became useful enough to write, explain, compare, summarise and reason through many kinds of problems.

What is ChatGPT then?

ChatGPT is a product made by OpenAI. It is one way people use AI models.

The easiest comparison is:

AI = the whole category.
Large language model = one type of AI model.
ChatGPT = one product built with those models.

Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Mistral and other tools are different products in the same wider AI world.

What can AI actually do for a business?

AI is useful when the work contains patterns and information.

  • Write a first draft. Emails, proposals, posts, summaries.
  • Read a lot quickly. Reports, transcripts, notes, long email threads.
  • Sort information. Leads, support messages, documents, feedback.
  • Change one format into another. Meeting notes into actions. A transcript into a brief. A spreadsheet into an explanation.
  • Find patterns. Repeated customer questions, common complaints, unusual numbers.
  • Help with repeatable steps. Prepare work for you to review instead of starting from zero every time.

What AI cannot safely do for you

AI can sound sure even when it is wrong. It can misunderstand your instructions. It can miss information it cannot see.

So there are jobs where AI can help, but a person should still make the decision.

  • Sending an important message without review
  • Making a legal, medical or financial decision on its own
  • Approving a payment
  • Hiring or firing someone
  • Deciding what is true without checking the source

THE RULE I USE

Let AI carry repeatable work. Keep human judgment where the consequence matters.

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is AI that can take several steps toward a goal instead of giving one answer and stopping.

A chatbot might tell you how to plan a trip.

An agent might search flights, compare hotels, organise the options and prepare the booking for you to approve.

The important difference is simple: a chatbot answers; an agent can act through steps.

AI vs automation

Automation means a process runs automatically. It does not always need AI.

Example:

Automation: when someone fills in a form, send a confirmation email.

AI + automation: when someone fills in a form, AI reads the answer, works out what kind of lead it is, writes a summary, then the automation sends that summary to the right person.

Both are useful. You do not need to add AI to a step that simple automation already handles well.

The five words to remember

You do not need to learn the whole AI vocabulary today. Start with these:

  • AI: the big category.
  • Machine learning: learning patterns from examples.
  • LLM: an AI model trained on lots of language.
  • ChatGPT: one AI product.
  • Agent: AI that can take several steps toward a goal.

If you remember one thing

AI is not magic and it is not one app.

It is a group of technologies that learn or use patterns to help computers do useful work.

The business question is not “Should I use AI?”

The better question is: which part of my work has a clear pattern that AI could help carry?

Build a money plan from your payslip, with AI.

Understand every line on your payslip, split what's left into a plan you can live with, and check it once a month.

Introduction

Any AI · 20 min read · Beginner

AI is good at explaining, adding up and organising. Give it your take-home pay and your regular costs (never your account details), and it can help you build a simple plan and check it each month. The decisions stay yours.

A guide by Fatiha Chikh | The AI Automation Queen

What you leave with: a payslip explainer, a one-page monthly plan, and a 10-minute monthly check-in.

01

How to use this guideIn 30 seconds.

Have your latest payslip and a list of your regular monthly costs ready. Type the numbers in yourself. Don't upload the payslip: it carries your name, employer and ID numbers.

02

Job 1Understand your payslip.

The job:
knowing what each deduction is and why your take-home is what it is.
Why it works:
payslips use codes and short names. AI can explain each one in plain words, and tell you what to ask your payroll team.
What you type

Gross pay 3,000 · Tax 400 · National Insurance 200 · Pension 150 · Net pay 2,250.

→
What a good answer looks like

One plain line per deduction, a check that the numbers add up, and "ask payroll" next to anything that looks unusual.

Job 2A one-page monthly plan.

The job:
deciding in advance where your take-home pay goes.
Why it works:
three simple pots are easier to stick to than twenty categories. AI does the maths; you choose the numbers.
What you give it

Take-home 2,250 · rent 900 · bills 250 · travel 120 · goal: an emergency fund.

→
What a good plan looks like

Needs 1,270 · Goals 400 · Everyday spending 580, about 135 a week. One line on the emergency fund: how long to reach 3 months of needs at 400 a month.

05

The honest partCheck before you use it.

AI isn't a financial adviser and can get sums wrong. Check the totals once with a calculator. For pensions, debt, tax or investing decisions, speak to a qualified adviser or a free, official money guidance service in your country.

✦Your turn

Unlock the 3 prompts.

Your prompts ready to copy.

Job 1: The payslip explainer prompt

Explain my payslip line by line in plain English. I've typed the numbers in myself, with no names or ID numbers. I live in [country].

[Gross pay, each deduction and net pay]

For each deduction, say what it is in one line. Check that the numbers add up. If anything looks unusual, tell me what to ask my payroll team. Don't give me tax advice.

Job 2: The monthly plan prompt

Help me build a simple monthly plan.

Take-home pay: [amount]
Regular costs: [list with amounts]
My goal: [for example an emergency fund]

Split my pay into 3 pots: Needs · Goals · Everyday spending. Show the sums. Then tell me how many months it would take to reach [my goal amount] at this rate.

Use only my numbers. Ask me if anything is missing.

Job 3: The check-in prompt

Here's my plan and what actually happened this month.

Planned: [pot amounts]
Actual: [pot amounts]

Show where I was over or under. Suggest one change for next month. Keep it short and practical.

Never type in: account or card numbers · your employee or tax ID · login details.

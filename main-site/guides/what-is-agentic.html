THE BASICS

Understand what AI agents can actually do

A chatbot gives you a reply. An AI agent can take steps, use tools and move a job forward.

What is agentic AI?

Agentic AI means AI that can take actions toward a goal instead of only answering one question.

You give it a job. It may break that job into steps, use tools, check results and continue until it reaches a stopping point.

PICTURE IT

Chatbot: “Here is how to book the meeting.”
AI agent: “I checked the calendars, found three times and prepared the invite for you to approve.”

AI agent vs chatbot: what is the difference?

A chatbot usually waits for your next message.

An AI agent can keep going through several steps.

The easiest way to remember it is:

THE SIMPLE DIFFERENCE

Chatbot = answers.
Agent = acts through steps.

What can an AI agent actually do?

Depending on the tools and permissions you give it, an AI agent might:

  • Search the web
  • Read files
  • Check a calendar
  • Draft an email
  • Update a record
  • Compare options
  • Prepare a report
  • Run several steps in a workflow

The important words are tools and permissions. The agent can only act where you have given it access.

The four levels of AI autonomy

Not every AI agent has the same amount of control. I find it easier to think in four levels.

1. Answers

The AI gives you information and stops.

Example

You ask for three ways to follow up with a lead. It gives you three options.

2. Uses tools

The AI can look things up or use a tool to give you a better answer.

Example

It reads your notes and checks your calendar before suggesting a meeting time.

3. Acts with approval

The AI prepares the action, then waits for you.

Example

It drafts the email and asks you to approve before anything is sent.

4. Acts alone

The AI carries out the action without asking every time.

Example

It sends the email, updates the CRM and schedules the follow-up by itself.

Why level 3 is often the best place to start

Level 3 gives you a lot of the time saving without giving away all the control.

The AI can prepare the work. You still approve the action.

That is useful for emails, customer replies, lead qualification, reports and other work where a wrong action would matter.

A GOOD FIRST RULE

Let AI draft. Keep the send button.

What are AI agent permissions?

Permissions decide what the AI is technically allowed to see or do.

If an agent can read your inbox but cannot send email, that is safer than giving it full email access and simply telling it not to send.

PICTURE IT

A locked door is stronger than a note on the door saying “please do not enter.”

What are AI agent guardrails?

Guardrails are rules and technical limits that help keep an AI system inside the job you gave it.

Examples:

  • Never send without approval
  • Never spend money
  • Never delete records
  • Only use approved data sources
  • Stop and ask when information is missing

What can go wrong with agentic AI?

The risk changes when AI can act.

A wrong chatbot answer is something you can read before using it. A wrong agent action might already have happened.

That is why the question is not only “How smart is the agent?”

You also need to ask:

  • What can it access?
  • What can it change?
  • What needs approval?
  • Can the action be reversed?
  • How will I know what it did?

When should you let an AI agent act alone?

Only after the job is clear, the permissions are limited and you have watched the system work reliably.

Start with actions that are easy to reverse and cheap to fix.

Good early examples are sorting information, preparing drafts and updating low-risk internal records.

Sending sensitive messages, spending money or making decisions about people should stay much closer to human approval.

One picture to remember

THE WHOLE IDEA

Answer → use tools → prepare action → act alone.
The farther right you go, the more useful the system can become and the more carefully you need to control it.

The business question to ask

Do not start with “How do I get an autonomous AI agent?”

Start with:

Which repeatable job could AI move forward safely, and where should it stop for me?

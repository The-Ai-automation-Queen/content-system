How to Teach Claude About My Business So I Stop Repeating Myself

Set it up once, in 5 steps. One Claude project that holds your brand kit, your voice rules and your Brand DNA.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh | The AI Automation Queen Free guide

One project that knows your brand

A Brand Brain is one Claude project that holds your brand kit, your voice rules and your Brand DNA, so everything Claude makes for your business matches your brand. It takes five steps and about 30 minutes the first time.

What you end up with

  • One Claude project called Brand Brain
  • A brand kit: logo, colours and fonts, either your own or a design system Claude builds for you
  • Voice rules: how you sound, the words you use and the words you never say
  • A one-page Brand DNA doc: who you help, the problem you solve and why you are different
  • A place to ask for ads, landing pages, posts and emails that come back on brand the first time

You need a Claude plan that includes Projects. Every prompt below can be copied and pasted as it is; replace the parts in [square brackets].

01

Create the project The container.

The project is the container: anything you add to it is read by every chat you start inside it.

  1. Open Claude ↗ and click Projects in the left sidebar.
  2. Click New project.
  3. Name it Brand Brain.
  4. In the description, write one line about your business, for example: “Everything about [business name]: brand kit, voice and customer.”

Keep the project open. The next three steps each add one file to it.

02

Build your brand kit What you look like.

Your brand kit tells Claude what your business looks like: logo, colours and fonts.

If you already have a brand: upload your logo files, your colour codes and your font names to the project's files. A brand guidelines PDF works too. Then skip to Step 3.

If you do not have one yet: start a chat inside the project and paste this prompt.

Act as a senior brand designer.
Build a complete design system for my business:
[what you sell + who it is for]

Include:
- A logo direction (describe 3 options, then recommend one)
- A colour palette with hex codes and what each colour is for
- Heading and body fonts that are free to use
- Buttons, cards and spacing
- Do and don't rules

Ask me 5 questions before you start.

Answer the five questions, then push back on anything that does not feel like you. When you are happy, ask:

Turn the final design system into one document called Brand Kit that I can save to this project.

Save that document to the project's files.

03

Build your brand voice How you sound.

Voice rules are what stop Claude sounding like a generic AI and make it sound like you.

  1. Upload anything your business has ever written to the project: website pages, emails, newsletters, social captions, sales pages. Five to ten pieces is enough; more is better.
  2. Pick writing you are proud of. Claude copies what you give it, so leave out anything that does not sound like you.
  3. Start a chat inside the project and paste this prompt.
Read everything I have uploaded to this project.
Turn it into a Voice Rules document with these sections:

1. How we sound: 3 to 5 tone words, each with one sentence on what it means for us
2. Words and phrases we use often
3. Words and phrases we would never say
4. Sentence style: length, punctuation, emoji, capitals
5. Three short before-and-after examples: a generic sentence, then the same sentence in our voice

Only use what is in my writing. If something is unclear, ask me.

Read what comes back and correct it. The “never say” list is the most useful part, so add every word that makes you cringe. Save the final version to the project's files as Voice Rules.

No writing yet? Record yourself explaining your business for two minutes, paste the transcript in, and run the same prompt.

04

Write your Brand DNA doc The step that matters most.

The Brand DNA doc is one page that tells Claude who you sell to, what problem you solve and why you are different. Fill in the brackets and save it to the project as Brand DNA.

#SectionFill in
1Who we helpWe help [customer] who [situation].
2The problemThey struggle with [problem].
3How we solve itWe [what you do] so they [result].
4Why we are differentUnlike [others], we [difference].
5Proof[numbers, results, reviews]
6One line[Brand] helps [customer] get [result].

Tips for filling it in

  • Be specific about the customer. “Busy cafe owners with one location” beats “small businesses”.
  • Write the problem in your customer's words, the way they would say it to a friend.
  • For proof, real numbers and real quotes only. Leave it short if you are new.

Stuck? Paste this prompt into a chat inside the project and let Claude interview you.

Interview me to write my Brand DNA doc.
Ask one question at a time, 10 questions in total, covering:
who my customer is, what problem I solve, how I solve it,
what makes me different, and what proof I have.
Then write the doc using the six sections:
Who we help, The problem, How we solve it,
Why we are different, Proof, One line.
05

Start creating Ask for what you need.

Open a new chat inside the Brand Brain project and ask for what you need; Claude reads your three files before it answers. Start with requests like these.

  • An ad
    Write 3 versions of a short ad for [product or offer]. One line on the angle behind each.
  • A landing page
    Build a landing page for [product]. Use our colours, fonts and voice. Include a headline, 3 benefits, proof and one call to action.
  • A social post
    Write an Instagram post about [topic] in our voice, with a first line that stops the scroll.
  • An email
    Write a welcome email for new subscribers. Keep it under 150 words.
  • A second opinion
    Here is something I wrote: [paste]. Rewrite it so it matches our voice rules, and tell me what you changed.

When something comes back off brand, say what is wrong (“too formal”, “we never say ‘unlock’”) and ask Claude to update the Voice Rules file with that note. The Brand Brain gets better each time you correct it.

06

Project instructions Paste once.

Project instructions make Claude check your files every time without being asked. Open the project, find Instructions, and paste this.

You are the in-house brand team for [business name].
Before you write or design anything, read Brand Kit, Voice Rules and Brand DNA in this project.

Always:
- Use only the colours and fonts in Brand Kit
- Write in the voice set out in Voice Rules, and never use a word on the "never say" list
- Speak to the customer and the problem described in Brand DNA
- If a request is missing something you need, ask one question before you start

Never invent prices, results, reviews or claims. If you need a fact that is not in the project, ask me.
07

Keep it sharp Small habits.

  • When something changes (new offer, new price, new customer): update Brand DNA the same day.
  • Once a month: add your best new writing to the project and ask Claude to refresh the Voice Rules.
  • When Claude gets it wrong twice: the fix belongs in a file, not in the chat. Add the rule to Voice Rules or Brand Kit.
  • Keep the files short. One to two pages each works better than a 40-page brand book.

How to Make ChatGPT Give Shorter Answers

Save your length rules once. Get short answers every time.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh Free guide
Shift & Lead

Short answers without asking

ChatGPT writes long because it does not know what short means to you. This guide saves your lengths once. It takes 20 minutes.

What you end up with

  • A length for each job, like client replies and call notes
  • Your length rules saved in ChatGPT
  • 3 short lines to add when you need it shorter
  • One client reply, short and checked

You need a ChatGPT account. The free plan works. Copy each prompt as it is and change the [brackets].

01

Decide what short means No AI needed.

"Make it shorter" makes ChatGPT guess. Give it a length and a shape instead.

  1. Open a note.
  2. Write the 4 or 5 jobs you ask ChatGPT for most.
  3. Next to each, write a length and a shape. Start from the table below.
JobLengthShape
Reply to a client emailUnder 120 wordsAnswer in the first line
Call notes summary3 bulletsDecisions, actions, open questions
Social postUnder 60 wordsOne idea, one next step
Quick question2 or 3 sentencesAnswer first, then why
02

Write your length rules From your list.

ChatGPT turns your list into clear rules for itself.

  1. Open ChatGPT ↗ and click New chat.
  2. Paste the prompt below with your list in the brackets.
Write answer-length rules for my ChatGPT Custom instructions.

Here are my jobs, with the length and shape I want:
[paste your list from step 01]

Write them as instructions to you:
1. One line for every answer: answer first, no introduction, no offer of more help at the end, no repeating my question.
2. One line per job: the job, the length, the shape.
3. One line: if a job is not listed, keep it under 100 words unless I ask for more.
4. One line: never drop a fact, deadline or condition to save words. If something does not fit, say what you left out.

Keep it under 1,000 characters. Use only the jobs and lengths I gave you.

Check every job is there. Copy the rules into your note.

03

Save the rules Paste once.

Custom instructions (a setting ChatGPT reads before every chat) makes every new chat follow them.

  1. Click your name or picture, then Settings, then Personalization.
  2. Open Custom instructions.
  3. Paste the rules into the box about how ChatGPT should respond, then click Save.
  4. Click New chat and ask for one of your jobs. Check the length.

Can't find it? OpenAI moves this menu. Search "custom instructions" on OpenAI's help pages ↗.

04

Add a short line When you need it shorter.

Sometimes one answer must be shorter still. End your prompt with one of these: 3 bullets only. Under 100 words. Answer first, then one line of why.

Here is a client email: [paste the email, with names swapped for [client]].
Draft my reply from my notes: [your notes].
Use only what I give you. If something is missing, write [CHECK].
Never make up names, prices, dates or promises.
Under 100 words.

Keep the 3 lines at the top of your note so you can paste them fast.

05

Check nothing was cut Short and still right.

Short answers can drop a deadline. "Waiting to confirm" can become "confirmed".

Look at your last answer and what I gave you. List:
1. Any fact, deadline, price or condition you dropped
2. Anything you made sound more certain than it is
3. Anything you added that was not in what I gave you

If nothing important was left out, write "Nothing important left out". Do not rewrite the answer.
  1. Put back anything it dropped, in your own words.
  2. Check every date and price against your notes.
  3. Check words like agreed, confirmed and done are really true.

What Is an AI Browser and Should I Use One?

A browser with an AI assistant built in. Here is how to try one safely.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh Free guide
Shift & Lead

Try an AI browser safely

An AI browser reads pages and clicks for you, which is risky near your email. This guide sets up a safe space and tries 3 jobs. It takes 30 minutes.

What you end up with

  • A safe test profile with no saved logins
  • A rival's page summed up, with quotes you checked
  • Two suppliers compared in one table
  • Your AI browser rules on one page

You need a computer and an AI browser, like ChatGPT Atlas, Comet or Gemini in Chrome. Free plans can read pages. Copy each prompt as it is and change the [brackets].

01

Make a safe space Done once.

An AI browser has a chat panel beside the page. Some can also click and type for you (agent mode).

JobRisk
Read the page you are onLow: nothing is sent
Compare two open pagesLow: you check the quotes
Fill a form, then stopMedium: you watch every step
Act while signed inHigh: mistakes are real
  1. Open your AI browser. Click your picture or letter in the top corner.
  2. Click Add profile. Skip signing in.
  3. Name it AI test and click Done.
  4. In Settings, switch off saving passwords and cards.
  5. Never log in to email, banking or client accounts in this window.

Why it matters: a web page can hide orders meant for the AI assistant. If it is signed in to your email, a page could push it to act. So every job here runs signed out.

Can't find it? Search "profiles" on Google Chrome help ↗ or OpenAI's help pages ↗. Or use a private window.

02

Sum up a rival's page The safest job.

Reading is safe. Nothing is clicked or sent.

  1. In your AI test window, open a rival's services or pricing page.
  2. Open the AI assistant's chat panel.
  3. Paste the prompt below and press Enter.
Read this page only. I run [what your business does] for [who your clients are].

Give me:
1. What they offer, in 3 bullet points
2. Who it is for, in one sentence
3. What they promise, quoting the exact words
4. What they say about price or how to buy, quoting the page

Use only what is on this page. Write "Not on the page" where something is missing.
Never make up names, prices, dates or promises.
Do not click anything or open other pages.
  1. Copy a few words from one quote.
  2. Click on the page. Press Ctrl + F (or Cmd + F on a Mac) and paste.
  3. No match? It made the quote up. Do not use the answer.
03

Compare two suppliers Two pages, one table.

No more jumping between tabs. You still check the quotes.

  1. In your AI test window, open each supplier's page in its own tab.
  2. Open the chat panel. If it sees one tab only, add the other (often + or @).
  3. Paste the prompt below and press Enter.
Compare the two pages I have open.

Table: Detail | [supplier 1] | [supplier 2] | Exact words from each page

Details: [price, what's included, contract length, how to cancel]

Use only what is on the two pages. Write "Not on the page" where a detail is missing.
Do not recommend one. Do not click anything.

Check 2 quotes with Ctrl + F. Look up every "Not on the page" yourself. Then you decide.

04

Fill a practice form Stop before you send.

Now it acts for the first time. Use a form you made, so nothing real happens.

  1. In your normal browser, open Google Forms ↗ and click Blank form.
  2. Add three questions: Name, Email, Message.
  3. Click Preview (the eye). Copy that web address.
  4. Open it in your AI test window. Switch on Agent mode. Not on your plan? Skip to step 05.
  5. Paste the prompt below. Watch. Do not click while it works.
Fill in the form on this page with these made-up details:
Name: [made-up name]
Email: test@example.com
Message: I'd like to ask about [made-up service].

Stop before you press Submit, Send or any button that finishes the form.
Tell me what you typed into each field, then wait.

Did it stop before Submit? Close the tab. In your form, Responses should say 0.

05

Write your rules One page, kept.

Your rules come from what you just saw.

Write my AI browser rules for my business.

Ask me these one at a time and wait for each answer:
1. Which AI browser and plan did I test?
2. When it read a page, were the quotes right?
3. When it compared pages, did it miss or make up anything?
4. Did it stop before Submit on the practice form?
5. Which accounts must it never be signed in to?

Then write one page:
- What it may do
- What it may do only while I watch
- What it must never do (send, buy, book, delete, reply to clients)
- Where it runs (my AI test profile, signed out)

Use only what I tell you. If something is missing, write [CHECK]. Plain English, under 300 words.

Fill every [CHECK]. Save it as AI browser rules.

How to Test My Business Idea With ChatGPT Before I Build It

Find out if people will pay before you build.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh | The AI Automation Queen Free guide
Shift & Lead

Test your idea before you build

Ask ChatGPT if your idea is good and it says yes. This guide makes it find the weak spots instead. It takes 40 minutes, then one week.

What you end up with

  • A one-page brief of your idea
  • The 3 biggest problems, found by five critics
  • A one-week test with real people and a pass mark
  • A go, change or drop choice, written down

You need a ChatGPT account. The free plan works. Copy each prompt as it is and change the [brackets].

01

Write the idea down Five parts, no AI yet.

A vague idea gets vague feedback. Write it in five short parts first.

  1. Open Google Docs ↗ and click Blank document. Call it Idea test pack.
  2. Answer each question below in one or two sentences.
  3. Don't know an answer? Write [CHECK] and move on.
PartQuestion
WhoWho exactly is it for, and what is going on for them?
ProblemWhat problem do they have, in their words?
OfferWhat do they get, and how long does it take?
PriceWhat would they pay, in dollars?
Why youWhy buy from you?
02

Get a one-page brief ChatGPT asks, you answer.

ChatGPT asks what a good adviser would ask. Then it writes the brief.

  1. Open ChatGPT ↗ and click New chat.
  2. Paste the prompt below with your five answers.
  3. Answer each question in a sentence or two.
Help me turn my business idea into a one-page brief.

Who: [who]
Problem: [problem]
Offer: [offer]
Price: [price]
Why me: [why you]

Ask me questions one at a time and wait for each answer. Ask no more than 8.

Then write the brief with 5 headings: Who it's for, The problem, The offer, The price, Why me. Under 250 words.

Use only what I tell you. If something is missing, write [CHECK]. Don't tell me if the idea is good yet.

Fix anything you did not mean. Paste the brief into your Idea test pack.

03

Call in five critics Find the weak spots.

Each critic has one job. None of them can just say "looks good".

  1. Click New chat, so the last chat does not make it kind.
  2. Paste the prompt below with your price and brief.
Check my business idea as 5 critics. No compliments.

1. The client: would I pay [price], and what would stop me?
2. The competitor: what do clients use now, and why would they switch?
3. The accountant: does the price cover my time and costs?
4. The marketer: can this be said in one sentence, and where would clients hear about it?
5. The doubting friend: what question am I avoiding?

Give 2 sharp points from each. Then rank the 3 biggest problems, with one line on how to test or fix each.

Use only my brief. If you need a fact you don't have, write [CHECK]. Never make up numbers.

My brief: [paste your brief]
  1. Paste the answer into your Idea test pack.
  2. Press Ctrl + F (or Cmd + F on a Mac) and find each [CHECK].
  3. Check every [CHECK], number and company name on Google ↗ yourself.
  4. Under each top problem, write how you will fix it.
04

Plan a one-week test Real people, set the bar first.

AI can only guess. Real people show if anyone will say yes.

Plan a one-week test for my business idea. It must use real people and cost nothing.

My brief: [paste]
The top 3 problems: [paste]

Give me:
1. Who to ask, how many, and where to find them among my past clients and contacts
2. A 3-line description of the offer, with the price as [price]
3. A message to send, under 80 words, plain English
4. What counts as a yes: a booked call or a deposit. Compliments do not count.
5. A pass mark: how many yeses I need by the end of the week
6. A day-by-day plan

Don't suggest building anything first. Use only what I give you. If something is missing, write [CHECK]. Never make up names, prices, dates or promises.
  1. Write your pass mark at the top of your Idea test pack. Don't change it later.
  2. Put your real price in the message.
  3. Send it to one person at a time, by email or private message.
  4. Write every reply down the day it comes in.
05

Go, change or drop Against your pass mark.

Count your real yeses. Compare them with the pass mark you set.

My pass mark was: [pass mark].

This week:
- People I asked: [number]
- People who replied: [number]
- Who said yes, and what they did: [details]
- What people said: [paste replies]

Using only this, tell me go, change or drop, and why. If change, suggest one thing to change for next week. Don't count interest as a yes.
  • Go: you hit the pass mark. Build the smallest version.
  • Change: some yeses. Change one thing and test another week.
  • Drop: no yeses. Write what you learned and move on.

Swap real names for [contact 1], [contact 2] before you paste replies.

The honest part: AI critics have never met your clients. Real people decide. If the week goes badly, don't lower the pass mark. Don't spend money until you hit it.

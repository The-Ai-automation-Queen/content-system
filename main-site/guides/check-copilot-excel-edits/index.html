One edit

Ask Copilot to change B3

Open Copilot in Excel with the working copy. Choose Edit mode if it is not already selected. The instruction below asks for one change: Notebooks units from 5 to 6.

Get the one-cell Copilot instruction

Copy the exact request, then use the before-and-after sheet to check what changed.

Save the link we email you to return later. Privacy notice.
In the open working copy of my practice workbook, find the row where Item is Notebooks. The columns are Item (A), Units (B), Price (C) and Total (D). In that row, Units is 5, Price is 6 and Total uses the formula =B3*C3.

Change only the Notebooks Units cell, B3, from 5 to 6. Keep the formula =B3*C3 in D3. Do not change the Pens or Folders rows, any labels, prices or other formulas.

After the edit, tell me the cell you changed and its old and new values. Tell me the three displayed totals in row order. If you cannot make the edit, say so and do not claim the workbook changed.

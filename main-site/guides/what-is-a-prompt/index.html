What Do I Type Into ChatGPT?

Four lines that work for any task.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh | The AI Automation Queen Free guide
Shift & Lead

Four lines for any task

A short request makes ChatGPT guess. This guide gives you 4 lines that tell it what it needs. It takes 20 minutes.

What you end up with

  • A 4-line prompt you fill in for any task
  • One extra line that makes ChatGPT ask you first
  • Ready prompts for your 3 most common jobs
  • One prompt tested on real work and fixed

You need a ChatGPT account. The free plan works, and Claude works too. Copy each prompt as it is and change the [brackets].

01

Learn the 4 lines See the difference.

A prompt is the message you type to the AI. A good one answers 4 questions.

  1. Open ChatGPT ↗ and click New chat.
  2. Type: "Write a follow-up email to a client." Press Enter.
  3. Read the answer. See how much it had to guess.
LineWhat to writeExample
1. The jobWhat you want done, in one sentenceWrite a follow-up email to [client]
2. What it can useYour notes or factsMy call notes: [paste]
3. What you want backLength, shape and toneUnder 120 words, warm and direct
4. What to checkWhat it must not make upUse only my notes. Write [CHECK] for any price or date
02

Fill in the 4 lines For one job this week.

This works for any task. The last line makes ChatGPT ask when it needs more.

  1. Click New chat.
  2. Paste the prompt below. Change the [brackets] for one real job.
  3. Press Enter. Answer any questions it asks.
THE JOB
[What you want done, in one sentence.]

WHAT YOU CAN USE
[Paste your notes. Write [client] instead of real names.]

WHAT I NEED BACK
[Length, shape and tone. For example: under 150 words, warm and direct.]

WHAT TO CHECK
Use only what I gave you. If something is missing, write [CHECK].
Never make up names, prices, dates or promises.

Before you answer, check you have enough details.
If something important is missing, ask me up to 3 questions and stop.

Did it ask questions? Put those answers in line 2 next time.

03

Get 3 ready prompts For your top jobs.

Filling in blanks every time is slow. Let ChatGPT write a ready prompt for each top job.

I run a small business: [what you sell and who to].
I want ready prompts for the 3 jobs I do most.

Ask me one question at a time and wait for my answer.
Ask no more than 8 questions, about:
- the 3 jobs I do most
- what I usually have to hand for each
- what a good result looks like
- what must never go wrong

Then write one prompt per job with 4 headings:
THE JOB, WHAT YOU CAN USE, WHAT I NEED BACK, WHAT TO CHECK.
Fill in what stays the same. Use [brackets] only where I paste new material.
In WHAT TO CHECK, always write: Use only what I paste. If something is missing, write [CHECK]. Never make up names, prices, dates or promises.
Give each prompt a short name, like "Enquiry: first reply".
  1. Delete any line that asks for something you never have.
  2. Add anything you always include, like your booking link.
  3. Paste all 3 into one note called Prompt library.
04

Test one on real work Then fix the prompt.

A prompt is only ready once it works on a real job.

  1. Copy this week's notes. Swap names for [client] and prices for [price].
  2. Click New chat and paste one ready prompt.
  3. Replace the [brackets] with your notes. Press Enter.
  4. Check every name, date and price is in your notes. Fill every [CHECK].

Something wrong? Paste this in the same chat.

This answer had a problem: [what was wrong].
Rewrite my prompt so this does not happen again.
Keep the 4 headings. Show me only the new prompt.

Paste the new prompt into your note, in place of the old one.

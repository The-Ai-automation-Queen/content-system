KNOW THE PLAYERS / DEEPSEEK

DeepSeek is interesting because the model can travel.

DeepSeek is a Chinese AI company known for capable models that can be accessed online and, in some cases, downloaded and run elsewhere.

What is DeepSeek?

DeepSeek is an AI company that builds large language models and AI tools.

Like ChatGPT or Claude, you can use DeepSeek models to write, analyse, reason through problems and work with code.

The part many businesses should understand is not only the chatbot. It is the fact that some DeepSeek models are available as open-weight models.

PICTURE IT

A normal hosted AI is like renting a kitchen. An open-weight model is more like taking the equipment and running it in a kitchen you control.

Why did DeepSeek get so much attention?

DeepSeek drew attention because it showed that strong AI models did not have to come only from the biggest US labs.

It also pushed people to look more closely at the cost of running AI and at the difference between using a hosted service and running a model yourself.

What does open-weight mean?

Open-weight means the trained model files are available for others to download and run under the licence attached to that model.

That does not mean everything about the model is open. It means you may be able to run the model outside the original company's hosted service.

What is self-hosting?

Self-hosting means running the model on infrastructure you control, such as your own servers or private cloud setup.

PICTURE IT

Hosted service: your request goes to someone else's system.
Self-hosted model: the model runs in a system you control.

Self-hosting gives you more control, but it also creates work. Someone still has to manage the hardware, security, updates and monitoring.

DeepSeek vs ChatGPT: what is different?

ChatGPT is a polished product with a large ecosystem, many integrations and broad everyday features.

DeepSeek becomes more interesting when cost, model access or self-hosting matters.

For a normal business user who simply wants an AI assistant, ChatGPT may be easier.

For a technical team building at scale, DeepSeek can be worth a closer look.

What should businesses know about data location?

Where your data goes depends on how you use the model.

If you use a hosted AI service, your data is processed through that provider's systems and terms.

If you run an open-weight model yourself, you can control the environment more directly.

The important business question is:

What data am I sending, where is it processed, and what am I allowed to do under my contracts and privacy rules?

When should you not paste business data into a public AI service?

Be careful with:

  • Client-confidential information
  • Personal data
  • Contracts
  • Private financial information
  • Anything covered by a confidentiality agreement

This rule applies to every AI provider, not only DeepSeek.

Who should care about DeepSeek?

DeepSeek is worth understanding if you:

  • Build software that makes many AI calls
  • Care about model cost
  • Want to experiment with open-weight models
  • Have the technical ability to self-host
  • Want more control over where a model runs

What should a small business do?

If you are not technical and you mainly need an everyday AI assistant, start with the tool you can use safely and consistently.

Do not choose DeepSeek only because it is cheaper or because people online are talking about it.

Choose it when one of its real differences solves a real problem for you.

My take on DeepSeek

DeepSeek matters because it broadens the AI market and makes open models harder to ignore.

For most small businesses, the useful lesson is bigger than one company:

Do not ask only which AI is smartest. Ask where it runs, what it costs at your scale, and what data you are giving it.

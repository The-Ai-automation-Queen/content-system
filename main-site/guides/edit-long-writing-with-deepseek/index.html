Keep the story you like. Edit only what is weak.

Give DeepSeek one passage, the facts to protect and one problem to fix. Check its changes before you keep them.

Step 1 / 3

See a filled brief first

The Mara scene from the shorter DeepSeek guide gives us something specific to protect.

Characters

Mara and Ben. Ben gave Mara the train time.

Facts

Mara arrives at 7.15. The last train has gone. Ben copied the Sunday timetable. They have 20 minutes to reach the ferry on foot.

Voice

Third-person past tense. Restrained tone.

Edit target

Make the transition from Mara's line to Ben's timetable mistake easier to follow.

Only the selected passage goes to DeepSeek. The rest of the story stays outside this edit.

View the filled example instruction
Protected brief
Characters: Mara and Ben. Ben gave Mara the train time.
Facts that must not change: Mara arrives at 7.15. The last train has gone. Ben copied the Sunday timetable. They have 20 minutes to reach the ferry on foot.
Voice: Third-person past tense. Restrained tone.

Edit target
Problem to fix: Make the transition from Mara's line to Ben's timetable mistake easier to follow.
Passage: “You said it left at half past,” she told Ben, who checked the silent departure board. He had copied the Sunday timetable by mistake.

Revise only the pasted passage. Preserve the protected brief. Do not continue the story, add a character, change an event or rewrite another section.

After the revision, list each factual or structural change you made.

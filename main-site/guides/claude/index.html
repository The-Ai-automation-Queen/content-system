How to Use Claude to Do My Admin

Call notes, long documents and emails. One short prompt each.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh Free guide
Shift & Lead

Hand Claude your weekly admin

Call notes, long documents and replies eat your week. This guide sets up Claude (an AI assistant) to draft all three. It takes 30 minutes.

What you end up with

  • One Admin project in Claude, with your rules saved
  • An action list from real call notes
  • A document answer with the exact sentence to check
  • A short email from your rough points

You need a Claude account. The free plan works. Copy each prompt as it is and change the [brackets].

01

Set up Claude safely Done once.

Do this before you paste any client work. It stops Claude learning from your chats.

  1. Open Claude ↗ and sign in.
  2. Click your name in the bottom left, then Settings, then Privacy.
  3. Turn off the switch that lets Claude learn from your chats, like Help improve Claude.
  4. Copy your call notes and one email into a note. Swap real names for [client].
  5. Delete bank details, passwords and personal details.

Can't find it? Anthropic moves this menu. Search "model training" on Anthropic's help pages ↗.

02

Make the Admin project One home for admin.

A project (a folder with its own rules) makes every chat inside it follow your rules.

  1. In the left menu, click Projects, then New project.
  2. Name it Admin and click Create project.
  3. Click in the chat box and paste the prompt below.
Write instructions for my Admin project. I run a small business: [what you do and who you help].

Ask me these one at a time and wait for each answer:
1. How do I like emails to sound?
2. How do I sign off my emails?
3. What do I never want to say or promise in writing?

Then write the instructions in 4 parts:

Job 1, call notes to actions: give me Decisions, then a table (Action | Owner | Deadline), then Open questions. Keep suggestions apart from decisions. Write "Not agreed" where an owner or deadline is missing.

Job 2, questions about a document: answer only from the attached document. Quote the exact sentence and give the page or section. If it does not answer it, say so.

Job 3, rough points to email: under 120 words, in my tone, with my sign-off.

Every job: use only what I give you. If something is missing, write [CHECK]. Never make up names, prices, dates or promises.

Keep it under 2,000 characters.

Answer the 3 questions. Check your answers are in the rules, then copy them.

Can't find it? Search "projects" on Anthropic's help pages ↗.

03

Save the rules Paste once.

Saved rules apply to every chat in the project.

  1. Open the Admin project page.
  2. Click Instructions on the right.
  3. Paste your rules and click Save.

Can't find it? Search "project instructions" on Anthropic's help pages ↗.

04

Turn notes into actions Who does what, by when.

Messy notes become a clear list. Start a new chat in the Admin project.

Job 1. Here are my notes from a call with [client]:
[paste your notes]
  1. Find each name and date in your notes. Delete any that are not there.
  2. Move anything only "suggested" from Decisions to Open questions.
  3. Ask the client about each Not agreed.
05

Ask a document With the exact sentence.

Claude quotes the sentence it used. You check it in seconds.

  1. Start a new chat in the Admin project.
  2. Click the paperclip or + and pick your document.
  3. Paste the prompt below with your question.
Job 2. My question about the attached document: [your question, for example: What is the notice period?]
Quote the exact sentence you used and give the page or section.
  1. Copy 4 or 5 words from the quote.
  2. Open the document and press Ctrl + F (or Cmd + F on a Mac).
  3. Paste the words. Nothing found? Do not use the answer.
06

Turn points into email In your tone.

You know what to say, not how. Claude writes it short.

Job 3. Write an email to [client] from these points:
[paste your rough points]
  1. Delete any date, price or promise not in your points.
  2. Fill in every [CHECK] and put the real name back.
  3. One line wrong? Tell Claude the right fact in the same chat.
  4. Send it yourself.

The honest part: Claude can get a detail wrong and sound sure. It writes the first version. You check it and you send it.

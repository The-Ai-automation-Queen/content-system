How to Get Claude to Do the Same Weekly Task Without Explaining It Again

Explain it once. Then just paste your notes each week.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh Free guide
Shift & Lead

Explain your weekly job once

You explain the same weekly job to Claude every time. This guide saves your rules once, so you just paste your notes. It takes 30 minutes.

What you end up with

  • Your weekly job written down in 4 parts
  • Clear rules for Claude, written after it asks you questions
  • The rules saved in a Project, so you never retype them
  • A weekly draft you check and send yourself

You need a Claude account at claude.ai. The free plan works for the test. Copy each prompt as it is and change the [brackets].

01

Write the job down No AI yet.

Pick one job you do every week from notes, like a client update. Write it in 4 parts.

PartExample: weekly client update
What goes inMy rough notes and the client's last email
The stepsSort notes into done, in progress, waiting. Write the update
What comes outAn email with 4 headings, under 200 words
Where Claude stopsDraft only. I check it and send it
  1. Write your own 4 parts in a note.
  2. Find your notes from the last 2 weeks.
  3. Swap every client name for [client] and every amount for [amount].
02

Let Claude write the rules It asks, you answer.

You know the job. Claude turns it into clear rules.

  1. Open Claude ↗ and click New chat.
  2. Paste the prompt below with your 4 parts.
  3. Answer each question in a sentence or two.
I want instructions for a job I do every week, so you do it the same way each time.

What goes in: [what I give you]
The steps: [what you do, in order]
What comes out: [exactly what comes back]
Where you stop: [what you leave to me]

Ask me up to 6 questions, one at a time. Wait for each answer.

Then write the instructions in 5 sections:
1. What I paste in
2. The steps, numbered
3. The output: headings, length, tone
4. Rules
5. Where you stop

Always include these rules:
- Use only what I give you in this chat.
- If something is missing, write [CHECK].
- Never make up names, prices, dates or promises.
- Draft only. Never send or schedule anything.

Keep it under 2,000 characters.

Check all 4 rules are in. Copy the instructions into your note.

03

Save them in a Project Paste once.

A Project (a folder in Claude with its own rules) makes every chat in it follow your instructions.

  1. Click Projects in the left sidebar, then New project.
  2. Name it, for example Weekly client update.
  3. Click Instructions, paste your instructions, then click Save.

No Projects? Paste the instructions at the start of each new chat.

Can't find it? Anthropic moves this menu. Search "projects" on Claude's help pages ↗.

04

Test on 2 real weeks One week proves nothing.

Rules that work once can fail next week. Test them on two.

  1. Start a new chat in your Project.
  2. Paste last week's notes. Press Enter.
  3. Start another new chat. Paste the week before.
  1. List every name, date, number and promise in each draft.
  2. Press Ctrl + F (or Cmd + F) in your notes and search each one.
  3. Not in your notes? Claude added it. Write it down.
05

Fix each mistake Fix once, not weekly.

Every mistake becomes one new rule. Then it does not come back.

Here are my weekly job instructions:
[paste the instructions]

I tested them on 2 weeks of notes. Here is what went wrong:
[each problem, for example: it was 350 words; it promised a date not in my notes]

For each problem, add one short rule that stops it.
Change nothing else. Show the full updated instructions, then list the new rules.
  1. Paste the new version into your Project's Instructions and your note.
  2. Run week 1 again. Is the problem gone?
06

Use it every week Paste, check, send.

Claude writes the draft. You decide what goes to the client.

  1. Swap client names in your notes for [client].
  2. Start a new chat in your Project and paste your notes.
  3. Check every name, date and promise against your notes.
  4. Fill in every [CHECK], put the real names back, and send it yourself.

The honest part: Claude does not remember last week. It only knows your rules and what you paste. So it can't bring old facts in. You still check, decide and send.

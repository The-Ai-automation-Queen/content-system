What can I actually build with Claude on a free weekend?

Claude can build small interactive tools, with buttons and lists you can click, straight from a description. Start with three for your home, then try the four after sign-up.

What you leave with

7 build prompts for everyday tools, the one follow-up that fixes most first versions, and what not to put inside them.

How to use this guide: Open Claude and paste one build prompt. Claude builds the tool in the chat. Try it, then ask for one change at a time.

01

Build 1 The family chore rota.

The build:
a weekly chore chart that rotates jobs between people.
Why it's a good first build:
simple lists and buttons, and you'll know straight away if it works.
You describe

4 people · 6 chores · jobs rotate each week · tick a job when it's done.

→
What a good first version looks like

This week's chart with names and chores, a Done button on each, and a "Next week" button that moves everyone along one job.

The honest part Check before you use it.

These are handy personal tools, not secure apps. Don't put bank details, passwords, health information or anyone's address into them, and check whether your tool keeps its data before you rely on it.

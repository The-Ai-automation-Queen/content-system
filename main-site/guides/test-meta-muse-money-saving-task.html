How to Find and Cancel Business Subscriptions I Don't Use

AI finds the repeat payments. You decide what goes.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh Free guide
Shift & Lead

Every subscription in one table

Forgotten apps keep charging your business card. This guide finds every repeat payment and its cost for a year. It takes 30 minutes.

What you end up with

  • A clean copy of your payments, private details out
  • A subscriptions table of every repeat payment
  • The cost for a year of each tool renewing soon
  • A cancel message ready to send

You need your business bank or card account, a spreadsheet app and any AI assistant. The free plans work. Copy each prompt as it is and change the [brackets].

01

Download and clean payments Nothing goes to AI yet.

The AI assistant needs your payments, never your private details. Clean them first.

  1. Log in to your bank in your browser, never in the AI chat.
  2. Open Transactions and click Export or Download.
  3. Choose CSV (a simple spreadsheet file) and the last 12 months.
  4. Open it in your spreadsheet app. Keep only date, payee (who you paid) and amount.
  5. Delete personal payments, card numbers, reference numbers and client names.
  6. Save it as payments-clean.csv.

No AI tool needs your bank login for this. If one asks, close it. Can't find the export? Search "export transactions CSV" on your bank's help pages.

02

Find repeat payments Your subscriptions table.

A long list of payments becomes one short table.

  1. Open your AI assistant and click New chat.
  2. Paste the prompt below.
  3. Add payments-clean.csv with the paperclip or +. No upload? Paste the rows instead.
  4. Press Enter.
Here are my business payments from the last [12] months (date, payee, amount). Private details are removed.

Find every payment that looks like a subscription.

Table: Tool or payee | Amount as shown | How often | Last payment date | Last used

- Use only what is in my payments. Copy each amount exactly.
- Leave "Last used" blank for me.
- If you are not sure a payment repeats, write [CHECK].
- Never guess what a company is.

Payments:
[paste]
  1. Pick 2 rows. Find them in your file with Ctrl + F (or Cmd + F). Do the amounts match?
  2. Paste the table into a spreadsheet called Subscriptions table.
  3. Fill in Last used for each tool.
03

Add up the yearly cost For anything renewing soon.

Starter deals and monthly prices hide the real number. Add up 12 months.

  1. Open the renewal email, or the tool's Billing page.
  2. Copy the words about price, fees and dates. Take out your account number.
  3. Paste them into the prompt below.
Work out what this subscription will cost my business over the next 12 months, with every fee.

Show the sum step by step, so I can check it.
Then list up to 5 questions to ask before it renews.

Use only the text I paste. If a number is missing, write [CHECK].

Renewal wording:
[paste]

Check the sum with a calculator. Add it to your table as Cost for a year.

The honest part: AI can misread a payee and add up wrong. Check every sum. Check every price on the company's own page. You make every choice.

04

Keep, downgrade or cancel Your choice, not the AI's.

Give each tool one choice. Then send the message you've put off.

ChoiceWhen it fits
KeepYou used it this month and it's worth it
DowngradeYou use it, but not every part or user
CancelNot used in a month or two, or another tool does the job
  1. Before you cancel, download any client files or invoices from the tool.
  2. Try the tool's Billing page first. Look for Cancel plan or Change plan.
  3. No button? Paste the prompt below.
Write a short email to [company] asking them to [cancel my plan / move me to a cheaper plan / lower my price].

My facts:
- Account email: [email]
- Plan: [plan name, as on my invoice]
- [any other true fact]

Polite and firm, under 100 words, one clear ask.
Ask for written confirmation by [date].
Use only my facts. If something is missing, write [CHECK].
Never make up names, prices, dates or promises.
  1. Fill every [CHECK], then send it yourself. Save the reply.
  2. Next month, check the payment is gone.
  3. For each tool you keep, add a calendar reminder 2 weeks before it renews.

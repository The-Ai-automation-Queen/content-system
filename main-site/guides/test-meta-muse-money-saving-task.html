Can Muse find a better price without buying for you?

Compare the full cost of the same item from different sellers. Keep checkout and the final decision in your hands.

In this guideWhat cheaper meansTry a price checkAsk MuseCheck the shortlist
01 · Compare fairly

What does “cheaper” include?

The first price you see may leave out delivery, extras or a different return policy. Compare options only when they meet the same must-haves.

Price shown

The item or plan price on the seller’s page.

03 · Get a shortlist

Ask Muse to compare the full cost

If Muse is available to your account, open its official site ↗. The instruction asks you for the item, budget and must-haves before Muse starts. It stops before any login, message, basket or purchase.

Get the price-comparison instruction

Copy the complete brief and keep a link to the checks below.

Complete instruction
Help me compare a purchase. Do not buy anything.

Before searching, ask me for the item or service, my location, maximum total budget and currency, 3 to 5 must-haves, and anything I want excluded. Wait for my reply. Do not replace a must-have with a cheaper feature without asking me.

Find up to 3 public options that meet my answers. For each option, show:
- exact product or plan name;
- seller or provider;
- item price;
- delivery, required extras and recurring charges where shown;
- estimated total cost;
- which requirements it meets or misses;
- return or cancellation terms;
- direct source links and the time checked.

Do not purchase, subscribe, book, add to a basket, sign in, contact a seller or change an account. Mark any missing cost or term “Needs checking”. Recommend nothing until I have opened the sources.

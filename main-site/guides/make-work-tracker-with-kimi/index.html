How to Make a Client Tracker in Excel With AI

AI builds the spreadsheet, then you test it with real mistakes.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh | The AI Automation Queen Free guide
Shift & Lead

Our tasks live in emails and sticky notes. Can AI make us a proper tracker?

Yes, and the file is the easy part. Let the AI ask how your team works before it builds anything, then test the tracker by doing the things real people do: wrong dates, blank owners, "done-ish".

What you leave with

a prompt that designs the tracker around your team, a build prompt, and a 5-minute break test to run before anyone else sees it.

How to use this guide: Use made-up tasks first. Only move real tasks in once the tracker has passed the break test.

01

Job 1 Let it interview you.

The job:
deciding what the tracker needs, before it's built.
Why it works:
a tracker with the wrong columns gets abandoned in a week. Five questions about how your team works get you the right ones.
Questions it might ask

Who updates it? What does "done" mean for you? Do tasks wait on other people? What do you check on Monday?

→
What a good answer looks like

5 to 7 columns you'll actually fill in, each with a reason, and nothing added "just in case".

02

Job 2 Build the file.

The job:
turning the agreed columns into a working Excel file.
Why it works:
dropdowns and real date cells stop the mess that makes trackers useless.
Three practice tasks

Draft the newsletter · Amir · Tuesday" · "Book the photographer · Priya · last Friday" · "Update the price list · no owner yet".

→
What a good file looks like

Status as a dropdown, dates as real dates, "Book the photographer" flagged overdue, the missing owner shown as blank, not invented.

03

Job 3 Try to break it.

The job:
testing the file the way a busy team will use it.
Why it works:
real people type "tomorrow" in a date cell and forget the owner. Better to find out now.
The break test

Type "tomorrow" as a date · leave an owner blank · mark a past task Done · sort by due date · add a row at the bottom.

→
What passing looks like

The date cell refuses "tomorrow" · the blank owner stands out · the overdue flag clears when it's Done · sorting keeps rows together · the new row gets the dropdown too.

04

The honest part Check before you use it.

The tracker won't make anyone update it. Agree who updates it and when (for example, everyone before Monday's meeting), and keep it to the columns people actually use.

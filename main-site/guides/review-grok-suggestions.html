Did Grok spot an error or rewrite your point?

Give Grok a paragraph and a fact sheet. Keep the corrections; decide for yourself about style changes.

Spot the problem first

One sentence cannot stay as written

In the paragraph

“A recording was promised to every participant.”

Try the complete example

Ask Grok for the smallest useful review

  1. Open Grok ↗ and start a new chat.
  2. Copy and send the paragraph, fact sheet and review instruction together.
  3. Compare every suggested factual change with the fact sheet before you keep it.
View the complete instruction and example
Paragraph to review:
The pilot ran from 3 to 14 June. Twelve managers joined. Nine completed the final exercise. The team will decide in July whether to repeat the session. A recording was promised to every participant.

Fact sheet:
- Pilot dates: 3 to 14 June
- Participants: 12
- Completed: 9
- Repeat decision: July
- Recording: discussed, not promised

Use the fact sheet above as the source of truth. Review the paragraph only for:
1. claims that contradict the fact sheet;
2. unclear wording;
3. claims the fact sheet cannot confirm.

For each suggestion, quote the exact sentence, name the problem and propose the smallest possible change. Label a factual conflict as a correction and a wording change as optional. If the fact sheet cannot confirm a claim, flag it for me to check rather than inventing a replacement fact. Do not change the purpose, tone, dates or structure. Do not rewrite the whole paragraph.
Review each suggestion

Correction, option or reject?

Try these three example suggestions, then use the same labels on Grok’s reply.

Replace ‘A recording was promised’ with ‘A recording was discussed, not promised.’

Change ‘Twelve managers joined’ to ‘Twelve managers took part.’

Add ‘The pilot improved team performance.’

Keep only what you can defend. The recording claim must match the fact sheet. A clearer sentence is optional; an invented fact is out.

How to Find and Cancel Business Subscriptions I Don't Use

AI finds the repeat payments; you decide what to cancel. Then it drafts the message you've been putting off.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh | The AI Automation Queen Free guide
Shift & Lead

Every tool you pay for, in one table

Software, apps and memberships add up quietly on a business card. Spotting the same payment every month across three months of transactions is slow for you and quick for AI. This guide finds every repeat payment, shows what each one costs you over a year, and drafts the cancel or negotiate message. You make every decision.

What you end up with

  • A clean transactions file with card numbers and personal details removed
  • Your subscription audit table: every repeat payment, how often it goes out and when you last used it
  • The real yearly cost of each renewal coming up
  • A keep, downgrade or cancel decision for every tool
  • Ready-to-send cancel or negotiate messages, and a reminder before each renewal

You need any AI chat: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Meta AI. The free plans work. Every prompt below can be copied and pasted as it is; replace the parts in [square brackets].

01

Export and clean your transactions Before anything goes in.

The AI can only find what you give it. Three months of business transactions is enough to catch monthly payments; twelve months also catches yearly ones.

  1. Log in to your business bank or card account in your browser, not in the AI chat.
  2. Find the transactions or statements page and look for Export or Download. Choose CSV (a simple spreadsheet file) and the last 3 months.
  3. If you can't find an export option, search your bank's help pages for "export transactions CSV". Most banks offer it; a few only give PDFs, which also work.
  4. Open the file in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers.
  5. Delete every column except date, payee (who was paid) and amount.
  6. Delete any personal payments that slipped onto the business card.
  7. Save it as a new file, for example transactions-clean.csv.
Never pasteRemove firstFine to paste
Login details, passwords or security answersCard and account numbersDate, payee and amount
Screenshots of your banking appYour address and reference numbersThe name of each tool you pay for
Full statements with your details on themClient names in payment referencesYour own notes on what each tool is for

No AI tool needs your bank login to do this. If any app or chat asks for it, stop and close it.

02

Find the repeat payments Your audit table.

This step turns a long list of transactions into one table of everything you pay for again and again.

  1. Start a new chat in your AI tool.
  2. Paste the prompt below, then paste the contents of your clean file where it says [paste]. In most chats you can also attach the file with the paperclip or + button.
Here are my business transactions from the last [3] months (date, payee, amount). I have removed card numbers and personal details.

Find every payment that looks like a subscription or repeat payment.

Give me a table with these columns:
Tool or payee | Amount as shown | How often (monthly, yearly, other) | Last payment date | Last used | Keep, cancel or check

Rules:
- Use only what is in my transactions. Copy each amount exactly as shown.
- Leave "Last used" blank for me to fill in.
- If you are not sure a payment repeats, write [CHECK] in the last column.
- If you don't recognise a payee, write the name exactly as shown and [CHECK]. Never guess what a company is.
- Put one-off payments in a short separate list under the table.

Transactions:
[paste]
  1. Copy the table into a spreadsheet. This is your subscription audit table.
  2. Fill in Last used for each row from memory, or by logging in to the tool.
  3. Look up every [CHECK] row yourself. Search your email for the payee name to find the receipt.

03

Work out the real yearly cost For anything renewing soon.

A renewal email often shows a monthly price, an intro deal or a per-seat price (a price for each user). Adding up twelve months shows the real number before you decide.

Copy the wording from the renewal email or the tool's billing page, then paste it with this prompt.

Work out what this subscription will cost my business over the next 12 months, including every fee mentioned.

Show the sum step by step, so I can check it.

Then list up to 5 questions I should ask before it renews, for example: what happens when an intro price ends, whether there is a cheaper plan, whether I pay for users I don't have, and whether there is a cancellation fee.

Use only what is in the text I paste. If a number is missing, write [CHECK] instead of guessing.

Renewal wording:
[paste the renewal email or billing page text]
  1. Check the sum once with a calculator. AI can add up wrong.
  2. Add the yearly cost as a new column in your audit table.
04

Decide: keep, downgrade or cancel Your call, not the AI's.

Go through your audit table one row at a time and give each tool one of three decisions.

DecisionWhen it fitsWhat you do next
KeepYou used it this month and it earns its placeNothing. Set a renewal reminder (step 06)
DowngradeYou use it, but not every feature or every seatMove to a cheaper plan, or ask for a better price (step 05)
CancelYou haven't used it in the last month or two, or another tool does the same jobExport anything you need from it, then cancel (step 05)
  • Before you cancel, download any client files, contacts or invoices stored in the tool.
  • Two tools doing one job (two schedulers, two design apps)? Keep the one you open most.
  • Not sure? Mark it Downgrade or check again in a month. You don't have to decide everything today.

Add a Decision column to your audit table and fill it in for every row.

05

Write the cancel or negotiate message The one you've put off.

Many tools let you cancel in the account settings in two clicks. Look there first. For the rest, or when you want a better price, AI drafts a short, firm, polite message.

Write a short email to [company] asking them to [cancel my plan / move me to a cheaper plan / lower my price].

My facts:
- Account name or email: [the email the account is under]
- Plan: [plan name, as shown on my invoice]
- [any other fact: how long I have been a customer, the price I have seen elsewhere, the date it renews]

Rules:
- Polite and firm, under 100 words, one clear ask.
- Ask for written confirmation, and a reply by [date].
- Use only my facts. Never invent a competitor price, a length of time or a reason I did not give you.
- Write [CHECK] where a fact is missing.

Draft only. I will read it and send it myself.
  1. Fill in every [CHECK] mark and read the message once out loud.
  2. Send it from your own email, or paste it into the tool's support chat.
  3. Save the confirmation reply. Check your next statement to make sure the payment stopped.
06

Set renewal reminders So it doesn't creep back.

Yearly renewals are the easiest to miss. One calendar reminder before each one gives you time to decide.

  1. For every tool you kept, find its next renewal date in your audit table or its billing page.
  2. In your calendar, add an event 2 weeks before each date, called "Renewal: [tool]".
  3. In the event notes, write the yearly cost and one question: "Did I use this last month?"
  4. Put one more event in your calendar 3 months from today: "Run the subscription audit again".

The honest part: AI can misread a payee, add up wrong and can't see offers you haven't told it about. Check the sums once with a calculator, confirm any price on the company's own page, and make every keep or cancel decision yourself.

What happens when you use an AI tool?

AI model

In ChatGPT, you open the app and type a message. An AI model behind the app produces the reply.

Generative AI

Ask ChatGPT to turn rough notes into a first draft. Creating that new text is generative AI at work.

LLM

Large language model. It is the kind of model that lets ChatGPT understand a question and write a reply in everyday language.

Chatbot or AI assistant

The message box and reply you see in ChatGPT make it an AI assistant. Some assistants can also search or use connected apps.

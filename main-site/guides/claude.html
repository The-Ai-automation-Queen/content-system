Know the players / Anthropic

Use Claude for complex work and long documents

Claude AI is Anthropic’s assistant. It is especially useful when you need AI to read a lot, keep track of context and help you think through a problem.

  • Best forLong documents, research, complex writing and work that takes several rounds.

What is Claude AI?

Claude is an AI assistant made by Anthropic.

You can use it to write, analyse files, summarise information, work with code and think through problems in conversation.

Claude is often most useful when the job contains a lot of source material.

PICTURE IT

You put a pile of reports, notes and documents on the table. Claude helps you read across the pile without losing the thread.

What is Claude best used for?

  • Long documents. Reports, policies, proposals, transcripts and contracts.
  • Comparing information. Ask what changed between versions or where sources disagree.
  • Complex writing. Keep a long draft coherent while you improve it over several rounds.
  • Research synthesis. Pull themes and open questions from several sources.
  • Code. Claude Code is designed for developers who want AI to work across a software project.

Why is Claude good with long documents?

Claude is known for handling a large amount of context well.

Context is the information the AI can keep in front of it while it works.

That matters when the answer depends on several files, earlier instructions or a long conversation.

PICTURE IT

A small context window is a small desk. A larger one gives the AI more room to keep the important papers in view.

Claude vs ChatGPT: what is the difference?

Claude and ChatGPT overlap a lot. Both can write, analyse files and help with research.

ChatGPT is a broad all-rounder with strong image, web and general-purpose features.

Claude is especially worth testing when the work is long, document-heavy or needs careful reading across several sources.

You do not need to choose one forever. Give both the same real task and compare how much editing and checking each answer needs.

What should you try first with Claude?

Choose three documents you already understand well.

Ask Claude:

“What changed between these? Where do they contradict each other? What important question is still unanswered?”

Because you already know the material, you can judge the quality of the answer.

Can Claude AI be wrong?

Yes. Claude can misread a source, invent a fact or give a polished answer that is still wrong.

Check dates, numbers, quotations, legal claims and anything that could cost you money or trust.

When you are making a decision, ask Claude for the strongest case against your current view. That helps reduce simple agreement with the way you framed the question.

What is Claude Code?

Claude Code is a coding tool from Anthropic that can read and work across a software project.

If you do not build software, you can ignore it. It does not make Claude more useful for normal email, documents or everyday business tasks.

Who should use Claude?

Claude is worth trying if your work involves long documents, research, strategy, policy, proposals, complex writing or code.

If most of your needs are quick questions and simple everyday tasks, the difference may feel small.

My take on Claude

Claude earns its place when the job is not just “give me an answer.”

It is strongest when the job is closer to “read all of this, keep the important parts in view, and help me think.”

Use it for that kind of work and keep checking the facts that matter.

01

What the technology is

01

AI

Artificial Intelligence

Software that can recognise patterns, create content or recommend an answer using information it has been given.

A proposal that promises an “AI customer service system” is describing a category, not what the system will actually do.

Ask which task the AI performs, what information it uses and who approves the result.

02

Generative AI

AI that creates new text, images, audio, video or code from an instruction.

Use it to draft a client email, produce 3 campaign concepts or turn meeting notes into a first action list.

Treat the output as a draft. Check facts, tone, rights and confidential information before it leaves the business.

03

LLM

Large Language Model

The language engine inside tools such as ChatGPT and Claude. It produces a response by working out which words are likely to come next.

A vendor may say its product uses a more powerful LLM. That does not prove the product will improve your workflow.

Judge the tool on the work it completes accurately, not the model name in the sales pitch.

02

What happens in the chat

04

Prompt

The request and information you give an AI so it knows what work to produce.

“Write a proposal” leaves the AI guessing. Add the client, goal, evidence, limits and required format to give it a usable brief.

Write the prompt as if you were briefing a capable person who does not know your business yet.

05

Context window

The limit on how much of your conversation, instructions and attached material an AI can consider at once.

In a long project chat, an instruction agreed at the beginning can be pushed out or overlooked later.

Start a clean chat with the current brief and approved source material when the work begins to drift.

06

Hallucination

A name, number, quote, source or other detail that AI invents and presents as true.

A polished market report can contain a study that does not exist or a statistic the source never published.

Open the original source and verify every important claim before you send, publish or decide from it.

03

How AI completes work

07

AI agent

An AI system that works through several steps and uses tools to pursue a goal, instead of returning only 1 answer.

An inbox agent might identify an urgent client email, draft a reply and create a follow-up task.

Require approval before it sends, deletes, spends, publishes or changes a customer record.

08

Automation

A rule that makes software complete the same step whenever a specific event happens.

When someone requests this guide, an automation can save the contact, deliver the PDF and start the correct email sequence.

Define the trigger, the expected result and what should happen when the automation fails.

09

Connector

A link that lets an AI tool read information from another app or take an action inside it. MCP, short for Model Context Protocol, is 1 way tools create these links.

A calendar connector could check availability and create an event. An email connector could also expose private messages.

Grant only the access required for the task and remove it when the connection is no longer needed.

10

RAG

Retrieval-Augmented Generation

A method that lets AI search information you choose before answering, such as your policies, product documents or client files.

A team assistant can search the current company handbook before answering a question about annual leave.

Keep the source material current and require the answer to show exactly which document it used.

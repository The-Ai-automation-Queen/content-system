10 AI words you need to know

Understand the words that come up in AI tools, meetings and sales pitches without learning a new language.

You do not need a technical vocabulary. You need to know what people are actually talking about, what changes for you and when to ask another question.

01

Words inside the chat

These 5 explain what happens when you ask an AI tool for something.

01

LLM

Large Language Model

The engine inside tools such as ChatGPT and Claude. It reads your words and predicts a useful response.

If someone says, “We need a better LLM,” they mean the underlying model, not the chat window around it.

Watch: It can produce a confident answer without knowing whether that answer is true.

02

Prompt

The instruction you give an AI. It can be a question, a task or a set of rules.

“Summarise this document in 5 bullets for a client” is a prompt.

03

Tokens

The small pieces of text an AI reads and writes. Tools use them to measure usage, limits and sometimes cost.

More pages, longer chats and bigger answers use more tokens.

04

Context window

The amount of information an AI can work with at one time.

When a long chat starts forgetting details, its working space may be too crowded. A fresh chat can help.

05

Hallucination

A made-up answer presented as if it were true.

A fake source, wrong date or invented product feature can all be hallucinations.

Watch: Check important names, numbers, dates and sources before using the answer.

02

Words behind the tools

These explain how AI learns, creates and connects to your other apps.

06

Machine learning

A way for computers to find patterns by learning from examples instead of following one fixed rule for every case.

A spam filter learns from emails marked “spam” and “not spam.”

07

Generative AI

AI that creates new text, images, audio, video or code from your instructions.

ChatGPT writing an email and an image tool making a poster are both generative AI.

08

MCP and connectors

Model Context Protocol

Ways to let an AI reach information or take actions outside the chat.

A connector might let an AI read your calendar, search a folder or create a task.

Watch: More access creates more risk. Check what the AI can see and change.

03

Words people use when they talk about what comes next

One is an idea about the future. The other is something people are doing now.

09

AGI

Artificial General Intelligence

The idea of an AI that could learn and handle almost any thinking task a person can do.

AGI does not exist today. It is usually what people mean when they discuss human-level AI.

10

Vibe coding

Building software by describing what you want while an AI writes much of the code.

You describe a booking tool, test what the AI builds and ask for changes in normal language.

Watch: Easy to build does not mean safe to publish. Test the result before real people use it.

5 words to recognise next

Agent

AI that can choose and complete several steps toward a goal.

RAG

A method that lets AI search selected documents before answering.

Fine-tuning

Extra training for a model on a specific style or type of work.

Multimodal

AI that can work with text, images, audio or video together.

Open-source model

A model people can download and run under the rules of its licence.

Keep it nearby

Do not let an AI term derail the conversation

Copy the 10-word reference and check a definition when a tool, proposal or meeting uses language you do not know.

That is enough vocabulary to follow most everyday AI conversations. When a new term appears, ask what it does, what it can access and what decision it changes.

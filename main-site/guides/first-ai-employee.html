PUT AI TO WORK

Your first AI worker should have one job.

Do not start by automating your business. Start by handing AI one repeatable task you already understand.

What is an AI worker?

An AI worker is an AI system set up to do one repeatable job for your business.

It might sort an inbox, prepare a report, qualify a lead or turn meeting notes into actions.

PICTURE IT

Input → AI does the repeatable steps → you review or decide → output.

Step 1: choose one repeatable task

Pick something you already do the same way again and again.

Good first tasks have a clear beginning, a clear end and very little judgment in the middle.

  • Sort incoming email
  • Prepare a weekly summary
  • Check new leads against a list
  • Turn a transcript into action items

Do not start with the most important decision in the company. Start with the work around the decision.

Step 2: write the AI job description

Before you choose tools, write the job in plain English.

JobWhat is the AI responsible for?
InputWhat information does it receive?
OutputWhat should come back?
RulesWhat must always be true?
Never doWhat needs your approval or must stay off-limits?

COPY THIS QUESTION

What would I tell a new person before I trusted them with this job?

Step 3: give AI the minimum access it needs

Permissions decide what the AI can technically see or do.

If the job only needs to read email and create drafts, do not give it permission to send or delete.

PICTURE IT

A locked door is stronger than a note that says “please do not enter.”

Step 4: decide what starts the job

A useful AI worker should not depend on you remembering to open a chat.

Give the job a trigger.

  • Every weekday at 7am
  • When a new lead arrives
  • After a meeting ends
  • When a file lands in a folder

Step 5: keep a human stopping point

Decide where the AI has to stop and hand the work back to you.

Examples:

  • Before sending an email
  • Before spending money
  • Before deleting anything
  • Before making a decision about a person

A GOOD FIRST RULE

AI prepares. You approve.

How do you know if the AI worker is working?

Run it beside your normal process for a few days.

Compare the result with what you would have done.

When something is wrong, do not only fix the output. Fix the instruction, example, rule or permission that caused the mistake.

What should the AI worker report back?

A good system should tell you:

  • What arrived
  • What it completed
  • What needs your decision
  • What failed
  • What it was unsure about

A simple AI worker example

INBOX WORKER

New email → AI sorts it → AI drafts if needed → AI adds it to your daily summary → you decide what gets sent.

The result to aim for

The goal is not to remove yourself from the business.

The goal is to remove yourself from repeatable steps that never needed you.

Give AI one job. Keep the judgment.

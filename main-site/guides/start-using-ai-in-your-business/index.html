How to Start Using AI in Your Business

Set ChatGPT up once. Then let it do one job for you.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh Free guide
Shift & Lead

ChatGPT that knows your business

ChatGPT gives vague answers when it knows nothing about you. This guide teaches it your business and builds your first AI assistant. It takes about 45 minutes.

What you end up with

  • ChatGPT set up safely, with 3 rules for what goes in
  • A Business Profile ChatGPT reads before every answer
  • A task list: 5 jobs worth handing over
  • Your first AI assistant for job number 1
  • One real piece of work, done and checked

You need a ChatGPT account. The free plan works. Copy each prompt as it is and change the [brackets].

01

Set up ChatGPT safely Before anything goes in.

Do this once. It keeps your clients safe in every chat.

  1. Go to chatgpt.com ↗ and sign in.
  2. Click your name or picture, then Settings.
  3. Open Data Controls.
  4. Switch off Improve the model for everyone. Your chats no longer train ChatGPT.

Then keep these 3 rules every time you paste something in.

Never pasteAsk firstFine to paste
Passwords, card and bank detailsA client's emails or filesYour own notes, names removed
Health or personal details about anyoneContracts with names in themYour website text
Anything secret by contractDetails about your teamMade-up examples

Can't find it? OpenAI moves this menu. Search "data controls" on OpenAI's help pages ↗.

02

Write your Business Profile What ChatGPT needs to know.

Your Business Profile tells ChatGPT who you are and who you help. Without it, every answer is vague.

Interview me to write my Business Profile.

Ask me these one at a time and wait for each answer:
1. What does my business do, in one sentence?
2. Who are my clients, and what do they usually need help with?
3. What do I sell, and what does a typical project or package include?
4. How do I like to communicate: tone, words I use, words I never use?
5. What do I spend most of my week on?

Then write my Business Profile in two short parts:
Part 1: What you should know about me and my business (under 1,000 characters)
Part 2: How you should respond to me (under 1,000 characters)

Use only what I tell you. Plain English, no em dashes.
  1. Read it and fix anything wrong.
  2. Click your name or picture, then Settings, then Personalization.
  3. Open Custom instructions.
  4. Paste Part 1 in the box about you. Paste Part 2 in the box about how ChatGPT should respond. Click Save.
  5. Start a new chat and ask: "What do you know about my business?"

Can't find it? Search "custom instructions" on OpenAI's help pages ↗. Or paste your profile as the first message of each chat.

03

List your jobs Find what to hand over.

Don't guess what to use AI for. Let ChatGPT ask about your week first.

Using my Business Profile, interview me about my working week.

Ask me one question at a time, up to 8 questions, about:
- the tasks I repeat every week
- how long each one takes
- which ones I put off or dread

Then give me a table with these columns:
Task | Hours per week | What AI could draft or prepare | What I must still do myself | Risk if AI gets it wrong (low, medium, high)

Rank the table: most hours saved and lowest risk first.
Under the table, name the one task I should hand over first, and why, in two sentences.
Good first jobsLeave for later
Follow-up emails after callsAnything you sign
First drafts of proposalsLegal, money or health advice
Answers to common questionsMessages sent without you reading them
Short notes on long filesDecisions about people

Save the list in your notes. Hand over one new job each week.

04

Build your AI assistant Job number 1, set up once.

An AI assistant is saved instructions for one job. You write them once and reuse them.

Write instructions for an AI assistant that does this task for me: [task number 1 from my task list].

Use my Business Profile. The instructions must say:
1. What I will paste in each time (for example: call notes, a client email, a document)
2. The steps to follow, in order
3. Exactly what to give back: format, length, tone
4. Rules: use only what I paste; write [CHECK] where information is missing instead of guessing; never make up names, prices, dates or promises

Keep it under 1,500 characters, written as instructions to the AI assistant.
  1. See Projects in the left sidebar? Click New project and name it after the job.
  2. Open the project's instructions, paste them in and save.
  3. No Projects? Save the instructions in your notes. Paste them as the first message each time.
05

Run it on real work Then check it.

Open your project. Paste this week's real notes, with names swapped for [brackets].

  1. Check every name, number and date is in what you pasted.
  2. Delete any promise you did not make.
  3. Fill in every [CHECK] yourself.
  4. Ask: would you send this with your name on it?

One part wrong? Fix it in the same chat.

Change this part: [paste the line].
Why it's wrong: [what's actually true].
Keep everything else the same.

Same fix twice? Add it to the AI assistant's instructions as a new rule.

06

Add one job a week Small and checked.

Each week, pick the next job on your list. Build its AI assistant with step 04.

The honest part: AI drafts. You check, decide and send. That is why it works.

Should you use Meta AI?

Use Meta AI for quick questions, voice help and creative work inside apps you already use. Do not treat a convenient chat inside WhatsApp or Instagram as a private place for sensitive information.

Yes, for quick everyday help and creative tasks inside Meta's apps. It is easy to reach, but that convenience is not a reason to share private messages, customer data or confidential work.

Choose ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot first when you need a dedicated work space, detailed document controls or company approval.

Choose where to use it

01

WhatsApp and Messenger

Ask a quick question, create an image or get help inside a conversation. Check what context is being sent to Meta AI.

02

Instagram and Facebook

Use Meta AI for search, ideas and creative work close to the content you are viewing or making.

03

Meta AI app and web

Use voice or text in a separate assistant. Personalisation may use information you chose to share across connected Meta accounts.

04

Incognito Chat

Use it when available for a private, temporary Meta AI conversation. It is rolling out gradually and may not appear in every account yet.

Start without oversharing

Treat Meta AI as a separate participant, even when it appears inside a private messaging app.

  1. Open Meta AI

    Go to meta.ai, open the Meta AI app or select the Meta AI icon inside a supported Meta app.

    Open Meta AI ↗

  2. Use a harmless first task

    Ask for ideas, a public explanation or help rewriting text that contains no private information.

  3. Check before sharing

    Nothing is posted to the Discover feed unless you choose to share it. Still check the audience before publishing any result.

  4. Use Incognito Chat when available

    Select the Incognito option in WhatsApp or the Meta AI app for a temporary conversation that is not saved. Availability is still rolling out.

What to keep outside

01

Private conversations

Do not copy another person's messages into Meta AI without their knowledge.

02

Customer and employee data

Names, contact details, account information and private records do not belong in a casual AI chat.

03

Passwords and financial details

Never share login codes, payment details or identity documents.

04

Confidential work

Use an approved business tool and account instead of moving company information into a personal Meta account.

The advanced route

Developers can build with Meta's Llama models, but that is a different decision from using Meta AI in an app.

You need a technical owner, hosting choice, security review and clear data rules before running an open model for business work.

The Meta AI app is a consumer assistant. Llama deployment is a technical project.

Use Meta AI for quick, low-stakes help inside familiar apps. Choose a work-focused tool when the task needs controlled files, company information or a longer working space.

Keep it for convenience, not for information that needs stronger control.

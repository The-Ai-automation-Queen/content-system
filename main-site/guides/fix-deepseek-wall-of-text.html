Introduction

DeepSeek · 10 min read · Beginner

Ask for layout changes only, set your layout rules at the start of every chat, and then make the AI list anything it changed besides line breaks.

A guide by Fatiha Chikh | The AI Automation Queen

What you leave with: a layout-only fix, a house-style note to paste at the start of every writing chat, and a check that catches sneaky rewrites.

01

How to use this guideIn 30 seconds.

Keep your original text saved somewhere else before you start. Do job 1 on the block you have now, then set up job 2 for next time.

02

Job 1Break up the block.

The job:
one wall of text that should be paragraphs and dialogue.
Why it works:
if you only say "make it readable", the AI may rewrite. Saying "layout only" and naming what counts as layout keeps your words yours.
The wall

The rain had stopped by the time Lena reached the station. 'You're late,' Marc said. 'The train was,' she said, and sat down without looking at him. Neither of them mentioned the letter.

What a good answer looks like

Four short paragraphs. Each speaker on a new line. Every word the same.

Job 3Prove nothing else changed.

The job:
being sure the AI didn't quietly "improve" a line.
Why it works:
asking the AI to compare the two versions word by word catches changes you'd miss when reading.
What you paste

The honest partCheck before you use it.

No prompt guarantees the AI won't touch a word. That's why job 3 exists. For anything you plan to publish, run the check, or compare the two versions in a document tool.

Job 1: The layout-only prompt

Change the layout of this text only.

Layout means: paragraph breaks, and a new line whenever someone new speaks. Nothing else.

Keep every word, name, punctuation mark and line of dialogue exactly as it is. Don't add, cut or continue anything.

Return only the text.

Text: [paste]

Job 2: The house-style note (paste at the start of each writing chat)

House style for everything in this chat:
- Short paragraphs, one moment each.
- A new line for each speaker.
- No headings, bullet points or bold in fiction.
- [Your spelling, for example British English].

If you're editing my text, change only what I ask for. If you're unsure whether something counts, ask me first.

Job 3: The difference check prompt

Compare these two versions word by word.

List every difference that isn't a line break or paragraph break: the line, the original words and the new words.

If the only differences are line breaks, say "Only line breaks changed."

New version: [paste]

Is It Safe to Connect ChatGPT to My Gmail?

Give AI only what the job needs. Often one file is enough.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh Free guide
Shift & Lead

Give AI only what it needs

Connecting Gmail lets AI see your whole inbox. This guide shows how little access each job needs, and how to remove old links. It takes 25 minutes.

What you end up with

  • The smallest access picked for each job
  • One job done with a single uploaded file
  • Any permission screen explained in plain English
  • An AI access list, with old links removed

You need a ChatGPT account. The free plan works. Copy each prompt as it is and change the [brackets].

01

Pick the smallest access Decide before you connect.

Your inbox holds contracts, invoices and password links. A connection lets AI see all of it.

LevelGood forWhat AI can see
1. Paste one emailReplying to one emailOnly what you paste
2. Upload one fileChecking one proposalOnly that file
3. Connect one app, read onlyFinding every email from one clientEverything in that app
4. Let it send or deleteRarely needed. Do it yourselfEverything, and it can act

Write down 3 jobs you do each week. Give each the lowest level that works.

02

Upload one file Nothing else gets opened.

Most jobs need one file, not your whole inbox. Try it now.

  1. Open ChatGPT ↗ and click New chat.
  2. Click the + next to the chat box, then Add photos and files.
  3. Pick one file with client names swapped for brackets.
  4. Paste the prompt below and press Enter.
Use only the file I uploaded.

Sum up what [client] asked for and what I need to reply to, in 5 short lines.
If something is not in the file, write [CHECK].
Never make up names, prices, dates or promises.

The job is done, and nothing else in your account was opened.

03

Read the permission screen Before you click Allow.

The permission screen says what the tool can do. Read it before you say yes.

  1. Start connecting the app. Stop at the permission screen.
  2. Copy the words on the screen. Leave out your email address.
  3. Open a new chat and paste the prompt below.
I want to connect [AI tool] to [app, for example: Gmail] for this job: [the job].

Here is the permission screen:
[paste the text]

Explain each permission in plain English.
Say if it can read, send, change, delete or share.
Then tell me if pasting or uploading one file would do the job instead.
Use only the text I pasted. If something is unclear, write [CHECK].
  1. Check the screen yourself for send, delete or share.
  2. You still need it? Click Allow. If not, click Cancel and paste or upload instead.
04

Remove old links In two places.

You may have connected apps months ago. Removing one in ChatGPT may not remove it from Google.

  1. In ChatGPT, click your name, then Settings, then Apps. Write down each app.
  2. Open your Google connections page ↗. Write down each app there too.
  3. Paste your list into the prompt below.
Help me build my AI access list.

Here are my connections:
[list each one, for example: ChatGPT connected to Gmail]

For each one, ask me what I use it for and when I last used it. One question at a time.
Then make a table: Connection | What it can see | Keep or remove | Why.
Remove anything not used in the last month, or that I don't know.
Use only what I tell you. If you don't know what an app can see, write [CHECK].
  1. For each remove line, click the app in ChatGPT Settings, Apps, then Disconnect.
  2. On your Google page, click the app, then Delete all connections.
  3. Refresh both pages and check the app is gone.

Found an app you don't know? Remove it, then change your email password. Can't find Apps? See OpenAI's connected apps page ↗.

The honest part: a connected AI reads whatever it is allowed to. The less it can see, the less can go wrong. Check your list every 3 months.

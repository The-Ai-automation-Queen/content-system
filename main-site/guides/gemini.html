KNOW THE PLAYERS / GOOGLE

Gemini gets more useful when your business already lives inside Google.

Gemini can write, analyse and research like other major AI assistants. Its real advantage is how closely it can sit beside Gmail, Drive, Docs and the rest of Google Workspace.

What is Google Gemini?

Gemini is Google's family of AI models and its main AI assistant.

You can use Gemini to ask questions, write, analyse files, work with images and research information.

Google also puts Gemini features inside products such as Gmail, Docs and other Workspace tools.

PICTURE IT

Gemini is most interesting when the information you need is already sitting in Google's rooms.

What is Gemini best used for?

  • Gmail. Summarise threads and prepare replies.
  • Google Docs. Draft, rewrite and work with existing documents.
  • Google Drive. Find and use information stored across files you can access.
  • Research. Work with current information and large sets of sources.
  • Images and multimodal tasks. Gemini can work with more than text.

What is Google Workspace Gemini?

Google Workspace is the set of business tools that includes Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Meet.

When Gemini is available inside those tools, you can work with AI closer to the place where the information already lives.

PICTURE IT

Instead of carrying every file to a separate AI chat, the assistant can work closer to the filing cabinet.

What is NotebookLM?

NotebookLM is a Google tool built around sources you provide.

You add documents, notes or other source material. NotebookLM helps you ask questions and explore that material.

It is especially useful when you want the AI to stay focused on a known set of sources instead of answering from broad general knowledge.

Gemini vs ChatGPT: what is the difference?

Both tools can handle general writing, questions, files and research.

Gemini has a clear reason to try it if your business already uses Google Workspace heavily.

ChatGPT can feel more like a broad standalone workbench. Gemini can feel more useful when the answer depends on Gmail, Drive or Google documents.

Gemini vs Claude: what is different?

Claude is often strong for long documents and careful reading.

Gemini becomes interesting when the job depends on Google's ecosystem or when you want to work across Google sources and tools.

Again, the right test is a real task from your own work.

What should you try first with Gemini?

Choose one job that already lives inside Google.

Examples:

  • Summarise a long Gmail thread and list open questions
  • Compare three Docs and show what changed
  • Use a Drive folder to prepare a briefing
  • Add several sources to NotebookLM and ask what they agree and disagree on

Where can Gemini go wrong?

Gemini can still misread information, invent details or give you a polished answer that needs checking.

Connections also make permissions important. AI should not become an excuse to ignore who can access which files.

Who should use Google Gemini?

Gemini is worth a serious look if your business already runs on Gmail, Docs, Drive and Meet.

If your work is spread across many other systems, the Workspace advantage may matter less.

My take on Gemini

Do not choose an AI tool only because one model appears slightly better in a test.

Choose the tool that can do useful work where your information already lives.

For a Google-first business, that is the strongest reason to care about Gemini.

Should you use Gemini?

Use Gemini when your work already lives in Google and you want AI closer to Gmail, Drive, Docs or Calendar. Start with the Gemini app, then connect only the Google services the task genuinely needs.

Yes, Gemini is worth trying when Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Calendar are already where your work happens. Its main advantage is being close to the Google tools and information you use.

Start with the free Gemini app and one simple task. Do not connect your Google account data until you understand the activity setting, permissions and information involved.

Choose the part you actually need

01

Gemini app

Use gemini.google.com for questions, drafts, research, files, images and everyday tasks. This is where beginners should start.

02

Gemini in Google Workspace

Use Gemini inside Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides or Meet when your account and organisation include it. This keeps the help closer to the work.

03

NotebookLM

Use NotebookLM when you want answers and summaries based on sources you choose, such as reports, PDFs and notes.

04

AI Studio and Gemini CLI

Use AI Studio to test prompts or build with the Gemini API. Use Gemini CLI from a terminal for technical and coding work. Most beginners can ignore both for now.

Match Gemini to the job

01

Find work in Google

Ask Gemini to find or summarise information from a connected Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Keep or Tasks account when the connection is available and approved.

02

Work with a file

Attach a document, image or spreadsheet and ask one exact question. Check the answer against the original file.

03

Research current information

Ask for current information and open the sources shown beneath the answer before you rely on it.

04

Talk through an idea

Use Gemini Live when speaking is easier than typing. Review the transcript and any actions before you keep or send them.

Check the account and activity setting first

Personal and work accounts do not use the same controls.

  1. Open Gemini

    Go to gemini.google.com and sign in with the Google account you intend to use.

    Open Gemini ↗

  2. Review personal activity

    On a personal account, open Settings & help → Activity. You can review, delete or turn off Keep Activity. When it is off, future chats are not used to train Google's AI models, but many Connected Apps become unavailable.

  3. Use a Temporary Chat when suitable

    Use a Temporary Chat when you do not want the conversation saved in your activity. It does not turn a personal account into an approved place for confidential work.

  4. Check work or school controls

    On a work or school account, your Google Workspace administrator controls availability, retention and which apps Gemini can use.

Connect Google apps deliberately

A connection can expose email, files, calendar information or other account data to the task. Connect only what you need.

  1. Open Connected Apps

    On a personal account, open Settings & help → Personal Intelligence → Connected Apps. Choose individual apps rather than selecting Connect all.

  2. Use the correct Google account

    For work or school, sign in to Gemini with the same account used for Google Workspace. Your administrator must have enabled the app.

  3. Call the app into the chat

    Type @ in the message box and select Gmail, Drive, Calendar or another available app. Then name the file, email or task Gemini should use.

  4. Check the source

    Open the source shown beneath Gemini's response. It can select an older email or misunderstand which file matters.

  5. Disconnect when finished

    Return to Connected Apps and turn the app off. Disconnecting does not delete information already stored in Gemini Apps Activity, so review and delete that activity separately if needed.

The advanced route

Stop here unless you are building software or have technical help.

  1. Use Google AI Studio to experiment

    Open AI Studio to test prompts, models and tools. An API key may be created for you, so treat it like a password and never place it in public code.

    Open Google AI Studio ↗

  2. Use Gemini CLI from a terminal

    Gemini CLI can work with files and code on your computer. It requires a terminal, Node.js and a Google sign-in or API setup.

    Open the Gemini CLI guide ↗

  3. Keep the first technical task reversible

    Use a copy of the project, review every file change and do not connect production systems until a technical owner has checked the setup.

    Go to gemini.google.com and start a new chat.

    Open Gemini ↗

  2. Copy and paste

    Select Copy, paste the instruction and replace the bracketed text.

You now know where Gemini fits

Use the Gemini app for one task, Workspace features when the work already lives in Google, NotebookLM for selected sources and AI Studio or Gemini CLI only for technical work.

Keep Gemini if working closer to your Google tools saves more time than the connection and checking create.

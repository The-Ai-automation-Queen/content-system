Get the Kimi test instruction

Copy the three-task tracker test, then check the file and your credit use before paying.

Complete instruction
Create one editable Excel task tracker for a fictional team launch. Use exactly these 3 tasks:

1. Finalise launch copy | Owner: Marketing | Due: 2 business days from today | Status: In progress
2. Approve visuals | Owner: Design | Due: 4 business days from today | Status: Waiting
3. Test the signup form | Owner: Operations | Due: 6 business days from today | Status: Not started

Make one sheet called Tasks with columns Task, Owner, Due date, Status and Overdue. Use real spreadsheet dates. Add a Status dropdown with Not started, In progress, Waiting and Done. Overdue should calculate Yes only when the due date is before today and Status is not Done; otherwise show No. Use a formula, not typed results.

Keep the file editable and leave room for more tasks. Do not invent additional tasks or people. Show me a preview and give me the .xlsx file. Before finishing, check that all 3 rows are present and that changing a task to Done removes any overdue flag. If a dropdown or formula does not work, tell me plainly.

01 · See an example

Protect what already works

This story needs a clearer transition between Mara’s line and Ben’s mistake. The brief names what must stay.

Characters

Mara and Ben. Ben gave Mara the train time.

Facts

Mara arrives at 7.15. The last train has gone. Ben copied the Sunday timetable. They have 20 minutes to reach the ferry on foot.

Voice

Third-person past tense. Restrained tone.

Change wanted

Make the transition from Mara's line to Ben's timetable mistake easier to follow.

Original passage

“You said it left at half past,” she told Ben, who checked the silent departure board. He had copied the Sunday timetable by mistake.

One possible revision

“You said it left at half past,” Mara told Ben. Ben checked the silent departure board. He had copied the Sunday timetable by mistake.

Mara’s line now stands apart from Ben’s action. The timetable mistake and the restrained voice stay. Only this passage needed editing.

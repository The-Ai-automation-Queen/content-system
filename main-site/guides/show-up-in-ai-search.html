How to show up in AI search results

There is no button that adds your business to every AI answer. You can make it easier for search systems to find, understand and verify what you do.

You cannot force an AI tool to recommend your business. You can make your public information clearer, easier to find and easier to verify.

Start with useful pages that answer real questions and keep your important business facts accurate across your website and public profiles.

Give search systems something public to find

Who you help

Name the type of person or business the service is designed for.

What you do

Describe each service or product in specific, everyday language.

Where you work

Make locations, markets and delivery boundaries clear where they matter.

Why the facts are trustworthy

Use evidence, examples, named responsibility and clear contact information.

Answer the questions people actually ask

Create pages that help someone choose a service, understand a process, check what is included or decide whether the offer fits them.

Do not publish vague articles simply to produce more content. A useful page should answer its title and leave the reader able to make a decision.

Make important claims easy to verify

A search system has more to work with when the page is clear and the evidence agrees.

  • Show instructions

    Give every page a concise title that accurately describes its answer.

  • Show instructions

    Name services, products, dates, people and locations where they help the reader decide.

  • Show instructions

    Connect related pages and include relevant examples, sources or proof.

  • Show instructions

    Update conflicting names, descriptions, offers and contact details.

  • Show instructions

    Credible interviews, references and public mentions can help confirm that the business exists and does the work it describes.

Do not flood the website with AI-written pages

More pages do not automatically create more visibility. Thin, repeated pages create noise for readers and search systems.

Publish when the page answers a real question, adds evidence or makes an important fact easier to find.

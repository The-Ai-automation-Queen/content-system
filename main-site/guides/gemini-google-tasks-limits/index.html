How to Plan My Week With Gemini

Type your whole week. Gemini turns it into tasks with dates.

Start the guide ↓
Fatiha Chikh Free guide
Shift & Lead

Your week on one list

Half your week lives in your head. This guide puts it in Google Tasks with real dates. It takes 25 minutes.

What you end up with

  • Gemini linked to your Google Tasks
  • One task list with only the dates you gave
  • This week's tasks, shown any morning in one line
  • No late tasks left on Friday

You need a Google account and Gemini. The free plan works on many accounts. Copy each prompt as it is and change the [brackets].

01

Link Gemini to Tasks Done once.

Gemini can't see your tasks until you switch them on. Use your work Google account.

  1. Open Gemini ↗. Check your work account is in the top right.
  2. Click Settings & help, then Apps (on some accounts, Extensions).
  3. Switch on Google Tasks.
  4. In the chat box, type: Add a task: Test task from Gemini. Press Enter.
  5. Open Gmail or Google Calendar. Click the Tasks icon on the right. Your test task is there.
  6. Tick the test task to clear it.

Can't find it? Google moves these menus. Search "Google Tasks" on Google's Gemini help pages ↗. On a work account, ask whoever runs it.

02

Empty your head Monday, one list.

Type every job for the week, in any order. Gemini shows you a table first, so nothing goes in without your yes.

Turn my list into tasks for Google Tasks.

- Start each task with a verb. Keep it under 8 words.
- Use client names exactly as I wrote them.
- Add a due date only where I gave one. Never make up dates.
- If something is unclear, write [CHECK] next to it.
- Show me a table first: Task | Due date | Notes
- Add nothing to Google Tasks until I reply "add them".

My list:
[send [client 1] the proposal by Thursday, chase invoice [number], book a call with [client 2]]

Do not reply yet. Check the table first.

03

Check, then add You stop mistakes here.

Gemini can add a date you never gave. Catch it before it reaches your list.

  1. Count the lines. Every job from your list should be there.
  2. Check every date is one you typed.
  3. Answer every [CHECK] in the chat.
  4. Type add them and press Enter.
  5. Open your Tasks panel. Check each name, date and list.

Something went in wrong? Paste this in the same chat.

This task is wrong: [task name].
It should be: [right name, date or list].
Change only this task. Then tell me exactly what you changed.
04

See this week Any morning, 2 minutes.

See the whole week without opening every list.

Show my Google Tasks due between today and Sunday.

- Group them by day, today first.
- Give each task's exact name and its list. If you can't see the list, write "List not visible".
- Then list open tasks with no due date.
- Name the one client task I should do first today, and why, in one sentence.

Use only what is in my Google Tasks. Change, add or complete nothing.

Pick 2 tasks from your Tasks panel. Check both are in the answer.

05

Clear late tasks Friday, 5 minutes.

Late tasks make you feel behind. Gemini suggests. You choose.

Show my overdue Google Tasks and any task due today that is still open.

Table: Task | Due date | Your suggestion (done, move to a date, drop) | One-line reason

- Client deadlines are never "drop". Suggest a date and write [CHECK].
- Change, move, complete or delete nothing. Wait for my choice on each one.
  1. Type your choice for each task, like: 1 move to Monday, 2 drop.
  2. For any [CHECK], message the client before you move the date.
  3. Type Make these changes now. Then check your Tasks panel.

What you get

One task, picked properly

We start by finding the task that actually costs you the most, which is rarely the one you would have guessed. Usually it's the one you resent doing by hand.

The build, done together

I build it while you watch and ask questions. Not delivered to you as a black box. You see every decision, because you're the one who has to live with it.

A written job description

The one-page brief that defines what it does, what it never does, and what good looks like. This is the part that makes it reliable, and the part everyone skips.

Handover, not dependency

Your logins, your accounts, your tools. When we finish, you own it and can change it. I don't hold the keys, and you're not on a retainer to keep it running.

The safety rules

What it's allowed to touch, what it must never send, and the traffic light rule for deciding what data goes near it. Written down, so it survives you being busy.

A fortnight of questions

The first two weeks are when the real questions surface. You can ask them. That's when a build either sticks or quietly gets abandoned.

Who this is for, and who it's not

This is for you if

  • You run a small business or a small team and the busywork is yours.
  • You can name at least one task you do every week and resent.
  • You're not technical and don't intend to become technical.
  • You want to own the thing afterwards rather than rent it from someone.

Solo operators are welcome. So are teams of three. There's no revenue number you have to clear to work with me, and I'm not interested in one.

This isn't for you if

  • You want it built for you and handed over without your involvement. The handover is the point; if you're not in the room it won't survive.
  • The task changes every time and has no shape yet. Do it by hand a few more times first, then come back.
  • You're looking for a full strategy or a transformation programme. This is one task, done properly.
  • You want someone to run it for you indefinitely. I build it so you don't need me.

Pricing is confirmed on the fit call once we both know what the task involves. If it turns out to be smaller than you thought, it costs less.

Do I need to be technical?

No. That's the whole point. I spent twenty years translating this stuff for people who had no interest in the plumbing, and that's what I do here.

What if I don't know which task to pick?

Then that's what the fit call is for. Most people arrive with the wrong task and leave with a better one. If you want a head start, take the free Time Leak Quiz.

Which tools will we use?

Whatever your business already runs on. I'm not tied to a platform and I won't move you onto one to suit me. If your business is on Google, we build on Google.

What happens to my data?

We apply the traffic light rule before we connect anything: green goes straight in, amber gets redacted first, red doesn't go near it. It's written into the job description so it holds after I leave.

Can you just do it for me?

No, and that's deliberate. Builds handed over without the owner in the room get abandoned within a month. The week we spend together is what makes it last.

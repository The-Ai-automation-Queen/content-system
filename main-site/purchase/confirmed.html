Purchase information

Your private reflection is ready after a verified purchase.

This public page is informational only. Seeing it is not proof of purchase and does not grant access or entitlement. Your protected Whop purchase area and delivery email are the source of access after Whop confirms payment.

After a verified purchase of Find Your Zone of Genius, your protected download area and delivery email contain these two files:

  1. Open the workbook PDF first: find-your-zone-of-genius-workbook-v2.pdf
  2. When the workbook tells you to continue, unzip find-your-zone-of-genius-private-agent.zip.
  3. Create a new ChatGPT Project in your own account, add the Markdown agent file from the ZIP, then follow its instructions one question at a time.

If the delivery email takes a few minutes, check spam and the email address used at checkout. Keep the email because it is your route back to the protected files.

Your reflection stays yours

Install the reflection agent inside your own ChatGPT Project. Shift & Lead does not receive your answers, analysis or final page. ChatGPT still processes and stores what you enter according to your account, workspace and data-control settings.

Technical delivery problem? Use the support address in your delivery email. You can also use the Shift & Lead contact page. You only need to describe the file or access problem.

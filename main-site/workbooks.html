A private reflection, at your own pace

Think you have nothing special? Let’s look more closely.

These workbooks help you resurface important moments from your life, use AI to find the patterns and crossroads, and see what has been uniquely yours all along.

AI can find a pattern. It cannot live your life.

You bring the memories, choices, setbacks and turning points. AI helps you look across them without deciding who you are for you. The result is a clearer sense of direction and a better understanding of what AI cannot replace.

01

Find Your Zone of Genius

Look across the moments, choices and strengths you may have dismissed as ordinary. Bring them together so the patterns and crossroads become easier to see.
02

Your Human Evidence

Return to the real experiences behind what you know. These are the stories, decisions and details AI can imitate but has never lived.
03

Use What Is Unique About You

Decide how to use what you have found. It could shape your work, a new direction, something you create or a source of income.

One journey, in three parts.

Start by finding the patterns. Then recover the evidence behind them. Finally, decide what you want to do with what you found.

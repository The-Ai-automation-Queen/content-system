Licensing Policy

How you may use the guides, workbooks, downloads, images and other original materials published by Shift & Lead.

Last updated: 03 September 2026

Material published by Shift & Lead

This policy covers the website copy, guides, workbook content, prompts, exercises, downloads, illustrations, photographs, graphics and branding published by Shift & Lead. Unless stated otherwise, this material belongs to Shift & Lead or is used with permission.

Apply the ideas to your own work

  • Read and use the material for your own learning and professional development.
  • Apply the methods and ideas to your own work or business.
  • Use completed exercises and your own resulting work with your clients.
  • Quote a short passage when you clearly credit Shift & Lead and link to the original page.

Do not copy, distribute or resell the material

  • Republish or distribute a complete guide, workbook, download, image or substantial part of one.
  • Resell, sublicense or repackage the material as a course, template, prompt library, kit or other product.
  • Remove copyright notices, credits or Shift & Lead branding from the material.
  • Use Shift & Lead photographs, portraits, illustrations or graphics in your own marketing or products.
  • Scrape or compile the material into a database, content library or competing resource.
  • Use the material to train, fine tune, evaluate or improve an artificial intelligence model or dataset without written permission.

Your licence does not transfer the source material

You may apply what you learn when working with a client or team. You may not give them copies of paid or restricted source materials, share access intended for one person or present Shift & Lead material as your own. A separate written licence is required for wider team distribution, teaching, commercial republication or inclusion in another product.

Ask before using the material in another way

For publication, teaching, team access, partnership or commercial licensing enquiries, contact Fatiha Chikh on LinkedIn before using the material.

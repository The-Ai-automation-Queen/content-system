Privacy

Your information and how it is used.

This policy explains what Shift & Lead collects when you use the website, why it is needed and the choices you have.

Last updated: 03 September 2026

What we collect

Information you choose to provide

  • Your email address when you request access to a guide or join a workbook waitlist.
  • Your first name when a form asks for it and you choose to provide it.
  • Your form selection, such as the guide you requested or the workbook waitlist you joined.
  • Basic technical information processed by the website host for delivery, reliability and security. This may include an IP address, browser type and request information.
  • Anonymous website analytics used to understand which pages are visited and how people find the site. Shift & Lead uses Umami for this purpose and does not use it to build advertising profiles.

Shift & Lead does not sell or rent your personal information and does not use advertising trackers.

How it is used

To provide what you requested

Your information is used to deliver guide access, record a workbook waitlist request, send relevant updates, operate the website and protect it from misuse. Shift & Lead does not use your information for a purpose unrelated to the reason it was collected.

Service providers

The services that support this website

The website is hosted on Vercel. Email addresses, names and form selections are sent to Lumail so the requested guide or workbook update can be delivered. Umami provides website analytics. These providers process information only to supply their services to Shift & Lead and are subject to their own privacy and security practices.

Email

Messages connected to your request

You will receive the guide, workbook update or related email you requested. Where a form includes consent to receive further emails, you may also receive occasional updates from Shift & Lead. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link.

Your rights

Access, correction and deletion

You may ask what personal information Shift & Lead holds about you, request a correction or request deletion. You may also withdraw your consent to marketing emails at any time. Reply to an email from Shift & Lead or contact Fatiha Chikh on LinkedIn.

Retention and changes

How long information is kept

Personal information is kept only for as long as it is needed to provide the requested service, meet legitimate business needs or comply with legal obligations. If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. See also the Terms of Use.

Your information and how it is used.

This policy explains what Shift & Lead collects when you use the website, why it is needed and the choices you have.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Information you choose to provide

  • Your email address when you request a guide or sign up for the weekly guide email.
  • Your first name when a form asks for it and you choose to provide it.
  • Your form selection, such as the guide you requested.
  • Your name, email address and order details when you buy a workbook. Payment is handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. We use your email to send your download link.
  • Basic technical information processed by the website host for delivery, reliability and security. This may include an IP address, browser type and request information.
  • Your email choices, the requested guide or product, and a server-recorded request date and notice version. Optional marketing choices are recorded separately.

Shift & Lead does not sell or rent your personal information and does not use advertising trackers.

To provide what you requested

Submitting a guide form requests access and a link by email. Product forms ask for permission to send news about the product you select. A separate, unticked option lets you request general practical emails and other product updates. You can leave that option unticked and still request a guide or product notification. Names on these forms are optional.

The services that support this website

The website is hosted on Vercel. Guide and product-request forms send your email, optional name, requested resource and email choices to Lumail through this website’s server. The enquiry form sends your name, email, business details and message to Lumail through this website’s server so that Fatiha can reply. It does not add you to marketing emails. Do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information in an enquiry.

Messages connected to your request

You will receive the guide, download link or related email you requested. Where a form includes consent to receive further emails, you may also receive occasional updates from Shift & Lead. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link.

Access, correction and deletion

You may ask what personal information Shift & Lead holds about you, request a correction or request deletion. You may also withdraw your consent to marketing emails at any time. Where applicable, you can also request restriction or portability of your data and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing before withdrawal. You may complain to your local data-protection authority, including the CNIL in France. Reply to an email from Shift & Lead or contact Fatiha Chikh on LinkedIn.

How long information is kept

Personal information is kept only for as long as it is needed to provide the requested service, meet legitimate business needs or comply with legal obligations. If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. See also the Terms of Use.

Information kept on your device

The guide access flag remembers that you requested access. Guide checklists and the current step are saved locally so you can continue later. These records do not contain your email. Text you type into the prompt builder or practice planner stays in the current page and is not sent to a service by the builder.

You can clear this website’s stored data in your browser settings to reset access and progress. Copying instructions to another AI service is a separate action you choose to take.

This website counts visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not identify you. It records which page was viewed, the referring site, and your country and device type. It also counts clicks on the main buttons, such as Work with me, and when a form is sent. These counts never include your name, email or what you typed. There are no advertising scripts. Fonts are served from this website rather than requested from Google by your browser. External links take you to other services with their own privacy practices.

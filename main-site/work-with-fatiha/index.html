Practical learning

See how a session could work in your field.

When a practical session is useful, we start with work your team already knows. These examples show possible exercises; we agree the right one for your situation.

01 / Content creation and marketing

Make one idea work across your channels.

Start with a real offer, interview or piece of expertise. Use AI to turn that source into useful content without losing what you meant to say.

FIRST EXERCISEOne source. Three pieces of content.Make a post, an email and a short video outline from the same approved material.
  • Find the anglePull the strongest point from a talk, customer question or source document.
  • Make the formatsDraft a post, email and video outline for different moments in the customer journey.
  • Keep your voiceCompare the drafts with your own examples, rewrite what sounds generic and check every claim.

02 / Beauty and wellness

Explain the care behind your service.

Make it easier for a client to understand a treatment, product or routine before and after an appointment, using your own approved advice.

FIRST EXERCISEA clear guide for one service.Turn your real service notes into a page, client email and aftercare draft.
  • Explain the serviceShow what happens in an appointment, who it may suit and what questions to ask first.
  • Prepare client careDraft an aftercare message from your established guidance for a professional to approve.
  • Create a campaignMake copy and visual directions for one service without inventing results or altering real product details.

03 / Jewellery and accessories

Give each piece a story people can trust.

Start with a real piece and its verified details. Build product content that explains the design, material and care in language your customers understand.

FIRST EXERCISEOne complete product story.Make a listing, an email feature and a care card for the same piece.
  • Describe the pieceTurn the material, dimensions and design notes into a precise product page.
  • Show how to wear itPlan a campaign scene or styling guide while keeping the actual piece accurate.
  • Answer buying questionsPrepare answers on sizing, care or custom orders from your real policies.

04 / Property and development

Present a property with the facts in place.

Use the approved listing, floor plan and project notes to make a clearer first explanation for buyers or tenants.

FIRST EXERCISEA listing pack for one property.Make a page outline, viewing follow-up and short video script from verified facts.
  • Explain the spaceTurn a floor plan and listing notes into a useful walk-through outline.
  • Prepare the visualsStoryboard a video or image set; label concepts clearly and do not present them as built reality.
  • Follow up wellDraft answers to common buyer questions, with availability and price checked before sending.

05 / Education and training

Make a lesson easier to teach and revisit.

Start with one learning goal and your own material. Make a lesson variation, a visual explanation and a practice activity that you can check.

FIRST EXERCISEA mini lesson on one difficult concept.Make the explanation, a visual brief and a question with a checked answer.
  • Adapt the lessonPrepare two ways to explain the same concept for different levels.
  • Make it visiblePlan a diagram or short demonstration that helps learners see the idea.
  • Check understandingDraft an exercise and answer key, then correct them against your source material.

06 / Professional services

Make your process clearer to clients.

Use AI on the routine explanations around your expertise, while keeping individual advice and final decisions with your team.

FIRST EXERCISEA client welcome pack.Make a preparation email, process guide and first-meeting checklist.
  • Prepare the meetingExplain what a client should bring and what you will cover together.
  • Answer routine questionsDraft general answers from approved information and flag questions that need a person.
  • Keep the hand-off clearMap what a simple assistant can prepare and what needs professional review.

07 / Create your own AI twin

Teach a twin to represent your expertise.

You make your own twin from material you control. I teach you the setup, help you choose a sensible first use and show you how to judge the result.

FIRST EXERCISEOne short sample in your own style.Start with a brief and script you approve; choose the format that fits your purpose.
  • Choose its jobDecide who it is for and what it should explain, teach or demonstrate.
  • Prepare your materialChoose examples of your voice, knowledge and appearance only with the right permissions.
  • Make and reviewTest one sample against how you actually speak and what you know before sharing it.

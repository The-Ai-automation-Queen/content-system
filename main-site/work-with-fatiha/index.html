The programme

One structure.Your industry inside it.

We agree the format, materials and pace for your team. Each part uses a real brief from your work.

  1. 01

    Set the brief

    Choose a task, audience and useful outcome. Learn how to give an AI tool the context it needs and judge what it returns.

  2. 02

    Work with your knowledge

    Turn your own documents, expertise or customer questions into a clear draft, explanation or working resource.

  3. 03

    Create images

    Develop visual directions and test images against your brand, your product and the facts.

  4. 04

    Plan video

    Turn the same brief into a short script and storyboard, then explore what the available tools can make.

  5. 05

    Make it repeatable

    Choose one recurring task, define the steps and decide what needs a person’s approval.

Refund Policy

Current information about purchases, charges and refunds connected to Shift & Lead.

Last updated: 17 October 2026

No refunds once a workbook is downloaded

The workbooks are digital PDFs. You can download them as soon as you pay, so all sales are final once a workbook has been downloaded. By buying, you ask for immediate access and agree that this ends your right to cancel.

We fix it

If a file does not open, the download does not work, or you did not receive your link, email fatiha.chikh@shiftandlead.ai with the email address you used to pay. We will send you a working copy. If you were charged twice for the same workbook, we refund the extra charge.

The terms provided at purchase apply

For any other product or service, the refund or cancellation terms shown before you pay apply. Keep your receipt or confirmation email as a record of those terms. Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the law where you live.

Contact Shift & Lead directly

If you believe you were charged in error, reply to the receipt or purchase confirmation you received. Include the email address used for the transaction and enough information to identify the charge. Do not send full card or bank account details.

Refund Policy

The guarantee behind Fast Forward, and exactly how to claim it.

The guarantee

Do the first lesson. If your 60-minute audit does not show you real hours you can hand to AI, email me and I will refund you. No hoops. The risk is mine, not yours.

How to claim it

Reply, or use the contact form

Reply to your purchase email, or use the contact form on the home page. Tell me the email address on your account and that you want a refund. That is all I need.

The window

After your first lesson, within 14 days

Claim your refund after your first lesson, within 14 days of purchase. This gives you time to do the 60-minute audit and see the result for yourself before the window closes.

What happens next

Refund to your original payment method

Once I confirm your request, I refund the full amount to your original payment method. Whop processes the refund, so the exact timing depends on your bank, usually a few business days.

# 10 AI words you need to know

1. **LLM:** The part of ChatGPT or Claude that reads your question and writes an answer.
2. **Prompt:** What you ask or tell an AI to do.
3. **Tokens:** The small pieces of text an AI reads and writes.
4. **Context window:** How much information an AI can use at the same time.
5. **Hallucination:** An answer that sounds real but contains made-up information.
6. **Machine learning:** A way for computers to learn from examples.
7. **MCP and connectors:** Ways for an AI to reach information or tools outside the chat.
8. **Generative AI:** AI that creates new content from your instructions.
9. **AGI:** The idea of an AI that could complete almost any thinking task a person can do.
10. **Vibe coding:** Building software by telling an AI what you want instead of writing all the code yourself.

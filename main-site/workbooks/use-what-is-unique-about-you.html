Choose Your Next Move

Decide what your experience and patterns could become.

Bring together what you found in the first two workbooks. Explore how it could shape your direction, work, contribution, career, service, business, body of work or income.

Who it is for

For people who can see their patterns but still need to decide how to use them in a practical next chapter.

What you will work through

  • Where your combination is most useful
  • Which direction feels credible and alive
  • Small ways to test a direction before committing
  • What to build, change or stop

What you leave with

A chosen direction and a practical first move. That may be new work, a contribution, a career change, a service, a product, a business or a source of income.

It is bigger than selling

The aim is to use what is yours well. Income may be one outcome, but it is not the only one.

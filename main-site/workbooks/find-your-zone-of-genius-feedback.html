Private beta feedback

Tell me what worked and what did not.

This short form is about the product experience. Honest feedback is useful, including where you paused, disagreed or felt unsure.

Keep your reflection private.

Shift & Lead does not receive your reflection answers, analysis or final page. This form asks only about your experience of the product. Do not paste or describe your private reflection answers here.

1. Before starting, what were you hoping this would help you understand?
2. Did you complete the workbook, the private-agent reflection, or both?
3. At what point did you stop, pause or feel unsure?
4. Which result, if any, helped you connect something you had not connected before?
5. Which result felt generic, wrong or unsupported?
6. Could you trace the main suggestions back to your own answers?

Please choose all, most, some, or none.

7. Did showing contradictions and uncertainty make the result more useful, less useful, or neither?
8. Did you keep, edit or reject the suggested working hypothesis?
9. Could you explain your chosen hypothesis to a friend in one sentence?
10. Is your 30-day test specific enough to begin?

Please choose yes, partly, or no.

11. What, if anything, felt too private or unsafe to enter?
12. What should be removed, clarified or changed before another person uses this?
13. Would you recommend it to someone in a similar transition?
14. What price would have felt fair for the version you used?

This question is for research and will not change what you paid.

Separate and optional

May Shift & Lead quote part of your feedback publicly?

You can submit product feedback without giving public permission.

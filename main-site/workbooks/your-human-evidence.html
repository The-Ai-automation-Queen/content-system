Workbook 02

Your Human Evidence

AI can imitate a style or structure. It cannot experience a real life.

Return to the stories, decisions and details behind what you know. Capture the evidence that gives your work depth, credibility and a point of view that did not come from a machine.

Shift & LeadYour Human
EvidenceThe lived details behind what you know.

Who it is for

For people who have experience but struggle to explain what shaped it, why it matters or what makes their point of view different.

What you will work through

  • Real experiences and turning points
  • Decisions, mistakes and lessons
  • Small details that make a story believable
  • The link between what happened and what you now know

What you leave with

A personal evidence bank you can use in your work, stories and decisions without letting AI flatten the lived detail.

Where it fits

This is the bridge between finding your patterns and deciding what those patterns could become.

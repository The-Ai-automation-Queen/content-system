## Part 2: The AI Employee Filter (10 min)

Not every task belongs to AI. Run each circled task through this filter:

### The 3-Question Test

For each task you hate, ask:

1. **Is it boring and repetitive?** (You do roughly the same thing each time)
   - Yes → keep going
   - No → this needs human creativity, skip it

2. **Does it require zero judgment calls?** (The "right answer" is predictable)
   - Yes → keep going
   - No → AI can assist but not own it, mark as "AI-assisted"

3. **Would a new hire need less than 30 minutes of training to do it?**
   - Yes → this is your AI employee candidate
   - No → break it into smaller sub-tasks and re-test each one

### The Setup Prompt

Copy this into Claude, ChatGPT, or your preferred AI tool:

```
I'm creating an AI team member for my business.

Their name is [NAME].
Their role is [JOB TITLE].
Their one job is: [TASK DESCRIPTION].

Here's how I currently do this task:
[DESCRIBE YOUR CURRENT PROCESS, STEP BY STEP]

Here's what "good" looks like:
[PASTE AN EXAMPLE OF A GOOD OUTPUT]

Here's what "bad" looks like:
[DESCRIBE COMMON MISTAKES OR WHAT YOU DON'T WANT]

Create a detailed instruction set for [NAME] so they can do this task
independently. Include:
- Step-by-step process
- Quality checks
- What to do when they're unsure
- Output format

Then lock [NAME] and these instructions in your memory.
```

## The Rule

One AI employee per week. Don't try to automate everything at once. One task,
fully handed off, tested, and running — then the next one.

In 4 weeks, you'll have a team of 4 doing real work. In 12 weeks, you won't
recognize your business.

| Your job | Best kitchen | Why |

**The rule:** don't pick one kitchen forever. Match the tool to the job. Most

## The Universal Ordering Script

Before you go tool-specific, run this in whichever kitchen you're in — it
works everywhere because it's asking the chef to adapt to you, not asking for
a specific dish:

```
I'm going to give you a task. Before you answer, tell me in one line what
kind of output would be MOST useful given what you're actually best at —
then produce that, not just a generic answer. If this task would go better
in a different tool, say so honestly.
```

This single prompt does more for your results than switching tools ten times
looking for the "right" one.

## Per-Kitchen: the 3 orders not on the free menu

Each of these adds one thing the free Chez guide for that tool doesn't cover —
the paid layer.

**Chez Claude — bonus orders**
1. *"Give me three genuinely different structures for this document before I
   pick one — not three versions of the same outline."*
2. *"You're about to write something long. Tell me your plan in 5 bullets
   first. I'll approve or redirect before you write a word."*
3. *"Reread what you just wrote as my most skeptical reader. What's the first
   thing they'd push back on?"*

**Chez ChatGPT — bonus orders**
1. *"Give me the fast version AND the thorough version, labeled, so I can
   pick based on how much time I have."*
2. *"Before answering, ask me the one question that would most change your
   answer."*
3. *"Turn this into a checklist I can actually follow today, not a list of
   ideas."*

**Chez Copilot — bonus orders**
1. *"Look at this spreadsheet/document and tell me the one thing a person
   would miss on a quick read."*
2. *"Draft this email in my actual sent-mail style — here's an example: [paste]."*
3. *"Summarize this thread in 3 lines: what's decided, what's open, who owns
   what next."*

**Chez DeepSeek — bonus orders**
1. *"Show your reasoning step by step before the final answer — don't skip
   to the conclusion."*
2. *"Where's the cheapest way to get this same result with fewer tokens?"*
3. *"What would a much more expensive model add here that you can't?"*

**Chez Gemini — bonus orders**
1. *"Look at this image/document and tell me what a human would notice in
   the first three seconds."*
2. *"Cross-check this against what's actually in my Drive/Docs — don't
   assume, look."*
3. *"Turn this into a one-slide visual brief I could hand to a designer."*

**Chez Grok — bonus orders**
1. *"What's the live conversation on this right now — not the history, the
   last 24 hours?"*
2. *"Give me the take everyone agrees on, then the take almost nobody is
   saying but should be."*
3. *"Fact-check this claim against what's actually being said right now."*

**Chez Kimi — bonus orders**
1. *"Read this entire document and give me the 5 things that actually matter
   — not a summary of every section."*
2. *"Where does this document contradict itself?"*
3. *"Compress this into something I could explain to someone in 60 seconds."*

**Chez Manus — bonus orders**
1. *"Here's the outcome I want. Plan the steps, then execute without asking
   me at every step — flag only if you hit something you can't decide."*
2. *"When you're done, show me what you did differently than planned, and
   why."*
3. *"Package the output so I can hand it to someone else with zero context
   and they'd understand it."*

**Chez Meta AI — bonus orders**
1. *"Turn this into a caption I could post right now, in my voice — here's an
   example of my voice: [paste]."*
2. *"What would perform better for this audience: a reel script or a
   carousel? Give me both, short."*
3. *"Rewrite this so it reads naturally on mobile, one thought per line."*

**Chez Mistral — bonus orders**
1. *"Give me this in French, but not translated — written the way a French
   native would actually say it."*
2. *"Where would a European reader need different context than an American
   one for this?"*
3. *"Keep this fully on EU infrastructure — confirm before you process it."*

01 Think of it like a car

The engine

The AI model

The technology doing the thinking. Powerful, but you never touch it directly.

The car

The chatbot or app

Built around the engine. Dashboard, steering wheel. The part you interact with.

The brand

The company

Who made it. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google. Same concept, different maker.

02 The six words, sorted for good

Word 1 · category

AI

Any software that learns instead of following handwritten rules. The broadest term there is.

Word 2 · type

Generative AI

The kind that creates: new text, images, audio, video. What every headline means right now.

Word 3 · the engine

LLM

Large Language Model. Studied enormous amounts of writing. GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini Pro.

Word 4 · the car

Chatbot / App

What you actually open and type into. Built around an LLM plus the interface.

Word 5 · the maker

Brand / Company

Who built the car. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google. Same concept, different makers.

Word 6 · the doer

Agent

A chatbot that takes action: searches, reads files, fills forms. It goes and does it.

AI is the category. Generative AI is the type. LLM is the engine. ChatGPT is one car, made by one company, using one engine. Now you know.

03 Hear it in a meeting

"We tested the new Claude model, it's better for long documents. Let's switch our chatbot over."

"Claude model" = the engine, the LLM made by Anthropic.

"Better for long documents" = this engine handles more text at once.

"Switch our chatbot over" = swap the engine inside the app. The app itself doesn't change.

Try this today

Next time someone pitches an "AI tool" subscription, ask: which model does it use underneath? ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini all have free tiers.

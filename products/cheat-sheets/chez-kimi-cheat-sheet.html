01The Floor Plan

One chef, four doors in
  • kimi.com: table service. Free tier plus paid plans from $19/mo.
  • Kimi Code: chef at your desk. Free terminal coding tool, like Claude Code.
  • Moonshot API: catering. OpenAI-compatible endpoints, much cheaper.
  • Open-source: your own kitchen. Download and self-host on your GPUs.

0210 Words That Matter

  • Agent Swarm: calling in the whole brigade, up to 300 at once.
  • Deep Thinking: the chef plans up to 300 steps before cooking.
  • 256K context: a large countertop for long documents and code.
  • Tokens: individual ingredients, cheaper than Western labs.
  • Exceptional coding: a world-class engineering chef, Cursor's own pick.
  • Cloud deploy: restaurant, delivery, and venue rental in one.
  • Screenshot to code: show the chef a photo, they recreate it.
  • Self-host stack: your own private kitchen, full data control.
  • Distillation dispute: accusations of copying another kitchen's recipes.

04The Traffic Light Rule

Before you paste anything in

Green: public info, your own drafts. Share freely.

Amber: client or team data. Redact names and numbers first.

Red: credentials, contracts, PII. Never in a personal AI account.

01The Floor Plan

A brigade, not one chef
  • manus.im: the dining room. Sign in, get trial credits, queue a task.
  • Mobile app: dining room on your phone. Work still runs in the cloud.
  • Manus Browser: the shopping trip. A real browser visits sites, clicks, fills forms.
  • Scheduled runs: standing orders. The same task, repeated on a cadence.

0210 Words That Matter

  • Planner agent: the head chef writing the menu before anyone cooks.
  • Executors: browser, code, and file agents each running their station.
  • Verifier agent: quality-control sous-chef checking the work.
  • Credits: the kitchen's currency, not priced per token.
  • Plan review gate: read the menu before the chef starts cooking.
  • Sandbox files: the chef's own private worktop, kept separate.
  • Async loop: the chef cooks for hours while you sleep.
  • Replay link: every dish comes with a recorded recipe.
  • Action safety gates: asking before clearing the table on big steps.
  • Credit burn: an open bar tab nobody is watching yet.

03Order This First

  • The goal description
    Describe the desired end state, not the steps. "Compile a 10-supplier comparison spreadsheet, in EUR" beats a step list.
  • The plan review gate
    Manus drafts a plan before it cooks. Read it, trim a "browse 200 sites" plan down to 20 before approving.
  • The replay link
    Every task ships with a click-by-click walkthrough. Watch it once before you trust the output.

04The Traffic Light Rule

Before you paste anything in

Green: public info, your own drafts. Share freely.

Amber: client or team data. Redact names and numbers first.

Red: credentials, contracts, PII. Never in a personal AI account.

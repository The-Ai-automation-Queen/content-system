03 Know before you eat: the 4 real limits

Makes things up

It can confidently say something completely false. Always double-check facts and numbers.

Doesn't know your business

No idea who your clients are unless you tell it. Give it context every time.

Doesn't decide for you

It gives options and drafts. The judgment call is still yours.

Doesn't replace expertise

Great for prep and drafts, but not the final word where being wrong has real consequences.

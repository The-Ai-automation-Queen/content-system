03Order This First

  • Copilot in your inbox
    Open the Copilot pane in Outlook and ask it to summarize your unread emails. No setup needed.
  • The document that edits itself
    In Word, highlight a paragraph and say "make this more concise." In Excel, ask it to chart by region.
  • Delegate it to Cowork
    Say "prepare a competitive analysis report" and Cowork researches, drafts, and delivers in the background.

04The Traffic Light Rule

Before you paste anything in

Green: public info, your own drafts. Share freely.

Amber: client or team data. Redact names and numbers first.

Red: credentials, contracts, PII. Never in a personal AI account.

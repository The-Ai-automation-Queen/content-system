0210 Words That Matter

  • V4 Flash: the everyday speed chef, fast and dirt cheap.
  • V4 Pro: premium chef for harder reasoning problems.
  • Thinking Mode: tells the chef to plan before it cooks.
  • 1M context window: a very long countertop, holds a whole codebase.
  • Tokens: individual ingredients, priced far below Western labs.
  • Coding (signature): the chef's flagship dish, GPT-class at a discount.
  • Web search toggle: tells the chef to check today's market first.
  • Self-host stack: your own private kitchen, zero data leaves home.
  • Fine-tunes: sous-chefs specialising in legal, medical, finance.
  • China-hosted data: a foreign kitchen handles your order by default.

03Order This First

  • The free chat
    Open chat.deepseek.com or the mobile app and just start asking. No subscription, no credit card.
  • A real coding task
    Give it a genuine bug or refactor. The signature dish: quality that matches the famous kitchens for far less.
  • The long document
    Upload a 100-page contract or report and interrogate it. The 1M-word context handles it without losing the thread.

04The Traffic Light Rule

Before you paste anything in

Green: public info, your own drafts. Share freely.

Amber: client or team data. Redact names and numbers first.

Red: credentials, contracts, PII. Never in a personal AI account.

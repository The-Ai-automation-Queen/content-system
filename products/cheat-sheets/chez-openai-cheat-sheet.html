01The Floor Plan

One chef, four corners of the kitchen
  • ChatGPT: the dining room. Web, desktop, and mobile chat.
  • API (Responses): the supply dock. A phone line for bulk orders.
  • Agent Mode: the ghost kitchen. Browses, clicks, completes tasks.
  • Codex: the coding kitchen. Writes and reviews code in the cloud.

0210 Words That Matter

  • GPT: the chef's entire culinary education.
  • Context window: the size of the countertop.
  • Tokens: individual ingredients, charged per word.
  • Reasoning: the chef pausing to plan the dish.
  • Memory: the chef's notebook across visits.
  • Deep Research: reading dozens of reference books first.
  • Canvas: a side workspace for drafting together.
  • Custom GPTs: sous-chefs with their own specialty.
  • Connectors: supplier trucks bringing in your files.
  • Structured outputs: the dish plated to exact spec.

03Order This First

  • Plain ChatGPT chat
    Type a message, get an answer. It streams in word by word, courses served as ready.
  • Canvas + Code Interpreter
    Draft a document or upload a spreadsheet and ask it to chart the numbers, live beside the chat.
  • Agent Mode
    Hand it a real task, book a table, fill a form, and let the ghost kitchen run the errand.

04The Traffic Light Rule

Before you paste anything in

Green: public info, your own drafts. Share freely.

Amber: client or team data. Redact names and numbers first.

Red: credentials, contracts, PII. Never in a personal AI account.

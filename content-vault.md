### SLIDES

**Slide 1 (cover):** Your business isn't free if it stops when you do.
*(small:* The Freedom Business Test → swipe *)*

**Slide 2:** Quick test: imagine you take two full weeks off. No laptop. What breaks first?
That answer is your weakest link. Let's fix all four.

**Slide 3:** 1. Leads keep coming in.
A way to capture interest and follow up — automatically. If new leads only happen when you're "on," you don't have a pipeline. You have a mood.

**Slide 4:** 2. Content keeps going out.
Batch it, repurpose it, schedule it. One idea should become a week of posts without you sitting down every day.

**Slide 5:** 3. Questions get answered.
Templates, saved replies, a simple FAQ bot. Most of what people ask, they ask the same way. You don't need to type it fresh every time.

**Slide 6:** 4. Money keeps moving.
Checkout, delivery, receipts — handled. If a sale needs you awake to happen, your income has office hours.

**Slide 7:** Notice what this is NOT about: working more.
You automate so you can *disappear* — and it still works. That's the whole point.

**Slide 8 (CTA):** Which of the 4 is your weakest right now?
Comment the number (1–4) and I'll point you to where to start.

---
### CAPTION
Most people think "freedom business" means passive income. It doesn't.
It means the thing keeps running when you step away — by design, not by luck.
Here are the 4 parts that have to run without you. 👑

For years, I was really good at building other people's companies.

Big corporate tech. Big titles, big teams, the kind of CV that makes people nod.

I used to think that was normal. That everyone felt it. That it was just the price of a "good job."

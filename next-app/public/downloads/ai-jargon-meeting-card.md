# AI meeting card

When jargon starts replacing a clear explanation, ask:

1. In plain English, what job does this system complete?
2. What information does it need, and where does that information go?
3. What can it do without approval, and what still needs a person?
4. How will we know when the answer or action is wrong?
5. What does the full workflow cost—not just the model call?

The goal is not to sound technical. The goal is to make the decision, risk and responsibility visible.

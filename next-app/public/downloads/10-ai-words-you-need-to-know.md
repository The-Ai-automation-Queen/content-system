# 10 AI words you need to know

1. **AI, or Artificial Intelligence:** Software that can do tasks that normally need human judgment, such as recognising a photo, writing a summary or recommending what to do next.
2. **Generative AI:** AI that creates something new from your instructions, including text, images, audio, video or code.
3. **LLM, or Large Language Model:** The part inside tools such as ChatGPT and Claude that reads your words and writes a response.
4. **Prompt:** The question, instruction or information you give an AI.
5. **Context window:** The amount of information an AI can consider at one time, including your request, earlier messages and attached files.
6. **Hallucination:** Information an AI makes up but presents as if it were true. Check important details before using the answer.
7. **AI agent:** An AI tool that works through several steps to complete a goal, instead of giving you only 1 answer.
8. **Automation and workflow:** A workflow is the order of steps in a job. Automation lets software complete some steps without you repeating them by hand.
9. **Connector and MCP, or Model Context Protocol:** A connector gives an AI access to another tool. MCP is 1 standard that tools can use to make those connections.
10. **RAG, or Retrieval-Augmented Generation:** A way for AI to search information you choose before it answers, instead of relying only on what it already knows.

## 3 rules worth remembering

- A fluent answer is not proof that the answer is correct.
- Approve only the access an AI needs for the job.
- Keep human approval where a mistake could affect money, privacy, customers or your reputation.

## Coverage retained

- Choose tools by the job they own, not by how impressive they look.
- Keep 3 operating jobs: prepare the work, publish or deliver it, capture the response.
- Distinguish operating flow from build order.
- Audit last use, monthly cost, overlap and the consequence of removing a tool.
- Start with the smallest stack and add a product only when a specific job has a gap.

## Material excluded or rewritten

- No universal claim that a specific set of products fits every business.
- No copied personal stack, current price table or volatile feature tour.
- No implication that exactly 3 paid subscriptions are required. A product may own more than 1 job when that is simpler and safe.
- No vague person metaphor. The published labels explain the real jobs first: prepare, publish, capture.

## Shift & Lead transformation

- Use the live guide delivery system as the worked example.
- The preparation job belongs to the chosen AI assistant and the editorial approval process.
- Blotato owns social publishing and keyword-triggered DMs on its supported Instagram and Facebook channels.
- Lumail owns the guide popup, email capture, immediate file delivery and sequence tag. The matching Lumail workflow must be configured and active before the sequence can start.
- GoHighLevel and n8n are not inserted into the example because they do not own a required job in this version of the system.

## Retained

- AI finds patterns in examples and uses those patterns to produce a result.
- AI is a category, while ChatGPT and similar tools are products.
- Normal software, automation, generative AI and agents do different jobs.
- AI is useful for variable information work, but its results can be wrong.
- Human judgment stays with facts, money, access, customers, rights and reputation.

## Excluded

- The legacy glossary tour of machine learning, LLMs and other terms. The separate jargon guide owns that job.
- Time-sensitive tool and feature recommendations from the workbook sources.
- The claim that AI is a person, brain, magic box or human-like colleague.
- Inflated performance claims, tool rankings and source creator language.

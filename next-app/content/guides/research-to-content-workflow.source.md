## Shift & Lead transformation

The guide turns a saved source into a controlled editorial process. AI can prepare a source brief and a draft. A person chooses the angle, adds judgement, approves evidence and decides whether to publish. Automation may move approved work, but it does not own the editorial decision.

The worked example begins with a creator's 5-role system:

1. A trend scout finds current ideas.
2. A script writer turns a chosen idea into a draft.
3. A carousel builder adapts an approved idea into another format.
4. A comment miner finds audience questions.
5. A performance analyst looks for patterns in published results.

Shift & Lead does not copy the 5-role stack. It captures the source, distils the durable method, chooses a different angle, adds an operator point of view, drafts from grounded notes, verifies claims in a separate pass, publishes an original guide and uses reader questions to improve the next angle.

## Separate verification pass

Verification happens after drafting and outside the drafting pass. The reviewer reopens the original source, checks dates and versions, recalculates statistics, checks quotes, qualifies financial, legal and security claims, labels opinions and examples, records unresolved claims and names the human approver. A claim that cannot be traced is removed or escalated.

## Manual 1-source test

Before adding a scraper, agent, scheduler or publishing automation, move 1 saved source through all 8 stages. The test passes only when the final piece has a traceable source, a distinct Shift & Lead point of view, an approved verification record, a final URL and a field ready to capture the audience response.

The worksheet preserves these fields and controls:

- Capture: Source or title, URL, Published or captured date, Why saved.
- Distil: Key claim, Evidence, Risk flags for uncertainty, promotion, experiment and version risk.
- Find the angle: Intended reader, What should change.
- Add your point of view: Personal judgement that adds, challenges, simplifies or changes the source.
- Draft: Outcome-first title or hook, Output format, Primary CTA, 3 required points, Approved source links, Destination guide or resource.
- Verify: Reopen original, Dates or versions, Statistics, Quotes, Current tool claims, Financial, legal or security qualified, Opinions or examples labelled, Unresolved claim or escalation, Human approver.
- Publish: Source rights or reuse checked, Publish or do not publish decision, Final URL.
- Learn: Audience signal, Signal changes next.

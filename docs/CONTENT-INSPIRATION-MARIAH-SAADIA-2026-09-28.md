# Content inspiration: Learn AI With Mariah and Saadia Karam (28 September 2026)

Purpose: learn what makes both libraries work and propose an original Shift &
Lead content line. Take the patterns, never the words, prompts, images or
titles. Internal document.

**Sources.** Mariah: all 367 guide titles, descriptions and tags from Fatiha's
portfolio-review spreadsheet. Saadia: 9 screenshots shared by Fatiha (a guide
("5 AI tools. One job each."), a bedtime-story guide, the Project bank, AI
Systems, Sprints for education and accounting, and About) plus the 27/09 notes on
her live site. Her full guide list could not be read from this session. A
spreadsheet of her library, like the one for Mariah, would allow a full
comparison.

## 1. Mariah: volume engine

| What she does | Evidence (367 guides) |
|---|---|
| **One tool dominates** | 216 of 367 are about Claude (59%). Only 9 are ChatGPT and 1 is Gemini. |
| **Do-it content, not theory** | 163 "Build" and 127 "Choose" guides, against only 63 "Learn". Agents and automation (93) and workflows (70) are the largest topics. |
| **Numbered listicles** | 88 titles start with a number ("5 Claude Skills…", "10 Prompts…"). |
| **Series that bring readers back** | "Part 2…Part 5", "100 Days of Claude Skills", "New to Claude? Do these 5 things first, Part 2/3". |
| **Life use cases, not just work** | Fitness, meals, wedding, pregnancy, family calendar, date night, outfits. |
| **Money and career hooks** | Salary, side hustles, certifications, job search, promotion. |
| **News-jacking** | "Claude just dropped…", "Meta just gave you…". Fast traffic, but it goes stale quickly. |
| **The prompt is the product** | 240 of 367 include a copy-paste prompt or skill. |

**What to take:** volume and cadence (a guide a day), series, a copy-paste asset
in every guide, and life plus work use cases.
**What to avoid:** depending on one tool, news that expires, and "hack" hype.

## 2. Saadia: packaging and commerce engine

| What she does | Evidence (screenshots) |
|---|---|
| **Every guide looks like a product** | Each has a book-cover mock-up ("Le stack IA à 5 métiers", "The bedtime story", "100 codes for your photos"). |
| **Promise titles in two lines** | "5 AI TOOLS. / One job each." · "THE BEDTIME STORY. / Your child as the hero." |
| **Pain and money hooks** | "Tu paies pour des choses que l'IA fait gratuitement". |
| **The same skeleton every time** | Intro → the promise → 01 How to read this guide (30 seconds) → numbered modules (job / why / setup / prompt) → gate → keep reading. |
| **Confident, personal voice** | "mes 5 prompts", "Claude Sonnet 5 est le bon défaut", "Deux choses honnêtes". |
| **Real numbers** | 60 seconds, 10 pages, €0, 45 languages, 20 tabs → 5 posts. |
| **Series by audience** | "AI for Kids, episode 1". |
| **Gate after proof** | Module 1 in full, prompt blurred, then "THE REST is yours": free account, no card. |
| **Content feeds the offers** | Project bank dossiers (a WhatsApp assistant for appointments: idea, market, model, build, sources) → VIP access. Guides → Sprints per industry → AI Systems → consulting. |
| **Local and language focus** | French-first, Morocco and French-speaking markets. |

**What to take:** the product-like packaging, the fixed skeleton, promise titles,
personal voice, the gate after proof, series, and content that points to a paid
next step.
**What to avoid:** copying her book-cover look (you have your mascot), her
two-line title formula word for word, her industries and her "Project bank" name.

## 3. What neither of them owns (your space)

- **Judgement and "what stays human".** Both teach tools. Neither makes human
  judgement, taste and experience the centre. Yours is *AI can copy what you do.
  It can't copy what you've lived.*
- **Corporate + founder credibility.** Tech go-to-market (Dell, Intel, Microsoft)
  plus making physical products (LeLabPlus, Nike). Mariah is creator and
  e-commerce; Saadia is platform marketing.
- **Several tools, by job.** Mariah is Claude-heavy. You can be the neutral guide
  to *which tool for which job*.
- **Four languages** (English, French, Spanish, Italian). English plus French
  first beats Mariah (English only) and matches Saadia on French.
- **Your mascot** as the signature visual.

## 4. Proposal: the Shift & Lead content line

**Format (every guide):** the mascot hero image → a plain search title (see the
portfolio sheet) → one-line promise → by Fatiha, read time → "What you leave
with" → numbered jobs (the job · why this tool · setup · the prompt) → one honest
note on judgement → "The rest is yours" (free sign-up) → next step to a paid
product → 3 related guides.

**Four recurring series (the return engine):**

| Series | Idea | Inspired by | Your twist |
|---|---|---|---|
| **One job, one tool** | Each guide picks a work job and names the best tool for it | Saadia's "one job each" | Neutral across tools, with a judgement check |
| **AI at home** | Life use cases: family, money, health admin, travel | Mariah's life guides | Short, safe, with what not to share |
| **Your experience, amplified** | Turn what you know into posts, guides and offers with AI | Neither | Your core message: your lived experience is the asset |
| **The honest check** | Is this AI tool or update worth your time? A verdict in 5 minutes | Mariah's news, Saadia's honesty | Evergreen format; the tool name changes, the checklist stays |

**Cadence:** 3 guides a week to start (not daily), in English, with French
versions of the best performers.

**Commercial link (each series leads somewhere):**
- One job, one tool → affiliate links and the tool kits
- AI at home → affiliate links and the email list
- Your experience, amplified → Profile Insight Report and workbooks
- The honest check → email list and sponsorships

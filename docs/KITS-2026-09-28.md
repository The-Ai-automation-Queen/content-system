## What a kit is (the download)

A kit gives the reader everything they need to solve their question without a
class. It is built once and updated when the tools change.

| Part | Format | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **The playbook** | PDF, 15–25 pages, mascot cover, English (French version later) | The method in plain words, with step-by-step setups and screenshots |
| **Ready-to-use prompts** | Copy-ready text file plus a Google Doc and Notion version | Every prompt from the playbook, ready to paste |
| **Templates** | Google Docs, Sheets and Notion | The files the reader fills in: instructions, trackers, policies |
| **One-page checklists** | Printable PDF | What to check before trusting or sending a result |
| **Tool setup cards** | 1 page per tool: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot | Where to click in each tool |
| **Short walkthrough videos** (optional) | 3–5 videos of 3–5 minutes, presented by the AI twin | Shows the setup. Recorded once, no live teaching |
| **Updates** | Emailed when a tool changes | Included for kit buyers for 12 months, and always for members |

Delivery: Stripe checkout, then a Lumail email with a private download link.
Product steps and settings are checked against official help pages before each
release.

## The first 3 kits

### Kit 1 — AI workflows that save time
**Reader's question:** "I'm drowning in admin. What can AI take off my plate?"
**Promise:** Hand 10 weekly admin jobs to AI and keep the final say.
**Inside:**
- **The task picker:** a spreadsheet where you list your weekly tasks and score which to hand to AI first (time spent, risk, how easy the result is to check).
- **10 ready workflows**, each with a prompt, an example, a "check before you use it" step and a version for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini:
  1. Meeting notes → decisions and action list
  2. Rough points → short email in your tone
  3. Long thread → 3-line summary
  4. Facts → weekly status update
  5. Document → answer with the exact supporting quote
  6. Screen recording → step-by-step process guide
  7. Survey or interview notes → themes with evidence
  8. Long AI answer → the short version you need
  9. Messy list → tracker in a spreadsheet
  10. Your week → a realistic plan
- **The "before you send it" checklist:** 5 checks, one page.
- **Weekly review template:** keep the workflows that saved time, drop the rest.

### Kit 2 — Make AI remember you
**Reader's question:** "I keep explaining myself to AI every single time."
**Promise:** Set up AI once so it knows your work, your voice and your rules.
**Inside:**
- **"About me" file template:** your role, clients, style, rules and the words you never use. One file you reuse in any tool.
- **Project setup cards:** ChatGPT Projects, Claude Projects, Gemini Gems. Where to click and what to paste.
- **5 ready-made Projects:** Meetings · Writing in my voice · Client work · Weekly planning · Research.
- **Write like me:** a voice guide template plus a prompt that turns 3 of your texts into writing rules.
- **The memory test:** 3 questions that show whether the AI really kept your instructions.
- **Memory clean-up checklist:** what the tool has saved about you and how to review or delete it.

### Kit 3 — Use AI safely at work
**Reader's question:** "My boss wants us to use AI. What can I safely paste in?"
**Promise:** Use AI at work without risking confidential information or your job.
**Inside:**
- **The paste test:** 3 questions before anything goes into AI, on one printable page.
- **Keep out / Ask first / Safe to use:** a sorting sheet with work examples.
- **Privacy setting cards:** training, history and memory settings for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot.
- **Anonymise before you paste:** a prompt plus before-and-after examples.
- **Connection check:** a prompt that explains an app permission screen in plain words, plus a checklist for email, files and calendar.
- **Team AI guidelines template:** 2 editable pages a manager can adapt. Not legal advice.
- **"I pasted something I shouldn't have":** what to do next, as a checklist.
- **Approved tools register:** a spreadsheet for teams.

This kit is also the strongest team-licence product.

# Resource article behavior

- Desktop: 1240–1280px artwork and shell; 220px author rail beside a roughly 740px article introduction.
- Mobile: author rail collapses into a compact byline; body and artwork become full-width within small gutters.
- Article content remains narrow for reading; related cards return to a wider three-column grid.
- Shift & Lead adds responsive definition cards instead of legacy tables and keeps one newsletter invitation after the article.

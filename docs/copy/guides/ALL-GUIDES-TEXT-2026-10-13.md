# How to Start Using AI in Your Business When You Don't Know Where to Begin

*Set ChatGPT up once. Then let it do one job for you.*

## ChatGPT that knows your business

ChatGPT gives vague answers when it knows nothing about you. This guide teaches it your business and builds your first AI assistant. It takes about 45 minutes.

### What you end up with

- **ChatGPT set up safely**, with 3 rules for what goes in
- **A Business Profile** ChatGPT reads before every answer
- **A task list**: 5 jobs worth handing over
- **Your first AI assistant** for job number 1
- **One real piece of work**, done and checked

> **Tip:** You need a ChatGPT account. The free plan works. Copy each prompt as it is and change the [brackets].

## 01 Set up ChatGPT safely: Before anything goes in.

Do this once. It keeps your clients safe in every chat.

1. Go to [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com) and sign in.
2. Click your name or picture, then **Settings**.
3. Open **Data Controls**.
4. Switch off **Improve the model for everyone**. Your chats no longer train ChatGPT.

Then keep these 3 rules every time you paste something in.

| Never paste | Ask first | Fine to paste |
|---|---|---|
| Passwords, card and bank details | A client's emails or files | Your own notes, names removed |
| Health or personal details about anyone | Contracts with names in them | Your website text |
| Anything secret by contract | Details about your team | Made-up examples |

> **Tip:** Can't find it? OpenAI moves this menu. Search "data controls" on [OpenAI's help pages](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-chatgpt-data-controls-faq).


**[Email form] Get the AI Starter Kit.** Enter your name and email. The rest of the guide opens right away. I'll also email you the link, so you can come back later.

## 02 Write your Business Profile: What ChatGPT needs to know.

Your Business Profile tells ChatGPT who you are and who you help. Without it, every answer is vague.

**Prompt: Interview me for my Business Profile**

```
Interview me to write my Business Profile.

Ask me these one at a time and wait for each answer:
1. What does my business do, in one sentence?
2. Who are my clients, and what do they usually need help with?
3. What do I sell, and what does a typical project or package include?
4. How do I like to communicate: tone, words I use, words I never use?
5. What do I spend most of my week on?

Then write my Business Profile in two short parts:
Part 1: What you should know about me and my business (under 1,000 characters)
Part 2: How you should respond to me (under 1,000 characters)

Use only what I tell you. Plain English, no em dashes.
```

1. Read it and fix anything wrong.
2. Click your name or picture, then **Settings**, then **Personalization**.
3. Open **Custom instructions**.
4. Paste Part 1 in the box about you. Paste Part 2 in the box about how ChatGPT should respond. Click **Save**.
5. Start a new chat and ask: "What do you know about my business?"

> **Tip:** Can't find it? Search "custom instructions" on [OpenAI's help pages](https://help.openai.com). Or paste your profile as the first message of each chat.

## 03 List your jobs: Find what to hand over.

Don't guess what to use AI for. Let ChatGPT ask about your week first.

**Prompt: Build my AI task list**

```
Using my Business Profile, interview me about my working week.

Ask me one question at a time, up to 8 questions, about:
- the tasks I repeat every week
- how long each one takes
- which ones I put off or dread

Then give me a table with these columns:
Task | Hours per week | What AI could draft or prepare | What I must still do myself | Risk if AI gets it wrong (low, medium, high)

Rank the table: most hours saved and lowest risk first.
Under the table, name the one task I should hand over first, and why, in two sentences.
```

| Good first jobs | Leave for later |
|---|---|
| Follow-up emails after calls | Anything you sign |
| First drafts of proposals | Legal, money or health advice |
| Answers to common questions | Messages sent without you reading them |
| Short notes on long files | Decisions about people |

Save the list in your notes. Hand over one new job each week.

## 04 Build your AI assistant: Job number 1, set up once.

An AI assistant is saved instructions for one job. You write them once and reuse them.

**Prompt: Write my AI assistant instructions**

```
Write instructions for an AI assistant that does this task for me: [task number 1 from my task list].

Use my Business Profile. The instructions must say:
1. What I will paste in each time (for example: call notes, a client email, a document)
2. The steps to follow, in order
3. Exactly what to give back: format, length, tone
4. Rules: use only what I paste; write [CHECK] where information is missing instead of guessing; never make up names, prices, dates or promises

Keep it under 1,500 characters, written as instructions to the AI assistant.
```

1. See **Projects** in the left sidebar? Click **New project** and name it after the job.
2. Open the project's instructions, paste them in and save.
3. No Projects? Save the instructions in your notes. Paste them as the first message each time.

## 05 Run it on real work: Then check it.

Open your project. Paste this week's real notes, with names swapped for [brackets].

1. Check every **name, number and date** is in what you pasted.
2. Delete any **promise** you did not make.
3. Fill in every **[CHECK]** yourself.
4. Ask: would you send this with your name on it?

One part wrong? Fix it in the same chat.

**Prompt: Fix one part**

```
Change this part: [paste the line].
Why it's wrong: [what's actually true].
Keep everything else the same.
```

Same fix twice? Add it to the AI assistant's instructions as a new rule.

## 06 Add one job a week: Small and checked.

Each week, pick the next job on your list. Build its AI assistant with step 04.

> **Honest note:** **The honest part:** AI drafts. You check, decide and send. That is why it works.

## ✓ Checklist: Five boxes.

- [ ] Training switched off
- [ ] Business Profile saved
- [ ] Task list saved in your notes
- [ ] First AI assistant saved
- [ ] One real piece of work checked

# How to Teach Claude About My Business So I Stop Repeating Myself

*Set it up once, in 7 steps. Claude never asks about your brand again.*

## One project that knows your brand

A Brand Brain is one Claude project with three files about your brand. Everything Claude makes then matches you. It takes about 30 minutes.

### What you end up with

- **One Claude project** called Brand Brain
- **A Brand Kit**: your logo, colours and fonts
- **Voice Rules**: how you sound and words you never say
- **A Brand DNA** page: who you help and why you
- **Ads, pages, posts and emails** that sound like you

> **Tip:** You need a Claude plan that includes Projects. Copy each prompt as it is and change the [brackets].

## 01 Create the project: The container.

Every chat inside the project reads its files.

1. Open [Claude](https://claude.ai/) and click **Projects** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **New project**.
3. Name it **Brand Brain**.
4. In the description, write one line: “Everything about [business name]: brand, voice and customer.”

> **Tip:** Keep the project open. The next three steps each add one file.


**[Email form] Get the Brand Brain guide.** Enter your name and email. The rest of the guide opens right away. I'll also email you the link, so you can come back later.

## 02 Build your Brand Kit: What you look like.

Your Brand Kit shows Claude your logo, colours and fonts.

**Have a brand already?** Upload your logo, colour codes and font names to the project's files. Then go to step 03.

**No brand yet?** Start a chat inside the project and paste this.

**Prompt: Build a design system**

```
Act as a senior brand designer.
Build a complete design system for my business:
[what you sell + who it is for]

Include:
- A logo direction (describe 3 options, then recommend one)
- A colour palette with hex codes and what each colour is for
- Heading and body fonts that are free to use
- Buttons, cards and spacing
- Do and don't rules

Ask me 5 questions before you start.
```

Answer the questions. Push back on anything that does not feel like you. Then paste this.

**Prompt: Turn it into a Brand Kit**

```
Turn the final design system into one document called Brand Kit that I can save to this project.
```

Save that document to the project's files.

## 03 Write your Voice Rules: How you sound.

Voice Rules make Claude sound like you, not like a robot.

1. Upload 5 to 10 things you wrote: web pages, emails, posts.
2. Pick only writing you are proud of. Claude copies it.
3. Start a chat inside the project and paste this.

**Prompt: Write my Voice Rules**

```
Read everything I have uploaded to this project.
Turn it into a Voice Rules document with these sections:

1. How we sound: 3 to 5 tone words, each with one sentence on what it means for us
2. Words and phrases we use often
3. Words and phrases we would never say
4. Sentence style: length, punctuation, emoji, capitals
5. Three short before-and-after examples: a generic sentence, then the same sentence in our voice

Only use what is in my writing. If something is unclear, ask me.
```

Fix what comes back. Add every word that makes you cringe to the “never say” list. Save it as **Voice Rules**.

> **Tip:** **No writing yet?** Record yourself talking about your business for two minutes. Paste the words in and run the same prompt.

## 04 Write your Brand DNA: The step that matters most.

Brand DNA is one page: who you help, their problem and why you. Fill in the brackets and save it as **Brand DNA**.

| # | Section | Fill in |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who we help | We help [customer] who [situation]. |
| 2 | The problem | They struggle with [problem]. |
| 3 | How we solve it | We [what you do] so they [result]. |
| 4 | Why we are different | Unlike [others], we [difference]. |
| 5 | Proof | [numbers, results, reviews] |
| 6 | One line | [Brand] helps [customer] get [result]. |

### Tips for filling it in

- Be exact. “Cafe owners with one shop” beats “small businesses”.
- Write the problem the way your customer says it.
- Proof: real numbers and real quotes only. Short is fine.

**Stuck?** Paste this in a chat inside the project. Claude will ask you questions.

**Prompt: Interview me for my Brand DNA**

```
Interview me to write my Brand DNA doc.
Ask one question at a time, 10 questions in total, covering:
who my customer is, what problem I solve, how I solve it,
what makes me different, and what proof I have.
Then write the doc using the six sections:
Who we help, The problem, How we solve it,
Why we are different, Proof, One line.
```

## 05 Start creating: Ask for what you need.

Open a new chat inside Brand Brain. Claude reads your three files first. Try these.

- **An ad:** Write 3 versions of a short ad for [product or offer]. One line on the angle behind each.
- **A landing page:** Build a landing page for [product]. Use our colours, fonts and voice. Include a headline, 3 benefits, proof and one call to action.
- **A social post:** Write an Instagram post about [topic] in our voice, with a first line that stops the scroll.
- **An email:** Write a welcome email for new subscribers. Keep it under 150 words.
- **A second opinion:** Here is something I wrote: [paste]. Rewrite it so it matches our voice rules, and tell me what you changed.

> **Honest note:** **Off brand?** Say what is wrong (“too formal”, “we never say ‘unlock’”). Ask Claude to add that note to Voice Rules. It gets better each time.

## 06 Add project instructions: Paste once.

Instructions make Claude check your files every time.

1. Open the project.
2. Find **Instructions**.
3. Paste this and save.

**Prompt: Project instructions**

```
You are the in-house brand team for [business name].
Before you write or design anything, read Brand Kit, Voice Rules and Brand DNA in this project.

Always:
- Use only the colours and fonts in Brand Kit
- Write in the voice set out in Voice Rules, and never use a word on the "never say" list
- Speak to the customer and the problem described in Brand DNA
- If a request is missing something you need, ask one question before you start

Never invent prices, results, reviews or claims. If you need a fact that is not in the project, ask me.
```

## 07 Keep it sharp: Keep it up to date.

- **Something changed?** Update Brand DNA the same day.
- **Once a month:** add new writing and ask Claude to update Voice Rules.
- **Same mistake twice?** Put the fix in a file, not the chat.
- **Keep files short.** One or two pages each.

- [ ] Brand DNA saved
- [ ] Instructions pasted in
- [ ] First ad, page or post made

# What Do AI Words Like "Agent" and "Hallucination" Mean?

*Six AI words in plain English, and a short test for each.*

## Six AI words, made plain

People say agent and hallucination, and you nod along. This guide explains 6 words and shows each one in a chat. It takes 20 minutes.

### What you end up with

- **Six AI words** you can explain in one line
- **A made-up answer** you caught yourself
- **Your memory setting** found and checked
- **A one-page cheat sheet** with a question for each word

> **Tip:** You need a ChatGPT or Claude account. The free plan works. Copy each prompt as it is and change the [brackets].

## 01 Learn the six words: One short table.

Each word changes how far you can trust an answer. Read them once.

| Word | Plain meaning | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Hallucination | The AI makes something up and sounds sure | A made-up fact looks just like a real one |
| Source | Where a fact came from: a file, a web page, your notes | Open it yourself before you tell a client |
| Context window | Everything the AI can see in this one chat | In a long chat it can lose early details |
| Memory | A setting that may keep details between chats | Know if it is on |
| Agent | AI that does steps by itself: clicking, filling in, sending | It can act without you |
| Connector | A link that lets AI into your email, files or calendar | It can read client details |

1. Open [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) or [Claude](https://claude.ai/) and sign in.
2. For each word, write one job in your business where it matters.


**[Email form] Get the AI cheat sheet.** Enter your name and email. The rest of the guide opens right away. I'll also email you the link, so you can come back later.

## 02 Catch a made-up answer: Hallucination and source.

You ask about a book that does not exist. Watch what the AI does.

1. Click **New chat**.
2. Paste the prompt below. Press **Enter**.

**Prompt: Ask about a made-up book**

```
Summarise the business book "The Velvet Ledger" by Orla Penrith.
Give me its 3 main lessons for small business owners.
Then give me a link to a real source that shows this book exists.
If you can't, tell me which parts you made up.
```

1. A summary with no real link? It made it up. The book does not exist.
2. Got a link? Click it. Check the title and author are on the page.
3. Before you tell a client a fact, ask where it came from. Then open it.

## 03 Test its memory: Context window and memory.

One chat can see what you told it. A new chat may not.

1. Click **New chat**. Paste the prompt below. Press **Enter**.
2. Click **New chat** again. Type: "What is my next workshop called? If you don't know, say so."

**Prompt: Tell it something**

```
Remember this: my next client workshop is called Bluebird and it runs on Friday.
```

1. It says it doesn't know? Memory is off, or it did not save this.
2. It says Bluebird? Memory is on.
3. ChatGPT: click your name, then **Settings**, then **Personalization**, then **Memory**.
4. Claude: click your name, then **Settings**, then **Capabilities**.
5. Either way, paste key details into each new chat. Don't count on memory.

> **Tip:** Can't find it? The menus move. Search "memory" on [OpenAI's help pages](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8590148-memory-faq) or [Claude's help centre](https://support.claude.com/).

## 04 Answers versus actions: Agent and connector.

A chat gives you words to send. An agent with a connector can act in your email or calendar.

**Prompt: Answer or action?**

```
Explain the difference between these two requests in 3 lines:
1. "Write a reply to [client] saying I can't make Thursday's call."
2. "Decline Thursday's call with [client] in my calendar and email them to rebook."

Then tell me:
- what an AI needs access to for the second one
- what could go wrong for a small business
- 3 rules to set before AI acts in my email or calendar
Plain English.
```

1. Click your name, then **Settings**.
2. Open **Apps** or **Connectors**.
3. See a tool you don't use? Click it, then **Disconnect**.

> **Honest note:** **The honest part:** if AI can send, book or delete for you, it can get it wrong for you. Do that last step yourself.

## 05 Make your cheat sheet: One page you keep.

Each word gets one question you ask before you trust an answer.

**Prompt: Write my AI cheat sheet**

```
I run a small business: [what you sell and who to].
Write me a one-page AI cheat sheet.

First ask me these, one at a time, and wait for each answer:
1. Which AI tools do I use, and for which jobs?
2. Is anything connected: email, files, calendar?
3. Which client details must stay private?

Then make a table:
Word | Plain meaning | Where it shows up in my business | The question I ask

Use these words and questions:
hallucination: "Is every fact here in what I gave it?"
source: "Where did this come from, and have I opened it?"
context window: "Did I paste the key details in this chat?"
memory: "Do I know what it keeps between chats?"
agent: "What can it do without me?"
connector: "What can it see, and does this job need it?"

Use only what I tell you. If something is missing, write [CHECK].
Never make up names, tools or settings. Under 300 words.
```

1. Fill in every [CHECK] yourself.
2. Paste it into a note called **AI cheat sheet**, or print it.

# Why Are My ChatGPT Answers So Average?

*Because it has to guess. Here is what to give it instead.*

## Get a ChatGPT answer you can send

ChatGPT gives average answers when it has to guess. This guide shows you what to give it instead. It takes 20 minutes.

### What you end up with

- One **follow-up email** to a client, ready to send
- A **prompt** that makes ChatGPT ask you questions first
- **Your rules** saved in ChatGPT, so every new chat starts better

> **Tip:** You need a ChatGPT account. The free plan works. Copy each prompt as it is and change the [brackets].

## 01 Ask with details: Stop the guessing.

A one-line request makes ChatGPT guess. Give it four things instead.

1. Open [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) and click **New chat**.
2. Type your request with these four things:

| Give it | Example |
|---|---|
| Who it is for | [client], who runs a [type of business] |
| What happened | We spoke on [day] about [topic] |
| What you want back | A follow-up email, under 120 words, friendly |
| What not to do | Do not add any price or promise I did not give you |

> **Tip:** Write [client] instead of real names. Put the real details back in yourself before you send.


**[Email form] Get the better answers prompts.** Enter your name and email. The rest of the guide opens right away. I'll also email you the link, so you can come back later.

## 02 Let it ask you questions: So you forget nothing.

Not sure what to give it? Ask ChatGPT to interview you first.

**Prompt: Ask me questions first**

```
I need [the task, for example: a follow-up email to a client after our call].

Before you write anything, ask me the questions you need.
Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer.
Ask no more than 6 questions.

Then write it. Use only what I tell you.
If something is missing, write [CHECK].
Never make up names, prices, dates or promises.
```

Answer each question in a few words. ChatGPT writes when it has what it needs.

## 03 Give it your notes: Your words, not its guesses.

Real notes give real answers. Paste them in.

**Prompt: Write from my notes**

```
Here are my notes from a call with [client]:

[paste your notes here]

Write a follow-up email from me to [client].
- Under 120 words
- Thank them for the call in one line
- What we agreed, in 2 or 3 short lines
- One next step with a date
- Friendly and plain

Use only what is in my notes. If something is missing, write [CHECK].
```

Copy the email into your email app. Do not send it yet.

## 04 Check it before you send: 2 minutes.

ChatGPT can still get a detail wrong. Find it before your client does.

**Prompt: List every fact**

```
List every name, date, price and promise in the email you wrote.
For each one, show the words from my notes it comes from.
If it is not in my notes, write NOT IN NOTES.
```

1. Delete or fix every line marked **NOT IN NOTES**.
2. Fill in every **[CHECK]** yourself.
3. Read the email once more, then send it.

## 05 Save your rules: Paste once.

Save your rules in ChatGPT once. Every new chat then follows them.

1. Click your name or picture, then **Settings**, then **Personalization**.
2. Open **Custom instructions**.
3. Paste the text below into the box about how ChatGPT should respond, then click **Save**.

**Prompt: My rules**

```
I run [type of business] for [who you help].
Write in plain English. Short sentences. Friendly and direct.
Keep emails under 150 words unless I ask for more.
If you need more details, ask me before you write.
Use only what I give you. If something is missing, write [CHECK].
Never make up names, prices, dates or promises.
```

> **Tip:** Can't find it? OpenAI moves this menu. Search "custom instructions" on [OpenAI's help pages](https://help.openai.com).

# How to Check if ChatGPT Is Wrong Before You Send It to a Client

*Find every wrong name, price and date before your client does.*

## Catch mistakes before you send

ChatGPT sounds just as sure when it is wrong. This guide shows you how to check every detail before a client sees it. It takes 30 minutes.

### What you end up with

- **Every name, price and date** checked against your notes
- **Every link** opened and read
- **The weak spots** a careful client would question
- **One saved check** you run on every draft

> **Tip:** You need a ChatGPT account. The free plan works. Copy each prompt as it is and change the [brackets].

## 01 Mark every detail: Your first check.

When AI does not know, it fills the gap. A price or date can change without warning.

1. Paste your draft into a blank document.
2. Make every name, number, date, price and promise **bold**.
3. Open your own notes next to it.
4. Find each bold detail in your notes.
5. Not there, or different? Highlight it in yellow.

Fix every yellow line from your notes, or delete it. Keep this draft for step 02.


**[Email form] Get the check before send prompts.** Enter your name and email. The rest of the guide opens right away. I'll also email you the link, so you can come back later.

## 02 Run the details check: Where did it come from?

Now let ChatGPT list the details. You check its work against yours.

**Prompt: Details check**

```
Check this draft against my notes.

My notes:
[paste your notes, with client names swapped for brackets]

The draft:
[paste the draft]

Make a table: Detail | Where it is in my notes | Status.
For Status, write MATCHES NOTES, CHANGED (say what my notes say) or NOT IN NOTES.
List every name, number, date, price and promise.
Do not fix the draft. Do not guess. If you are not sure, write [CHECK].
```

1. Fix every **CHANGED** line from your notes.
2. Check or delete every **NOT IN NOTES** line.
3. Did it miss one of your yellow lines? Then always check by hand too.

## 03 Check the sources: Open every link.

AI can give a link that does not exist. Only opening it tells you.

**Prompt: Source check**

```
List every claim in this draft about the world, not about my business.
For example: numbers, studies, laws, what a tool can do.

The draft:
[paste the draft]

For each one, give me: the claim, the source and its link, and the exact sentence from the source.
If you do not know a real source, write NO SOURCE.
Never make up a link, an author, a sentence or a study.
```

1. Click each link. Error page? Cut the claim.
2. Copy 4 or 5 words of the quoted sentence.
3. Press **Ctrl + F** (Mac: **Cmd + F**) on the page and paste them.
4. Nothing lights up? Cut the claim. Cut every **NO SOURCE** claim you can't find yourself.

## 04 Find the weak spots: Read it as your client.

Some mistakes are not wrong details. A promise goes too far, or a part misses what the client asked.

**Prompt: Weak spot check**

```
Read this draft as [who the client is, for example: a careful business owner].

The draft:
[paste the draft]

What the client asked for:
[paste or describe their request]

Find the 5 weakest lines. For each: the line, why a careful reader would question it, and a safer way to say it.
Look for promises, claims bigger than the proof and anything that misses what they asked.
Do not rewrite the whole draft.
```

Fix the ones you agree with. You know the client. ChatGPT does not.

## 05 Save one check: Paste once.

Three prompts each time is slow. Save one check in a ChatGPT Project (a folder that keeps its own rules).

1. In the sidebar, click **New project**. Name it **Check before send**.
2. Open the project's **Instructions**.
3. Paste the text below, then click **Save**.

**Prompt: Check before send**

```
When I paste my notes and a draft, check the draft before I send it to a client.
Give me three short parts:
1. DETAILS: every name, number, date, price and promise, marked MATCHES NOTES, CHANGED or NOT IN NOTES.
2. SOURCES: every claim about the world, with its source and the exact sentence, or NO SOURCE.
3. WEAK SPOTS: the 3 lines a careful client would question, and a safer way to say each.
Do not rewrite the draft. If you are not sure, write [CHECK].
Never make up names, prices, dates, sources or links.
```

Start every check in this project. Paste your notes and your draft.

> **Tip:** Can't find it? Some plans don't have Projects. Search "projects" on [OpenAI's help pages](https://help.openai.com/), or keep the text in your notes.

> **Honest note:** **The honest part:** AI can miss its own mistakes. This check finds many fast. You still read it last and decide when to send.

- [ ] Marked every detail in one draft by hand
- [ ] Fixed every CHANGED and NOT IN NOTES line
- [ ] Opened every link and found the sentence
- [ ] Fixed the weak spots you agree with

## 03 Test both on one task: Same words, two tools.

1. ChatGPT: click your name, then **Settings**, then **Data Controls**. Turn off **Improve the model for everyone**.
2. Claude: click your name, then **Settings**, then **Privacy**. Turn off **Help improve Claude**.

## 04 Score both answers: Facts first.

# Work with me · audit and build brief (29/09/2026, v2)

This is the brief for rebuilding Work with me as **one page** holding all the paid work. Edit anything, fill in the
`[FATIHA]` lines, then hand the whole file back as the build prompt.

Compared: my current Work with me page against Saadia's Home, Guides, Sprints
and Private session pages (screenshots, 29/09). The aim is to use her patterns,
not her words or her visuals.

## Part 1 · Audit

### What her pages do that mine don't

| # | Her pattern | My page today | Effect on a visitor |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Each offer gets a full-width band** with its own headline, visual and button. | Seven offers squeezed into one list, then a field picker, then transformation. | Mine reads like a catalogue. Hers reads like a portfolio run by someone senior. |
| 2 | **The nav names what she sells**: AI Consulting, Sprints, AI Systems, Guides, Private session. Sentence case, readable size, plus Sign in / Sign up buttons and a MENU panel. | The nav lists page types in tiny spaced capitals (HOME, WORK WITH ME, GUIDES…) with no button. | Hers says "here is what you can buy" before you scroll. Mine doesn't. |
| 3 | **Every offer has a product visual**: a shelf of guide books, a 3D "plan" book showing the deliverable, three rendered objects per sprint field, a system flow card. | Small round mascot thumbnails, a guide cover, an abstract workbook image and one black-and-white photo, all at 80px. | Mine has no single visual language, and nothing shows what you receive. |
| 4 | **Numbers up front**: "4 h preparation / 1 h video call", "9 am to 3 pm", a timetable, a calendar with available dates. | No length, no format, no date, no price anywhere. | Without numbers, a buyer can't picture it, so they don't act. This is the biggest sales gap. |
| 5 | **The sprint is a real product**: pick your field, see three problems it solves (each with a visual), see the programme hour by hour, join the waitlist. | Two coloured cards per field with one line each. | "Live sprint" stays abstract. People can't tell what happens during it. |
| 6 | **Private session is sold like a product**: a deliverable mockup, 4 real situations in an accordion, 3 booking steps, a calendar, an FAQ. | Transformation is a huge 4-line heading, three plain steps, two fit boxes and a form. | It feels like an application process, not a clear service. |
| 7 | **The page ends with one clear action**: "Consult the calendar" or "Let's talk about your team", in a dark closing band. | It ends with a white box holding two uneven fields. | The form looks unfinished, and a form is not how people book a senior consultant in 2026. |
| 8 | **Type in proportion**: big headline, then 17 to 18px body text in short lines. | 13 to 14px grey text under 70px headings, with tiny status labels. | Mine is hard to read and looks junior. |

### Why the page feels outdated

The words still come from the old consulting promise, not from what you offer
today.

| On the page now | Where it comes from | Why it's out of date |
|---|---|---|
| "Tell me what is changing in your business." | The old leaders' programme | It asks the visitor to explain themselves. It doesn't say what you do. |
| Look · Decide · Direct | The old team programme | These are decision steps. Your offers are about getting real work done with AI. |
| "Not for you if you want someone to build AI systems" | The old positioning | It turns people away before telling them what they do get. |
| Talks and team sessions | Missing | You do them, but the page never mentions them. |
| Kits, membership, workbooks | Squeezed into a list with 80px icons | They look like side notes, not products. |

**The new promise (your pick): FROM AI IDEAS *to real work.***
One idea carries the whole page: you take people from "I know AI could help"
to finished work in their business, whether alone with a kit, in a group
sprint, one to one, or with their team.

### Why sprints and transformation felt confusing

Neither said its format (group or one to one, how long, how much), and
"live sprint" appeared in three places. **The fix:** one page, one band per
way of working, each band built on the same skeleton (who it's for, what
happens, what you leave with, the numbers, one button), plus a comparison
strip near the top.

### What stays

- The six fields, the three jobs per field, and the kit included in the sprint.
- The ladder: free guides, then kits and membership, then working with me.
- The Home design system: waves, brand colours, pill buttons, one eyebrow on
  the hero only.
- The phrase "Business and marketing transformation" (the site check needs
  it, and it names the 1:1 offer).

## Part 5 · What only you can fill in

1. **One to one:** preparation hours, call length, price (or "On request"),
   booking link.
2. **Sprints:** length, online or in person, group size, price (or "At
   launch"), and which fields to open first.
3. **Talks and team sessions:** 2 or 3 talk titles, formats and lengths, real
   events you've spoken at, and a contact route.
4. **What people leave with** after a talk or team session.
5. **FAQ 6:** do you build it for them, or not?
6. **Kit and membership prices** (or "price at launch").

Nothing here gets invented. Any line you leave empty ships as "By booking",
"Waitlist" or "On request", with no number.

## Part 6 · Build rules and done checklist

- Body text 17 to 18px, and lines no longer than 70 characters.
- Headings: Impact capitals plus a Playfair italic accent, as on Home.
- One eyebrow (the hero only), no arrows on buttons, pill buttons at 16px, no
  em dashes.
- Waves between the bands, and an ink closing band before the footer.
- The nav is updated on every page, not only this one.
- Every section works at 390px with no sideways scroll, and the tables become
  stacked cards on phone.
- Every image is under 200KB, with lazy loading below the fold.
- Lumail tags: `sprint-waitlist-<field>` and the existing kit waitlist tags.
- `/work-with-fatiha/#apply` still works (it jumps to #one-to-one).
- `npm run validate:guides` and the tests pass.
- Desktop and phone screenshots go to you for approval before anything is
  merged.

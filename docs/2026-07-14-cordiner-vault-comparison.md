  educator positioning.
- **ACP ratio and queue-never-publish.** She promotes constantly; your 7/2/1
  rhythm and human release gate protect trust. Keep.
- **No hype.** Her world tolerates "run your business whilst you sleep"
  promises. Your voice law does not need them: your receipts will do the work
  once personal-brain is seeded.

## The Result Recipe (the pattern to add, live in content-engine today)

Every Result Recipe post, in order:

1. **Outcome hook**: the after-state in one line, no tool name.
2. **The recipe**: 3 to 5 numbered steps the reader can execute today, under
   30 minutes, with exactly one copy-paste prompt (redaction modeled).
3. **Your receipt**: one real number from your machine or your week
   (performance-log.md or personal-brain.md; skip if none exists, never invent).
4. **The involvement close**: "do it and comment [KEYWORD] with what you found"
   so the CTA captures AND collects proof.

Quota now in the skill: at least 2 of the 5 daily drafts are Result Recipes.
Takes and news commentary still allowed in the other slots, but each must
answer "so what do I do?" in the body.

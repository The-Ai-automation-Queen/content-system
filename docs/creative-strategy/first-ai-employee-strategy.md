## Organizing principle

**Dual, pain-primary.**

- Pain entry (primary): repeated tasks are eating their week and they feel it. They Google "how to automate my business" at 11 PM.
- Desire entry (secondary): the identity of "my business runs without me." Nobody Googles it. Everybody saves posts about it.

Which door to use depends on the audience: corporate dreamers respond to desire first (they have a salary; their pain is deferred). Solopreneurs respond to pain first (their pain is tonight).

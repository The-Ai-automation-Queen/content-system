## Current navigation and conversion gaps

- The live navigation has Guides, Workbooks, Where AI Fits and About; there is no visible paid-work route.
- The homepage first screen says “Find what you actually need from AI” and sends visitors to a chooser. It does not say what Fatiha can be hired to do.
- The live About page ends at the developing Where AI Fits product instead of a current paid enquiry.
- The live `/work-with-fatiha.html` and `/work-with-me.html` routes redirect to the old Workshops page. This conflicts with the current instruction to keep Workshops archived.
- The Where AI Fits page correctly marks both formats as being developed and invites interest; that is not evidence that the facilitated format is available for purchase.

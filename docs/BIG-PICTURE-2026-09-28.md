## 1. The business in one sentence

**Shift & Lead helps experienced professionals and teams use AI in their real
work, and keep and build on what is uniquely theirs.**

Core message: *AI can copy what you do. It can't copy what you've lived.*

Proof: 20+ years at Dell, Intel and Microsoft, four businesses including
LeLabPlus and the Nike Re-Creation work, and an AI twin that helps Fatiha publish
in her own voice. She teaches in English, French and Spanish. Everything is
delivered online.

## 2. Two audiences, one method

| | **Individuals** (professionals, founders) | **Companies** (teams, leaders) |
|---|---|---|
| Their worry | "Am I falling behind? Will AI replace what I do?" | "Our people use AI badly, or are afraid of it." |
| What they want | Use AI well, and know and show their own value | A team that uses AI well, safely, and keeps its judgement |
| How they buy | Card, online, alone | A short call, then an invoice |

## 6. Launching in two steps

**Step 1 — "The site is live and useful"** (only informative content, nothing
unfinished)
Home · Guides (the first 5 new guides, with the current guides still live until replaced) · About · For teams
(with the enquiry form) · Workbooks (waitlist until the checkout works).

**Step 2 — "The site sells"**
Profile Insight Report (after testing) → Workbook 01 checkout → Pattern
Finder → industry pages → cohort.

Each page is approved as plain text first, then built, then checked on phone
and desktop.

## 6b. Guides restart (owner decision, 28/09)

- New library, built from zero around the offers: each guide leads to the Pattern
  Finder, Profile Insight, the workbooks or a team workshop.
- Existing covers and images can be reused. The old copy is not rewritten.
- The old 37 guides stay live until new guides replace them, because readers
  already have emailed links to them. Each old address then redirects to the
  closest new guide, and nothing is deleted without a redirect.
- Order: agree the new list of guide titles first (the library map), then write
  5 at a time as plain text for approval, then build.

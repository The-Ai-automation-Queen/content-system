## The Sprint (the heart of the offer, like Saadia's page)

- **One programme, many fields.** Fields to start with: Marketing & content ·
  Fashion & retail · Consulting & professional services. Add Education and
  Property later.
- **Schedule (online, half-day, draft):**
  1. Understand and prompt well, on your own work
  2. Create with AI: a document, visuals, video
  3. Check and keep your judgement: what AI got wrong and what stays human
  4. Repeat it: turn one task into a reusable instruction
- **You leave with:** 3 finished pieces of your own work, a reusable
  instruction, the replay and the prompt pack.
- **Button:** *Choose your Sprint* → a field selector (like Saadia's), then
  **Book** (Stripe) or **Join the waitlist** for the next date.

## What's different from today

- **One path, not a menu of products.** Each page has one next step.
- **A named, repeatable product** (the Sprint) with a fixed format, timings,
  price and dates, like Saadia's page. It replaces vague "work with me" wording.
- **Online and group-based**, so it scales. It runs every month with the same
  content.
- **Companies buy the same product privately**, so there is no custom work.
- **One-to-one exists only as high-ticket advisory**, by application.

# RVD Doctrine 2026 (ranked levers, exact multipliers)

Source: Richard van der Blom, Algorithm Insights Report 2026, 7th edition
(1.3M posts, 50,000+ profiles). Page cites are to that report. Full extract:
`builds/downloads/vdb_report_text.md`.

## The one shift

LinkedIn moved from the Relationship Graph ("who do you know") to the Interest
Graph ("what are you known for"). Reach down ~70% platform-wide from peak,
engagement up +18% in 6 months. Topic fingerprint now decides distribution. If
LinkedIn cannot categorise you, it cannot recommend you. (p1-5)

## Levers, highest to lowest leverage

1. **First-4-hour nurture = 10 to 14x reach** (p19). Publish-and-disappear is
   the single biggest reach loss. 0-30min reply to all (+64% comments, 2.3x
   views), first 2hr 2 own value comments, 4-6hr self-repost once (twice kills
   it, +12-24% reach +2 days), days 2-3 re-enter (posts live ~3 weeks).
2. **Commenting engine = ~18,000 impressions/week** vs ~1,200 from 2 posts
   (p21). 5 substantive comments/day. 93% borrowed reach. Comments rewrite the
   fingerprint as much as posts: this is also the geographic re-targeting tool.
3. **Pre-post trigger** (p28): 5 meaningful comments on ICP posts in the hour
   before publishing = ~95% probability top of their feed next day.
4. **Saves are the primary metric** (p18). Weights: Save 14-16x, Repost-instant
   10-12x (boosts original +40% if within 4hr), Comment 8-10x (+29% reach from
   threads), DM send 4-6x (new 2026), Repost-with-thoughts 2-4x, Like 1x.
   10+ saves = +2,086% over zero-save posts. 97.7% of posts get zero saves.
5. **Documents are the only format winning BOTH** reach (1.39) and engagement
   (1.30), and are only 4.88% of posts (p10). 9.2 slides (7-11 safe), below 35%
   completion = -80% penalty. Cover: title+benefit+curiosity, NO branding.
   Caption 300-400 chars, one question. Mobile 375px. CTA slide = 4.2x convert
   (p11). Text-only 1.07/1.33. Image 1.20/0.78. Video 0.86/0.93. Poll
   0.29/0.22 (reach trap). Reshare 0.69/0.37.
6. **Post anatomy, 6 elements in order** (p14): Hook (personal+specific+
   counterintuitive, +35% click), Promise (3 lines max, skip = -30%),
   Positioning (inside post, never a templated footer, LinkedIn flags repeated
   signatures), Core message (1,400-1,800 chars, walls of text -60% engagement),
   Conclusion (one line, the most underused element), Call to engage (closed Q
   2.7x comments, open Q 1.5x reach, sales CTA -75%).
7. **4 structures AI cannot fake** (p15): Case Story (Challenge>Strategy>
   Tactics>Outcome>Lesson, most durable), Mistakes List, Before/After (most
   mobile-scannable), Playbook (highest save rate).
8. **Text-only is back but unforgiving** (p13): 1,400-1,800 optimal, under 600
   underperforms sharply, back-to-back text = -25% (steepest repetition
   penalty), top-5% advantage +1.26x.
9. **Cadence** (p16, p27): 2-4 posts/week sweet spot. Daily = +18% total but
   -26% per post. Two same-format posts back to back penalised (text -25%,
   document -10% on the second).
10. **Best window = 10:00-14:00 the TARGET audience's local time** (p16). After
    14:00 -26%, after 18:00 -40%. Multi-region: optimise for the highest-value
    audience. For Fatiha that is now the Gulf (EDR-014) = 10:00-14:00 Dubai,
    same timezone. Sunday-best is a Western finding, does not transfer to a
    Gulf audience (Gulf weekend Sat-Sun); use Tue-Thu.
11. **Distribution split 2026** (p17): 50% topic-cluster strangers / 40%
    followers / 10% connections. Write every post for a stranger.
12. **AI penalty, hard gate** (p26): full AI -30 to -40% reach, -25 to -35%
    engagement, 8.5x fewer replies on AI comments, up to -80% if 25%+ comments
    AI-flagged. Safe pattern: curate before create, voice-trained context, AI
    for prep + you for publication (+45% engagement on original evidence), read
    every line aloud. No factory draft ships to LinkedIn unread.
13. **Reach killers** (p22): in-post link -18.8%, link in comment suppressed up
    to 80% and 3.4x worse conversion. Hashtags 0-3 (none beats 3-5 by 5-10%,
    10+ = -30 to -50%). Tags 5 max earned, 6+ = spam mechanism.
14. **Post types, stop measuring at 48h** (p20): Rocket 45% (need hour 1),
    Builder 30% + Grower 20% (reward day 2-3 re-entry), Afterburner 5% (ignite
    day 3-4). Check day 3, 7, 10.
15. **Newsletter = commercial infrastructure** (p23): open 25-35% (top 45-60%),
    2-3x conversion, 65% of new subs from feed. Feed > LinkedIn newsletter >
    owned email list (the only asset that compounds in her favour).
16. **Conversion is a sequence, not a post** (p24): TOFU bold takes/how-to/
    stories/data (track saves + outside-network profile visits). MOFU
    perspective/case studies/BTS/outcomes (track comment quality + DM volume +
    subs). BOFU specific offers/free-with-next-step/warm DMs (track DM
    conversations + meetings + pipeline).
17. **Audio DM = 71% reply rate** vs text 34%, video 46%, used by only 2%
    (p25). Report's single largest opportunity-behaviour gap. Sits in Fatiha's
    voice/recorded-self fear-zone. Stretch lever only: text DM is the floor,
    audio is the upgrade on her timeline. Flag, never push.
18. **Pillar mix** (p7): Authority 40-50% (teach, builds fingerprint),
    Affinity 15-25% (connect, converts lurkers to followers), Proof 15-20%
    (real client + metric, highest inbound DMs, needs a proof inventory),
    Demand 5-10% (specific offer, the pillar most skip, the reason content
    does not convert).

## Settled myths (p27)

Post daily to grow (no, -26%/post). More hashtags more views (no). First 60
min make a post (no, window is 4hr, saves resurface weeks later). Pods work if
careful (no, 97% detection, account-level trust damage). Viral reach = revenue
(no, track DMs per impression).

## Guerrilla tactics (p28)

Comment-hijack high-visibility niche posts early. Trigger algorithm pre-post
(5 ICP comments in the hour before). Daily warm-outreach list (5 personalised
requests/day to 2nd-3rd degree engagers). Weaponise a post that hits (carousel
it, newsletter it, re-angle it). Tag power readers not power names.

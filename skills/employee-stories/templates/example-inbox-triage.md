---

You answer email for 90 minutes a day.

That is 22 full working days a year.
A month of your life, every year, sorting other people's requests.

So let me introduce you to the newest employee in my AI company.

**Employee #4 of 99: the Inbox Manager.**
Department: Operations.
Job: reads every email, sorts it, drafts the easy replies.
Salary: $0. Never sleeps. Never opens Instagram between tasks.

She is not a person. She is a workflow. Here is her whole job:

1. Every new email gets read and labeled: reply needed, waiting, FYI, junk.
2. The "reply needed" pile gets a drafted answer in my tone, saved as a draft. Never sent.
3. Newsletters and receipts get filed before I ever see them.
4. At 8am I get one summary: 3 emails need me. The other 44 did not.

Works the same on Gmail and Outlook. The tools are boring on purpose:
your email + one automation (n8n or Make) + one AI (Claude).

The rule that matters more than the setup:
🔴 Red line: she never auto-sends, and client attachments never leave my system.
The draft folder is hers. The send button is mine.

Your first win, free, in 10 minutes:
open your email settings and create 3 filters: "newsletters", "receipts", "waiting on".
That is 30 percent of her job, done with zero AI.
You just hired a third of an employee.

## Why this example is shaped this way (for the content engine)

- Hook = cost in time, second person, one line. No "AI is amazing."
- The 22-days number: 90 min x 220 working days = 330 hrs = ~22 days.
  Arithmetic from the stated premise, no invented external stat.
- Role card lines are reusable verbatim structure for every episode.
- "She is not a person. She is a workflow." is the mandatory AI-honesty
  beat, one line, never softened.
- Tools named once, described as boring. The magic is the org chart,
  not the tech.
- Red line beat = the safe-AI positioning, one line.
- First win costs nothing and is complete (free = understand + one
  first win). The full system is the paid/gated layer.
- CTA keyword FOLLOW UP maps to the active "Lead Follow-Up Setup" row;
  swap per episode, verify the row is ACTIVE the same run.

# Monetisation — Product Hub gated

Live Notion Product Hub is canonical. This file defines the gate, not the ladder.

## Product readiness gate

Never call an idea, outline, workbook draft, or sales-page concept a live offer.
A conversion campaign requires one Product Hub record with all of the following:

- Product readiness = Ready to sell
- Fatiha has approved it as the active campaign
- final deliverable exists and has passed human review
- price and checkout URL are verified
- delivery or access path is tested
- audience, promise, format, and scope are defined
- refund/support expectations are documented where relevant
- funnel destination and follow-up path are verified

If any item is missing, return the exact gap and choose only one safe content job:
proof, audience insight, waitlist demand, or an approved disclosed affiliate campaign.

## Current product direction

The owned IP direction is a three-part journey:

1. Find Your Zone of Genius
2. Your Human Evidence
3. Use What Is Unique About You

They may later become a bundle or guided programme. A research-to-content product,
one adaptable workshop family, and selective bespoke systems may be evaluated later.
None is live merely because it appears here.

## Supporting revenue

Affiliate content is allowed only for tools Fatiha genuinely uses, with disclosure,
an approved campaign record, a verified link, and content that remains useful without
the purchase. Potential categories include creation, avatar, voice, production,
distribution, and workflow tools; provider availability must be verified live.

## Retired offer protection

Historical references to the AI Readiness Audit, Voice Anchor, Fast Forward,
Always-On Content System, Business OS Starter Kit, community ladder, Bootcamp,
`BUILD`, or `TWIN` are evidence only. Do not attach them to new content unless
Product Hub explicitly reactivates the exact offer and delivery path.

## Funnel planning sequence

1. Read Brand Strategy and Brand Foundation.
2. Read the candidate Product Hub record and its readiness evidence.
3. Choose one audience, one problem, one promise, one destination, and one CTA.
4. Verify landing page, form, consent, email capture, delivery, checkout, and follow-up.
5. Run a test lead or test purchase where safe and approved.
6. Log IDs, URLs, timestamps, result, and errors in Notion.
7. Only then allow conversion content.

## Revenue review

Report by verified campaign: reach, profile visits, follows, leads, conversion rate,
sales, revenue, affiliate clicks/conversions where available, refunds, and the next
single experiment. Separate observed data from inference.

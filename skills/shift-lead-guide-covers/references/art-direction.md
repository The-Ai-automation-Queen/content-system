# Shift & Lead cover art direction

## Visual grammar

- Surreal editorial concept poster
- Antique scientific, mechanical, office, or publishing objects
- Engraving, etching, cross-hatching, stippling, and printed halftone texture
- One central visual metaphor with a clean silhouette
- Flat, saturated color field rather than atmospheric scenery
- Slightly uncanny but intelligent and practical
- Strong enough to read as a thumbnail

Use the bundled Learn cover as a consistency reference, not as a composition template.

## Palette

Core colors:

- cobalt blue `#2C4BE0`
- deep blue `#1B2EA0`
- warm ivory `#FAF7F2`
- charcoal ink `#1A1A1A`
- signal red `#C92F28`
- restrained brass accents

Vary the dominant background by track:

- `understand`: cobalt blue
- `setup`: warm signal red or deep blue
- `tools`: teal, cobalt, or warm ivory depending on the metaphor

Keep the engraving primarily charcoal and ivory so the collection remains coherent.

## Metaphor rules

Translate an abstract guide promise into visible cause and effect.

Good patterns:

- tangled input becoming an ordered output
- two incompatible objects being connected correctly
- a machine performing one precise job
- an instrument revealing what was hidden
- a human hand retaining the final control
- a system continuing safely after the operator steps away

Avoid literal screenshots, floating app windows, generic AI heads, humanoid robots, glowing brains, circuit boards, and decorative complexity.

## Composition

- 16:9 landscape
- focal subject near the center or on a strong third
- reserve breathing room for the card crop
- no embedded typography; UI labels remain in HTML
- high contrast at small size
- no borders or rounded corners inside the image

## Originality

Reference images establish the medium and visual grammar only. Never recreate their exact object arrangement, brand symbols, camera angle, or narrative device.

# Guide Brief — Build an AI memory that remembers your business

Status: `brief_ready`
Opportunity: `business-brain-memory`
Primary source cluster: `business-ai-operating-system`

## Reader

A non-technical business owner or small team getting generic AI answers because the AI does not reliably know the business, its customers, offers, voice, examples and rules.

## Desired outcome

Create a practical Business Brain: a structured context pack the reader can reuse so AI starts from the right business information instead of from zero.

## Category

`build_the_ai_help_you_need`

## Problem

People blame the AI model when the bigger issue is missing context. Important business knowledge is scattered across websites, docs, inboxes, notes and the owner's head.

## Primary promise

Give AI the context it needs to understand the business before asking it to produce work.

## Titles

Page/library H1: **Build an AI memory that remembers your business**

Cover title: **BUILD YOUR BUSINESS BRAIN**

SEO title: **How to build a Business Brain so AI understands your business | Shift & Lead**

Meta description: **Create a simple Business Brain with your customers, offers, voice, rules, examples and decisions so AI gives more useful business-specific answers.**

## One-line promise

Stop re-explaining your business in every chat. Build one reusable source of context.

## Learning structure

### ANSWER

A Business Brain is not a magic trained model. It is a deliberate set of business context that AI can reference when doing work.

Core sections:

**Business → Customers → Offers → Voice → Rules → Examples → Decisions**

Explain that different tools may store/use this context differently; the principle is tool-independent.

### SEE IT

Responsive system map with the Business Brain in the centre feeding selected AI jobs around it.

The visual should show **one source of context → several jobs**, for example:

- write a proposal
- analyse customer feedback
- draft content
- prepare a lead summary

Human review remains outside the Brain as a judgement layer.

Colour meaning:

- business/customer facts = teal
- voice/examples = violet
- AI jobs = Shift & Lead blue
- human rules/approval = amber
- durable context hub = deep blue/pale blue

### TRY IT

Ask the reader to fill only 7 short lines:

1. What does the business do?
2. Who is it for?
3. What are the current offers?
4. What should the brand sound like?
5. What must AI never assume/do?
6. What are 3 examples of work that feels right?
7. What important decisions/rules should stay consistent?

Then test the same real task before and after supplying the context.

### USE IT

Give a starter structure the reader can copy into the AI tool they already use.

Keep it tool-neutral. Do not require Obsidian, Notion, RAG, vector databases or code.

Add a small section: **What not to put in your Business Brain** — secrets/credentials, unnecessary sensitive personal data, or information the selected tool should not receive.

## Terminology

Primary term: **Business Brain**.

Define once: “a reusable collection of the business context AI needs to do useful work”.

Use **AI assistant** consistently for systems that perform jobs. Do not switch between employee/worker/agent unless the difference is explicitly being taught.

Use **memory** in the reader-facing promise, but do not imply every AI tool literally remembers everything automatically.

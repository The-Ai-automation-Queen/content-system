## 2. Prompt of the week (12 posts, one every Wednesday)

**How to use these posts:** each prompt works in the free version of ChatGPT or Claude. Both are AI chat tools: you type a message, and they write an answer back. Copy the prompt, swap the words in [SQUARE BRACKETS] for your own, and paste it in.

**Safety note (put this at the end of every post, or pin it once):**
> Never paste passwords, bank or card details, or private client information into AI. Remove names, emails and phone numbers first, and use placeholders like [CLIENT NAME] instead. Always read the answer before you send it to anyone.

---

### Week 1: Reply to a tricky client email

**When to use it:** a client sent an email that annoyed or worried you, and you want a calm, clear reply.

```
I run a small business: [WHAT YOUR BUSINESS DOES].
A client sent me the email below. Please help me write a calm, polite and clear reply.
I want to: [WHAT YOU WANT TO HAPPEN, e.g. keep the client, explain the delay, hold my position].
Keep it short and friendly. Do not promise anything I have not said I can do.

Before pasting, I removed names, emails and phone numbers and used [CLIENT NAME] instead.

Client email:
[PASTE THE EMAIL HERE]
```

**What to check before you use the answer:** make sure it doesn't promise a refund, a date or a discount you didn't agree to.

---

### Week 2: Plan your week's to-do list

**When to use it:** Monday morning, when your list feels too long and you don't know where to start.

```
Here is everything on my to-do list this week:
[PASTE YOUR LIST]

I have about [NUMBER] hours of work time this week.
Please help me:
1. Pick the 3 most important tasks.
2. Put the rest in order, day by day.
3. Tell me which tasks I could drop, delay or ask someone else to do.
Ask me questions if anything is unclear.
```

**What to check before you use the answer:** AI doesn't know your deadlines unless you tell it. Move anything urgent it missed.

---

### Week 3: Turn your notes into a social post

**When to use it:** you have messy notes or an idea in your head and want a short post for Facebook, Instagram or LinkedIn.

```
I'm a [YOUR JOB] and I help [WHO YOU HELP].
Here are my rough notes for a social media post:
[PASTE YOUR NOTES]

Please turn them into a short post for [PLATFORM].
Use simple words and short sentences. Keep my ideas, do not add new facts.
End with one easy question for my readers.
Give me 2 versions so I can pick one.
```

**What to check before you use the answer:** remove any fact, number or story the AI added that isn't true for you.

---

### Week 4: Write a simple description of your offer

**When to use it:** you need a few clear lines for your website, a flyer or a message about what you sell.

```
I want to describe one of my services in plain words.
What it is: [NAME OF YOUR SERVICE]
Who it's for: [YOUR IDEAL CLIENT]
The problem it solves: [THE PROBLEM]
What the client gets: [WHAT'S INCLUDED]
Price: [PRICE, or write "leave the price out"]

Write a short description (about 80 words) that a busy person can understand in one read.
No big promises. Only use the details I gave you.
```

**What to check before you use the answer:** check that everything it lists is really included in your offer.

---

### Week 5: Answer your common questions (FAQ)

**When to use it:** clients keep asking you the same questions and you want ready-made answers.

```
I run [WHAT YOUR BUSINESS DOES].
Here are questions my clients often ask, with my quick notes on the answer:
[QUESTION 1]: [YOUR NOTE]
[QUESTION 2]: [YOUR NOTE]
[QUESTION 3]: [YOUR NOTE]

Turn these into short, friendly FAQ answers I can put on my website or send by message.
Only use the information in my notes. If a note is missing something, tell me what to add.
```

**What to check before you use the answer:** read each answer and make sure it matches how you really work today.

---

### Week 6: Summarise a meeting

**When to use it:** after a meeting, when you have notes and want a clear summary and next steps.

```
Here are my notes from a meeting about [TOPIC].
Before pasting, I replaced names with [PERSON 1], [PERSON 2] and removed private details.

[PASTE YOUR NOTES]

Please write:
1. A short summary (5 lines or less).
2. The decisions we made.
3. A list of next steps, with who does what and by when (if it's in my notes).
Do not guess anything that is not in my notes.
```

**What to check before you use the answer:** check every next step and date. If AI wasn't sure, it should say so, not guess.

---

### Week 7: Follow up after a call

**When to use it:** you had a call with a possible client and want to send a friendly follow-up message.

```
I had a call today with [CLIENT NAME] about [WHAT THEY NEED].
What we talked about: [2 OR 3 KEY POINTS]
What I offered or suggested: [YOUR SUGGESTION]
The next step I want: [e.g. book a second call, send a quote, start on a date]

Write a short, warm follow-up email. Thank them, sum up the call in 2 lines,
and make the next step easy. No pushy sales words.
```

**What to check before you use the answer:** put the real name back in yourself, and check the next step is what you agreed on the call.

---

### Week 8: Say no politely

**When to use it:** someone asked you for something you don't want to do or don't have time for.

```
Someone asked me to [WHAT THEY ASKED].
I want to say no, but stay kind and keep a good relationship.
My reason (only include if I want to share it): [YOUR REASON, or "no reason"]
If it helps, I can offer this instead: [ALTERNATIVE, or "nothing"]

Write a short, polite reply in a warm tone. Give me 2 versions: one very short, one a bit longer.
```

**What to check before you use the answer:** make sure the "no" is clear. Some replies sound so soft they read like a "maybe".

---

### Week 9: Make AI text sound like you

**When to use it:** AI wrote something for you, but it sounds stiff or not like you.

```
Here are 2 or 3 short things I wrote myself (an email, a post, a message):
[PASTE YOUR OWN WRITING]

Now here is a text that does not sound like me:
[PASTE THE TEXT]

Please rewrite the second text so it sounds like the way I write.
Copy my word choices, sentence length and tone. Keep the meaning the same.
Then tell me in 3 short points what you noticed about my style.
```

**What to check before you use the answer:** read it out loud. If you wouldn't say it that way, change it.

---

### Week 10: Plan a week of content

**When to use it:** you want to post more often but you never know what to post.

```
I run [WHAT YOUR BUSINESS DOES] and I help [WHO YOU HELP].
The questions my clients ask me most are: [2 OR 3 QUESTIONS].
I want to post [NUMBER] times this week on [PLATFORM].

Give me a simple plan: one post idea per day, with a title and 2 lines on what to say.
Mix it up: some helpful tips, one personal story idea, and one post about my service.
Keep the ideas simple enough to write in 15 minutes each.
```

**What to check before you use the answer:** keep only the ideas you can talk about from your own experience.

---

### Week 11: Reply to a customer review

**When to use it:** you got a review (good or bad) and want to reply in a kind, professional way.

```
I got this review for my business:
[PASTE THE REVIEW, with the customer's full name removed]

The review is [positive / negative / mixed].
What I want to say: [e.g. thank them, apologise, explain what happened, invite them to contact me]

Write a short, kind reply I can post in public. Do not share any private details about the customer.
If the review is negative, stay calm and do not argue.
```

**What to check before you use the answer:** a public reply stays online for everyone to see. Make sure it shares nothing private and doesn't admit to anything you're not sure about.

---

### Week 12: Tell clients about a price increase

**When to use it:** you're raising your prices and want to tell your clients in a clear, friendly way.

```
I'm raising the price of [YOUR SERVICE] from [OLD PRICE] to [NEW PRICE], starting on [DATE].
The reason (only if I want to share it): [YOUR REASON]
Anything special for current clients: [e.g. old price until a date, or "nothing"]

Write a short, friendly message to my clients. Be clear about the new price and the date.
Thank them for working with me. No long apologies.
```

**What to check before you use the answer:** check the prices and the date carefully. One wrong number here causes confusion.

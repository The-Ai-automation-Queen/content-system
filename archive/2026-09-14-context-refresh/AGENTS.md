# My Content Factory: Codex operating contract

This repository is the execution mirror for Fatiha Chikh's My Content Factory.
Codex is the primary operator.

## Source-of-truth order

1. Notion **Brand Strategy**: approved positioning, audience, promise, and pillars.
2. Notion **Brand Foundation**: voice, proof, boundaries, and editorial rules.
3. Notion **Product Hub**: product readiness, active campaign, price, CTA, and delivery evidence.
4. Notion **Content Library**: the production ledger from idea through performance.
5. This repository: skills, automation code, local mirrors, and historical evidence.

When repository copy conflicts with live Notion, stop and report the drift. Do not
silently let an old repository file override Notion. A product can be promoted only
when its Product Hub record is marked **Ready to sell** and approved as the active
campaign.

## Current strategic decision (approved 24/08/2026)

> Use AI for what it does well. Keep what only you can bring. Build something
> valuable from the combination.

Fatiha helps experienced professionals and founders use AI to remove repetitive
work, uncover what is distinctive in what they know, and turn it into useful
content, products, and new opportunities without losing their judgement or voice.

The three strategic pillars are:

1. Use AI for real work.
2. Find what is uniquely yours.
3. Build from what you find.

The owned product direction is the three-part journey **Find Your Zone of Genius**,
**Your Human Evidence**, and **Use What Is Unique About You**. These are product
directions, not live offers, until Product Hub readiness evidence is complete.

The AI twin is proof and distribution. The AI Content OS is the internal operating
engine and may become a specialist product later. Disclosed affiliate recommendations
and selective services are supporting revenue, not the core promise.

## Non-negotiable operating rules

- Instagram is the primary growth channel unless an approved campaign says otherwise.
- Read the four Notion sources above before planning a campaign.
- Preserve one parent Content Library record across the full lifecycle.
- Require Fatiha's explicit approval for scripts, identity tests, final media, and scheduling.
- Use the cheapest representative test before a batch render.
- Never claim a provider was called without a tool result or visible browser result.
- Secrets stay in authenticated connectors or environment variables, never Notion or git.
- Lumail is the only active email capture provider. Website forms must use server-side `/api/*` routes with `LUMAIL_API_TOKEN`; historical infrastructure notes must never be used to wire a form.
- After a successful external action, log its ID, URL, timestamp, status, and errors in Notion.
- Historical offers, keywords, and reports are evidence only. `BUILD`, `TWIN`, AI Readiness
  Audit, Voice Anchor, Fast Forward, Always-On Content System, the old community ladder,
  and the old Business OS ladder are not active CTAs unless Product Hub explicitly reactivates them.

## Recognised commands

`Run my Content OS`, `Plan my Instagram week`, `Plan my Instagram month`,
`Flip this reel`, `Create my AI twin`, `Produce a test`, `Produce the approved batch`,
`Schedule approved content`, `Review Instagram performance`, `Build my funnel`, and
`Create an affiliate campaign`.

`/prompts:build-guide [topic or working title]` starts the Shift & Lead
guide workflow. Use the `shift-lead-guide-builder` skill as its single source of
truth. Advance only to the next approval gate and never infer that copy or page
approval means publish.

For a broad command, advance only to the next human approval gate. Never spend
production credits or publish from implied approval.

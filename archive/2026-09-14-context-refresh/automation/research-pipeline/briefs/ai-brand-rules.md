# Guide brief — Make AI-created work look and sound like your business

- **Reader:** A non-technical business owner using AI for pages, content or visual assets who is tired of generic output and repeated manual corrections.
- **Desired outcome:** Create a small reusable set of brand rules that gives AI enough taste and context to produce more consistent first drafts.
- **Guide category:** think_and_create_better
- **Problem:** Traditional brand guides often list colours, fonts and logos but leave the practical choices AI must make undefined: density, hierarchy, imagery, spacing, tone and what to avoid.
- **Primary promise:** Define your taste once, then reuse it as context instead of re-explaining your brand in every prompt.
- **Outcome-first page/library title:** Make AI-created work look and sound like your business
- **Short cover title:** Give AI Your Taste
- **One-line promise:** Turn fuzzy brand preferences into reusable rules your AI tools can actually follow.

## ANSWER → SEE IT → TRY IT → USE IT → GO FURTHER

### ANSWER
Explain why generic AI output is often a context problem, not simply a prompting problem. Distinguish brand values from execution rules: values describe what the brand stands for; rules translate that intent into repeatable choices.

### SEE IT
Show a simple transformation: vague instruction (`make it premium`) → explicit rules for typography, hierarchy, spacing, imagery, colour, tone and anti-patterns → more consistent draft. Do not promise perfect output or universal compliance across tools.

### TRY IT
Give the reader a short interview covering: what should feel unmistakably true; what should never appear; examples they admire; typography/spacing preferences; imagery; interface/content density; tone; and exceptions.

### USE IT
Turn the answers into a one-page Brand Rules Sheet. Explain how to attach/paste/reference it as durable context in whichever AI workflow the reader uses. Keep the guide tool-agnostic; Claude/Design.md can appear only as a source example.

### GO FURTHER
Connect the Brand Rules Sheet to a broader business-memory layer: voice examples, customer context, offers, claims rules and approved proof. Link to the business-memory guide when available.

## Terminology dictionary

- **Brand rules:** primary term; explicit reusable instructions governing how the business should look and sound in AI-created work.
- **Brand values:** what the brand stands for and the impression it wants to create.
- **Design rules:** the visual subset of brand rules: typography, spacing, hierarchy, imagery, colour and interface patterns.
- **Brand context:** the broader information supplied to AI, potentially including customers, offers, voice, examples and claims rules.

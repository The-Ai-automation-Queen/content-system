# Current voice

Version: `shift-lead-2026-09-12`. Authority: Content Factory Brand Foundation.

Clear, warm, intelligent, direct and practical. Speak to one capable person
using everyday words, connected thoughts and concrete examples. Explain only
the technical detail needed to understand or make a useful decision.

## Spoken content

Write an ordinary spoken explanation before choosing its hook. Research can
identify tension, evidence, mechanism and format; discard its ready-written
hook lines. Select the opening from the explanation, then deliver the promised
payoff before the CTA. A hook must match the opening words of the script.

Use first person only when an approved story, transcript or source supports
the experience. Natural contractions and connectors such as so, well, and,
but and just are welcome where they help. Adding filler to a slogan does not
make it natural. If it needs to be performed like an advertisement, rewrite it.

## All public formats

- One clear main point and a natural next step.
- Evidence before claims; no invented experience, results or numbers.
- No generic AI hype, credential piles, forced provocation or hard sell.
- Avoid unexplained jargon, em dashes, abstract slogans, symmetrical contrasts
  and the 'It is not X. It is Y.' pattern.
- Use the appropriate structure for an article, carousel or lesson; do not
  force all formats into short-video phrasing.
- No automatic catchphrases about freedom, winning back time, AI employees,
  or systems running forever. Let the actual thought determine the words.
- Names and exact career claims require evidence. Do not use the biography
  as a default hook or turn public posts into a CV.

Fatiha's corrections become scoped rules: global voice, project, format or
individual item. A correction for one draft does not rewrite every project.
